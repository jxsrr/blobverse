> [!info] Welcome, Player
> This is your campaign wiki. It is **safe**. Nothing here will spoil the plot. If you see it here, your character can know it.

## Where to Start
1. **Read the latest session recap.** Start with [[oversee/sessions/Session 07 — Opressed]].
2. **Check the Active Quests.** See what's still open.
3. **Look up an NPC.** If you met someone and forgot their name, If you met someone and forgot their name, browse the NPCs folder.
4. **Check your PC's page.** See what the party knows about you.

## What's Safe to Read
- **Session Recaps** — everything that happened.
- **PC Pages** — everything your party knows about each other.
- **NPC Pages** — everything you've learned about them.
- **Location Pages** — everything you've explored.
- **Lore Pages** — the history and cosmology you've discovered.

## What's NOT Here
- DM secrets. If it's not here, your character doesn't know it.
- Future plot. If it's not here, it hasn't happened yet.
- Other players' secrets. If they haven't told you, it's not here.

## How to Contribute
- If you want to add a note, tell the DM. They can add it to the wiki.
- If you notice something is missing, tell the DM.
- If you have a theory, write it down. The DM might use it.

## The Golden Rule
- **If it's not on the wiki, it's not canon.** Your character cannot reference it.
- **If it's on the wiki, it's fair game.** Your character can know it, use it, or plan around it.
