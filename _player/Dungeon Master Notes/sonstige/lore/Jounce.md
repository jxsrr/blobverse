> [!info] Jounce
> The traditional team sport of magic in Sonstige. Centuries old. Its ball is a living creature — the **Jounceling**.

## A Brief History
- **Jounce originated in the rural communities of Sonstige**, centuries ago.
- It was first played by village children using a small magical creature called a **Jounceling**.
- Jouncelings naturally float, are resilient, and are drawn to motion.
- Children competed to herd the creature into a wooden pen.
- Over time it grew into an organised inter-village sport.
- The name "Jounce" comes from an old regional word tied to the creature's bouncing motion.
- Today, Jounce is played in schools, universities, local clubs, and professional leagues across Sonstige.
- Very popular among students — it demands athleticism, coordination, strategy, and familiarity with magical creatures.

## The Jounceling
- A small domesticated magical creature. Not a magical object — a **living creature** with its own instincts and personality.
- Size: 25–35 cm in diameter. Weight: 1–3 kg.
- Appearance: Round body, small limbs, protruding eyes.
- Movement: Rolls, bounces, floats, flies short distances.
- Temperament: Curious, energetic, sometimes stubborn.
- Can be trained to recognise basic signals, but **does not always obey**.
- Each match uses **one official Jounceling**. Inspected before and after.

## The Jounceling Rules (Official)
1. The Jounceling must be healthy and trained.
2. Players are forbidden to harm or frighten the Jounceling.
3. Magical manipulation of the Jounceling during a match is **forbidden**.
4. Each match uses one registered Jounceling.
5. The Jounceling is inspected before and after each match.

## The Gimmick — The Jounceling Has Its Own Will
- A Jounceling is not an ordinary ball. It is a living creature that actively responds to how players treat it.
- **Temperament:** Each Jounceling has its own personality — Playful (often breaks loose, changes direction), Timid (avoids crowds), Competitive (moves into open space), Stubborn (refuses to be passed), Curious (gets distracted).
- **The Bond:** A player who trains often with a particular Jounceling can learn its behaviour — but that does not guarantee obedience. The best players are not the strongest throwers, but those who **know where the Jounceling will go before it goes**.
- **The Rebound:** When thrown, a Jounceling can decide for itself — continue, speed up, bounce away, change direction mid-air, or hover motionless for a moment.
- **The Unclaimed Ball:** If no one is holding the Jounceling, it becomes an independent participant. It can move freely, avoid both teams, even enter a goal by itself. But it **cannot score** without possession.

## The Field
- A rectangular field with two goals.
- **Centre Circle** — starting/restart point.
- **Open Field** — the main area.
- **Scoring Arc** — the shooting zone around each goal.
- **Goal Zone** — a forbidden area near the goal.
- Goals: a **high hoop** atop a sturdy frame.

## Teams — 5 vs 5
| Position | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Keeper** | Protects the goal, prevents scoring |
| **Striker** | Primary scorer |
| **Vouter** | Controls possession, directs the attack |
| **Guard** | Defends, intercepts the opposition |
| **Runner** | Opens space, moves between positions |

## Match Rules
- **Duration:** 2 halves × 15 minutes. 5-minute break. Time controlled by the referee.
- **Extra time:** 5 minutes if the score is level in a knockout.
- **Starting:** Both teams line up on the edge of the Centre Circle. The referee releases the Jounceling into the air. The first team to gain possession takes the opening possession.
- **Possession:**
  - Permitted: hold with both hands, carry for up to 5 steps, pass, throw to a teammate, guide with hand signals, allow it to move on its own.
  - Forbidden: hold longer than 5 seconds, kick or strike the Jounceling, deliberately throw it at the ground, magic, hide it in clothing.
  - **The Escape:** If the Jounceling breaks loose on its own, play continues. Both teams may contest it.
- **Scoring:**
  - 1 point: from inside the Scoring Arc.
  - 2 points: from outside the Scoring Arc.
  - 3 points: aerial from beyond the shooting line.
  - A goal only counts if the Jounceling passes fully through the hoop.
- **Defending:** Permitted to intercept, block, mark, contest, and position between the Jounceling and the goal. Physical contact is limited to shoulder-to-shoulder. Grabbing, tackling, and pushing are forbidden.
- **Turnovers:** Possession changes if the Jounceling is intercepted, a possession violation occurs, the Jounceling leaves the field, or a foul is committed. If the Jounceling breaks loose on its own — it does not automatically go to either team.
- **Fouls:**
  - Illegal physical contact → free possession.
  - Deliberately harming the Jounceling → **immediate suspension**.
  - Magic affecting the match → **immediate suspension**.
  - Hold >5 seconds / >5 steps → turnover.
  - Illegally entering the Goal Zone → free throw.
  - Interfering with the Keeper → penalty shot.

## Special Situations
- **The Free Flight:** The Jounceling flies independently — it may be caught, but flying magic may not be used.
- **The Wild Run:** The Jounceling breaks loose and moves freely — the referee gives 10 seconds. If no one gains possession, restart at the Centre Circle.
- **The Golden Jounce:** Extra time — the first team to score **wins outright**.

## Jounce at FBA
- Jounce is the **most popular extracurricular** at FBA.
- Each year FBA holds the **Jounce Cup** — an inter-house championship.
- Each house has its own official Jounce team.
- Students may attend **open tryouts** at the start of the school year.
- Seniors often coach juniors.
- Inter-house rivalries often spill off the field.
- The Jounce Cup final draws students and staff from the whole school.

## FBA's The Bateleur Eagle — The Varsity Team
- **The Bateleur Eagle** is **FBA's Jounce varsity team** — the all-star team that represents the school in inter-school and professional-level competition.
- Composed of the best players from **all four houses**.
- Current captain: **Lilia Etheridge II**.
- Coach: **Coach Garrick**.
- The name "Bateleur" is taken from the **Bateleur eagle** — a bird famous for its distinctive rocking flight, echoing the unpredictable behaviour of the Jounceling.
- **The Jounce Cup** (inter-house) is separate from **The Bateleur Eagle** (inter-school varsity).

## FBA Traditions
- Before the Jounce Cup final, both teams are introduced alongside their Jounceling. The Jounceling is given a name, a number, and its own team insignia. Sometimes the Jounceling is more popular than the players.
- Retired professional Jouncelings are often adopted into comfortable lives.
- Specialist breeders raise Jouncelings for competitive matches. Certain bloodlines are highly sought after.
- Professional Jounce leagues exist in the great cities. International tournaments exist.
