> [!quote] Lirik lagu terakhir Oliver Deen, dirilis posthumously pada 2016.

## Verse 1
The eagle flies backwards tonight,
Its wings are clipped, its eyes are white.
The garden grows where no one looks,
The fruit is ripe, the branch is hooked.

## Chorus
Don't eat the fruit, don't take the bite,
The life you steal will steal your light.
The eagle flies backwards, the Roost is bare,
The Black Cloak waits, the Void is there.

## Verse 2
Two thousand one, the first went down,
Two thousand sixteen, the eagle drowned.
Two thousand twenty-six, the third will fall,
The Bride will rise, the Void will call.

## Bridge
I saw the light above the sky,
I saw the truth behind the lie.
They silenced me, but not my song,
Remember me when I am gone.

## What It Means
- "The eagle flies backwards" — Bateleur Eagle terbang ke Void.
- "Don't eat the fruit" — jangan makan buah berbentuk kepala bayi.
- "Two thousand one, the first went down" — 2001 First Delivery.
- "Two thousand sixteen, the eagle drowned" — 2016 Bateleur Incident.
- "Two thousand twenty-six, the third will fall" — 2026 Third Thickening.
- "The Bride will rise" — Lilie menjadi Bride of Bellweather.
