> [!info] The World of Sonstige
> The third world in the known blobverse — after **Oversee** and **Aede**. The story centres on the city of **Calamara**, in the country of **Negara Anderes**. Magic is entirely ordinary here.

## World Hierarchy
- **Sonstige** — the third world in the blobverse.
- **Negara Anderes** — the primary country of the campaign.
- **Calamara** — a small, prosperous city. Its reputation is flawless. Its history is not.
- **The Free Bird Academy (FBA)** — the elite school in Calamara.

## Calamara
- A small, prosperous city. Magic is entirely ordinary here.
- Its reputation is flawless. Its history is not.
- Home to respected families, magical businesses, temples, duelling athletes, academics, and politicians.

## The Void
- The **Void** is the local name for the **Gap** — the wound in reality.
- Sonstige's Void is thinning. The Overseer is beginning to notice.
- The official solution: **thicken the Void** by using the school as a battery.

## What Is Known
- Campaign year: **2026**.
- Three important dates: **2001, 2016, 2026**.
- FBA has a myth about **The Bell** — a bell that rings before major exams, before the social season's dances, and above all before graduation.
