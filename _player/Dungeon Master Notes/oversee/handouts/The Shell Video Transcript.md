> [!quote] A transcript of Guanik Wiseman's video, as recorded on the primitive space probe. Translated from Old Oversee.

## Minute 1:00
*"This is Guanik Wiseman. The date is the 7th day of the 10th month, 1040 BV. I am recording this for... for whoever finds it. For whoever needs to know."*

## Minute 1:30
*"For six minutes, the sky turned a color I cannot describe. Not light. Not dark. Something in between. My eyes hurt. My mind... my mind refused to understand it."*

## Minute 2:15
*"The object is descending. It is not a meteor. Meteors burn. This one is... slowing down. It is braking. Something is controlling it."*

## Minute 3:00
*"It is humanoid in shape. It is larger than the moon. It has a face — but the face is wrong. It is... it is like an insect and a fish had a child."*

## Minute 4:30
*"The probe has reached it. I am looking at the surface. It is... scaly. Like a fish. Like a serpent. And there are cracks. Deep cracks. Along the back."*

## Minute 5:15
*"The probe is over the back now. I am looking down into the cracks. There is... nothing. It is hollow. The entire object... is hollow."*

## Minute 5:45
*"This is not a creature. This is a shell. A shed skin. Something wore this. Something enormous. Something that is no longer here."*

## Minute 6:00
*"If this is only its skin... where is the creature now? And when will it return for its old shell?"*

## End of Recording
