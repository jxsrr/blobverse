> [!info] The cipher was found in Graen, Rioquan East. Nenek Laksmi provided the key.

## The Cipher Text
TABVPTXLQYURAQIOUOVQPA!VOZJINOULSUINXAUPVTRREPSIQDWJVHPPVIPXHFPSPVEAGRIWUXVZBNURH

## The Key
**ALEJANDRO**

## The Decoded Message
> *"TARGET UTAMA ADALAH TOPAJU! DI TANGAN GURU. AKU SUSUL. PERGI KE OBSERVATORIUM RQ BARAT."*

## Translation
> *"The main target is TOPAJU! In the hands of the teacher. I follow. Go to the West RQ Observatory."*

## What It Means
- **Topaju** is one of the four Gemhearts. It is inside **[[oversee/npcs/Lei Darvin Thorne|Darvin]]**.
- "The teacher" refers to **Lei Alejandro** — the "guru" (teacher) of [[oversee/npcs/Lei Livia Vane|Livia]].
- The message was written by someone tracking Alejandro.
- The **West RQ Observatory** is the destination.
