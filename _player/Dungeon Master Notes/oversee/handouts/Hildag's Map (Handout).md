> [!info] A hand-drawn map on old parchment. Four continents are visible. Some areas are blank.

## What Is Marked
- **Harka** — fully mapped. Cities, villages, rivers, mountains. Complete.
- **Pirizia** — mostly mapped. The north is blank. The east is marked only with "Here be storms."
- **Rioquan** — the south is mapped. The east is marked "The Tear." The rest is blank.
- **Elis** — only the coastline is mapped. The interior is completely blank.

## What Is Missing
- The deep north of Pirizia. (The Spire of Whispering Ice.)
- The deep canyons of Rioquan. (Canyon of Lament.)
- The jungles of Elis. (The First Tree, Chenalia, the Arleschia lands.)
- The location of the **Bahtera Bawah Tanah**.
- The **Gap-touched zones**.

## Hildag's Note (in the margin)
> *"I have mapped what I can. The rest is for my descendants. Fill in the blanks. Do not let the world be forgotten."*
