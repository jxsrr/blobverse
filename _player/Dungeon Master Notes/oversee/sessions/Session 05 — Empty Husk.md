> The party lived the last moments of the Initial Six. A dragon was healed. A cousin was killed. And a shell cracked open.

## Part 1: The Body of a Bard

They sailed on the same ship that had carried them from **[[Pulau Dima]]** to **[[Kota Juto]]** — but now it belonged to **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/factions/Will of Grin|Will of Grin]]**.

**[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Accarus Grin|Accarus]]** was with them. So was the body of **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Sam Hurls|Sam Hurls]]** — wrapped in cloth, laid in a small cabin, preserved by something none of them understood.

During the voyage, **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Syl Frost|Syl]]** — who had spent long nights with the **Berkas Penelitian Cycle Clan** at the Lyceum — finally understood what the four gems were.

> *"They are not separate treasures. They are organs of a single being — the Architect. Together, they are called the **Gemhearts**."*

She named them:
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/items/Emelanica|Emelanica]]** — green, time and mana flow
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/items/Safayer|Safayer]]** — blue, purification of souls
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/items/Rubion|Rubion]]** — red, instant annihilation
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/items/Topaju|Topaju]]** — yellow, structure and stability

The party had two of these. They needed the other two.

Later, they asked about the legendary weapons — the six blades of Simon Greenbottle's legacy. Accarus answered:

- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/items/Gustaero — The Swan Bow|Gustaero]]** — Accarus carried it
- **Rayio** — lost in **[[Hutan Garberro]]**
- **Kkana** — carried by **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Mira Nouveau|Mira Nouveau]]**
- **Frostfang** — lost in **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/locations/Frosthaven Peak|Frosthaven Peak]]**
- **Zenahhi** — lost in **[[Ngarai Ratapan]]**
- **Terrde** — lost in the ruins of the Rapture
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/items/Swaith — The Soul-Thirsting Blade|Swaith]]** — in **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Shin Van Hel|HVS]]**'s hand

## Part 2: The Memory of Sam Hurls

They entered the cabin where Sam's body lay.

**[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Husgus|Husgus]]** touched it first.

The world went cold. Then white. Then **gone**.

They woke in a room that was not a room. Blackness, and stars, and the feeling of being untethered from their bodies.

But they were not themselves.

**[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Husgus|Husgus]]** looked down at his hands and saw the hands of a half-orc, old and scarred. **[[Vya Arleschia|Vya]]** felt a beard on her face. **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Syl Frost|Syl]]** was a dragon of orange-gold scales. **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Zure Ama|Ama]]** felt the weight of a sailor's coat. **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Shin Van Hel|HVS]]** was shorter, grey-skinned, smiling with the eternal grin of a Grin.

They were inside the memory of **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Sam Hurls|Sam Hurls]]** — playing the roles of the **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/lore/The Initial Six|Initial Six]]**.

They could not speak freely. The memory forced words from their mouths, one after the other, in the order Sam had remembered.

> **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Lei Alejandro|Lei Alejandro]]** *(through Vya)*: *"Do not look down. Look at the vortex. What we begin today will be the foundation of a thousand years of peace in Oversee."*

> **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Atreus Grin|Atreus Grin]]** *(through HVS)*: *"Ah, the earth trembles so much! I can feel the roots moving — opening their mouths to welcome our sweet flesh!"*

> **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Sam Hurls|Sam Hurls]]**: *"Listen to the melody... not a fanfare of victory, but a quiet requiem creeping from behind the stars. Beautiful. And deadly."*

> **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Gzirmo Kopling|Gzirmo Kopling]]** *(through Syl)*: *"Position locked. Energy parameters stable in the red zone. Lei, make sure your calculations for the containment capacity are accurate."*

> **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Atlan Nouveau|Atlan Nouveau]]** *(through Ama)*: *"My thoughts drift to Reins and Mira... something tells me tomorrow's sun will feel colder than usual."*

> **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Olaf Guirela|Olaf Guirela]]** *(through Husgus)*: *"You talk too much of flowers. Focus! My skin is crawling, and not because of your magic. Something is **wrong** here."*

> **Alejandro**: *"Do not fear the pain. Pain is only a signal that we are trading the mortal for the absolute."*

> **Atreus**: *"Hahaha! Look! The sky is smiling too! So wide — exactly like my face! It's hungry, Lei! It's so hungry, just like my ancestors!"*

> **Sam**: *"I'll pluck the last string as loud as I can! Let the Great Hunger hear us and come down to dance on the ashes!"*

> **Gzirmo**: *"Wait — my armor indicators are lighting up. An external override on my movement system. Why can't I step back?"*

> **Atlan**: *"Lei! The mana flow has reversed! This isn't channeling into the Portal — this is **extraction**! You're draining our souls!"*

> **Olaf**: *"It's a trap! This circle isn't a shield — it's a **cattle pen**! Draw your weapons, now!"*

> **Alejandro**: *"Forgive me... truly. But a foundation that stands must be built from the hardest stone. And only your souls are strong enough to hold back His hunger."*

> **Atreus**: *"You think you've won, Lei? This smile... this curse... will **haunt** you! Let history remember me as a monster — but I am the one who laughs last!"*

> **Sam**: *"A sour note of betrayal... a bad climax, Lei. You cut the music right before the best part."*

> **Gzirmo**: *"System failure... Brotherhood protocol... betrayed. Lei, you didn't account for... the variable... of **conscience**."*

> **Atlan**: *"Whoever hears this — tell **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Mira Nouveau|Mira]]**! Don't let the truth die with us! This wasn't destiny — this was **murder**!"*

> **Olaf**: *"You coward, Lei! You didn't have the courage to face your own death, so you **stole** our breath! Curse you!"*

The memory shattered.

They woke in the cabin, hands still touching Sam's body. They pulled back as if burned.

Sam's face was peaceful. But they knew now that he had not died in peace.

## Part 3: The Smiling Heirs

They arrived at **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/locations/Maw Desert|Maw Desert]]** — the Will of Grin's underground base, three basement floors deep beneath the sand and stone.

They met the Grin family:
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Geneva Grin|Geneva Grin]]** — Accarus's wife, kind but hard
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Hildag Grin|Hildag Grin]]** — Geneva's mother, old, sharp-eyed, always smiling
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Jarko|Jarko]]** — Accarus and Geneva's son. **Vya's cousin.** Their fathers — Accarus and **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Antrem Klaus|Antrem]]** — were twins.

Hildag, they learned, was a **cartographer**. She had spent her entire life mapping the world — and had never finished. No one had. She gave them her map — incomplete, marked with blank spaces — and asked them to finish it.

She also gave them a letter. **Atreus Grin's** last words, written before the ritual, before everything.

> *For whoever inherits this Smile.*
>
> *We will not return home.*
>
> *The stage is set, but our Puppeteer forgot the full script. He stands there with his arrogant green eyes, thinking he can calm the storm with nothing but Wind's Whisper.*
>
> *How laughable! He thinks himself the Gatekeeper, when he is no more than a page serving the night's meal to its master.*
>
> *And Sam... ah, poor Sam. He keeps humming beside him. His song is off-key, but somehow it feels so sweet.*
>
> *Don't look for my body on this earth. I am no longer there. I have become roots, holding these cracked walls from ruin.*
>
> *If you want to understand this bitter joke... if you want to know why the world hasn't collapsed today... or how to collapse it later...*
>
> *Don't look for who. Look for what has been lost. All keys reside in Emelanica.*
>
> *Laugh for me. I will perform my last show.*
>
> *— A.G.*

## Part 4: Vya and Antrem

**[[Vya Arleschia|Vya]]** finally met her father.

**[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Antrem Klaus|Antrem Klaus]]** was not what she expected.

He was a simple man. Slow. Kind. Not clever. He looked at Vya the way a dog looks at the sun — glad that it exists, unsure of what it means.

He explained that he had not been kidnapped. He had not been held. He had been **summoned** — by his brother **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Accarus Grin|Accarus]]**, who told him it was for a great purpose. He had left Vya and **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Vreina Arleschia|Vreina]]** behind because Accarus told him he had to.

He did not seem to understand what he had done. He did not seem to know the cost.

Vya also learned that the man who had raised her — **[[Kopling]]** — shared a surname with **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Gzirmo Kopling|Gzirmo Kopling]]**, one of the Initial Six. Distant relatives. Or perhaps closer than anyone had realized.

Two mysteries remained:
- **Who burned Chenalia?** Will of Grin was blamed. But Will of Grin had not been in Elis at the time. Someone else had done it.
- **Where is Vreina?** She had vanished the night Chenalia burned. No body. No grave. No trace.

## Part 5: The Frosthaven Mission

A new mission arrived.

**Retrieve Safayer** from the belly of the silver dragon **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Vythrax|Vythrax]]** — who had swallowed it three centuries ago to protect her egg. The dragon lived at **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/locations/Frosthaven Peak|Frosthaven Peak]]**, in the village of **[[Frostheart Village|Frostheart]]** — **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Syl Frost|Syl]]**'s birthplace.

Accarus equipped them with anti-freeze gear, potions, and heavy cloaks. **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Husgus|Husgus]]** asked for **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/items/Gustaero — The Swan Bow|Gustaero]]** — the bow Accarus carried, the bow that had belonged to **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Olaf Guirela|Olaf Guirela]]**, then **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Gordon|Gordon]]**, then Husgus. Accarus said he would give it to him after the mission.

They departed: the Five Sticks, Accarus, **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Antrem Klaus|Antrem]]**, and **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Jarko|Jarko]]**.

The journey from desert to ice took days. The temperature dropped with every mile.

## Part 6: The Unacknowledged Heir

The climb began.

Wild snow snakes ambushed them halfway up. One nearly killed **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Shin Van Hel|HVS]]** with a bite to the throat. **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Syl Frost|Syl]]** froze it before it could strike again.

At the top: **[[Frostheart Village|Frostheart]]** — carved into the cliffside, igloos and ice-brick homes, the wind never stopping.

Syl had arrived first. She stood at the edge of the village and remembered.

She remembered the children throwing stones. She remembered the word *"anak haram"* — bastard child. She remembered the way the elders had looked at her when she had asked about her mother.

She lost her hope.

The party was lost without her — she was the only one who spoke **Dragonborn**. But **Accarus** spoke it too, and he gave Syl a **Will of Grin** helm and cloak — enough to hide her face and her wings. Enough to walk through the village unrecognized.

They entered.

They found:
- **The Breathstone Replica** — a giant orb the clan had worshipped for centuries
- **The Frostscale Family Mural** — carved into ice, listing every name in the clan for 500 years
- **Syl's name was not on it.**

They sought information. The villagers said Syl had been raised by "a merchant" — a cabbage seller.

They found him at the edge of the market.

When Syl revealed herself, the old cabbage merchant **wept**.

He held her hands. He touched her face. He said *"my little ice shard"* and could not stop crying. He told her that the real Breathstone was in the **Temple of Vythrax**.

## Part 7: The Cracked Egg

They entered the Temple.

The Breathstone — the holy relic the clan had worshipped for 300 years — was **cracked**.

Like an egg that had hatched.

They pieced it together: **Syl was Vythrax's son.** The Breathstone was her egg. The clan had lied for centuries.

They went to the priests to warn them about Will of Grin's true intentions. The priests **refused to believe them.** Will of Grin had been a friend of the clan. Will of Grin had promised to heal Vythrax.

Syl remembered something.

Every time she hurt herself, she dreamed of Vythrax. Every cut, every bruise, every drop of blood — she saw the silver dragon.

She cut herself.

The sound of giant wings filled the sky.

Vythrax descended — but not in peace. She was **in rampage mode** — poisoned by **Safayer**, purple veins visible across her silver scales. She did not recognize her daughter. She did not recognize anyone.

**Accarus**, **Jarko**, and **Antrem** drew their weapons.

**HVS** acted fast. He cast **Suggestion** — *"Hand the mission to us. Step back. Do not attack."*

They obeyed.

The party improvised. **Renal** — Ama's Imp — was wrapped in Husgus's rope and mixed with Syl's blood in HVS's holy water bottle. The plan: get Vythrax to drink.

**Syl** stepped forward and offered the bottle.

Vythrax drank.

The purple veins faded. Her eyes cleared. She looked at Syl and **saw her**.

She wrapped herself around her daughter — a coil of silver and love — and pulled **Safayer** from her own tongue. Then she coughed up **Renal**, wet and indignant, and set him down carefully.

She spoke telepathically — not in words, but in feeling. Gratitude. Love. Apology.

Then she flew back to the **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/locations/The Spire of Whispering Ice|Spire of Whispering Ice]]**.

From the village, the entire **Klan Frostscale** had watched.

They knelt.

They carved **Syl Frost** onto the family mural.

## Part 8: The Raid

Five days passed. The party had earned Will of Grin's trust. **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/items/Gustaero — The Swan Bow|Gustaero]]** was given to **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Husgus|Husgus]]**.

They trained in **Basement 3** with **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Jarko|Jarko]]**.

But something was wrong. The upstairs was too quiet.

**Renal** and HVS's familiar scouted. They returned with a report: **infiltrators had taken out every guard.**

Accarus, Antrem, Geneva, and Hildag were **not in the building**.

The party prepared to fight. **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Zure Ama|Ama]]** led the way to Basement 2.

Four **Garda Besi** soldiers met him.

Ama fell. The other four fought — and won.

They healed. And then, from above, they felt it.

**[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Lei Iron Gort|Lei Iron Gort]]**.

The party tried a **Minor Illusion** — a false report that everyone on Basement 2 was dead. The illusion flickered in front of Gort as he sat smoking a cigarette.

Gort did not laugh.

He put out his cigarette. He struck the wooden floor with his fist — and **shattered two floors**.

The party and **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Jarko|Jarko]]** fell to the bottom level.

Gort descended slowly. The wind around him was **visible** — swirling, choking. Each of them felt their throat close. They could not breathe. They could not move.

Jarko, terrified, tried to run. He threw a **fire punch** at Gort — it landed, and it did nothing.

Gort grabbed Jarko by the head.

He slammed his knee into Jarko's face — once, twice, three times. And then he crushed Jarko's skull against the floor.

Brains scattered. Blood pooled.

**Vya ignited.**

Something inside her — something she had never felt before — **woke up**.

**Husgus** — barely able to breathe — held up the **Berkas Penelitian Cycle Clan**. Gort took it. Husgus **snatched it back** and ran.

They climbed the stairs. They broke through the upper doors. They reached the docks. They stole the Will of Grin ship for the **third time**.

They set sail for **Elis** — the wilderness continent, the only place the Commonwealth could not follow.

## Part 9: When Your Body Is a Shell

For **six months**, they survived on the ship.

They guessed at directions. They dodged patrols. They were wanted criminals of the **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/factions/One Voice Commonwealth|Commonwealth]]**.

Each of them changed.

- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Husgus|Husgus]]** trained in stealth. He learned to move without sound, without trace. He became a rogue. And his body began to **crack** — faint golden lines appearing across his skin, as if something inside him was pushing out.
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Shin Van Hel|HVS]]** began to worship **Valla**. He had been saved so many times — by luck, by fate, by something — that he could no longer deny it. He became a **paladin**.
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Syl Frost|Syl]]** learned to heal. She studied the sacred texts of Vythrax's clan and became a **cleric**.
- **[[Vya Arleschia|Vya]]** felt something burning in her chest — ever since Jarko's death. A fire that had always been dormant, and was now awake.
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Zure Ama|Ama]]** carried the weight of everything. His arm. His failures. His debts.

After six months, they found it.

**The Ruin of Cycle.**

A place the Commonwealth had been searching for. A place the Grin had been searching for. A place no one was meant to find.

They entered.

HVS collapsed. Severe headache. He clutched his skull and fell to his knees.

**[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Syl Frost|Syl]]** — who understood the Cycle Clan files best — read the inscription on the wall. It said **HVS**. She could not read the rest.

The others went deeper.

**[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Husgus|Husgus]]**, **[[Vya Arleschia|Vya]]**, and **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Zure Ama|Ama]]** found a **mural** — robed figures standing before a portal, with **four colored stones** set at the doorway.

They returned to Syl.

She had finished translating.

> *"HVS. Human Vessel Shell."*
