## Part 1: Seven Days

**[[oversee/npcs/Accarus Grin|Accarus]]** had given them seven days.

Seven days to decide: join **[[oversee/factions/Will of Grin]]**, or return to **[[oversee/factions/Keeper of Secret]]**. The offer hung over them like a weight they could not put down.

They did not decide yet.

Instead, they went to **[[oversee/locations/Javan Town]]** — the port town where Accarus's ship waited, and where **[[oversee/npcs/Grilnax]]** had a Keeper contact who could take them back to **[[oversee/npcs/Mira Nouveau|Mira]]**.

Both paths began here.

They spent the day in the market, trying to sell a gold ring at **Mata Elang** — the luxury shop — but the merchant lowballed them so badly they walked out.

They visited the standard stops:
- **Ransel Penuh** — adventuring gear
- **Debu Peri** — alchemy
- **Baja Hitam** — the blacksmith

At **Baja Hitam**, they met **[[oversee/npcs/Mr. Andreas]]** — a soot-stained man with kind eyes and a wife who needed an escort.

> *"Two hundred gold. She has an event in the capital — West Harka. Tomorrow at dawn. Do we have a deal?"*

They did.

## Part 2: A Night in the Cemetery

That evening, instead of an inn, the party went to **Pemakaman Jiopa** — the cemetery of Javan Town.

They wandered among the graves. Read names. Accidentally frightened the elderly gravekeeper — **Old Wabi** — who nearly collapsed when they emerged from between the headstones.

> *"We want to guard the graves. Just for the night. Please."*

Old Wabi, too tired to argue, let them sleep in his house.

## Part 3: The Road to Padre

Dawn.

At **Dora's Rhino Stable**, they met **[[oversee/npcs/Dora]]** — a middle-aged dwarf woman with no patience for nonsense.

Before they could depart, they were intercepted.

A patrol of **Commonwealth soldiers** stood on the road. They were looking for **[[oversee/npcs/Agus]]** — the missing Keeper agent — now accused of "terrorist activity."

The party lied. Fluently.

They claimed they had never met Agus. They claimed they were ordinary adventurers. And while lying, they **pumped the soldiers for information** — learning about the **[[oversee/lore/The Triase|Triase]]**, the three-pillar leadership of the **[[oversee/factions/One Voice Commonwealth|Commonwealth]]**.

The soldiers left before they could be questioned further — a report had come in about bodies found in **[[oversee/locations/Ghuffghuff Town]]**.

## Part 4: Padre City

They reached **[[oversee/locations/Padre Megalopolis]]** by midday.

It was built on a mesa — white granite walls, streets that glowed softly at night, a sense of order so complete it felt like a warning.

The **Festival of the Great Lei** was underway. Ten days before the holy day of **Valla**. Banners everywhere. Crowds in every square.

They parted from **[[oversee/npcs/Mrs. Ratna|Pak Andreas's wife]]** at the palace steps. She smiled, thanked them, and disappeared into the crowd with her crates.

The party pushed forward. They found a place in the crowd in front of the **Governor's Palace** — the starting point of the parade.

## Part 5: The Parade

**[[oversee/npcs/Hanzel Lei Krow]]** — Governor of West Harka — opened the parade with a speech about unity. Then he introduced the three pillars of the Triase.

The first was **[[oversee/npcs/Lei Livia Vane]]** — Wakil Keadilan, leader of the **[[oversee/factions/The Rose Guard|Garda Mawar]]**. She stood on a floating platform of churning wind, dressed in white and silver, eyes cold, rapier at her hip. She did not wave. She did not smile.

The crowd applauded politely, but there was fear in it.

The second was **[[oversee/npcs/Lei Iron Gort]]** — Wakil Pertahanan, leader of the **[[oversee/factions/The Iron Guard|Garda Besi]]**. He rode on a massive iron war-cart pulled by four armored rhinos. His soldiers did not march — they swaggered. They drank. They laughed.

Gort, spotting the crowd, threw a half-empty bottle of ale into the nearest group.

**[[oversee/pcs/Husgus]]** caught it.

Without hesitation, he uncorked it and drank.

Gort stared at him for a moment — then burst into laughter.

> *"You've got guts, kid! Come find me at the barracks if you ever want real work!"*

The third was **[[oversee/npcs/Lei Valerius Thorne]]** — Kanselir Agung. He rode in an open carriage pulled by two massive rhinos, their horns carved with bronze ornaments. He was dressed simply — white shirt, open jacket — and his smile was warm.

The crowd **roared** for him. Genuine affection. Not fear.

The party noticed.

## Part 6: The Lyceum

When the parade ended, the party went in search of information.

They knew the names now: **The Initial Six**. They had one painting's worth of truth. They needed more.

The **[[oversee/locations/Lyceum University of Arcane Arts]]** stood in the middle ring of the city. They found their way to the **rector's office** — and to **[[oversee/npcs/Professor Alistair Donta]]**, the oldest priest of Valla in Oversee.

He was tired. He was clearly afraid of something he would not name. But he was willing to help.

He showed them a painting — the only known portrait of the Initial Six:

- **[[oversee/npcs/Lei Alejandro]]** — tall, wind mage, green eyes, imperious.
- **[[oversee/npcs/Atreus Grin]]** — smiling, small-horned, red-skinned.
- **[[oversee/npcs/Sam Hurls]]** — chubby, lute in hand.
- **[[oversee/npcs/Gzirmo Kopling]]** — dragonborn, orange-gold scales.
- **[[oversee/npcs/Atlan Nouveau]]** — human, sea-worn face.
- **[[oversee/npcs/Olaf Guirela]]** — half-orc, scarred, bow in hand.

They asked about the gems. Donta identified the green one as **[[oversee/items/Emelanica]]** — and his voice dropped when he said the name.

> *"You should speak to my colleague, Professor Howdy. He has studied the Cycle Clan for years. He lives in the Outer Ring."*

## Part 7: Howdy's House

The house was open.

Coffee on the table. Cold. Door half-closed. Footprints — large, heavy — leading out into the street.

**[[oversee/npcs/Professor Howdy]]** was gone.

They searched the house. In the corner, a **cockroach** — enormous and unsettling — scuttled across a pile of papers. The party cornered it, and somehow — impossibly — spoke to it.

The cockroach led them to a hidden drawer.

Inside: a folder. **"Berkas Penelitian Cycle Clan"** — the Cycle Clan Research File.

They took it.

When they returned to the university to find Donta, they were caught sneaking in. Fined **200 gp** for trespassing — money they did not have.

They returned to Howdy's house. This time, they forced the door.

They found the file. They read it. And they understood, for the first time, that the world was **older and stranger** than they had known.

That night, they had no money for an inn. They slept in the **Valla Temple**.

## Part 8: Donta's Death

The next morning, **[[oversee/npcs/Professor Alistair Donta]]** was not well.

He was pale. His hands shook. He could not meet their eyes.

> *"Howdy is dead. Heart attack. Yesterday. I'm sorry."*

The party did not believe him.

They pressed. They accused. They said the words out loud: that the **Triase** or **Klan Lei** had killed Howdy because of what he had learned — that they would kill Donta too.

And then, without warning, Donta stood.

He said nothing.

He walked to the window, opened it, and stepped out.

By the time they reached him, he was dead on the stones below.

## Part 9: The Revival

The party panicked.

A priest — the oldest in Oversee — had just committed suicide in front of them. The city would assume they had done it. They would be hunted.

**[[oversee/pcs/Shin Van Hel|HVS]]** acted first.

He drew **[[oversee/items/Swaith — The Soul-Thirsting Blade|Swaith]]** and fed it a Hit Die from his own body. Then he drove the blade into Donta's chest.

The body gasped.

Donta rose — **not alive, not dead** — his eyes a faint grey. He would live for twenty-four hours. Long enough to walk to the magistrate and state clearly that the party had done nothing. Long enough to say his own death was a suicide.

Then he would collapse again. And this time, no one could bring him back.

They watched him walk into the street. They watched the guards find him. They watched the story spread: *the oldest priest took his own life, and no adventurers were to blame.*

It worked.

But something inside the party changed that day. Something about how far they were willing to go.

## Part 10: The Bloodlines of Power

That afternoon, they went looking for more answers.

At **The Mana Dreg** — a bar in the Outer Circle — the bartender, **Selow**, told them about **Klan Lei**. The four branches. The four elements. The aristocratic families who had ruled Oversee since the founding.

> *"They live in the Inner Circle. They don't mix with us. They think their blood is purer than ours. Maybe it is. But that doesn't make them better."*

Later, they visited **Yunao University of Agriculture** and met **[[oversee/npcs/Joni]]** — **[[oversee/pcs/Zure Ama|Ama]]**'s adoptive father, now a professor of wheat and grain.

He explained the four Klan Lei bloodlines:
- **Lei Wendigo** — Wind
- **Lei Firage** — Fire
- **Lei Watigi** — Water
- **Lei Roiga** — Earth

> *"None of them are as strong as **[[oversee/npcs/Lei Alejandro]]** was. He was the first of the first. He held all four elements at once. But each branch has a scrap of that power. Enough to rule."*

Ama listened in silence. His father — the man who raised him — spoke of these bloodlines as if they were **facts of nature**, not the manipulations of history.

## Part 11: The Choice

By the sixth day, they were broke.

They washed dishes in a tavern for silver. They slept in **[[oversee/npcs/Cindy Mektol]]**'s temple — the Legendary Noodle Temple in **[[oversee/locations/Gudameng Town|Gudameng]]** — because **[[oversee/npcs/Guo Lao]]** would not turn them away.

And on the seventh day, they made their choice.

The path to **[[oversee/npcs/Mira Nouveau|Mira]]** was still open. **[[oversee/npcs/Grilnax]]** would take them back. Keeper of Secret would receive them. They could go back to what they knew.

But that path led to **more secrets**.

The path of **[[oversee/npcs/Accarus Grin|Accarus]]** was harder. Will of Grin was hunted. Its methods were brutal. But it promised **action**.

They boarded Accarus's ship.

And the ship set sail for **Pirizia**.
