## Part 1: The Recovery Ward

They returned to **[[oversee/locations/Arujaya Metropolis]]** with **[[oversee/pcs/Zure Ama|Ama]]** barely alive.

The **Modification Parlor** took him. **[[oversee/npcs/Dr. Vektor]]** and his machines worked through the night. When the sun rose — or whatever passed for dawn in a city with no sky — Ama was alive, but not yet whole.

His left arm was a soft, grey thing. Jelly. Not fully bone. Not fully flesh. **[[oversee/items/Renal]]**'s tissue had fused with his own, but the Imp was still waking up.

> *"Give it time,"* Dr. Vektor said. *"The Imp needs to remember it is part of you. It will."*

Ama stared at his new arm for a long time. Then he stood, flexed it, and said nothing.

## Part 2: Adam

While Ama recovered, the party explored.

**[[oversee/npcs/Adam Findias]]** — the first boy they had met in Arujaya — found them near the clinic. He was small, sharp-eyed, and nervous. He had a mechanical heart that clicked when he ran.

> *"You are asking about the Cult of the Cycle? The Mnemonic Archive has what you need. But you should be careful. **[[oversee/npcs/Besik Findias]]** is not… normal."*

They went to the archive.

**[[oversee/npcs/Besik Findias]]** was waiting for them. He was old — older than Arujaya itself — and his body was more machine than flesh. He spoke in a voice that was half-human and half-static.

He showed them the **[[oversee/npcs/Gzirmo Kopling|Echo Gzirmo]]** — not the cyborg they had met at the reactor, but the **body** of it. The original. The relic. It was now used as a **power source** for the city. His consciousness was the battery that lit the streets.

Besik also showed them the Shell video again. The same video. The same horror.

**[[oversee/pcs/Shin Van Hel|HVS]]** asked if he could see the Shell itself — the **actual** Shell, not just the video.

Besik said yes.

## Part 3: The Shell

They descended into a place that should not exist — a chamber deep beneath Arujaya, where the **[[oversee/locations/The First Tree|First Tree]]**'s roots met the city's foundations. And there, embedded in the rock, was a fragment of the Shell.

A piece of the 20-kilometer skin that had fallen from the sky 1,000 years ago.

It was **hollow**. Empty. Still.

**HVS** drew **[[oversee/items/Swaith — The Soul-Thirsting Blade|Swaith]]** and stabbed it.

Nothing happened.

The blade did not drink. The Shell did not react. It was not dead. It was not alive. It was simply **there**.

**[[oversee/pcs/Syl Frost|Syl]]** pressed her hand against it. She felt **cold**. Not the cold of ice. The cold of something that had never been warm.

They left.

## Part 4: The Depths

The party needed to leave Arujaya. But the way up was blocked by the Commonwealth. The only other exit was through **The Depths**.

**[[oversee/npcs/Fidale]]** was waiting at the edge.

She was an old woman — wrinkled, hunched, half-mad — who lived alone at the border of the Depths. She tried to sell them "anti-smog masks." They were rags soaked in oil. They would not work.

When **[[oversee/pcs/Husgus]]** tested one by dropping it into the Depths, it **melted** on contact with the air.

> *"No one can go down there,"* Fidale said. *"No one comes back."*

**[[oversee/pcs/Syl Frost|Syl]]** went down anyway.

She was immune to poison. If anyone could survive the smog, it was her.

## Part 5: The Depths of Arujaya

The Depths were a nightmare.

Four doors. Three of them were useless — one locked, two full of melted storage. The fourth door held a **crack** in the wall, leaking black sludge and toxic smog.

The emergency exit required **two people** to activate. Syl was alone.

She went into the crack.

Inside, the world **changed**.

Magic saturated everything. The air was thick with it. Her clothes and armor began to **melt**. She returned to the surface briefly — healed, re-equipped — then went back in.

This time, she saw it.

**Perception (Nat 20).**

Above her: **black tentacles of smoke**, reaching down from a sky that was not a sky.

She tried to cast **Mirror Image**. Nothing happened. She tried **Tidal Wave**. Nothing. She felt **magicless** — as if her soul was being drawn out of her body, pulled toward something above.

She ran. She jumped. She climbed past gravity itself.

And above her, she saw:

**The Overseer.**

Not a god. Not a demon. A **presence**. Blob-like entities with tentacles, drifting in a space that was not space. And beyond them — **something vast**.

She lost consciousness.

## Part 6: The Wandering Demon

When Syl woke, she was back at the edge of the Depths.

She did not remember what she had seen. But something had changed. There was a **presence** nearby — a silhouette that vanished when she tried to focus on it. A **wandering demon**, perhaps. Something that had pulled her out.

Later, **[[oversee/npcs/Gzirmo Kopling|Echo Gzirmo]]** would say: *"The demons of the Gap fear water. They will not follow you if you are wet. You were wet, weren't you?"*

She had been.

## Part 7: The Imp Awakens

The party did not know Syl was missing. They had been interrogating Echo Gzirmo.

Gzirmo — cooperative after being stabbed with **Swaith** — explained more than he had before.

> *"The sludge in the Depths is **Ambitio-Ether**. Residual. Left over from the Gap. It is toxic to mortals because it is not meant for mortals."*

> *"Demons fear water. It is the only thing that can cleanse them."*

**[[oversee/pcs/Zure Ama|Ama]]** looked at his new arm. The soft, grey, jelly thing that was now half-Imp.

He **spat on it**.

The Imp woke.

**[[oversee/items/Renal]]** — the servant bound to Ama by the **Devil** Fate Card — stirred. The arm hardened. The jelly became **bone**. Ama flexed it, and felt **power** — the power of a creature that could eat what the Overseer discarded.

He walked into the Depths.

The sludge **parted** before him. The smog **cleared**. Renal's arm ate it, filtered it, consumed it.

Ama found Syl near the crack. She was alive. She was confused. She did not remember what she had seen.

They left together.

## Part 8: The Escape

The emergency exit opened. They climbed out of Arujaya, into the sun of **Elis**.

Waiting for them: **[[oversee/npcs/Lei Livia Vane]]**.

She had been tracking them. She had been waiting. She had not expected them to come out with **Renal awake** and **Ama at full strength**.

They captured her.

**[[oversee/pcs/Shin Van Hel|HVS]]** bound her hands. **[[oversee/pcs/Husgus]]** kept an arrow pointed at her back. **[[oversee/pcs/Vya Arleschia|Vya]]** watched her with something that was not quite hatred — something colder.

They walked.

## Part 9: The Rune of the Cycle

They stopped at the **[[oversee/locations/Rune of the Cycle]]** — a stone circle in the desert, warded against scrying. A safe place.

They rested.

**[[oversee/pcs/Shin Van Hel|HVS]]** sat apart from the others. He had been quiet since the escape. He was reading something — a fragment of the Cycle Clan research file that had survived the raid. He traced his finger over a line of text.

> *"HVS."*

He read the words around it. And then he looked up.

> *"HVS stands for **Human Vessel Shell**."*

No one spoke.

**[[oversee/pcs/Husgus]]** — whose body was beginning to crack, showing golden light beneath the skin — said nothing.

**[[oversee/pcs/Zure Ama|Ama]]** — whose arm was now half-Imp, half-human — said nothing.

**[[oversee/pcs/Syl Frost|Syl]]** — whose blood was slowly killing her — said nothing.

**[[oversee/pcs/Vya Arleschia|Vya]]** — whose fire was waking up, whose family had lied to her, whose cousin had been sacrificed — said nothing.

They did not know what it meant yet. But they knew it meant **something**.

## Part 10: Livia

**[[oversee/npcs/Lei Livia Vane]]** was tied to a rock, away from the fire.

**[[oversee/pcs/Zure Ama|Ama]]** went to speak with her. He did not hate her — not after everything. He had lost an arm to her, yes. But he had also seen her, in that moment before she struck, and she had looked like someone who was **afraid**.

She was afraid now.

> *"You don't understand,"* she said. *"Valerius… he will come for me. He will not stop. He will not rest. He will hunt me to the ends of the world because I know what he is."*

They asked her what she meant.

She told them about the **twin-killing tradition** of **Klan Lei**. When twins are born to a Lei mother, one is kept and one is killed. The survivor inherits the full power. The dead twin is forgotten.

> *"I was supposed to be killed. I was the younger one. My sister was supposed to live."*

She paused.

> *"But my mother could not do it. She killed my sister instead. And she told Valerius that I had died. She hid me away."*

> *"Valerius has known, all these years, that I was the one who should have died. That I am the one who stole his sister's life. And he has never told anyone."*

> *"He is waiting for the right moment to take me. To avenge her."*

**[[oversee/npcs/Lei Darvin Thorne|Darvin]]** had been listening. He looked at Livia, and then at nothing, and said:

> *"He is my brother. He is my twin. And he has been hunting me for ten years."*

Livia stared at him. She had not known.

The party looked at Darvin, and then at Livia, and then at each other.

They had a hostage. They had a secret. And they had a **twin** who was the key to everything.
