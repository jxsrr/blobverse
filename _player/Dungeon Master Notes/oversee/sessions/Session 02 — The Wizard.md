> Trapped on an automated ship, the party dreamed of fate and arrived in a vertical city of coral and steam.

## Part 1: The Dream of Fate

The Will of Grin ship moved without a crew.

No sails. No wheel. No one to speak to. Only the hum of the engine, the endless sea, and the slow creep of hunger.

For days, the party drifted. They rationed what they had. They rationed again. The walls of the ship were metal and cold, and the sea beyond the portholes was a mirror of nothing.

And then, in the space between hunger and sleep, they dreamed.

Not one dream. A **shared** dream.

Each of them saw a figure — not a face, but a presence. A dealer. A reader of cards. And each was offered a card.

Each card carried a blessing and a curse.

- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Syl Frost|Syl]]** was dealt **The Magician** — cursed to be the center of every magical failure, to feel her own spells turn wild and burn her. But **Temperance** balanced it — her blood was purified, a universal cure for poison, disease, and curse.
- **[[Vya Arleschia|Vya]]** drew **Wheel of Fortune** — a systematic erosion of her luck, small chaos chasing her every step. But **Death** gave her peace — a quiet, unshakable calm in the face of terror.
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Husgus|Husgus]]** saw **Judgement** — a legend written before the deeds, his name known wherever he walked. But **The Lovers** bound him — a magical dread of losing the companions beside him.
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Zure Ama|Zure Ama]]** was cursed by **The Hanged Man** — his smile frozen forever, maddening to all who saw it. But **The Devil** gave him **Qain** — a bound Imp, later renamed **Renal**, whose loyalty was absolute.
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Shin Van Hel|HVS]]** was given **The Tower** — the blade **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/items/Swaith — The Soul-Thirsting Blade|Swaith]]**, able to steal life from the dying and return the dead to breath. But it whispered. It gnawed at his peace.

They woke separately. They did not speak of the dream — not yet.

But each of them felt heavier.

## Part 2: The Confession Field

They searched the ship for the galley. Along the way, they found a room that was not on any schematic — the **Observation Room**.

Inside, the air was wrong. It was heavy. It pressed against the mind.

And then the questions began.

The room forced truth.

Each of the Five Sticks was asked three questions by the others. The room would not let them lie.

- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Zure Ama|Zure Ama]]** was asked about **Reins**. He spoke, for the first time without defending himself, about the friend he had lost. About the guilt that had driven him across worlds.
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Shin Van Hel|HVS]]** was asked about his gloves. About the Curse Mark. About **Zargoyle**. He spoke, and the room listened.
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Syl Frost|Syl]]** was asked why her magic was self-taught. Why her clan had never accepted her. She answered.
- **[[Vya Arleschia|Vya]]** was asked about the burning of her village. About **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/factions/Will of Grin|Will of Grin]]**. About the nightmares she could not explain.
- **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Husgus|Husgus]]** was asked about **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Gordon|Gordon]]**'s last words. About the spell that had turned him from a boy into a halfling. About the curse that had made him what he was.

When the last question was answered, the room released them.

And in the center of the room, a small object appeared: the **Meal Key**.

## Part 3: Arrival in Kota Juto

They ate.

And then — without knowing how — the ship's course changed.

**[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/locations/Juto Port City|Juto Port City]]**.

A city of coral and steel. A city that climbed cliffs on pillars of ancient reef. A city built vertically, where the poor lived in the tidal districts and the wealthy lived above the floods.

The party arrived at the **Floating Docks**.

Within minutes, they were arrested.

Commonwealth police had been watching the docks for weeks. A group of heavily armed strangers, dressed in **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/factions/Will of Grin|Will of Grin]]** armor, arriving on a ship with no registered crew — the arrest was automatic.

**Ama's** armor was confiscated as evidence.

They were held for a day. Questioned. Released with a warning: *"You are not the first to cosplay as Grin. Do not make us regret letting you walk."*

They had no money, no lodging, and no plan.

## Part 4: Exploring the City

They needed a place to sleep.

Their first stop was the **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/locations/Juto's Observatorium|Juto's Observatorium]]** — the Center of Knowledge that had, over centuries, become the heart of Kota Juto. They thought it might have information about where they were, or what had happened to them.

But the Observatorium was closed for the evening.

So they went to **Mutiara** — the Pearl — an elite tavern where wealthy adventurers and merchant captains gathered. It was full. It was expensive. It was also hosting a celebration of *"the great adventurers of Oversee."*

They did not stay long.

But they did meet a fighter named **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Agus|Agus]]** — a young man with a confident smile and a story about a legendary weapon called *Drei*. The party would learn later that Agus's smile hid more than it showed.

They left Mutiara. They could not afford it.

## Part 5: The Fall into the Tortle's Shop

Rather than pay for the **Lift Karang** — the vertical transport that moved people between the city's tiers — the party decided to jump.

This was a mistake.

They fell through the awning of a small shop — **Teritip Tua** — and landed in a sprawl of diving helmets, harpoons, and strange oxygen tanks made from giant nautilus shells.

The shop belonged to **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Pops Tortle|Pops]]** — an ancient Tortle, a thousand years old, deaf as stone, and slow as the tide.

They had to shout to be heard.

But Pops remembered. He remembered a name — **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Atlan Nouveau|Atlan Nouveau]]** — and a children's book Atlan had written. He told them about Atlan's love of the sea, and about the lighthouse where Atlan had once stood and watched the horizon for someone who never came.

They did not understand the significance yet.

That night, they found **[[Nenek Elara|Karang Tidur]]** — a former private residence of a Juto mayor, now a free guesthouse for adventurers. It was old, and small, and smelled of salt and coral.

They slept.

## Part 6: The Stolen Gems

At dawn, a scream.

**[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Elara|Elara]]** stood at the entrance, pale, hands over her mouth.

> *"Someone... something... came in the night!"*

The party's emerald and ruby — the gem from Greyland and the gem from Pulau Dima — were gone.

**Atlan's Compass** vibrated in **Ama**'s pocket. It had been silent for days. Now it was alive.

They followed it.

It led to the **Kuil Lautan** — the Sea Temple — a cave shrine on the lower tier of the city where the priests read the patterns of waves.

They descended.

And inside, they found **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Babibubebo|Babibubebo]]** — a small fish-man, hunched and trembling, surrounded by the shells and crumbs of a dozen stolen meals.

Not the gems. Just food.

He was not the thief. He was simply hungry.

The party realized the compass was pointing at the same location — but at a different layer. Directly **above** the Sea Temple, carved into the same cliff, was the **[[The Observatorium|Observatorium]]**.

## Part 7: The Hidden Base

They went up.

The Observatorium was quiet. Scholars moved with silent efficiency. One of them — a woman with sharp eyes and a scholar's posture — watched them pass.

They found a locked secretary's office. A little persuasion. A little luck. A little **Ama** shouldering the door.

Behind a bookshelf, a hidden staircase.

They descended into a space that had not been touched in centuries.

**[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Atlan Nouveau|Atlan Nouveau]]**'s base.

There was a stone pedestal with a pale blue eternal flame — a flame that did not flicker in the drafts. Beside it, a bowl of silver ash. And carved into the pedestal:

> *"Burn here, forever in my heart, the one I love."*

On a table, a diary — water-stained, the ink faded but still legible.

They read it.

- **Entry 1:** *"Day 10 in this new world. I found another one. She was floating in the sea near the lighthouse, just like me... and she also 'Fell' from Aede. Her name is Reins."*
- **Entry 2:** *"We built a life here, in Rioquan. Our daughter, Mira, has been born. But Reins is restless."*
- **Entry 3:** *"I made a pact with the Keeper of Secret."*
- **Entry 4:** *"If you are my descendant and read this, the pact still holds. Bring the compass to the 'Locked Sea Archive.'"*

**Ama** went very still.

**Reins.**

The name he had been chasing across two worlds. The friend he had lost. She had been here. She had been alive. She had fallen into Oversee **500 years before him.**

He had been searching for her across time without knowing it.

## Part 8: Mira

To open the final door, the party had to write two names in the silver ash.

**Reins** and **Atlan**.

They wrote them.

The door opened.

Behind it stood a woman — half-elf, sharp-eyed, carrying a sword that glowed faintly with ancient magic. Beside her, **[[Nenek Elara|Elara]]**, holding the green gem and the red gem in her hands.

The woman spoke.

> *"So. My father was right. Someone would come."*

Her name was **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Mira Nouveau|Mira Nouveau]]**. Daughter of **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Atlan Nouveau|Atlan Nouveau]]** and **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Reins|Reins]]**. Founder of the **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/factions/Keeper of Secret|Keeper of Secret]]**.

She told them she had been a child when her father disappeared. She had seen it — the ritual, the light, the man called **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Lei Alejandro|Lei Alejandro]]**. She had spent her life building an organization to find the truth.

She offered them a place in it.

They accepted.

She also told them something else: **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/items/Safayer|Safayer]]** — the second Gemheart — had been swallowed by a silver dragon in the mountains of **Pirizia**. The dragon was called **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Vythrax|Vythrax]]**. It had been poisoned for centuries.

To face a dragon, they would need **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/items/Gustaero — The Swan Bow|Gustaero — The Swan Bow]]** — the legendary bow once carried by **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Olaf Guirela|Olaf Guirela]]**, then passed to **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Gordon|Gordon]]**, then to **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/pcs/Husgus|Husgus]]**.

And that bow, she believed, was in **[[Ghuffghuff]]** — the village of fried rice, deep in Harka.

She assigned **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Agus|Agus]]** to travel with them.

The party accepted.

And they did not know — not yet — that Agus was already working for someone else.
