## Part 1: Forming the Company and the Road to Greyland

The story begins in the memory of a young girl named **Vya**, called V by those who love her — a child watching from the hillside as a ship arrived carrying soldiers whose faces were carved into permanent smiles.

Years later, grown and raised by her adoptive father **<a href="/Kopling">Kopling</a>**, V set out to find her own path. She arrived at a village called **<a href="/oversee/locations/Yawning%20Town">Yawning Town</a>** — the starting point for new adventurers — and registered for her **SIP** (Adventurer's Permit) at the Guild.

There she met **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Grilnax">Grilnax</a>**, the tired goblin clerk, and four other would-be adventurers: **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Husgus">Husgus</a>**, **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Zure%20Ama">Zure Ama</a>**, **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Shin%20Van%20Hel">HVS</a>**, and **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Syl%20Frost">Syl Frost</a>**. Grilnax grouped them into a single party and registered them under the name **The Five Sticks**.

Their first assignment was simple: retrieve a relic from the long-abandoned **<a href="/oversee/locations/Greyland%20Castle">Greyland Castle</a>**.

Before leaving, they shopped for supplies at **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Senta">Senta</a>**'s store. V bought a flower — the first thing she had ever purchased with her own coin.

At the **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Samual">Tipsy Trident</a>**, the bartender **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Samual">Samual</a>** mentioned that **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Mr.%20Poise">Mr. Poise</a>** — a rhino driver — offered free transport to new adventurers. The party tracked him down. Poise, a gruff dwarf with braided white beard and a soft spot for his rhino **Gertrude**, agreed to take them across the rocky peninsula.

Their destination: Greyland. A goblin-held ruin.

## Part 2: The Road Through Stone

The cart rattled across the peninsula under a sky the color of wet slate. Mr. Poise warned them, once, in his grinding voice:

> *"Never turn your back on the sea in Greyland. Nonsense, the waves can drag you under in an instant."*

The party arrived at a castle that barely deserved the name. Towers leaned. A six-pointed star banner hung in tatters. Two goblins guarded the bridge — one large, one small. Neither looked particularly clever. (Though the party would later learn that goblin kings often have mixed blood, and mixed blood means sharp minds.)

## Part 3: Into the Castle

The party split.

**<a href="/oversee/pcs/Husgus">Husgus</a>** and **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Zure%20Ama">Zure Ama</a>** descended beneath the bridge, and — by accident — found a hidden sea cave that connected to the castle's supply room.

Inside the cave, carved into the stone, they found a message older than any of them:

> *"The star has fallen. Six have left us. We must keep this secret safe, so their power is not misused. We hid the key of the sea lord's legacy here, beneath forgotten watch."*
> — **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Alejandro">Lei Alejandro</a>**, centuries past

They did not know who Alejandro was. They did not know what the star was. They did not know that the six had left anyone. But they recognized the words were heavy with meaning — the kind of meaning people died to protect.

Meanwhile, the rest of the party tried the front gate. **Ama** attempted diplomacy, offering a flower to a goblin. It went poorly. (It is worth noting that Ama would later refine this technique.)

Inside the supply room, **Ama** discovered a **<a href="/oversee/items/Worn-Out%20Bag%20of%20Tricks">Worn-Out Bag of Tricks</a>** — a curious little thing that produced random animals when reached into. It would become a recurring joke.

Eventually, the party reunited. They entered the castle proper and found themselves before a large door — the entrance to the throne room.

## Part 4: The Sleeping King

Inside the throne room, **King <a href="/oversee/npcs/Grolnok">Grolnok</a>** — the goblin king — lay asleep on his throne.

The party tried every polite approach they could think of:
- Careful discussion
- Water splashed on his face
- Fake rooster sounds

(It is unclear which of these woke him, but it is likely the rooster.)

Grolnok woke without anger. He was willing to negotiate. He was willing, even, to give up the relic — a **green gem** — to adventurers who had come this far.

And then the party mentioned **Lei Alejandro**.

Everything changed.

Grolnok knew Alejandro. He knew him well. His daughter — **<a href="/Ratu%20Ghaniba">Ratu Ghaniba</a>** — had married Alejandro. She had died in childbirth, and the child had been stillborn. The gem was not treasure. It was **inheritance**. It was meant for goblin heirs. It was meant for the children of a daughter who never lived long enough to see them.

The revelation that Alejandro — the revered ancestor, the honored name — had loved a goblin queen shattered everything the party thought they knew about the world's history.

Grolnok attacked.

The party won. They took the gem. And because they did not know what the gem truly was, they could not control it. The castle shook. Stones fell. Towers collapsed. And Greyland Castle — along with whatever remained of Grolnok's throne — sank into the sea.

## Part 5: The Fisherman

The party woke on a small fishing boat, coughing seawater, surrounded by the smell of fish.

An old dwarf fisherman named **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Guanik%20Smith">Guanik</a>** had pulled them from the wreckage. He brought them to his home on **<a href="/Pulau%20Dima">Dima Island</a>** — a tropical island with white sand and coconut trees — where he lived with his wife **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Janja">Janja</a>** and their three children: **<a href="/Dontol">Dontol</a>** (the eldest), **<a href="/Huan">Huan</a>** and **<a href="/Nila">Nila</a>**.

At first, it was peace. Recovery. A warm home. Guanik told them stories about the sea. His wife made tea. The children showed the party their shell collection.

And then came the truth Guanik had been waiting to share.

The green gem was one of **four treasures of Lei**, he said. Four gems. Four heirs. Four names:
- **Wendigo** — wind
- **Firage** — fire
- **Watigi** — water
- **Roiga** — earth

The party did not understand what they had stolen. But someone else did.

## Part 6: The Will of Grin

The taking of the green gem from its anti-magic chest had awakened something sleeping. A group known as **<a href="/oversee/factions/Will%20of%20Grin">Will of Grin</a>** arrived at Dima Island while the party was away.

They burned everything. They killed Guanik. They killed Janja. They scattered the children.

When the party returned, they found ashes.

They fought the attackers — led by a young Grin commander named **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Jarko">Jarko</a>** — in a battle that nearly killed half of them. The forest burned around them. The sand was stained red.

And then something impossible happened.

The five adventurers — all of them atheists, all of them skeptics, all of them abandoned by gods and by fate — felt something touch them. A warmth. A weight. Like a hand on a shoulder that was not there.

They would not understand it until later. But in that moment, **something divine noticed them for the first time.**

They defeated Jarko. They took a **red gem** from his body.

And then, without knowing what they had done, they boarded the **Will of Grin ship** anchored off the coast.

The ship did not need a crew. It moved on its own. It had a destination already set.

**Pirizia.**
