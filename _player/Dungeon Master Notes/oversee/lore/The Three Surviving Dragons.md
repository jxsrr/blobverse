> [!info] The Last of the Ancient Dragons
> Once there were thousands. Now there are three. They are hiding.

## <a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a> — The Silver Wind
- **Color:** Silver. **Element:** Cold.
- **Location:** Spire of Whispering Ice (Frosthaven Peak).
- **Status:** Alive, weakened by Safayer poison.
- **Role:** Syl's mother. Patron of Klan Frostscale.
- **Personality:** Protective. Loyal. Broken by what was done to her.

## Verdantus — The Green Shadow
- **Color:** Green. **Element:** Poison.
- **Location:** Kanopi Tanpa Ujung (Eastern Forest).
- **Status:** Alive, hidden.
- **Role:** Guardian of the last primordial forest.
- **Personality:** Ancient. Slow. Wise. He has not spoken to a mortal in 1,000 years.

## Aurelia — The Blue Depths
- **Color:** Blue. **Element:** Water.
- **Location:** Danau Ajaib (Elis, deep lake).
- **Status:** Alive, hidden.
- **Role:** Guardian of the pure water sources of Elis.
- **Personality:** Unknown. She has not been seen in 2,000 years.

## What the Party Knows
- They know <a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a>. They do not know the others exist.
- The dragons are the last of their kind.
