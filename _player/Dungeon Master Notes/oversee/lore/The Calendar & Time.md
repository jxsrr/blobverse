> [!info] How Time Works in Oversee
> The current year is **700 AV** (After Valla). But time is not straightforward — and the calendar is a lie.

## The Calendar
- **AV** = After Valla. Year 1 AV is when **Lei Firsa forced Simon Greenbottle into the Valla role** — not when she "declared herself the first Valla." That was a rumor she encouraged.
- **BV** = Before Valla. The old calendar. Now used only by scholars.
- The current year is **700 AV**.

## Time Anomalies
- The **Tower of Time** in Padre City has a clock that runs **backwards**.
- **Emelanica** can modulate time.
- The **Gap** has no time. It is outside causality.
- **Echoes** exist outside time. They are fragments of moments.
- **The Valla** experiences time differently. Simon has been conscious for 700 years — but it feels like an eternity. He does not age. He does not sleep. He simply **is**.

## What the Party Knows
- The current year is 700 AV.
- The calendar was set by Lei Firsa after she forced Simon into the Valla role.
- The world believes "Lei the Great" ascended to godhood. The party does not know the truth yet.

## What the Party Does NOT Know
- The calendar is a lie. Lei Firsa changed the year count to make herself the center of history.
- The "real" year is closer to **1700 BV** — not 700 AV. The party is living in a rewritten timeline.
- If the party ever restores the true calendar, it will destabilize the Commonwealth. The legitimacy of the Triase, the Klan Lei, and the entire One Voice Commonwealth rests on the lie that 1 AV was a holy year.
- The Tower of Time's backwards clock is counting down to something. No one knows what. **The DM should decide.** It may be counting down to the Rapture. It may be counting down to Lei Firsa's return. It may be counting down to the moment the seal finally breaks.
