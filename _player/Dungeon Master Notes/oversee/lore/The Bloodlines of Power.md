> [!info] Who Has Magic and Why
> Magic is not random. It follows bloodlines. It follows the Klan Lei.

## The 20% Rule
- Only **20%** of the population has magical talent.
- The other 80% are **Amagis** — non-magic users. They are the workers, farmers, soldiers, and craftsmen.
- The 20% are the **Magis** — the elite. They are the wizards, sorcerers, and clerics.

## The Bloodlines
- **Klan Lei** — the four branches (Wendigo, Firage, Watigi, Roiga). They have the strongest magic because they are directly descended from Simon, the Valla.
- **The Grin** — magic-deaf. They cannot use magic. This is their curse and their protection.
- **The Inbu** — extinct. Their blood is diluted in all races.
- **The Tinkers** — mostly Amagis. They use technology instead of magic.
- **The Dragons** — not a bloodline. They are the source of magic for the Dragonborn.

## The Truth
- Magic is not hereditary in the traditional sense. It is a **connection to the Valla**.
- Simon is the Valla. His soul is stretched across the world. Everyone with magic is connected to him.
- The Klan Lei has the strongest magic because they are **closest** to Simon. They share his blood.
- If Simon is freed, the magic will fade. The Klan Lei will become Amagis.

## What the Party Knows
- Magic is rare. The Klan Lei is powerful.
- They do not know the connection to Simon.
