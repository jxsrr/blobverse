> [!info] The Last Giants
> Ancient creatures from before the Rapture. Gentle. Massive. Dying.

## What Are Behemoths?
- Mammalian giants. The size of small hills. Slow-moving. Gentle.
- They eat the **primordial flora** — plants that filter Ambitio-Ether. They stabilize the land by their presence.
- They were once thousands. Now there are only a dozen.
- They live in **Pegunungan Kabut** (Elis Selatan). They are the last of their kind.

## The Last Behemoth
- The oldest Behemoth is **dying**. It has been alive since before the Rapture.
- It is not sick. It is **tired**. It has seen too much. It is ready to go.
- It is guarded by the **Hermit of the Misty Mountains** — an old man who has been caring for it for 50 years.

## The Behemoth's Tear
- A Behemoth, when it dies, sheds a single tear. This tear is a **gemstone**.
- The tear can **heal any wound** once — including lost limbs, severed souls, and death (within 1 minute).
- It cannot heal the effects of the Edisi Law override clause.

## What the Party Knows
- They have heard legends of the Behemoths.
- They do not know the Tear exists.
