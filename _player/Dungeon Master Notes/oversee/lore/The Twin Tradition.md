> [!info] The Darkest Secret of Klan Lei
> When twins are born to the Klan, one must die. It is the law. It is the curse.

## What Is the Tradition?
- When a Klan Lei mother gives birth to twins, the elder twin is kept. The younger twin is **taken**.
- The younger twin is killed in a quiet ceremony. Their blood is offered to the Valla.
- The Klan believes this **concentrates the power** in the surviving twin.
- It has been practiced for roughly **650 years**.

## Why?
- The Klan believes magic is a finite resource. Twins split the magical potential in half. Killing one twin concentrates the power in the other.
- **This is a lie.** The Klan invented the tradition to **control** their bloodlines and to **feed** their matriarch.
- The real reasons are:
  - **Control.** Twins are unpredictable. They can be more powerful than expected. They can challenge the hierarchy.
  - **Fuel.** Every killed twin's soul is drawn toward the Valla — and the Klan's matriarch has learned to skim that energy for herself. The tradition is not just eugenics. It is a **feeding mechanism**.
  - **Weakness.** Twins are **weaker** than single-born heirs. Their power is split. Klan Lei could not afford weak children — not when their entire legitimacy rested on being the strongest.

- **She discovered a pattern:**
  - When a child is born **alone** — no twin — the full elemental power of the bloodline flows into them. They are strong. They are compatible with the Gemhearts.
  - When **twins** are born, the power is **split**. Each child receives only half — sometimes less.
  - **The intelligent twin** — the one who develops faster, thinks sharper, speaks sooner — gets the **least** magic. Their mind takes the power that their body would have carried.
  - **The unintelligent twin** — the one who is slower, quieter, simpler — gets the **most** magic. Their mind does not compete for the power. Their body absorbs it.
- **This was the key.** The twin tradition was not just about concentrating power — it was about **which twin** to keep. The Klan kept the **elder** twin — usually the smarter one. But the smarter one was the **weaker** one. The tradition was flawed. It was killing the wrong twin.

## The Fix — Hildag Grin's Experiments
- **She had read about the Arleschia family.** A minor noble family in Elis with a strange bloodline. The Arleschias carried a **fragment of the earth element** — not enough to be mages, but enough to **stabilize** other bloodlines. The Arleschia blood acted as a **bridge** — it allowed the Lei and Grin blood to coexist without canceling each other out.
- **Hildag realized the Arleschias were the key.** She needed a child who was **Grin** (from her line), **Lei Firage** (from the father), and **Arleschia** (from the mother). That child would be the first **True Heir** — a being who carried both anti-magic and pure elemental power, without the weakness of twins.
- **Accarus and Antrem were her rough drafts.** Twin sons. Both had the Grin anti-magic. Both had dormant Lei Firage fire. But they were **twins** — their power was split. Accarus got the intelligence. Antrem got the magic. Neither was whole.
- **Vya was her magnum opus.** She bred Vya after Jarko's death. Vya is not a twin. She is the **full inheritance** — Grin anti-magic, Lei Firage fire, and Arleschia stability. She is the first True Heir. She is the child Hildag had been trying to create for centuries.

## Who Invented the Tradition?
- Before 50 AV, twins were common and celebrated among the Lei. After 50 AV, they were a death sentence.

## The Survivors
- **Valerius Thorne** — the older twin. Kept. Raised in the light. He knows his twin was hidden, not killed. He has been hunting **Darvin** ever since.
- **Accarus Grin** and **Antrem Klaus** — the **only surviving twins** of the tradition. Sons of **Hildag Grin** and a **Lei Firage descendant**. Their survival was accidental. Hildag protected them. They are the exception that proves the rule.

## Who Is NOT a Twin
- **Livia Vane** is **not a twin.** She was alone from the start. She was adopted by an old man who was later revealed to be **Lei Alejandro**. Her "twin who should have died" story is a fabrication — possibly her own, possibly Alejandro's, possibly the Commonwealth's. She believes it. She is wrong.
- **Vya Arleschia** is **not a twin.** She is **Hildag Grin's magnum opus** — a later child, deliberately bred after **Jarko's death** to combine the anti-magic protection of the Grin bloodline with the dormant fire of Lei Firage and the stabilizing blood of the Arleschia family. She was never part of a twin pair. She is the refinement, not the experiment.

## What the Party Knows
- They know the tradition exists.
- They know Vya is a True Heir.
- They know Darvin is a True Heir.
- They do not know Livia's full story.
- They do not know Vya's full origin.
