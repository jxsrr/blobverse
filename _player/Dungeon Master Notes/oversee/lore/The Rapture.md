> [!info] The End of the High Magic Age
> A cosmic event that destroyed every advanced magical civilization in Oversee. It happened many times. It will happen again.

## What Is the Rapture?
- A **cosmic harvest**. The Overseer notices a civilization that has produced too much Ambitio-Ether. It sends a **Devourer Comet** — a shard of its hunger.
- The comet does not burn. It **absorbs light**.
- When the comet passes over a region, every creature with magical talent is **pulled into the sky**. They do not scream. They do not resist. They simply dissolve into mist and rise.
- The comet leaves behind empty clothes, useless magic items, and silent cities.
- The Rapture has happened **at least four times** in recorded history.

## The Signs
- The sky flashes a color beyond the visible spectrum.
- Mages feel dizzy. Then euphoric. Then they begin to dissolve.
- Animals flee. Plants wilt. The air smells of ozone and salt.
- The comet appears in the sky, moving slowly. It is visible for weeks.

## The Survivors
- Only the **magic-deaf** survive. The Grin. The Amagis. The common people.
- They inherit the ruins. They rebuild. They forget.
- The cycle repeats.

## The Silent Rapture
- The **Silent Rapture** is the **same event** as the Rapture — not a separate phenomenon. It is the **quiet** version. The slow, invisible drain of Ambitio-Ether from a civilization that never notices it is being eaten.
- The Grin are the only race that is immune to both — because they are magic-deaf. They are invisible to the Oversight.

## What the Party Knows
- The Rapture is a legend. Most people believe it is a myth.
- The ruins of ancient cities are proof that it happened.
