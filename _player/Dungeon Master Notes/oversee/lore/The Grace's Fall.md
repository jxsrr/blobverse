> [!info] The Demon Hunters Who Died
> Shin's old order. Destroyed in **690 AV** during one of his awakenings. Only he survived.

## What Was The Grace?
- An order of demon hunters. Based in Elis. Trained to fight Gap-touched creatures.
- They were not a religious order. They were **soldiers**. They fought what others would not.
- They were the **only defense** against the things that slipped through the Gap.
- They were **not** a Cycle Clan front. They were independent. They were funded by local lords, by grateful villages, by the families of the people they saved.
- **They were a family.** The leader — **Helius** — was the father. Every member was his child. They called each other "brother" and "sister." They called him "Father."
- They found Shin when he was dying of starvation on the shore of Elis. They took him in. They fed him. They gave him a name. They gave him a home.

## The Members
- **Helius** — the father. The leader. An old man who had been an adventurer once, a long time ago. He had lost his party. He had lost everyone he loved. He founded The Grace as a family — a new family, one that would not die. He was not a warrior. He was not a mage. He was a **father**.
- **Shin Van Hel** — the youngest. The one with the smile. The one with the gloves. The one who never talked about his past. He was found on the shore, dying of starvation. Helius took him in. The others treated him as their younger brother.
- **Edra** — the warm one. She befriended Shin on his first day. She sat beside him without being asked. She never treated him as strange. She never asked about the gloves. She never asked about the smile. She was the heart of The Grace.
- **Crest** — the disciplined one. He observed. He documented. He suspected something was wrong with Shin — but he never blamed him. He tried to save him. He was the brain of The Grace.
- **Wati** — the quiet one. A young woman with a gift for healing. She was the one who patched up the others after every fight. She was the one who kept them alive. She was the one who died first.

## The Fall
- In **10 AV**, a **massive Gap tear** opened in Elis. Demons poured through.
- The Grace fought. They held the line. They died.
- **A barbed devil** — a creature from the Gap, a predator with bone spikes and burning eyes — led the assault.
- Shin, Edra, Crest, and Wati fought it together. They were **defeated**. The barbed devil was too strong.
- And then **something happened**.
- **Shin's body changed.** Something rose from behind him — not a creature, not a spell, but a **presence** — a shadow of something ancient and terrible. It was **Zargoth** — the father of the Grin, the demon who loved Gavaevodata, the fragment that had been sleeping inside Shin since he was created.
- Zargoth did not take control. He **helped**. He fought beside his family — beside Shin's family. He protected them.
- But the barbed devil was not alone. It called its master — **Zargoyle** — the predator, the servant of the Overseer, the monster who would one day destroy everything.
- Zargoyle came.
- And when Zargoyle **choked Shin** — when his claws closed around Shin's throat — Shin's body **froze**. A dome of ice erupted from him — not a spell, not a curse, but a **reaction** — a desperate, instinctive burst of power that froze everything around him.
- When the ice melted, **Wati was dead**. **Edra was dying**. **Crest was dying**. The barbed devil was gone. Zargoyle was gone.
- And Shin was **still alive** — holding both Zargoth and Zargoyle inside him, though he did not know it.
- **He remembers only white. And the screams of his siblings.**

## What Shin Knows
- He was found by The Grace. He was raised by Helius. He was loved by Edra and Crest.
- He does not remember what happened during the fall. He remembers only white, and screaming, and then falling back into slumber before waking again in **700 AV**.
- He believes he destroyed The Grace. He believes he killed his family.
- He does not know about Zargoth. He does not know about Zargoyle. He does not know about the frozen dome.
- He does not know that Edra and Crest did not blame him. He does not know that they tried to save him.
- He does not know that Edra wrote him a letter before she died. It is in Zundabun. He has never read it.
