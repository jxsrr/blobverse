> [!info] Four Paths
> When the seal finally cracks, the party must choose. There is no "right" answer. There is only the path they can live with.

## The Situation
- **Simon Greenbottle** is the Valla. He has been holding the seal for 700 years. His soul is cracking.
- **The Architect** is still holding the seal alongside him. It is fading. It has been holding for thousands of years. It has almost nothing left.
- **The Overseer** is always hungry. It is always watching. It is always waiting.
- **The Rapture** will happen again — within three years — if the party does nothing.
- **The Choice** must be made. There is no way to avoid it. There is no way to save everyone. There is only the path they can live with.

## Path 1: The Sacrifice
- One PC becomes the new Valla. They sit at the threshold for eternity. They hold back the Overseer. They suffer.
- The Architect is released. Simon is released. The new Valla takes up the burden.
- **Requires:** A willing sacrifice, purified by **Safayer**. All four Gemhearts. The original **Edisi Law**. The Architect's consent.
- **Outcome:** The world is saved. The PC is lost. The seal holds — for now.
- **Epilogue:** Bittersweet. The world continues. The PC's name is remembered. The new Valla is honored — but they are also a prisoner.

## Path 2: The Bitter Sacrifice
- The party chooses a "strong soul" to feed the seal — but not as a Valla. As a **battery**. A soul that is drained slowly, over centuries, to keep the seal stable.
- This is what **Lei Alejandro** did to the **Initial Six**. It is what **Lei Firsa** did to **Grin Qain**. It is what the Cycle Clan did for thousands of years.
- **Requires:** A strong soul. A willing or unwilling sacrifice. The Gemhearts to anchor the ritual. No Safayer — because the soul is not meant to be purified. It is meant to be drained.
- **Outcome:** The seal holds longer. The party wins time. But the cost is moral. They have become what they fought against.
- **Epilogue:** Dark. The party wins, but they are not heroes. They are survivors. They have blood on their hands. They will never wash it off.

## Path 3: The Third Path — The Architect's Mercy
- The party refuses both options. They gather all four Gemhearts, all six blades, all three Swan relics, and the original Edisi Law.
- They perform the **Architect's Mercy** — the override clause. They free the Valla without killing him. They release the Architect. They seal the Gap forever.
- **Requires:**
  - **All four Gemhearts** — Emelanica, Safayer, Rubion, Topaju.
  - **All six blades** — Swaith, Rayio, Kkana, Frostfang, Zenahhi, Terrde.
  - **All three Swan relics** — Gustaero, Niette, Alnean.
  - **The original Edisi Law** — the promise Guanik Wiseman signed.
  - **The Architect's consent** — which means Guanik Wiseman must be present.
- **Outcome:** The Valla is freed. The Architect is released. The Gap is sealed. The world is saved. Magic becomes wild — released from the Valla's filter. The world is changed forever.
- **Epilogue:** Transformative. The world is not the same. Magic is free. The Valla is free. The party are legends.

## Path 4: The Tyranny
- One PC becomes the new Valla — but refuses to be a passive seal. They seize the power of the Gap. They become the **gate** itself.
- They control the flow of energy between the world and the Overseer. They become a **god-king**. They rule Oversee forever.
- **Requires:** A PC with enough power — and enough ambition. The Gemhearts. The Swan relics. The blades. The Edisi Law. But not the Architect's consent. They take the power by force.
- **Outcome:** The world is stable. The Overseer is held back. But the party has become a tyrant. They have become what they fought against.
- **Epilogue:** Dark. The PC is immortal. The party is either subjugated or destroyed. The world is stable, but not free.
