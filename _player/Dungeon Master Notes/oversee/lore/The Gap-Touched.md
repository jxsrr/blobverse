> [!info] What Are They?
> Creatures and places that have been changed by proximity to the Gap.

## What Happens When the Gap Touches Something
- **Physical Mutation:** Limbs split, eyes multiply, skin turns translucent.
- **Mental Mutation:** Thoughts become audible. Dreams leak into reality. Memory and imagination blend.
- **Magical Mutation:** Spells become wild. Magic becomes unreliable. Sometimes it works better. Sometimes it fails catastrophically.

## Known Gap-Touched Zones
- **Garberro Forest** — the forest where Rayio was thrown. Small Gap tears open and close. The Spirit King lives here.
- **The Depths of Arujaya** — the sludge is Ambitio-Ether. Syl saw the Overseer here.
- **The Cloud Colony** — the fog is a permanent Gap-touched zone. It hides the colony from the Overseer — and from the world.
- **The Rune of the Cycle** — a mild Gap-touched zone. It is warded, but the ward is weakening.

## Known Gap-Touched Creatures
- **The Spirit King** — a Cycle Clan cartographer who got lost.
- **The Well Guardian** — a golden water elemental, spawned from Topaju.
- **The Overseer's Blobs** — tentacled things that exist in the Gap. They cannot cross into Oversee unless the Gap is opened.

## What the Party Should Know
- The Gap is not evil. It is a **wound in reality**.
- Being touched by the Gap is not a curse. It is a **change**.
- Some people survive it. Some don't. Some become something new.
