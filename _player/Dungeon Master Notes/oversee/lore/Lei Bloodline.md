> [!info] The Four Branches
> The ruling magical aristocracy of Oversee. Four bloodlines. Four elements. One source.

## Origins
- Founded by **Lei Firsa** in **01 AV** — after she forced **Simon Greenbottle** into the Valla role.
- She rewrote the calendar, rewrote history, and rewrote herself. The world was told "Lei the Great" ascended to godhood. The truth is Simon is the Valla, and Lei Firsa built her dynasty on top of his sacrifice.
- She had **four children** — conceived **before** Simon was placed on the altar. Lei Firsa was already pregnant with quadruplets when the ritual happened.
- Her four children — **Wendigo, Firage, Watigi, Roiga** — each inherited one elemental aspect of Simon's Valla-touched blood.
- Each branch was tied to a Gemheart — **but not always in the way the Klan claims**.

## The Rules of Succession
- Only the **firstborn** of each branch may inherit the branch's Gemheart **connection**.
- The blood must be **pure** — no mixing with non-Lei blood.
- **Twins are a problem.** When twins are born, one must be killed. The survivor inherits the full power.
- This tradition is cruel. It has produced monsters.

## The Twin Tradition — The Darkest Rule
- Invented by **Lei Firsa** around **50 AV** — not ancient, not natural. A deliberate policy to concentrate magical power in her bloodline.
- Every twin born to a Lei mother since then has been separated — one kept, one killed.
- The **surviving twin absorbs the power** of the dead twin's soul. This is the real reason the Klan Lei is so strong. It is not just inheritance — it is **accumulated sacrifice**.
- **Livia Vane** is **not** a twin. She was alone from the start and was adopted by an old man revealed to be **Lei Alejandro**.
- **Accarus** and **Antrem** are the exception — twins who were **not killed**. They are the sons of **Hildag Grin** and a **Lei Firage descendant**. Their survival was accidental. Their existence is a secret.

## Hildag Grin's Experiments
- Hildag Grin spent decades trying to breed a child who was both **anti-magic** (invisible to the Overseer) and **Lei-blooded** (carrying the pure elemental power of Simon's lineage).
- She experimented with different Lei bloodlines and different methods. Every attempt produced a child who was **one or the other** — never both.
- **Accarus and Antrem** were her maximum accidental success — twin boys, born with **anti-magic from their Grin mother** and **dormant Lei Firage fire from their father**. They were not killed. Hildag protected them.
- Later — **Vya** became her **magnum opus**. Vya is not a twin. She is Hildag's final refinement — the child who has the **full potential** of both bloodlines. Hildag recognized this only after **Jarko's death**, when Vya's fire ignited.

## What We Know
- The Klan is the true power behind the Commonwealth.
- Most high-ranking officials are Klan Lei descendants.
- They believe their power comes from their blood. **It does not.** It comes from **Simon** — the Valla. Every Lei descendant carries a drop of his sacrifice.
- If Simon is freed, the Klan Lei will lose their power. They will do anything to prevent this.
- **The "Lei Firsa declared herself the first Valla" story is a rumor.** No one outside the Cycle Clan knows Simon is the Valla. The public believes Lei Firsa / "Lei the Great" ascended. This lie is the foundation of the Klan's legitimacy.
