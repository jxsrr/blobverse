> [!info] The Space Between
> A dimension of failed creation. The barrier between Oversee and the Overseer.

## What Is The Gap?
- The **leftover matter** from the Overseer's failed digestion.
- Matter that was partially consumed but did not become the Architect.
- A chaotic space between dimensions. Neither fully real nor fully unreal.

## Who Lives There?
- **Devils, demons, tieflings, imps** — the "red-skinned" races.
- They are not evil in the moral sense. They are **scavengers**.
- They eat the crumbs of Ambitio-Ether that leak from Oversee.
- They need Oversee to survive — if the Overseer eats everything, they starve too.

## The Gap and Oversee
- The Gap **hides** Oversee from the Overseer.
- It is a thin membrane. It can be **thickened** or **thinned**.
- **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Valerius%20Thorne">Valerius</a>** wants to thicken the Gap — merge it with Oversee.
- The **sludge** in Arujaya's Depths is Ambitio-Ether residue from a Gap leak.

## Known Entities from the Gap
- **Zargoth** — an ancient, wise devil. Loved Gavaevodata. Father of the Grin bloodline.
- **Grin bloodline** — descendants of Zargoth and Gavaevodata. Eternal smile. Magic-deaf.
- **Renal (Qain)** — an Imp bound to Ama.

## What We Know
- The Gap is not hell. It is a **crack in reality**.
- iblis are not enemies. They are neighbors.
- The Gap can be opened. It has been opened before.
