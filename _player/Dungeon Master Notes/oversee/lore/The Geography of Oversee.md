> [!info] The Known World
> Four continents. One ocean. One sky.

## Harka — The East
- **Climate:** Temperate. Fertile. Green.
- **Terrain:** Rice fields, forests, mountains (Arbor Mountains), the Gruffgud Valley.
- **Cities:** Yawning Town, Nesia, Ghuffghuff, Gudameng, Padre City, Javan Town, Muangching Bay.
- **Notable Features:** The Great Lei Statue, the First Tree (hidden), the Wind Silo (hidden).

## Pirizia — The West
- **Climate:** Cold. Mountainous. Harsh.
- **Terrain:** Peaks (Frosthaven), deserts (Maw Desert), industrial wasteland (Ironhold), the Spire of Whispering Ice.
- **Cities:** Frostheart Village, Maw Desert (hidden), Ironhold.
- **Notable Features:** The Frostscale Monolith, the Frozen Legion.

## Rioquan — The North
- **Climate:** Tropical. Island. Divided.
- **Terrain:** Vertical city (Juto Port City), canyons (Canyon of Lament), islands, the Silent Docks.
- **Cities:** Juto Port City, Graen, Zundabun, Thainarv.
- **Notable Features:** The Observatorium, the Well of Wonders (hidden), the Bahtera Bawah Tanah (under the mountains).

## Elis — The South
- **Climate:** Arid. Wild. Mysterious.
- **Terrain:** Deserts, jungles, mountains (Pegunungan Kabut), the First Tree (deep jungle).
- **Cities:** Chenalia (rebuilt), Ghur'Thar (ruined), Dur-Khazim (ruined).
- **Notable Features:** The Shell (in the sky), the Behemoths, the ruins of The Grace.

## The Ocean
- The **Elis Ocean** — separates Elis from everything.
- The **Harka Sea** — between Harka and Pirizia.
- The **Rioquan Straits** — between Rioquan and the other continents.
- The **Gap** — not an ocean. A dimensional tear. The only way to reach the Overseer.

## What the Party Knows
- They have visited Harka, Rioquan, Pirizia, and Elis.
- They have not explored the full map.
