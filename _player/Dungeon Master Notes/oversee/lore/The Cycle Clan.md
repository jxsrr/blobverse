> [!info] The Keepers of the Secret
> The organization that maintains the seal. Founded by Guanik Wiseman. Now scattered.

## Origins
- Founded by **Guanik Wiseman** in 1040 BV.
- He learned the truth: the world is a seal. The Valla is a sacrifice. The Overseer is always hungry.
- He founded the Cycle Clan to protect this secret and maintain the seal.
- He was the **first Kanselir Agung** of the One Voice Commonwealth — the search for the next shepherd.

## Purpose
- **Protect the secret** from those who would misuse it.
- **Find the shepherd** — the next Valla, the next person willing to hold the seal.

## The Fall of the Cycle Clan
- She scattered the six legendary blades across the world.
- She rewrote history: Simon became "Lei the Great." She became a male wizard.
- She corrupted the One Voice Commonwealth. She created the Triase. She placed her descendants in key positions.
- The Cycle Clan was shattered. The original members — the ones who remembered Guanik's promise — were killed, silenced, or erased.
- Some joined **Keeper of Secret**. Some joined **Will of Grin**. Some went mad.

## What We Know
- The Cycle Clan is not evil. It is **desperate**.
- It made terrible choices for the survival of the world.
- Its secrets are the key to the endgame.

## What the Party Does NOT Know
- The Cycle Clan was **originally good**. It was a search for the shepherd, not a dynasty.
- **Rivan** is the main Cycle Clan contact the party will meet. He is an artist who drew the HVS schematics under Guanik's guidance. He lives in the Half House in Zundabun.
- There is no four-Cipher structure. The Ciphers were a fabrication — or a later corruption — invented to replace the original organization.
