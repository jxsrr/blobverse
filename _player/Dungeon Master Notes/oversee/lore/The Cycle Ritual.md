> [!info] What the Party Knows
> The ritual that maintains the seal. It is not a replacement. It is a **maintenance**. It has been performed for thousands of years — but the Valla has only been replaced **once**.

## What the Ritual Really Is
- The Cycle Ritual is **not** a ritual of replacement. It is a ritual of **maintenance**.
- It was designed by the **Architect** — the first Valla — to slow the burning of its own soul. It is a way to keep the seal stable. It is a way to buy time.
- The ritual requires the **Gemhearts** — the four organs of the Architect. They are placed at the four corners of the altar. They create a **Stasis Field** — a space where time is slowed, where Ambitio-Ether is filtered, where the Architect's soul can rest.
- The ritual does **not** replace the Valla. It **sustains** the Valla. It gives the Architect another century, another decade, another year. It is a **maintenance procedure** — not a sacrifice.

## The Only Replacement — Simon Greenbottle
- The ritual has been performed **thousands of times** — but always for the same purpose: to maintain the Architect. To slow its burning. To keep the seal stable.
- The Valla has been replaced **only once**. In **01 BV**. When **Lei Firsa** forced **Simon Greenbottle** into the role.
- She did not perform the ritual correctly. She did not use **Safayer** to purify him. She did not release the Architect. She simply **added** Simon to the seal — a second soul, a second prisoner, a second patch on a wound that was never meant to be patched this way.
- This is why the seal is unstable. This is why Simon is in pain. This is why the Echoes exist.

## The Steps — The Maintenance Ritual
1. **Preparation:** The Cycle Clan gathers the Gemhearts they have. They are placed at the four corners of the altar.
2. **Stasis Field:** The Gemhearts create a sphere of slowed time. Ambitio-Ether is filtered. The Architect's soul can rest.
3. **The Offering:** The Cycle Clan offers a small portion of pure Ambitio-Ether — drawn from their own souls, not from a sacrifice. It is a tithe, not a death.
4. **The Anchor:** The Gemhearts re-anchor the seal. The Architect's soul stabilizes. The world is safe for another generation.
5. **The Release:** The Cycle Clan members who participated in the ritual are exhausted for days. They recover. No one dies. No one is sacrificed. The ritual is **maintenance** — not murder.

## The Steps — The Corrupted Ritual (Lei Firsa's Version)
1. **Preparation:** Lei Firsa gathered the Gemhearts she had — Emelanica, Rubion, Topaju. She did not have Safayer.
2. **Stasis Field:** The Gemhearts created a sphere of stopped time. No Ambitio-Ether could leak out. No one could escape.
3. **The Old Valla:** The Architect was **not** released. It was left in place — weakened, fading, still holding the seal.
4. **The Call:** The Overseer's hunger was momentarily unsealed. It reached for the strongest soul in the Stasis Field.
5. **The New Valla:** Simon was taken. His soul was stretched across the threshold. He became the second Valla.
6. **The Seal:** The Gemhearts re-anchored the seal. The world was safe again — but the seal was unstable. The Architect was still there. Simon was still there. Two souls, one seal. It was never meant to be this way.

## What Can Go Wrong
- **Missing Gemheart:** The ritual is incomplete. The Architect's soul is not fully stabilized. The seal weakens faster.
- **No Purification (no Safayer):** If the ritual is used to replace the Valla (which it should never be), the soul is not purified. The new Valla retains memory and emotion. Echoes form. This is what happened to Simon. This is why the Echoes exist.
- **Interruption:** If the ritual is interrupted, the seal collapses. The Overseer breaks through.
- **Corruption:** If the ritual is used for the wrong purpose — to add a soul, to steal power, to open the Gap — it becomes something else entirely. It becomes a weapon. This is what Lei Firsa did.

## What the Party Can Do
- They can **perform the maintenance ritual** — with the Gemhearts they have, with the Cycle Clan's original steps. This will slow the Architect's burning. It will buy time. It will not free Simon.
- They can **use the override clause** — the Architect's Mercy. This requires all four Gemhearts, all six weapons of the three trios, the original Edisi Law, and the Architect's consent. This is the third path.
- They can **replace the Valla** — but this requires a willing sacrifice, purified by Safayer. The old Valla is released. The new Valla takes up the burden. This is the sacrifice path.
- They can **pierce the Gap** — face the Overseer directly. This requires the Swan Trio, the Bat Trio, and the Fool Trio. Without them, the party cannot survive the Gap. Without them, the third path is impossible.
