> [!info] What the Party Knows
> The ritual that maintains the seal. It is not a replacement. It is a **maintenance**. It has been performed for thousands of years — but the Valla has only been replaced **once**.

## What the Ritual Really Is
- The Cycle Ritual is **not** a ritual of replacement. It is a ritual of **maintenance**.

- This is why the seal is unstable. This is why Simon is in pain. This is why the Echoes exist.

## The Steps — The Maintenance Ritual
1. **Preparation:** The Cycle Clan gathers the Gemhearts they have. They are placed at the four corners of the altar.
3. **The Offering:** The Cycle Clan offers a small portion of pure Ambitio-Ether — drawn from their own souls, not from a sacrifice. It is a tithe, not a death.
5. **The Release:** The Cycle Clan members who participated in the ritual are exhausted for days. They recover. No one dies. No one is sacrificed. The ritual is **maintenance** — not murder.

2. **Stasis Field:** The Gemhearts created a sphere of stopped time. No Ambitio-Ether could leak out. No one could escape.
4. **The Call:** The Overseer's hunger was momentarily unsealed. It reached for the strongest soul in the Stasis Field.
5. **The New Valla:** Simon was taken. His soul was stretched across the threshold. He became the second Valla.

## What Can Go Wrong
- **No Purification (no Safayer):** If the ritual is used to replace the Valla (which it should never be), the soul is not purified. The new Valla retains memory and emotion. Echoes form. This is what happened to Simon. This is why the Echoes exist.
- **Interruption:** If the ritual is interrupted, the seal collapses. The Overseer breaks through.

## What the Party Can Do
- They can **replace the Valla** — but this requires a willing sacrifice, purified by Safayer. The old Valla is released. The new Valla takes up the burden. This is the sacrifice path.
- They can **pierce the Gap** — face the Overseer directly. This requires the Swan Trio, the Bat Trio, and the Fool Trio. Without them, the party cannot survive the Gap. Without them, the third path is impossible.
