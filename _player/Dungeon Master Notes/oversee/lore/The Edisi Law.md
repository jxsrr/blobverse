> [!info] What Is It?
> The legal code of the Commonwealth. But it is also more than that.

## The Public Version
- A thick book of laws. Covers everything from taxes to murder.
- Enforced by the **Garda Mawar** and the **Garda Besi**.
- Every judge in the Commonwealth carries a copy.
- It is the foundation of the Triase.
- It is **not** the original. It is a doctored version — rewritten during the era of **Lei Firsa's** consolidation of power. It enshrines Klan Lei supremacy, hides the truth about the Valla, and makes the twin-killing tradition "legal."
- Most people believe this is the only version. They are wrong.

## The Secret Version
- The original copy is in the **Bahtera Bawah Tanah** — the Underground Ark.
- It is not a book of laws. It is a **promise**.
- It was signed by **Guanik Wiseman** — the first mortal to see the Shell, the founder of the Cycle Clan, the first Kanselir Agung of the One Voice Commonwealth, and the first friend of the Architect.
- It was written by Guanik and the Architect together — the Architect speaking through Guanik's hand, because the Architect was **inside him**.
- It defines the terms of the seal: how the Valla is chosen, how the Gemhearts are used, how the world is protected.
- It contains an **override clause** — the Architect's Mercy.

## The Promise
- The Edisi Law is not a legal code. It is the **promise Guanik Wiseman made to the Architect**.
- Guanik was not afraid. He had seen the world — the killing, the kingdoms taking everything, the chaos. He wanted peace. He wanted **one voice**.
- He told the Architect: *"I will find a way. I will not let your world end in the Rapture. I will find a shepherd. I will protect the seal. I will make one voice for all of Oversee."*
- The Architect accepted. It gave Guanik the **override clause** — the Architect's Mercy — as part of the promise. If Guanik could find a way to free the Valla without destroying the world, the Architect would allow it.
- Guanik founded the **One Voice Commonwealth** to fulfill this promise. It was not originally a government. It was a **search** — a search for the next shepherd, the next Valla, the next person willing to hold the seal.

## The One Voice Commonwealth — Before the Corruption
- **Guanik Wiseman** was the **first Kanselir Agung**. He was not a politician. He was a shepherd — a man who had promised the Architect that he would find a way to save the world.
- The original **Garda Mawar** (Rose Guard) was **not** a law enforcement arm. It was a group of scholars, historians, and witnesses — the ones who remembered the truth and preserved it.
- The original **Garda Besi** (Iron Guard) was **not** a military. It was a group of volunteers — the ones who protected the search, the ones who kept the promise safe.
- The original Commonwealth was **good**. It was not perfect. But it was honest. It was searching for a shepherd.
- **Geldfre** was the Kanselir Agung at the time of the **Initial Six** (200 AV). He was not the first — he inherited the role from Guanik's legacy. He was the one who welcomed the Initial Six. He was the one who trusted **Lei Alejandro**. He was the one who watched the Six disappear.
- After the Great Disappearance (200 AV), the Commonwealth began to change. **Lei Firsa** had been pulling strings for centuries. She had been placing her descendants in key positions. She had been rewriting history. Geldfre was the last Kanselir Agung who remembered the original promise. After him, the Commonwealth became the Triase's Commonwealth — and the Triase became Lei Firsa's.

## The Corruption
- The One Voice Commonwealth was **good** at first. It was not a government. It was a group of people who wanted to protect the world.
- Then **Lei Firsa, Grin Qain, and Simon Greenbottle** arrived.
- Firsa corrupted it. She turned the search for a shepherd into a dynasty. She turned the promise into a lie. She turned the Commonwealth into the Klan Lei's throne.
- The original members of the One Voice Commonwealth — the ones who had sworn to Guanik's promise — **died**. Some were killed. Some were silenced. Some were erased. Firsa, Qain, and Simon are remembered as the trio who ended the original Commonwealth, but only Firsa is guilty. Qain was sacrificed. Simon was forced.
- The Cycle Clan was shattered. The original promise was buried. The Edisi Law — the real one — was hidden in the Bahtera Bawah Tanah.
- **The Garda Mawar and Garda Besi were changed.** The original Rose Guard was a group of scholars and witnesses. The original Iron Guard was a group of volunteers. Under Lei Firsa, they became the law enforcement and military arms of the Triase. The current versions are **not** the originals. They are Firsa's versions.

## What the Party Knows
- Donta's private collection contained a fragment.
- **Rivan** has a copy of the map to the Bahtera Bawah Tanah — and a fragment of the Edisi Law in his basement.
- The override clause might be the key to the "third path."

## What the Party Does NOT Know
- The override clause exists. It is called **The Architect's Mercy**.
- It requires all four Gemhearts, all six blades, and all three Swan relics.
- Without the Swan Trio — Gustaero, Niette, Alnean — the override cannot be performed.
- **Lei Alejandro is alive** and hiding in the Bahtera Bawah Tanah. He is guarding the Edisi Law. He is waiting for someone worthy.
- **Guanik Wiseman is alive.** The Architect is inside him. It has kept him alive for over a thousand years. He is not a ghost. He is not an echo. He is a man — old, tired, and still holding the promise.
- The original Garda Mawar and Garda Besi were **not** the same as the current ones.

### The Public Version — A Lie
- The public Edisi Law was rewritten during the era of Lei Firsa's consolidation of power — after the Initial Six disappeared (200 AV).
- It was designed to:
  - Enshrine Klan Lei supremacy.
  - Hide the truth about the Valla (claiming "Lei the Great" ascended, not that Simon was forced).
  - Make the twin-killing tradition "legal" and "traditional."
  - Give the Triase absolute authority over magic, law, and military.
  - Erase the original promise. Erase Guanik. Erase the search.
- It is enforced by the **Garda Mawar** and the **Garda Besi** — but these are not the original institutions. They are the corrupted versions.
- **Geldfre** was the Kanselir Agung at the time of the Initial Six. He was not the first — he inherited the role from Guanik's legacy. He was the last Kanselir Agung who remembered the original promise.

### The Original Version — Guanik's Promise
- The original Edisi Law is in the **Bahtera Bawah Tanah** — the Underground Ark.
- It was signed by **Guanik Wiseman**.
- It was written by Guanik and the Architect — the Architect inside him, speaking through his hand.
- It is not a legal code. It is a **promise** — Guanik's promise to the Architect that he would find a way to save Oversee, to find a shepherd, to make one voice.
- It defines:
  - The role of the Valla (a living seal, not a god).
  - The function of the Gemhearts (organs of the Architect, not tools).
  - The maintenance rituals for the seal.
  - The **override clause** — the Architect's Mercy.
- It is guarded by **Lei Alejandro** — alive, hiding in the Ark, waiting for someone worthy.

### The Architect Inside Guanik
- The Architect is **inside Guanik Wiseman**. This is how Guanik received his visions. This is how he knew the truth about the Shell. This is how he wrote the Edisi Law — the Architect guided his hand.
- The Architect is **not** an echo. It is not a fragment. It is the **Architect itself** — the same entity that became the first Valla. It split itself: part of it holds the seal at the threshold; part of it lives inside Guanik.
- This is why Guanik has lived for over a thousand years. The Architect sustains him. He is old, tired, and still holding the promise.
- **Guanik is alive.** He is not in the Bahtera Bawah Tanah — he is somewhere else, hidden, waiting. The party may meet him in Season 3. He is the last living founder of the Cycle Clan.
- **If Guanik dies, the Architect fragment inside him will be released.** It will return to the first Valla — the part of the Architect that holds the seal. This will strengthen the seal — briefly. But it will also mean the Architect can no longer guide anyone. Guanik's death is the end of the Architect's voice in the world.

### The One Voice Commonwealth — History
- Founded by **Guanik Wiseman**. He was the first Kanselir Agung.
- It was originally a **search** — a group of people sworn to find the next shepherd, the next Valla, the next person willing to hold the seal.
- It was **not** a government. It was a **promise**.
- The original **Garda Mawar** was a group of scholars, historians, and witnesses — not law enforcement. They remembered the truth and preserved it.
- The original **Garda Besi** was a group of volunteers — not a military. They protected the search.
- At the time of the **Initial Six** (200 AV), **Geldfre** was the Kanselir Agung. He welcomed the Six. He trusted Lei Alejandro. He watched them disappear.
- After the Great Disappearance, **Lei Firsa** had been pulling strings for centuries. She had been placing her descendants in key positions. She had been rewriting history. **Geldfre was the last Kanselir Agung who remembered the original promise.** After him, the Commonwealth became the Triase's Commonwealth — and the Triase became Lei Firsa's.
- **The current Garda Mawar and Garda Besi are not the originals.** They are Firsa's versions. The original institutions died with the original Commonwealth.

### The Override Clause — The Architect's Mercy
- The override clause is called **The Architect's Mercy**.
- It is part of Guanik's promise. The Architect gave it to him as a gift — a way to free the Valla without destroying the world, if he could find a way.
- It states: *"If the Valla is willingly released and a new Valla is willingly chosen, the seal can be transferred without destroying the old Valla."*
- This is the secret Lei Firsa has been hiding for 700 years. She knew the override clause existed. She chose to ignore it because she wanted Simon to suffer.
- If the party uses the override clause, they can **free Simon** without killing him.
- But it is not easy. It requires:
  - **All four Gemhearts** — Emelanica, Safayer, Rubion, Topaju.
  - **All six blades** — Swaith, Rayio, Kkana, Frostfang, Zenahhi, Terrde.
  - **All three Swan relics** — Gustaero, Niette, Alnean.
  - **The original Edisi Law** — the promise itself.
  - **The Architect's consent** — which means Guanik Wiseman must be present, or his promise must be fulfilled.
- Without any one of these, the override cannot be performed. The party must gather them all.

### The Bahtera Bawah Tanah
- The Bahtera Bawah Tanah — the Underground Ark — is a massive structure built by the Cycle Clan beneath the **Pegunungan Arbor**.
- It is a **seed ship** — designed to preserve civilization if the Overseer ever consumed Oversee.
- It contains:
  - **The original Edisi Law** — in the deepest chamber.
  - **Cryo-pods** with preserved Cycle Clan members from 1,000 years ago. They are still alive.
  - **Lei Alejandro** — alive, hiding, guarding the Law.
- The entrance is hidden in the roots of Pegunungan Arbor. The party needs the **Peta Bahtera** — the Map of the Ark. **Rivan** has a copy in his basement.
- The Ark is sealed. The door requires the **Twin Shell Amulet** — found in Atlan's hidden base in Kota Juto. Mira has it. She has not told the party it is a key.

### Lei Alejandro
- **Lei Alejandro is alive.** He is hiding in the Bahtera Bawah Tanah.
- He has spent 500 years punishing himself for what he did to the Initial Six.
- He is guarding the Edisi Law. He is waiting for someone worthy.
- He is not the final antagonist. He is a broken man who wants to help.
- When the party meets him, he will not attack. He will ask them: *"Do you know what I did?"* If they say yes, he will nod. If they say no, he will tell them.
- He will offer them the Edisi Law — if they can prove they are worthy. The proof is not combat. The proof is **compassion**. If they can forgive him — or at least understand him — he will give them the Law.
- He will also offer them **Topaju** — the yellow Gemheart. He has held it for centuries. He does not want it anymore.
- If the party accepts him, he will join them as an **NPC ally**. He is old, broken, and tired — but he knows more about the Valla than anyone alive.
