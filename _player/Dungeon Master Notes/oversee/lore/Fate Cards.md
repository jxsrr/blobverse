> [!info] What Are Fate Cards?
> Each PC received a **Fate Card** during the dream on the Will of Grin ship (Session 2). Each card grants a blessing and a curse. They are permanent.

### Zure Ama — The Hanged Man & The Devil
- **Curse (Hanged Man):** Permanent frozen smile. Disadvantage on Persuasion and Insight. NPCs think he is mad or a cultist.
- **Blessing (Devil):** Bound Imp servant (Renal, formerly Qain). Can shift forms. Now fused into Ama's arm.

### Husgus — The Lovers & Judgement
- **Curse (Lovers):** If he is more than 30 ft from a randomly chosen party member, he is Frightened.
- **Blessing (Judgement):** His legend spreads magically. Everyone knows his name and treats him as a hero.

### Vya Arleschia — Wheel of Fortune & Death
- **Curse (Wheel):** Every even d20 result is reduced by 2. Small accidents happen constantly.
- **Blessing (Death):** 5 temporary HP each morning. Cannot be Frightened. Death saves reduced to 1.

### Syl Frost — The Magician & Temperance
- **Curse (Magician):** 50% chance any magic item or healing spell fails and deals 1d6 Force damage to her.
- **Blessing (Temperance):** Immune to poison and disease. Her blood is a universal cure.

### Shin Van Hel — The Tower & The Reversed Fool
- **Curse (Tower):** Wields **Swaith**, a soul-thirsting blade. The blade whispers. It binds him.
- **Blessing (Fool Reversed):** Destiny Token. If he dies, he returns once with full HP.

## The First Card — The Fool & The World
- **The Fool — "The Initial Lie":** Cannot benefit from Long Rest in cities over 100 people. Must sleep in wilderness.
- **The World — "The Keeper's Key":** Can ask one meta-game question to the DM. DM must answer truthfully.
- **Who has this card?** Unknown.
