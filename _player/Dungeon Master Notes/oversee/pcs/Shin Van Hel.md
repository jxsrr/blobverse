> [!info] The Party
> **Class:** Warlock (curse-bound, not pact) / Paladin (multiclass)
> **Role:** Melee striker, soul-blade wielder
> **Known as:** HVS

## Appearance
Half-Elf, chronologically over 700 years old. Looks young. Fedora or Bloodborne-style hat, Sakamoto-style glasses, gloves always covering his hands. Modern, sharp dresser. The eternal smile of a Half-Elf — not a curse, just a trait. He cannot stop smiling. He has learned to live with it.

## Known Backstory
- Found as a child on the shore of Elis. No memory. No name. Only an eternal smile.
- Raised by **Helius**, an old Valla-worshipper. Helius taught him to read, to listen, to sit in silence. Helius never asked him to remove his gloves. Helius never asked him to stop smiling.
- Joined **The Grace**, a demon-hunter organization. Made friends: **Edra** and **Crest**. Edra was warm. Crest was disciplined. They did not care about his smile. They did not care about his gloves. They were his family.
- **690 AV — The Fall of The Grace.** A massive Gap tear opened in Elis. Demons poured through. The Grace fought. They held the line. They died.
- Shin was possessed by **Zargoyle**. He destroyed the order. He destroyed Elis. He woke up **690 years later** — in the present era — with no memory of what happened.
- The Curse Mark on his body glows and causes pain when similar entities are near. It is not a curse. It is a **seal** — a fragment of **Zargoth** trapped inside him.
- His eternal smile is not a curse — it is a Half-Elf trait. He has always smiled. He will always smile.

## Personality
- Trusts only himself. Skeptical of gods. He has seen too much to believe in anything he cannot touch.
- Secretly a bit clumsy and oblivious. His serious image breaks at unexpected moments.
- Recently began worshipping Valla and multiclassed into Paladin after being saved repeatedly. He does not know why he was saved. He does not know by whom. He is grateful anyway.

## Fate Card
**The Tower & The Reversed Fool** — wields **Swaith**, a soul-thirsting blade. The blade whispers. It binds him. Has a "Destiny Token": if he dies, he returns once with full HP.
