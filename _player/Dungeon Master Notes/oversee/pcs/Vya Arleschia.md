> [!info] The Party
> **Class:** Ranger
> **Role:** Ranged damage, fire awakening
> **Known as:** The Fire Heir

## Appearance
Elf from Elis. Slender, tall. Pink hair adorned with white flowers. Shell-like armor that shines. Strikingly beautiful — people remember her.

## Known Backstory
- From **Chenalia Village** (Elis), a harmonious settlement of music, dance, and nature's blessing.
- Village was burned by **Will of Grin**. Separated from parents **Vreina and Klaus**.
- Raised for 10 years by the old man **Kopling** (a dragonborn-blooded wanderer).
- Kopling died. Vya joined the adventurer's guild seeking purpose and answers.
- In Session 5, learned her father **[[oversee/npcs/Antrem Klaus|Antrem]]** is alive and is the twin brother of **[[oversee/npcs/Accarus Grin|Accarus]]** (Will of Grin leader).
- Her mother Vreina's fate is still unknown.

## Recent Developments
- Learned that Klan Lei has a dark tradition: when twins are born, one is killed to preserve the other's power.
- Showed signs of being the **True Heir of Lei Firage** (fire element).
- After Jarko's death, something ignited in her.

## Fate Card
**Wheel of Fortune & Death** — every even d20 result is reduced by 2. But she has found true peace: 5 temporary HP each morning, cannot be Frightened, death saves reduced to 1.
