> [!info] The Party
> **Class:** Ranger
> **Role:** Scout, tracker, cook
> **Known as:** The Golden-Cracked

## Appearance
Halfling. Small frame, deceptively strong. Faint golden cracks appear on his skin when stressed — no one knows why. They glow faintly in the dark. They do not hurt. They do not heal. They are simply there.

## Known Backstory
- Found as a child in **Domido Forest** by the old dwarf **Gordon "The Noisy Rhino."**
- Gordon raised and trained him as a ranger. Gordon taught him to hunt, to track, to survive. Gordon taught him that the true guardian is not the one who seeks fame, but the one who protects the community.
- Gordon died in 695 AV, killed by **Malakor**, a Commonwealth agent. Husgus watched him die. He could not save him.
- Husgus killed Malakor with Gordon's bow. He burned Malakor's tower. He buried Gordon beneath the old tree in Domido Forest.
- When Malakor's spell hit him, Husgus's body glowed gold and he **transformed into a halfling** — his true form. He did not know what it meant. He does not know what it means now.
- Carries **Gustaero**, the legendary bow — the neck of the swan, one of the three ancient relics. It was given to him by **Accarus Grin** after the Frosthaven mission. Accarus carried it before him. Before Accarus, it was carried by **Olaf Guirela**, then **Gordon**, then **Gunnar Guirela**, who found it in his fishing net in Wind's Rest.

## Quirks
- **Atheist.** Believes in actions, hard work, and community. He does not trust gods. He trusts people.
- **Allergic to tomato juice.** Even a sip causes stomach cramps. He does not know why. He does not care.
- Cooks everything into **fried rice**. It is delicious. It is always fried rice. He will find a way to make fried rice out of any ingredient. This is both a gift and a curse.
- Believes he is cursed to never be parted from his companions (Fate Card).

## Fate Card
**The Lovers & Judgement** — if he is more than 30 ft from a randomly chosen party member, he is Frightened. His legend spreads magically wherever he goes. People know his name before he tells them.

### Who Husgus Is
- Husgus is **HVS-01** — a **Human Vessel Shell** designed to hold **the Architect**.
- He was created by the Cycle Clan — under Guanik Wiseman's guidance — hundreds of years ago. He was grown in a giant glass tube, submerged in clear liquid rich in Ambitio-Ether. He had no consciousness. He had no soul. He was an empty shell waiting to be filled.
- The Architect was never placed inside him. The transfer ritual was never performed. The shell was broken when Lei Firsa seized the Cycle Clan. It was swept into the darkness, lost.
- **But it did not stay empty.**
- In the emptiness of the shell, a new soul slowly grew. A soul born from nothing. A soul that became **Husgus**.
- The golden cracks are not a curse. They are his true structure showing through. The foundation of Inbu biology, visible beneath the surface of a mortal life. The shell was never meant to hold a soul. It was meant to hold a god. Now it holds a halfling — and the halfling is more than the shell ever was.

### The Transformation — What Really Happened
- When Malakor's spell hit Husgus, it triggered the shell's **emergency protocol**: *"If the shell is ever damaged and the vessel is still empty, collapse to the smallest possible form and seal the golden cracks."*
- Husgus became a halfling because the shell needed a smaller body to contain the damage. The golden cracks sealed. The shell stabilized. The soul — Husgus's soul — took full control.
- He does not know this. He thinks his transformation was a curse — some random magical backlash from Malakor's spell. He does not know he is a vessel. He does not know his body was designed to hold a god.
- **He is not empty anymore.** He is not the shell. He is the soul that grew inside it. He is Husgus. He is a halfling. He is a ranger. He is a person.

### The Golden Cracks
- The golden cracks appear when Husgus is stressed — in combat, in danger, in moments of intense emotion. They glow faintly. They do not hurt. They do not heal. They are simply there.
- They are the **shell's original structure** — the Inbu biology that Wiseman built into the vessel. They are visible because the shell was never fully filled. The soul that grew inside it left gaps — spaces where the shell is still empty.
- **They are not a curse.** They are not a disease. They are not a mark of the Overseer. They are a **sign** — a sign that Husgus is not what he seems. A sign that he is more than a halfling. A sign that he is a miracle.
- If the party ever meets **Guanik Wiseman**, he will recognize the cracks. He will weep. He will say: *"You are one of mine. You are the first one. You are the one who lived."*
- If the party ever meets **Echo Gzirmo**, he will recognize the cracks too. He will say: *"I drew the schematics for that shell. I never thought it would have a soul."*

### The Gustaero Bow
- Gustaero is the **neck of the swan** — one of the three ancient relics. It is not one of the six blades. It is not related to the Architect. It is older, separate, unknown.
- The bow slows the aging of its wielder. Gordon lived for over 500 years because he carried it. Husgus has not noticed this yet. He will.
- The bow chose Husgus. It hums in his hands. It fires true. It reaches farther than any mortal bow should. It is waiting for the moment when it will be needed — the moment when the swan must fly.
- **Gustaero is sentient.** It remembers Gunnar, Olaf, Gordon, and now Husgus. It does not want its wielder to die. It holds on. It protects.
- If Husgus ever meets **Echo Olaf**, the bow will recognize him. It will hum. Olaf will see his old bow in the hands of Gordon's boy — and he will smile.

### Gordon's Death
- Gordon was killed by **Malakor** — a wizard, a Commonwealth agent, a fanatic who believed fried noodles were sacred. He was placed in Gudameng to keep the Gruffgud War going, to hide the excavation at Gunung Rayu, to silence anyone who tried to reveal the truth.
- Gordon knew the truth. He had been trying to expose the Commonwealth's secret project for centuries. He was called "Badak Berisik" — the Noisy Rhino — because he never stopped shouting.
- Malakor killed him. Husgus watched. He could not save him.
- Husgus killed Malakor with Gordon's bow. He burned Malakor's tower. He buried Gordon beneath the old tree in Domido Forest.
- **He has never forgiven himself.** He thinks he should have been faster. He thinks he should have been stronger. He thinks he should have saved the only person who ever loved him.
- He does not know Gordon was over 500 years old. He does not know Gordon was kept alive by the Gustaero bow. He only knows Gordon was his father — in every way that matters — and he is gone.

### Husgus's Atheism
- Husgus does not believe in gods. He believes in actions, hard work, and community. He believes in the people beside him. He believes in the bow in his hands and the food in his pack and the friends at his back.
- He learned this from Gordon. Gordon was an atheist too. Gordon taught him that gods, if they existed, need not be worshipped — they need only be understood as part of the universe.
- **Husgus has been touched by the divine.** He does not know it. The shell inside him is a fragment of the Architect. The golden cracks are a sign of something cosmic. He carries the bow of the swan. He is part of a story older than the gods he does not believe in.
- **He will learn the truth.** He will learn he is HVS-01. He will learn he was meant to hold a god. He will learn he grew a soul instead. He will learn he is more than the shell ever was.
- When he learns this, he will not stop being an atheist. He will simply understand that the divine is not something to be worshipped — it is something to be survived. He has been surviving it his whole life. He will continue.

### Husgus and the Party
- Husgus is **loyal** to the party. He is the scout, the tracker, the cook, the one who keeps them fed and safe. He is the heart of the group — quiet, steady, reliable.
- He is **Frightened** when he is more than 30 ft from a randomly chosen party member (Fate Card). He does not know why. He does not question it. He simply stays close.
- His **legend spreads magically**. People know his name before he tells them. He does not know why. He does not question it. He simply accepts it.
- He is **the kindest person in the party** — the one who will share his food, who will carry the wounded, who will stand between his friends and danger without hesitation. He is Gordon's boy. He is the Noisy Rhino's legacy. He is a good person.
