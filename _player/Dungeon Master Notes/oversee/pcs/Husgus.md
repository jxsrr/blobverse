> [!info] The Party
> **Class:** Ranger
> **Role:** Scout, tracker, cook
> **Known as:** The Golden-Cracked

## Appearance
Halfling. Small frame, deceptively strong. Faint golden cracks appear on his skin when stressed — no one knows why. They glow faintly in the dark. They do not hurt. They do not heal. They are simply there.

## Known Backstory
- Found as a child in **Domido Forest** by the old dwarf **Gordon "The Noisy Rhino."**
- Gordon raised and trained him as a ranger. Gordon taught him to hunt, to track, to survive. Gordon taught him that the true guardian is not the one who seeks fame, but the one who protects the community.
- Gordon died in 695 AV, killed by **Malakor**, a Commonwealth agent. Husgus watched him die. He could not save him.
- Husgus killed Malakor with Gordon's bow. He burned Malakor's tower. He buried Gordon beneath the old tree in Domido Forest.
- When Malakor's spell hit him, Husgus's body glowed gold and he **transformed into a halfling** — his true form. He did not know what it meant. He does not know what it means now.
- Carries **Gustaero**, the legendary bow — the neck of the swan, one of the three ancient relics. It was given to him by **Accarus Grin** after the Frosthaven mission. Accarus carried it before him. Before Accarus, it was carried by **Olaf Guirela**, then **Gordon**, then **Gunnar Guirela**, who found it in his fishing net in Wind's Rest.

## Quirks
- **Atheist.** Believes in actions, hard work, and community. He does not trust gods. He trusts people.
- **Allergic to tomato juice.** Even a sip causes stomach cramps. He does not know why. He does not care.
- Cooks everything into **fried rice**. It is delicious. It is always fried rice. He will find a way to make fried rice out of any ingredient. This is both a gift and a curse.
- Believes he is cursed to never be parted from his companions (Fate Card).

## Fate Card
**The Lovers & Judgement** — if he is more than 30 ft from a randomly chosen party member, he is Frightened. His legend spreads magically wherever he goes. People know his name before he tells them.
