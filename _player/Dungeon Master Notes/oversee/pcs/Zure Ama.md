## Appearance
Man from another world (Aede). White hair, sharp blue eyes. White-and-blue robes with sun-motif arm guards. People mistake him for a destined hero — a title he never asked for. His left arm is not his own — it is a prosthetic fused with an Imp, still soft and jelly-like, still waking up. He keeps it covered when he can. He does not like people to see it.

## Known Backstory
- Came from **Aede**, another world, over 40 years ago.
- Lost his best friend **Reins** to the **Destroyer God**. Reins was empathetic, calm, wise — the one who understood people's emotions best. She sacrificed herself. Ama could not save her.
- Stranded in **Nesia Village** (Harka). Adopted by farmers **Joni and Siti**. Learned farming. Found peace.
- Left to adventure because the world still needs saving, and Reins is waiting — somewhere, somehow.
- **Atheist.** After seeing Reins's human sacrifice, he trusts only his own actions.
- Obsessed with saving everyone — it is penance for failing Reins.
- Shares snacks to bring small smiles. It is the only thing he knows how to do when words fail.

## Recent Developments
- Left arm was amputated by **<a href="/Livia%20Vane">Livia</a>**'s wind attack in Chenalia (Session 06).
- Underwent cybernetic surgery in **Arujaya** — his Imp **Renal** was fused into the new arm (Session 06).
- The arm is still soft/jelly-like and not yet fully "awake." It is slowly solidifying.
- Gained "limitless" strength by consuming residual Ambitio-Ether — at a cost to his HP and stamina (Session 07).
- **Joni and Siti betrayed him** at The Golden Sheaf (Session 06). They called the Commonwealth. They believed he was a terrorist. He has not recovered.

## Fate Card
**The Hanged Man & The Devil** — eternal frozen smile (disadvantage on Persuasion/Insight; NPCs think he is mad). Bound to the Imp Renal (now fused into his arm).
