> [!info] At a Glance

> **Attitude Toward Us:** Friendly
> **Base:** Observatorium, Kota Juto

## What We Know
- Secret organization dedicated to finding the truth about the Initial Six.
- Founded by **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Mira Nouveau|Mira]]** — daughter of **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Atlan Nouveau|Atlan Nouveau]]** and **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Reins|Reins]]**.
- Operates from the Observatorium in Kota Juto. Has cells in most major cities.
- Wants to expose **Lei Alejandro's** betrayal.
- Knows about the Gemhearts. Knows about the Echoes.
- They are hunted by the Commonwealth.

## What They Want From Us
Find Gustaero. Collect the Gemhearts. Expose the truth. Protect the world from the Commonwealth.
