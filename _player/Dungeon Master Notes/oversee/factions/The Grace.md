## What We Know
- A demon-hunter organization. Shin's old order.
- Trained hunters to fight demons, spirits, and Gap-touched creatures.
- Shin joined as a young man. He made friends: **Edra** and **Crest**.
- The order was **destroyed in 690 AV** — when a massive Gap tear opened in Elis and demons poured through.
- Edra and Crest died. Shin survived — but he was possessed by something. He destroyed everything. He woke up 690 years later.
- The symbol of The Grace is a **blue-silver seal** — a stylized wing.

## What Shin Knows
- The Grace had a base in Elis.
- They trained in demon-hunting, tracking, and combat.
- Edra was warm and welcoming. Crest was disciplined and observant.
- Crest suspected Shin was different. He documented anomalies.
- Edra never suspected anything. She trusted Shin completely.
- He does not know if they blamed him. He does not know if they died hating him.
