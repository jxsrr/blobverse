## What We Know
- The governing body of the Commonwealth.
- Three leaders, each representing a pillar of civilization.
- They balance each other. They check each other. They rule together.
- The current Triase is **Valerius Thorne** (Chancellor), **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Livia%20Vane">Livia</a>** (Justice), and **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Iron%20Gort">Gort</a>** (Defense).
- They control most of Harka, Pirizia, and Rioquan.
- They are hunting us.

## The Three Pillars
### Kanselir Agung — The Chancellor
- **Current:** <a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Valerius%20Thorne">Valerius</a>
- **Represents:** Wisdom, long-term vision, unity
- **Role:** Final decision-maker. Mediator. Symbol of stability.
- **Previous (at the time of the Initial Six, 200 AV):** <a href="/oversee/npcs/Geldfre">Geldfre</a>

### Wakil Keadilan — The Justice
- **Current:** <a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Livia%20Vane">Livia</a>
- **Represents:** Law, courts, internal affairs
- **Leads:** Garda Mawar (Rose Guard)
- **Role:** Oversees courts, contracts, disputes, doctrine.
- **Previous:** <a href="/oversee/npcs/Erard%20Gelnne">Erard Gelnne</a>

### Wakil Pertahanan — The Defense
- **Current:** <a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Iron%20Gort">Gort</a>
- **Represents:** Military, defense, intelligence
- **Leads:** Garda Besi (Iron Guard)
- **Role:** Commands the army. Handles external threats. Security.
- **Previous (at the time of the Initial Six, 200 AV):** <a href="/oversee/npcs/Hildag%20Grin">Hildag Grin</a>

## What We Know
- The Triase is not a democracy. It is an **aristocracy**.
- Most high positions are held by **Klan Lei** descendants.
- Valerius is the true power. Livia and Gort are tools.
- The Triase controls the narrative. It rewrites history.
