## What We Know
- Ruling government of Oversee. Based in Padre City.
- Led by the **Triase**: Chancellor, Justice, Defense.
- **Garda Mawar** (Rose Guard) — Justice. Led by [[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Lei Livia Vane|Livia]].
- **Garda Besi** (Iron Guard) — Defense. Led by [[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Lei Iron Gort|Gort]].
- Controls most of Harka, Pirizia, and Rioquan.
- Wants to control all magic. Suppresses technology.
- We are **wanted criminals**.

## What They Want From Us
Capture. Execute. Erase.
