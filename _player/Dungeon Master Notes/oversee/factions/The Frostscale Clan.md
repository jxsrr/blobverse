## What We Know
- Dragonborn clan of ice. Live at the top of Frosthaven Peak.
- Worship **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a>**, the silver dragon. Their "Breathstone" was actually Syl's egg.
- All members have **Ice Breath** — a sacred inheritance from Vythrax.
- Magic is taboo. Only ice breath is allowed. Self-taught magic is punishable by exile.
- They are **strict, disciplined, and honor-bound**.
- **Syl Frost is the son of Vythrax** and the heir to the clan. His name is carved on the family tree.

## The Rules
1. **Ice Breath** may only be used to freeze enemies and protect the clan.
2. **Absolute obedience** to the elders. Exile for refusal.
3. **Do not touch the Breathstone with magic.**
4. Exiles must bring back an enemy's head or a dragon relic to be allowed back.
5. **The Frostfang** may only be touched by elders. Violators must fight to the death or freeze.

## The Truth (as the Clan Now Knows It)
- Syl is the son of **Vythrax**.
- The "Breathstone" was Syl's egg.
- The clan lied for centuries to hide the truth.
- **Syl has been accepted back.** His name is carved on the family tree.
- Elder Jarl has named Syl as his successor.

## What They Want From Us
- Nothing directly. They are loyal to Syl.
- They will help the party if Syl asks.
- They can provide shelter, healing, and ice magic.
