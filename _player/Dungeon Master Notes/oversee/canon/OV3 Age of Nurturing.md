> [!info] The Age of the Guardian
> When the Cycle Clan found its greatest champion — and lost him to a woman with a plan.

## The Fragmentary Record

What follows is assembled from the **Berkas Penelitian Cycle Clan**, from oral histories preserved by the **<a href="/oversee/factions/Keeper%20of%20Secret">Keeper of Secret</a>**, and from fragments of the **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Edisi%20Law">Edisi Law</a>** that the party has recovered. It is incomplete. It is sanitized. The Cycle Clan's official record was written by the victors — and the victors did not want the truth remembered.

This is the story as the Cycle Clan wants it told.

The story of **Lei the Great** — the first Guardian of the world, the wizard who gave his life to become the Valla.

It is not the full story.

## Lei the Great

According to the Cycle Clan's record, **Lei the Great** was born to a merchant family in **Harka** — though the record does not name the family, and the record does not mention the mother. The record says only that Lei was a **gifted ranger**, skilled with bow and blade, and that he traveled widely before finding his destiny.

The record says Lei was **selfless**. That he wanted only to protect the world.

The record says Lei became the **Valla** through a ritual of voluntary sacrifice, and that he has held the seal ever since — the first Guardian of Oversee, the eternal protector of the world.

Every city in Oversee has a statue of him. Every temple has a hymn. Every child learns his name before they learn to read.



He was born in **Greenvale**, a small farming village in Harka, in **~60 BV**. He was three feet tall, with curly brown hair and a round belly from eating too many of his mother's pastries. He had no magic. He had no ambition. He had only one unusual quality: **he could not say no to anyone who needed help.**


But he also carried something else — not a blade, but a **relic**. The **<a href="/oversee/items/Gustaero%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Swan%20Bow">Gustaero</a>** bow, given to him by the old woman **Edna Gustaero** in **Wind's Rest**. Gustaero was not one of the six blades. It was one of the three ancient relics — the neck of the swan. But the Cycle Clan's records do not distinguish between them. They list all seven as "legendary weapons" — a simplification that has confused scholars for centuries.

Six blades. Six dying hands. Six last requests: *"Do not use them to conquer. Use them to protect."*

Simon kept them all. He never used them for himself. He carried them, and he protected them, and he never asked for anything in return.

He was, according to every surviving account, **the kindest person who ever lived.**

## The Two Companions

Simon did not travel alone.




They walked together for twenty years.


**~870 BV**

Deep beneath the **<a href="/oversee/locations/Observatorium%20Danau%20Kuno">Observatorium Ancient Lake</a>**, in a chamber that had never seen sunlight, **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Guanik%20Wiseman">Guanik Wiseman</a>** sat alone.


Wiseman had spent his life serving the Cycle Clan. He had led the excavation of the **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Gemhearts">Gemhearts</a>**. He had performed the **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Cycle%20Ritual">maintenance rituals</a>** dozens of times. But he was never at peace.


The voice answered — not in words, but in direct understanding. There was no other container strong enough. A mortal body would shatter. Only a soul could hold the seal.

Wiseman asked: *"Then why not create a body that can hold it? A vessel, built from the beginning to bear the weight?"*

There was a long silence. And then — for the first time in his life — Wiseman sensed something like **surprise** from the entity on the other side.


Wiseman spent decades studying the ancient records of the Inbu. He learned how their cells stored energy, how their tissue channeled **<a href="/oversee/lore/Ambitio-Ether">Ambitio-Ether</a>**, how their organs functioned for thousands of years without decay. He wrote it all down — a series of manuscripts that became known as the **Berkas Penelitian Cycle Clan**: the Cycle Clan Research File.

And then he built the **shells**.

## The Power Grab

**~01 BV**


She had spent that year learning everything the Cycle Clan knew. She had learned the location of the **Gemhearts**. She had learned the mechanisms of the ritual. And she had learned that Wiseman was old, and tired, and could no longer stop her.

She seized control of the Cycle Clan. The old members who opposed her were removed — quietly, without violence, but removed. The younger members who saw her determination and her intelligence chose to follow her. **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Guanik%20Wiseman">Guanik Wiseman</a>**, now ancient and fragile, could only watch as everything he had built was taken.

He warned Simon, once: *"Your wife is not who she says she is. She will use you. She will destroy what I have protected for thousands of years."*

Simon did not believe him. Simon believed in his wife.

He was wrong.

## The Ritual

The night of the ritual was clear, cold, and silent.




She did not resist.

She walked to the altar on her own feet, her head high, her eternal smile unchanged. She had known this was coming. She had known for months. And she had chosen not to run.

Simon realized what was happening only when he saw her. He screamed. He tried to run to her. But the **Stasis Field** — created by the Gemhearts — held him in place. He could not move. He could not speak. He could only watch.


She said, quietly, that this was how it had to be. That Simon was too good for the world. That as the Valla, he would protect everyone — including her. She asked him to be strong. She asked him to remember her.

And then **<a href="/oversee/items/Swaith%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Soul-Thirsting%20Blade">Swaith</a>** — the dagger Simon had given her, the dagger she had carried for twenty years — was drawn from her belt and driven into her heart.


Simon felt his body **stretch**. His soul was pulled — outward, upward — toward the void. Toward the Overseer. Toward the endless hunger.


> *"You were the best friend I ever had. The only one who saw me — not as a curse."*

Then she was gone. Her body crumbled to dust. **Swaith** fell with her, still warm.

## The Valla

When Simon woke, he was not in his body.

He was **everywhere**. His consciousness stretched across the world — the sky, the sea, the earth. He could feel every mortal life. He could feel the hunger above him, and the seal holding it back, and the terrible cost of every breath he took.

He was the **Valla**.



She was satisfied.

## The Legend

The world was told a different story.

The world was told that **Lei the Great** — a male wizard, a great hero, a man of wisdom and sacrifice — had become the Valla to protect the world from cosmic hunger. The story was written into every history book. The statues were raised in every city.




Only the **Keeper of Secret**, founded by **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Mira%20Nouveau">Mira Nouveau</a>** centuries later, preserved the truth.

Only they remembered that the greatest hero in the world was a Halfling who could not say no.

Only they remembered that **Lei the Great** was a lie.

## HVS-01 — The Golden-Cracked

**Hundreds of years later — Domido Forest**

A small child was found in the forest by an old dwarf named **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Gordon">Gordon</a>**. The child had no clothes, no memory, no name. He remembered only darkness, fire, and someone screaming.

Gordon named him **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Husgus">Husgus</a>**.

Husgus grew up. He learned to hunt. He learned to cook. He learned to laugh. He learned to love.

When the wizard **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Malakor">Malakor</a>** struck him with a spell, Husgus's body **shone gold**. And when the light faded, he was not a human boy anymore. He was a **Halfling**.


The golden cracks in his skin, which appeared only in times of stress, were his true structure showing through. The foundation of Inbu biology, visible beneath the surface of a mortal life.

He had no idea what he was.

## HVS-02 — The Shell

**Hundreds of years later — Elis**

The second shell was thrown further — into a remote valley in **Elis** that no map recorded. It was buried for centuries, asleep in the dark, waiting.

But inside the shell, something had happened.


The fragment slept. It dreamed. It waited.

Centuries later, it woke.

It did not remember who it was. It remembered only darkness, fire, and someone screaming. Just like Husgus.

It took the name **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Shin%20Van%20Hel">Shin Van Hel</a>**.

And it began to search for who it really was.
