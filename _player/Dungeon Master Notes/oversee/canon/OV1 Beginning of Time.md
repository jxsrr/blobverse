> [!info] The Making of the World

## The Making of the World


It was alone.

In the endless dark of the void, it was the only thing that existed — a spark of preservation in an ocean of hunger. And it was **lonely**.

So it made a decision. It would **sacrifice part of itself** to create a physical dimension — a place where life could exist, where something new could grow, where it would no longer be alone.




It is still there.

## The Crystallization of the Gemhearts


Its primordial body — the only body that had ever existed in the void — divided into two parts. One part became the physical dimension of **Oversee**. The other part — the most vital part, the part that still pulsed with life — **crystallized** in scattered fragments across the new world.

Those fragments became known as the **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Gemhearts">Gemhearts</a>**.


The four Gemhearts are:
- **<a href="/oversee/items/Emelanica">Emelanica</a>** — the green stone, the organ of time
- **<a href="/oversee/items/Safayer">Safayer</a>** — the blue stone, the organ of purification
- **<a href="/oversee/items/Rubion">Rubion</a>** — the red stone, the organ of annihilation

Each one has a purpose. Each one is essential. Each one is scattered across the world, waiting to be found.

## Emelanica — The Green Stone



Emelanica is the heartbeat of time itself — the pulse that keeps the world alive.

## Safayer — The Blue Stone



Safayer is the heart that purifies — the organ that filters the poison of consciousness into safe food for the cosmic hunger.

## Rubion — The Red Stone


In the primordial body, Rubion was the last line of defense — the ability to cut off a threatened part so the whole could survive. As a Gemheart, Rubion became an **instant antimatter weapon**. Its primary function is to sever the astral bond between soul and body, permanently. In nanoseconds, Rubion turns biological flesh to ash — preventing anomalous regeneration at the moment the soul is drawn into the cosmic seal.







## The First Life


The earliest ecosystem was dominated by **primordial flora and fauna** — creatures and plants bound to raw **<a href="/oversee/lore/Ambitio-Ether">Ambitio-Ether</a>**, the energy of creation that was not yet fully stable.

The primordial flora had a unique ability: they functioned as **filters** for Ambitio-Ether. Giant trees and **luminescent forests** absorbed wild mana from the air and stored it as crystalline sap. Creatures that consumed this flora received a concentrated supply of raw Ambitio-Ether — enough to grow **massive**. These forests were not just collections of trees. They were **organisms** — the lungs of the world in the most literal sense.

The primordial fauna were defined by their **colossal size** — a direct manifestation of the high concentration of mana in the environment.

- **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Three%20Surviving%20Dragons">Elder Dragons</a>** — though rare in the modern era — served as **ecological stabilizers** in the primordial age. They consumed Ambitio-Ether directly from the atmosphere and **exhaled it back as weather** — rain, storms, and winds that carried life across the world.
- **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Behemoths">Behemoths</a>** — slow-moving giant mammals — lived by consuming Ambitio-Ether-rich forests. Their presence, though seemingly destructive, **stabilized the continents** by preventing soil erosion caused by uncontrolled Ambitio-Ether release.


They were part of the solution.

## The Birth of the Inbu

The transition to consciousness began when these ancient creatures developed more complex nervous systems. More importantly, **self-awareness** sparked among some of them. This did not happen evenly across the world. It happened in specific places — where conditions allowed — creatures began to **know** they existed.

That awareness triggered the production of **<a href="/oversee/lore/Ambitio-Ether">Ambitio-Ether</a>** on a very small scale.

A new era began.

In the vast **Central Plains** and the **Southwest Coast** — a region rich in resources but never free of natural disaster — the first truly conscious life was born.

From the fruit of a **primordial tree** — one of the ancient flora that filtered Ambitio-Ether — came the **<a href="/The%20Inbu">Inbu</a>**.

Born directly from the essence of Ambitio-Ether that had been filtered and purified by the primordial tree, the Inbu were the first creatures with **extraordinary magical power**. They were the **primordial ancestors** — the mother of all humanoid races in Oversee.

## The Differentiation of the Races

Over thousands of years, the Inbu differentiated. Evolution was driven by three forces: **breeding**, **adaptation**, and **extreme environmental pressure**. From one root, many races grew — and filled Oversee with diversity.

- **Humans** — the Inbu who chose comfort and safety on fertile plains. They lost the specialized senses of their ancestors but gained **adaptability, ambition, and diversity**. They could thrive almost anywhere.
- **Elves** — the Inbu who chose the deep forests, living alongside Behemoths and Elder Dragons. To survive, they **sharpened their senses** to extraordinary levels, became creatures of the night, and developed a deep connection to the wild Ambitio-Ether around them.
- **Dwarves** — the Inbu who chose the underground, hiding from surface predators. Thousands of years in caves and mountains shaped them into **sturdy, resilient craftsmen and unparalleled miners**.
- **Halflings** — a distant branch of Humans who chose peace over ambition. They adapted to quiet rural life, **shrinking in stature** while inheriting the cheerfulness and prosperity of their Human kin. They loved peace, food, and the comfort of home.
- **Gnomes** — a mystery. Perhaps a lighter branch of Dwarves, perhaps an early cross between Elves and fey. Small, curious, and naturally drawn to illusion and the stranger side of nature's magic. They became **inventors, gem-cutters, and endless storytellers**.
- **<a href="/oversee/factions/The%20Frostscale%20Clan">Dragonborn</a>** — a different origin entirely. They were the Inbu who **migrated to the highest mountains**, where the Elder Dragons rested. Generation after generation, they were exposed to the magical weather cycles the dragons exhaled. Their bodies slowly changed. **Scales grew. Their breath carried elemental force. Their lifespans lengthened.** They inherited the pride, honor, and elemental power of the sky-guardians.
- **Half-Elves** — the union of Human and Elf. They inherited Human curiosity and ambition, and Elven senses and charm. They became explorers, diplomats, and bridges between two worlds — though often feeling at home in neither.
- **Half-Orcs** — the hard union of Human and Orc — a race believed to have evolved from Inbu exposed to harsh, destructive energy in remote regions. They inherited immense physical strength and ferocity — but also Human intelligence, willpower, and the ability to control their rage and prove themselves beyond the stigma they carry.

The nine races of Oversee inherited **uneven magic**. Some individuals have immense power. Some have none. Some Elven clans are entirely non-magical. Others carry deep traditions of magic. The same is true of Dragonborn, Humans, and all the rest.

Magic in Oversee is not evenly distributed. It is scattered in complex, unpredictable patterns.

That is the nature of the world.

## The Rapture

At the height of their glory, the civilizations of Oversee reached what became known as the **High Magic Society** — the highest level of magical advancement. Ambitio-Ether activity **soared** — a beacon burning bright in the cosmic dark.

The primordial flora were **cut down** to meet the demand for magical wood and settlement land. Dragons and Behemoths were **hunted** — both for the magical value of their organs and because they were seen as threats to expanding settlements. The natural filters and balancers of Ambitio-Ether **died out** or were pushed to remote corners, and the filtering system built since the beginning of creation **collapsed**.

The uncontrolled Ambitio-Ether surge created a ripple in frequency — a ripple that reached the cosmic depths, and **woke the hunger of the Overseer**.

From the darkness of space came a **comet** that did not emit light.

It **absorbed** light. It left a trail of emptiness in the night sky. It moved slowly — sometimes visible for weeks — as if **sniffing** for something. Searching for the source of the Ambitio-Ether that had leaked too brightly.

When the comet passed over a region, the people saw something strange and terrible: the stars behind it **disappeared** one by one, swallowed by moving darkness. On the ground, Ambitio-Ether began to rise in a thin glowing mist, visible only to the magically sensitive. The mist flowed like a river in the air, slowly but surely, toward the waiting comet.

The most terrible part of all: the process **did not hurt**.

The great wizards, the magical leaders, and all those rich in Ambitio-Ether felt only a slight dizziness — and then they began to **dissolve**. Starting from the fingertips, spreading to the arms, until the entire body dissolved into mist that joined the flow toward the comet.

There were no screams. No resistance. No panic. Only silence and the slow process of becoming **nothing**.

When dawn came and the comet drifted away, what remained were scattered clothes, useless magical tools, and empty buildings — still whole, but without inhabitants. The comet disappeared into the depths of space, carrying with it the entire magical essence of the region.

A civilization that had been thriving the night before was now only a memory and a silent ruin — a monument to its own folly.

Thus the **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Rapture">Rapture</a>** happened — repeatedly, across Oversee.


And every time, only the **humble** survived — those who had no excess Ambitio-Ether — to rebuild civilization from the ashes of their predecessors.

## The Devourer Comet

The scholars of the Cycle Clan later named the comet **the Devourer** — a shard of the Overseer's hunger, a piece of its vast attention, briefly focused on the world.

It is not a natural phenomenon. It is not a punishment. It is not even, strictly speaking, a **conscious** act. The Overseer does not decide to destroy. It simply **eats**.

And it will happen again.

It always does.
