> [!info] The Age of the First God
> When the calendar was rewritten. When the bloodline was founded. When the truth became a lie.

## The New Calendar

The year **1 AV** — *After Valla* — began in silence.




She had what she wanted. She had **immortality** through Simon. She had **power** through the **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Gemhearts">Gemhearts</a>**. She had **control** over the cycle that kept the world alive.

But there was one thing that still bothered her.

The **legendary weapons** — the blades and relics Simon had collected over his lifetime — were proof that Simon had existed. Each weapon had a story. Each story connected to a name — the name of a Halfling shepherd who had been too kind for the world.

**<a href="/oversee/items/Zenahhi%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Loyal%20Blade">Zenahhi</a>**. **<a href="/oversee/items/Rayio%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Wind%20Blade">Rayio</a>**. **<a href="/oversee/items/Kkana%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Stone%20Greatsword">Kkana</a>**. **<a href="/oversee/items/Frostfang%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Ice%20Fang">Frostfang</a>**. **<a href="/oversee/items/Terrde%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Patient%20Blade">Terrde</a>**. **<a href="/oversee/items/Swaith%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Soul-Thirsting%20Blade">Swaith</a>**. And **<a href="/oversee/items/Gustaero%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Swan%20Bow">Gustaero</a>** — the bow that was not a blade, but a relic.

She could not destroy them. They were ancient — older than the Klan, older than the Cycle Clan. Too strong to destroy. Too dangerous to leave in the wrong hands.

But she could **lose** them.

She could scatter them across the world — to places no one would look, in pieces no one could reassemble — and let the world forget.

She began that night.

## The Scattering of the Weapons

### Gustaero — The South Sea, Harka

The first weapon she took from the altar was **<a href="/oversee/items/Gustaero%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Swan%20Bow">Gustaero</a>**.

The longbow was pale, almost transparent — its invisible string hummed softly as she held it, as if in protest.

She remembered the story Simon had told her. He had received it from an old woman in **<a href="/oversee/locations/Wind%27s%20Rest">Wind's Rest</a>** — **Edna Gustaero** — who had trusted him because he was "a good person." Too many people knew about that meeting. Too many witnesses were still alive.

She traveled alone to the southern coast of **Harka** — to the highest cliff above Wind's Rest. She stood at the edge of the chasm, the sea wind whipping her face, the waves breaking on the rocks a hundred meters below.

> *"In the place where Simon received this trust,"* she said, *"the trust will end."*

She threw Gustaero into the sea.


> *"Let the sea forget it. As the world will forget Simon."*

### Rayio — The Garberro Forest, Pirizia


The trees here grew crooked. Their roots reached like wrinkled hands grasping at the soil. Thick fog covered every corner. Strange sounds echoed in the distance.


> *"Dance with the wind there,"* she said. *"Or be eaten by demons."*


### Kkana — The Misty Mountains, Elis



She planted Kkana in a crack between the stones and stepped back.

> *"You like stone? Then stay here."*


### Frostfang — Frosthaven Peak, Northern Pirizia


She had to move quickly. She carried Frostfang to northern **Pirizia**, to **<a href="/oversee/locations/Frosthaven%20Peak">Frosthaven Peak</a>** — the highest mountain on the continent, where eternal snow and ice storms never stopped. Here, the temperature was far below freezing, and the wind blew so hard it could tear flesh.


> *"You will be comfortable here,"* she said, her breath freezing in the air. *"Freeze forever."*

### Zenahhi — The Canyon of Lament, Eastern Rioquan


She carried Zenahhi to **Rioquan**, to the **<a href="/Ngarai%20Ratapan">Canyon of Lament</a>** — the deepest canyon on the continent, its bottom said to have never been touched by sunlight. Legend said that thousands of years ago, the gods wept here, and their tears carved the canyon. No one had ever descended to the bottom and returned alive.


> *"Your loyalty will die here,"* she whispered.


### Terrde — The Ruins of the Ancient City, Harka


She carried Terrde to **Harka**, to the ruins of an ancient city destroyed in **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Rapture">The Rapture</a>** centuries before. The city had once been the greatest center of magical civilization in its era, before the light-eating comet came and devoured all its wizards. Now only rubble remained, and bones, and a suffocating silence.


> *"You like waiting?"* she said. *"Then wait here forever."*

## The Rewritten History




She declared the year **1 After Valla**. The new calendar began. The old history was erased.


The weapons waited in their hiding places — in the sea, in the forbidden forest, on the mountain peak, at the bottom of the canyon, in the ruins, in the underground temple. They waited. They called. They would keep waiting until someone worthy found them.

## The Bloodline of the First God

**~01–20 AV**




She gave birth to **four children**. Each grew up in an environment filled with knowledge of the Gemhearts, of the ritual, and of their father who sat eternal on the altar. Each inherited something no one else in Oversee possessed: **pure elemental power**, flowing in their blood like a second pulse.

- **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Wendigo">Lei Wendigo</a>** — the firstborn. Wind. Received **<a href="/oversee/items/Emelanica">Emelanica</a>**.
- **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Firage">Lei Firage</a>** — the second. Fire. Received **<a href="/oversee/items/Rubion">Rubion</a>**.
- **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Watigi">Lei Watigi</a>** — the third. Water. Received **<a href="/oversee/items/Safayer">Safayer</a>**.

But no one knew the truth behind this miracle.


A father's helpless love — a father who wanted to protect his children from a distance — became the conduit connecting the altar to the wombs that bore Lei's descendants. Every time a child was born from his bloodline, Simon shed a drop of his essence, giving them a spark of pure elemental power that no one else could ever possess.

## The Rule of Succession


With the Gemhearts now in her possession — the legacy of the **Cycle Clan** she had fully controlled — she divided the four primordial stones among her four children.

Then she established a rule that could not be broken: **the four Gemheart fragments could only be passed down to the pure-blooded firstborn of each elemental line**. Only the firstborn of Lei Wendigo, Lei Firage, Lei Watigi, and Lei Roiga could inherit their ancestral Gemheart.

The reason was simple, and cruel: **the blood of the firstborn was closest to Simon's own blood** — Simon, who still sat eternal on the altar as the Valla. The purer Simon's blood flowed in a descendant, the stronger their bond with the Gemheart, and the greater the elemental power they could channel.

This rule became the foundation of Klan Lei's magical aristocracy — and the source of countless intrigues and conflicts that would color their history for generations.

## The Silent Guardians

Generations passed. **Klan Lei** grew into the most feared and respected magical aristocracy in all Oversee. They did not need to learn magic — they were magic itself. Grand palaces were built on the land that had once been the **Observatory of the Ancient Lake**. Nobles from other kingdoms came to bow, begging for protection or alliance. Klan Lei stood at the top of the world — rulers who did not need to raise a weapon to show their power.

But behind the splendor, Klan Lei held the darkest secret.

They knew that the source of their power — the pure elemental force flowing in every Lei descendant — was tied to **Simon's existence as the Valla**. As long as Simon sat on the altar, holding back the Overseer's hunger, his essence continued to flow to them. If Simon were ever replaced by a new Valla — if the maintenance ritual were performed and Simon's soul finally released — that source of power would vanish forever. Klan Lei would fall from their throne of magical aristocracy, becoming ordinary people no more special than a farmer in a field.

So Klan Lei guarded the cycle with extreme care. Every five hundred years, when the time of maintenance approached, they made sure no one disturbed Simon. No new volunteers were allowed near the altar. Loyal guards stood day and night, protecting the sacred chamber where Simon sat eternal. They did not perform the ritual of replacement — they only **waited**, hoping Simon could endure forever.

But nature has its own laws. A soul, even one stretched into a cosmic seal, eventually cracks. **Simon began to show signs of exhaustion.** His divided consciousness sometimes shifted too far to the cosmic side, leaving him unaware for months at a time. The Ambitio-Ether he channeled to his descendants began to fluctuate, unstable.

Klan Lei realized they had to do something to preserve their bloodline.

## The Darkest Secret

Here the darkest secret was revealed.

When a Lei descendant gave birth to **twins**, a strange phenomenon occurred: the destiny-soul that should have been whole for one generation was split in two. The pure elemental power that should have been inherited fully by one heir was divided, so each child received only half — or less — of the full potential. Some twin children were even born with no magic at all, like ordinary people.

For Klan Lei, this was a disaster. A magical aristocracy could not have weak heirs.

So a dark tradition emerged, kept tightly from the outside world. When a mother gave birth to twins, one baby would be taken immediately after birth. In the silence of the night, without a cry, without resistance, that baby would be killed. Its blood would be dripped onto a small altar dedicated to Simon — not as a vile offering, but as a way of returning the split soul to its twin.

The elders of Klan Lei believed that by killing one twin, the remaining soul of the dead baby would naturally return to the living one. This process, though horrifying, was believed to reunite the divided destiny, creating a single true heir with full elemental power. The surviving child grew with maximum potential, never knowing they stood on the grave of their own twin.

This tradition lasted for generations, kept in whispers in the corridors of Klan Lei palaces. Mothers wept in silence. Fathers turned away when the baby was taken. The elders performed their duty with expressionless faces.

They called it *"purification of the bloodline"* or *"unification of destiny."*

But no euphemism could cover the truth: **Klan Lei killed its own children to maintain power.**

## The Commonwealth of United Voices

The **<a href="/oversee/factions/One%20Voice%20Commonwealth">Commonwealth of United Voices</a>** was built on the ruins of chaos that followed decades after the disappearance of the greatest hero the world had ever known.

Simon's departure — or more precisely, his transformation into the Valla who sat eternal on the altar — created a power vacuum no one could fill. Adventurers, kingdoms, and small factions competed in uncertainty, while external threats began to resurface after being muted for so long by Simon's presence.



The Commonwealth was formed with a structure known as the **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Triase">Triase</a>** — a triumvirate in which each officeholder represented an essential pillar of civilization.

At the top stood the **Kanselir Agung** — the **Grand Chancellor** — the supreme leader who served as guardian of wisdom and long-term vision. The Chancellor acted as unifier, mediator, and final decision-maker after hearing the views of the other two, making the position a symbol of reason, stability, and political and spiritual trust.

Beside the Chancellor stood the **Wakil Keadilan** — the **Justice Deputy** — who led the **<a href="/oversee/factions/The%20Rose%20Guard">Garda Mawar</a>** (Rose Guard), the institution responsible for law, courts, and internal affairs. The Justice Deputy oversaw the court system, inter-regional contracts, dispute resolution, and maintenance of the Commonwealth's legal doctrine — requiring sharp analytical thinking, high integrity, and unusual diplomatic skill.

Meanwhile, the **Wakil Pertahanan** — the **Defense Deputy** — held control of the **<a href="/oversee/factions/The%20Iron%20Guard">Garda Besi</a>** (Iron Guard): the military, territorial defense, public security, and intelligence. The Defense Deputy was the face of the Commonwealth's power, ensuring every threat — from within or without — could be met with toughness and speed without waiting for bureaucratic decisions.


Those with Simon's blood flowing in their veins, who inherited the pure elemental power of their ancestors, were naturally placed in positions that allowed them to protect family interests while still running the government. A perfect vicious circle: magical power gave them legitimacy to rule, and political power gave them protection to preserve the purity of their bloodline and the secret behind the source of their power.

A clear example of this system could be seen in the Governor of West Harka — a leader named **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Hanzel%20Lei%20Krow">Hanzel Lei Krow</a>**, a direct descendant of the **Lei Wendigo** line, heir to the pure wind magic and guardian of **<a href="/oversee/items/Emelanica">Emelanica</a>**. His appointment as governor was not merely an ordinary political decision; it was a symbolic statement that the Commonwealth recognized Klan Lei's traditional authority while binding it within a modern government structure.

## The Doctrine of Simon's Ascension



## The Silent Legacy of the Wild Continent

Amid the bustle of the newly born Commonwealth — where the Doctrine of Simon's Ascension was being proclaimed and Klan Lei was consolidating its power — there was another world beating to a completely different rhythm.

Across the sea, in the wild and untouched continent of **<a href="/oversee/locations/Elis">Elis</a>**, time flowed as it had since the beginning of creation. There, among the misty peaks that pierced the sky and the valleys never trodden by mortal feet, the giant creatures from the age before consciousness still lived, hiding from a world that had forgotten them.

Of the thousands of **Elder Dragons** that had once ruled the skies in the era before consciousness, only **three** remained in all Oversee. They were the last survivors of the hunts of the **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Rapture">Rapture</a>** — creatures who had chosen to withdraw from the world rather than become victims of civilization's endless ambition. They hid in places impossible for ordinary mortals to reach, waiting in long silence.

- **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a>** — the Silver Dragon, called **"The Silver Wind"** by the **<a href="/oversee/factions/The%20Frostscale%20Clan">Klan Frostscale</a>**. She made her lair in the **<a href="/oversee/locations/The%20Spire%20of%20Whispering%20Ice">Spire of Whispering Ice</a>**, a peak so high the air was too thin to breathe. From that height, she watched over her domain — including **<a href="/oversee/locations/Frosthaven%20Peak">Frosthaven Peak</a>**, where Klan Frostscale built their settlement without fully realizing their deity was still alive, watching from a distance.
- **<a href="/Verdantus">Verdantus</a>** — the Green Dragon, guardian of the last primordial forest, buried beneath the canopy of the "Endless Canopy" in the Eastern Wilds. He was the guardian of flora's balance, ensuring the last ancient trees that filtered **Ambitio-Ether** were never cut down by greedy hands.
- **<a href="/Aurelia">Aurelia</a>** — the Blue Dragon, who had not surfaced in thousands of years. She swam at the bottom of the **Magic Lake** in the heart of Elis, managing the flow of mana in the water, ensuring the water sources of Elis remained pure and uncontaminated.

Meanwhile, the **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Behemoths">Behemoths</a>** — giant mammals who had once walked across the world in thousands — now numbered only about a dozen. They roamed a hidden grassland surrounded by mountains in the heart of Elis — a plateau called by ancient explorers *"The Forgotten Garden."* Their deep footprints stabilized the soil, preventing erosion. They had no high consciousness, no magic calling the hunger from the sky. There was only an ancient rhythm: eat, sleep, breed, and unknowingly keep the lungs of the world breathing.

**Elis was a museum of the last life** — where the first act of the world was still staged in grand silence, without audience, without applause.

## The First Sin of Lei

**~45 AV**

But even the sacred silence of Elis was not immune to ambition creeping from the other continent.

In the forty-fifth year after the Valla era, the name **Klan Lei** had become synonymous with absolute power. **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Watigi">Lei Watigi</a>** — the rightful heir of the water element from the **Lei Watigi** line — was the personification of that power. With **<a href="/oversee/items/Safayer">Safayer</a>** — the blue soul-purifying stone pulsing in rhythm with his heart — he was a being almost perfect in the eyes of the world.

But perfection often breeds boredom, and boredom is a chasm waiting to be filled with arrogance.

In an expedition to the remote reaches of **Elis** — supposedly to map resources for the Commonwealth — Lei Watigi encountered something he had never seen in any scroll.

On the slopes of **Mount Frosthaven**, when he separated from his group to follow a more remote path, he crossed paths with **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a>**. The silver dragon was resting on an ice cliff, her scales reflecting the twilight sun into millions of cold sparks that blinded. For the first time in his life, Lei Watigi felt **small**. Vythrax was not merely a dragon. She was an ancient entity whose wisdom exceeded the age of the entire Lei bloodline.

But in Lei Watigi's eyes — accustomed to seeing the world as an object to be conquered — the splendor only sparked another desire: **the desire to possess something no one else could possess.**

He was not foolish enough to believe he could conquer a silver dragon with force. So he chose another path: **conspiracy.**

He approached the high priests of **<a href="/oversee/factions/The%20Frostscale%20Clan">Klan Frostscale</a>** secretly — not with hostility, but with an offer they could not refuse. Great wealth. Influence in the Commonwealth. Access to the technology and resources of the Oversee continent. All he asked in return was one thing: **their help to have a child with their own deity.**

The priests — who for thousands of years had only been keepers of ritual without truly experiencing Vythrax's intimate presence — were tempted. They conspired in a scheme that would destroy the sanctity of their own clan.

## The Egg

The brief meeting between Lei Watigi and Vythrax — which initially seemed like chance — had actually been carefully arranged by the priests. They created a situation in which the silver dragon was in a vulnerable state — perhaps through a certain ritual that weakened her concentration, perhaps through a potion that made her more easily influenced. Lei Watigi returned again and again, bringing offerings of rare crystals said to strengthen the bond with the water element.

Vythrax — who had lived in solitude for thousands of years — initially regarded the small creature only as a nuisance. But Lei Watigi's words were full of flattery, and his eyes full of ambition spoke a language the dragon had never understood. With the charm of a noble who had never heard the word *no*, Lei Watigi **coerced** the silver dragon. A forbidden act that crossed the boundaries of race, instinct, and nature. And Vythrax — in a weakness she had never realized existed within herself — was **trapped**.

But the priests' conspiracy did not go smoothly. One of them — in a confession at a critical moment — leaked the foul plan to other clan members. News of the betrayal spread like fire across the ice field. The conspiring priests were tried by their own people, but it was all too late. **Vythrax — the deity they worshipped — had been seen to be pregnant.**

The dragon's womb — which for thousands of years had been only a vessel for pure ice heritage — now nurtured a foreign seed. Klan Frostscale was gripped by suffocating panic. The deity they worshipped — the protector they glorified with songs and offerings — was now carrying a mixed child of an arrogant noble from the other continent. This disgrace could not be known by anyone. The reputation of the clan, their relationship with Vythrax, the entire foundation of their trust — all threatened to collapse.

When Vythrax finally laid a large egg with a blue glow like ice frozen for thousands of years, the remaining elders — those not involved in the initial conspiracy but now having to clean up the mess — conspired in the greatest lie in the history of the clan.

They held a secret meeting in the temple's underground chamber, attended only by the highest priests still loyal. The egg, they decided to announce to the entire clan gathered in the temple, was not an ordinary egg. It was the **Breathstone** — a sacred heirloom, a direct gift from Vythrax, which would become a source of strength and eternal blessing for Klan Frostscale. They created a false worship ritual, built an intricately carved altar around it, and commanded the whole clan to bow every time the full moon came.

The egg was kept in the most sacred chamber in the heart of the temple, worshipped in silence for hundreds of years.

**Vythrax herself** — with a heart wounded but her oath of protection still held tightly — chose a lonely path. She could no longer remain among a clan that had built a lie on top of her disgrace. Before flying north to the **<a href="/oversee/locations/The%20Spire%20of%20Whispering%20Ice">Spire of Whispering Ice</a>**, she infiltrated the temple one night, looking at her egg one last time.


## 700 AV — The Egg Hatches

**The Present Day**

Hundreds of years passed.

The blue egg continued to be kept and worshipped as the **Breathstone** — a sacred heirloom said to maintain the clan's harmony. Generation after generation of Frostscale priests passed, inheriting the same worship ritual without knowing the truth behind it. **Lei Watigi** had long since died, his ambition turned to dust. **Vythrax** continued to endure the Safayer poison flowing in her veins at the peak of the **Spire of Whispering Ice**, still faithfully watching from afar despite being unable to return to the home she had once protected.

Then, one day in the year **700 AV**, when the whole clan gathered in the full moon ritual, something that had not happened for centuries finally happened.

**The egg cracked.**

All eyes turned to the sacred altar as the blue shell shattered into pieces. From within it emerged a **baby boy** — not a dragon, not entirely human. His skin was pale as ice. His hair was silver-white. Small, faint horns grew at his temples. His newly opened eyes looked around blankly, not understanding why hundreds of eyes stared at him with a mixture of fear, confusion, and shock.

A suffocating silence enveloped the temple. The priests looked at each other in panic. The secret they had buried for centuries now stood naked before them in the form of a baby. The truth could no longer be hidden, but a new lie had to be created immediately.

They could not let anyone know that their sacred deity — **Vythrax, The Silver Wind** — had given birth to a mixed child of a human. That would destroy Vythrax's sanctity in the eyes of the clan. That would destroy the foundation of trust they had built.

So the priests quickly made an emergency decision. The baby would be kept secret. They announced that what had just happened was a miracle — that the **Breathstone** had "given birth" to a child destined to become the new guardian of the temple. But they could not let him stay in the sacred chamber. A Frostscale woman who had long desired a child but never had one was secretly summoned and given a sacred mandate: **raise this child in complete secrecy. Let no one know his origin. Let him not leave your house except at night. He belongs to the clan, but no one must know he exists.**

The woman — whose heart was too sincere to refuse the priests' mandate — took the baby home and raised him with love in hiding. She named him **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Syl%20Frost">Syl Frost</a>** — a name that sounded like an ordinary ice child's, with no hint of his origin. During the early years, the secret was kept. Syl grew up in isolation, knowing only his adoptive mother and a few priests who occasionally came to check on him.

But blood never lies.

As he grew, Syl began to show strange, powerful magical abilities — something that a normal Frostscale child should not possess. He could control ice in ways never seen before, create snowstorms in closed rooms, and sometimes speak in ancient languages no one had taught him. In Frostheart society — where magic was viewed with suspicion and often considered taboo — Syl's talent became a major problem. Neighbors began to whisper. Rumors began to spread. His adoptive mother could no longer hide the reality that her adopted child was different.

The priests — who initially thought they could control the situation — began to panic. This secret could not last forever. If the truth about Syl's origin were exposed — that he was the child of Vythrax and Lei Watigi — then the entire lie about the sanctity of their deity would collapse. So they made a new decision: instead of protecting Syl, they would **ostracize** him. They began to spread rumors that Syl was a **bastard child**, the result of a forbidden relationship between his adoptive mother and a foreigner. They created a narrative that Syl's strange magic was proof of "dirty blood" flowing in his veins. That way, public attention would be diverted from the deeper truth: that he was actually the child of their own deity.

Syl grew up in that ostracism. He learned that he was different, that people looked at him with hatred and fear, that he would never be accepted as part of the clan. But he also began to feel something strange in his body — a weakness he could not explain. Sometimes he felt dizzy. Sometimes he saw blue flashes at the edge of his vision. Sometimes he felt something crawling in his blood. He did not know that it was the remnants of the **Safayer** poison Vythrax had swallowed centuries ago — the full stone, meant to protect him, but because he hatched hundreds of years later, his young, not-yet-fully-mature body could not overcome it. The magical resonance Vythrax created to protect her egg was now a curse gnawing at Syl from within.

**Vythrax** — from a distance at the peak of the Spire of Whispering Ice — felt that her child had finally been born. She felt the resonance of **Safayer** in Syl's blood, and her heart broke. Her attempt to protect had only hurt her child. But she was too weak, too poisoned by the blue stone, to come down and fetch him. She could only watch from afar, enduring the pain that continued to flow in her veins, hoping that one day her child would be strong enough to overcome the curse he had inherited.

And Syl — who knew nothing about all of this — continued to live in ostracism, carrying in his blood the poison of a forbidden love between an ambitious noble and a silver dragon — a love that should never have happened, but had created a child who would one day be one of the key figures in the journey of **<a href="/oversee/pcs">The Five Sticks</a>**.
