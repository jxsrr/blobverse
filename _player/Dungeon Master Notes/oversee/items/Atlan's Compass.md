> [!info] At a Glance
> **Type:** Wondrous item
> **Holder:** The Party
> **Rarity:** Rare

## What It Does
- Points to **lost things** — not north.
- The user must name what they seek. The compass needle swings to point.
- The item vibrates when it is near a **Gemheart**.
- It is the only compass that works in the **Gap**.

## History
- Atlan Nouveau's personal compass. Used for ocean navigation.
- Passed to [[oversee/npcs/Mira Nouveau|Mira]]. Given to the party.
- Used to track the stolen gems to the Sea Temple.
- Used to find the hidden door in the Observatorium.

## Current State
- Still with the party. Still pointing at things they haven't named yet.


%% DM SECRETS %%
- The compass does not point to the nearest "lost thing." It points to the **most important** lost thing.
- If the party names "Atlan Nouveau," the compass will point to **the Shell**. This is a huge reveal.
- If they name "Vreina," it will point to the secret Commonwealth facility in Elis.
- If they name "the fifth Gemheart," it will spin wildly — because there is no fifth Gemheart, but the compass is trying.
