> [!info] At a Glance
> **Type:** Longsword
> **Location:** Ruins of the Rapture (Harka)
> **Rarity:** Legendary

## Stat Block
- +1 longsword. 1d8 + Str/Dex. Magical.
- **Patient Blade:** Spend one full turn observing a single enemy without attacking. Your next attack against that enemy has advantage and deals +1d6 damage.
- **Weakening Cut:** Instead of +1 damage, force a DC 13 Con save. Fail = disadvantage on the target's next attack.
- **Crippling Strike (Two-Handed):** Make an attack with disadvantage. On hit, the target takes no extra damage but suffers -5 ft speed or -2 to attack rolls for 1d4 rounds (DC 13 Con negates).

## History
- One of six legendary weapons.
- Thrown into the ruins of an ancient city destroyed by the Rapture.
- **Not yet recovered.** It is in the lap of a skeleton wizard.

## What the Party Knows
- The ruins are cursed. The Rapture happened here.
- The skeleton wizard was once a great mage. Now it is an Echo.
