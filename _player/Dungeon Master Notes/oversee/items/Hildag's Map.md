## What It Does
- A hand-drawn map of the known world. Incomplete.
- Four continents: **Harka**, **Pirizia**, **Rioquan**, **Elis**.
- Hildag's handwriting is precise. Every mountain, river, and ruin is marked.
- But there are blank spaces: **the far north of Pirizia**, **the deep canyons of Rioquan**, **the jungles of Elis**.
- Hildag asked the party to **finish the map**.

## History
- Given by <a href="/oversee/npcs/Hildag%20Grin">Hildag</a> in the Maw Desert (<a href="/oversee/sessions/Session%2005%20%E2%80%94%20Empty%20Husk">Session 05</a>).
- Hildag is a legendary cartographer. This is her life's work.
