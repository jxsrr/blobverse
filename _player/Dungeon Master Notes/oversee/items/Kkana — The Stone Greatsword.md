## Stat Block
- +1 greatsword. 2d6 + Str. Magical. Heavy, two-handed.
- **Sweeping Strike:** On hit, target must succeed on a Str save (DC = 8 + prof + Str mod) or be pushed 5 ft. If pushed into a hazard or off an edge, they may fall prone.
- **Stand Firm:** While holding Kkana with two hands, advantage on Str saves against being pushed, pulled, grappled, or knocked prone.
- **Reactive Block:** When hit by an attack, use your reaction to gain +2 AC against that attack. If the attack misses, your next attack against that enemy has advantage.

## History
- One of six legendary weapons.
- Thrown into the Misty Mountains (Elis) by Lei Firsa.
- Recovered by **[[oversee/npcs/Mira Nouveau|Mira]]** through a Keeper of Secret expedition 12 years ago.

## What the Party Knows
- [[oversee/npcs/Mira Nouveau|Mira]] carries it. She has never used it in front of the party.
- It is the only thing keeping her safe from Commonwealth assassins.
