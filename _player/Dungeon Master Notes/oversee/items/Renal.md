> [!info] At a Glance
> **Type:** Bound Imp / Prosthetic Arm
> **Holder:** [[oversee/pcs/Zure Ama]]
> **Rarity:** Unique

## Stat Block
- **Form:** A small imp. Can shift into:
  - **Rat:** +5 Stealth, 10 ft speed, 1 HP.
  - **Raven:** +5 Perception, fly 30 ft, 1 HP.
  - **Termites:** A swarm. Can consume wood and soft materials. Cannot fight.
- **In Arm Form:** Gives Ama a **semi-sentient prosthetic left arm**. The arm is soft (jelly-like) and slowly solidifying. It cannot yet wield weapons.
- **Ambitio-Ether Consumption:** When Ama consumes Ambitio-Ether (the sludge in Arujaya's Depths), Renal grants Ama "limitless" strength for 1 minute (treat as a giant-strength potion, but with no cap on what can be lifted).
- **Communication:** Renal can speak telepathically to Ama. It is sarcastic. It complains.

## Current State
- Fused into Ama's arm. Still waking up.
- Dr. Vektor says it will take weeks to fully solidify.
