## Stat Block
- +1 dagger. 1d8 + Dex modifier. Magical.
- **Soul Charges** (max 5). Gain 1 charge by:
  - Killing a sentient creature with Swaith.
  - Spending 1 Hit Die as a bonus action.
- **Revive** (costs 3 charges): Stab a creature that died within the last minute. It returns with 1 HP and 1 level of exhaustion. The revived creature has glowing grey eyes for 24 hours and has nightmares about the blade.
- **Soul Thirst** (3+ charges): Disadvantage on Wisdom saves against fear and despair. The blade whispers to you.

## Curse
- The blade cannot be removed easily. It requires Greater Restoration or a full ritual to release.
- If forcefully removed: lose 2d6 max HP until the curse is lifted.
- The blade slowly binds to its wielder. It will not let go.

## History
- Swaith is **one of the six blades** — not an ancient relic.
- It is a **shadow fragment of Safayer** — a piece of the purification Gemheart that was forged into a dagger by the Architect.
- It was found in **Dur-Khazim** by **Simon Greenbottle**. He gave it to **Grin Qain**.
- After Lei Firsa scattered the six, **Swaith was magically delivered to Shin Van Hel**. It was not found as loot. It was **sent** to him.
- Shin has carried it ever since.

## What the Party Knows
- Swaith can release trapped Echoes. This was proven in Session 03.
- It is the only weapon that can interact with the Echoes.
- It whispers to Shin. It binds him.
