## What It Does
- One of the four Gemhearts. Blue.
- Connected to **purification of souls and memories**.
- Can strip a soul of ego, trauma, and identity — turning it into "tasteless" energy.
- Part of the seal that protects the world.
- Prevents the Overseer from tasting the soul of the world.

## History
- Swallowed by **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a>** to protect Syl's egg (300 years ago).
- Poisoned Vythrax for centuries. The poison passed into Syl's blood.
- **Returned by Vythrax** after Syl healed her (Session 05).
- Now in the party's possession.

## What the Party Knows
- It is the reason Echoes exist. Lei Alejandro did not use it during the ritual.
- It must not be used carelessly.
- It is one of the four Gemhearts.
