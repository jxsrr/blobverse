## What It Does
- One of the four Gemhearts. Yellow.
- Connected to **structure, foundation, and stability**.
- Prevents the continents of Oversee from being pulled into the Gap.
- Anchors sacrificial rituals to the mortal world.

## History
- Never found by the Cycle Clan.
- Carried by the first Lei Roiga (never recovered).
- Held by **Lei Alejandro** for centuries. He found it in the ruins of a Lei Roiga temple. He has kept it safe ever since.
- He does not want it. He will give it to the party if they prove they are worthy.

## What the Party Knows
- They know it is connected to the earth element.
- They **do not know** who holds it.
- They **do not know** it is with Lei Alejandro.
