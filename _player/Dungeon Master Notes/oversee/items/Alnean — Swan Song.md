## What It Does
- **Breathe Anywhere:** The wielder can breathe in water, in the Gap, in the void between worlds, and in any environment where air should not exist.
- **Gap Resistance:** While holding Alnean, the wielder has advantage on saving throws against the Gap's corrupting influence.
- **Swan Song (1/long rest):** The wielder can exhale a breath of pure Ambitio-Ether. This breath heals all allies within 30 ft for 4d8 HP and removes one level of exhaustion from each. If the wielder is at 0 HP when they use this, they stabilize and the breath still takes effect.
- **The Breath Is Not a Weapon:** Alnean cannot attack. It cannot cut. It cannot block. It is not a blade. It is a way to survive where no mortal should.

## The Swan
- Alnean is not one of the six blades. It is one of the **three ancient relics** — the **breath of the swan**.
- The breath is what lets the swan swim in water, dive into the deep, and rise again. Alnean reflects this: it lets the wielder survive the Gap, the void, and the depths.
- The other two relics are **Gustaero** (the neck — a longbow) and **Niette** (the wing — a spear). Together they form the swan that pierces the Gap.
- Without the breath, the swan cannot swim. Without Alnean, the third path is impossible.

## Why "Swan Song"?
- The name was given by the Cycle Clan, not by the Architect.
- A swan sings only once — at the moment of its death. Alnean is the breath that lets the swan sing.
- The Cycle Clan's records say: *"The swan does not fear the deep. It fears the silence. Alnean is the song that breaks the silence."*
- It is not a weapon of death. It is a song of survival.

## History
- Alnean is one of the three ancient relics. **It is not related to the Architect.** Its origin is older, or separate, or simply unknown.
- It was never carried by the Architect. It was never part of the Gemheart network.
- It has been kept by the **Kuo Lau Kingdom** — the underwater kingdom — for as long as their records go back. The Kuo Lau King is its current guardian.
- The Kuo Lau are the origin of **Babibubebo** — the small fish-man the party met in Juto Port City (Session 02). Babi is an exile, a fragment of the Kuo Lau who was lost in the mortal world.
- Alnean has never been recovered by anyone outside the Kuo Lau. The Cycle Clan knows it exists. They do not know where it is.
- No one has ever seen the Kuo Lau Kingdom and returned to tell of it.

## What the Party Knows
- They have never heard of Alnean.
- They know there are three ancient relics, but they have only seen one — Gustaero.
