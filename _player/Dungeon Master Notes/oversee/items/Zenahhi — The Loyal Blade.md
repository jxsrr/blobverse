> [!info] At a Glance
> **Type:** Longsword
> **Location:** Ngarai Ratapan (Rioquan East)
> **Rarity:** Legendary

## Stat Block
- +1 longsword. 1d8 + Str/Dex. Magical.
- **Loyal Blade:** Can make an opportunity attack while Dodging.
- **Defender's Strike (3/day):** When an ally within 5 ft is attacked, use your reaction to attack the attacker. Your attack resolves before damage to the ally.
- **Perfect Balance:** While one-handed with an empty off-hand, bonus action to pommel strike (1d4 bludgeoning, no +1). Target makes a Con save (DC 13) or has disadvantage on their next concentration check.

## History
- One of six legendary weapons.
- Thrown into Ngarai Ratapan by Lei Firsa.
- **Not yet recovered.** It is at the bottom of the deepest canyon in Oversee.

## What the Party Knows
- Legend says no one has ever descended to the bottom and returned.
- The blade was forged for **protection**, not conquest.
