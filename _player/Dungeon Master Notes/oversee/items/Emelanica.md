## What It Does
- One of the four Gemhearts. Green.
- Connected to **time and absolute mana flow**.
- Part of the seal that protects the world from the Overseer.
- Can modulate temporal flow at the center of the seal.
- Slows the burning of the Valla's soul.

## History
- Found in **Greyland Castle** by the party (Session 01).
- Was hidden in an anti-magic chest. Taking it woke Will of Grin.
- Handed to **Mira / Keeper of Secret** after Session 02.
- Mira has held it ever since. She has not told the party.
- **Accarus Grin never carried Emelanica.** He carried Gustaero — and gave it to Husgus after the Frosthaven mission.

## What the Party Knows
- They found it in Session 01. They know it is one of the Gemhearts.
- They do not know Mira took it. They do not know where it is.
- They do not know what it can do when fully awakened.
