## What the Party Knows
- A goblin king. Smarter than most goblins.
- Had a daughter who married **Lei Alejandro**.
- She died in childbirth with a stillborn child.
- **Emelanica** was meant for his heirs. He was guarding it.
- Was initially peaceful. Turned hostile when the party mentioned Alejandro.
- Died when the castle collapsed. His body was lost at sea.

## His Legacy
- The Green Emerald (Emelanica).
- The truth about Alejandro's love for a goblin queen.
- The carving in the sea cave.

## Current State
- Dead.
