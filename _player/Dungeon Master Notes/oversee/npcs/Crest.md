> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Shin's friend in The Grace. Disciplined, observant, suspicious — but loyal.
> **Where:** Died in Elis (690 AV)
> **Status:** Deceased

## What the Party Knows
- A member of **The Grace**. Disciplined. Kept detailed records. Always made sure Shin was briefed before a mission.
- **Suspected something was different about Shin** — he documented anomalies.
- Did not blame Shin for what happened. He tried to save him.
- Died in **690 AV**, when The Grace fell.

## Shin's Memory
- Crest was the one who taught Shin to be careful — but never made him feel like a threat.
- His notes on Shin were found by the Commonwealth after the fall. They are in the Lyceum archives.
- Shin has never read them. He thinks they are accusations.
