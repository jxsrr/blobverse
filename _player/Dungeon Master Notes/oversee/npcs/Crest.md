### Who Crest Is
- **Crest** was a member of **The Grace** — the independent demon-hunter order based in Elis. He was not the leader. He was not the strongest. He was not the warmest. He was the **brain**.
- He was **disciplined**. He was **observant**. He was **methodical**. He kept records of everything — every mission, every demon, every anomaly, every detail that might matter later. He was the one who made sure the others survived. He was the one who made sure the others knew what they were walking into.
- He met **Shin** on his first day — the same day Edra met him. He did not sit beside Shin. He did not welcome him. He watched him. He took notes. He noticed the gloves. He noticed the smile. He noticed the way the air around Shin seemed to **hum** when he was angry.
- He did not trust Shin. Not at first. But he did not hate him either. He was **suspicious** — but he was also **fair**. He would not judge Shin without evidence. He would not condemn him without proof.

### Crest and Shin
- Crest was **Shin's friend** — not in the way Edra was (warm, open, welcoming), but in a quieter, harder way. He was the one who trained with Shin. He was the one who sparred with him. He was the one who taught him to control his breathing, to steady his hands, to keep his emotions in check.
- He did not ask about the gloves. He did not ask about the smile. He did not ask about the past. He only asked about the present — *"Are you ready? Are you focused? Are you in control?"* — because those were the questions that mattered in a fight.
- **He knew Shin carried something.** He saw the curse mark glow. He saw the shadows move. He saw the way Shin's eyes changed when he was angry. He documented everything. He kept notes.
- **He did not blame Shin.** He never did. His notes were not accusations. They were **observations of a friend**. He was trying to understand. He was trying to help. He was trying to find a way to save him.
- **He was the one who figured it out first.** He realized that the curse was not a curse — it was a **seal**. He realized that Shin was not a monster — he was a **prison**. He realized that the thing inside Shin was not evil — it was **trapped**. He was going to tell Shin. He was going to help him. He never got the chance.

### Crest's Notes — The Truth in the Archives
- **Crest kept notes on Shin.** He documented every anomaly, every incident, every moment when the curse mark glowed or the shadows moved. He wrote them in a small leather journal, hidden in his quarters. He never showed them to anyone. He was not trying to expose Shin. He was trying to **protect** him.
- **One entry reads:** *"Subject shows signs of possession. Recommend non-lethal containment if triggered. Subject is not a threat. Subject is Shin."*
- **Another entry reads:** *"The mark on his hand is not a curse. It is a seal. Something is inside him. Something old. Something that is trying to get out. I do not know what it is. I do not know if it is dangerous. But I know Shin is not the danger. The thing inside him is."*
- **A final entry reads:** *"I am going to tell him tomorrow. I am going to tell him I know. I am going to tell him I do not care. He is my friend. Whatever he carries, he carries it with me."*
- **He never got to tell him.** The Gap tear opened the next day. The Grace fell. Crest died.
- **His notes were recovered by the Commonwealth** after the fall. They are in the **Lyceum archives** — in the restricted section, where the party may find them in Season 3. They were not used to condemn Shin. They were used to **study** him. The Commonwealth twisted Crest's words into a case for hunting HVS-02. This is how Lei Firsa learned about Shin. This is how she began to watch him.
- **Shin has never read the notes.** He thinks they are accusations. He thinks Crest suspected him. He thinks Crest died hating him. He is wrong.

### The Fall of The Grace — 690 AV
- **690 AV.** A massive Gap tear opened in Elis. Demons poured through. The Grace fought. They held the line. They died.
- **Crest fought beside Shin.** He did not run. He did not hide. He stood with him. He died with him — or so he thought.
- **Shin was possessed by Zargoyle.** He did not choose it. He did not want it. It happened in the chaos of the tear.
- **Zargoyle opened a small seal of Zargoth inside Shin** — accidentally, violently. The resulting explosion destroyed The Grace. It destroyed Elis.
- **Crest died in the explosion.** He did not blame Shin. He did not have time to blame anyone. He died trying to save him. He died trying to reach him. He died believing Shin was still in there.
- **His body was never found.** The explosion destroyed everything. The ruins of The Grace are still in Elis — a stone keep built into the side of a mountain, hidden from the world, protected by old wards. His body may still be there. Or it may have been vaporized. No one knows.
- **His sword** was recovered in Elis. It is a mundane longsword, but it bears his initials: **C.V.** It is in the Lyceum archives, along with his notes. The party may find it in Season 3.

### Crest and Edra
- **Edra** was Crest's closest friend in The Grace. They were opposites — she was warm, he was cold. She was impulsive, he was disciplined. She was the heart, he was the brain. They balanced each other.
- **They both loved Shin.** Not in the same way — Edra loved him as a brother, Crest loved him as a comrade — but they both loved him. They both tried to save him. They both died trying.
- **Edra's letter** — the one she wrote before she died — was written for Shin. **Crest's notes** — the ones he kept on Shin — were written for himself. Both of them were trying to understand him. Both of them were trying to protect him. Both of them failed.
- **If the party ever finds Crest's notes** — in the Lyceum archives — they will find the truth about Shin. They will find the truth about the curse. They will find the truth about The Grace. They will find the truth about **Zargoth** and **Zargoyle** — not by name, but by description. Crest did not know the names. He only knew there were two entities. One was a father. One was a monster. He did not know which was which. He only knew Shin was not the monster.

### Crest's Sword — The Blade in the Archives
- **Crest's sword** was recovered in Elis. It is a mundane longsword — no magic, no enchantment, no special properties. It is just a sword. But it bears his initials: **C.V.**
- It is in the **Lyceum archives**, along with his notes. It is in a locked case, in the restricted section, where the Commonwealth keeps the things it does not want anyone to see.
- **If the party ever finds it**, they can take it. It is not a legendary weapon. It is not a magic item. It is just a sword. But it is **Crest's sword** — the sword of the man who tried to save Shin. The sword of the man who died trying.
- **If Shin ever holds it**, he will feel something. Not magic. Not a curse. Just a memory. A memory of a man who trained with him, who sparred with him, who taught him to control his breathing. A memory of a friend.
- **He will weep.** He will smile. He always smiles. But for the first time in 690 years, the smile will reach his eyes.
