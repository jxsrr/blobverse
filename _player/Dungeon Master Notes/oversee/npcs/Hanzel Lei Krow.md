> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Governor of West Harka. Hosted the Festival of the Great Lei.
> **Where:** Padre City
> **Status:** Alive

## What the Party Knows
- Direct descendant of **Lei Wendigo** (wind element).
- Carries **Emelanica's twin** — no, wait, that's not right. Carries the **authority** of Emelanica, not the stone itself. The stone is with the party.
- Opened the Festival with a speech about the "Triase of the United Commonwealth."
- Political. Smooth. Well-dressed.
- Beloved by the crowds. Hated by Will of Grin.
- Was seen with [[oversee/npcs/Lei Iron Gort|Gort]] in private after the parade.

## Current Goal
Maintain power. Serve Valerius. Suppress dissent.
