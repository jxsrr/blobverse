## Overview
An elderly Grin woman who lives alone in a mountain cave known as the Hermitage near [[oversee/locations/Whisper Place]]. Small in stature with grey hair, sharp eyes, and the permanent, unmoving smile characteristic of the Grin curse, she rarely descends toward settled roads.

## Reputation
- **The Hermit of Whisper Place:** Travelers passing through the region know of her mostly as a gruff, solitary figure who values her privacy and expects basic courtesy from anyone approaching her cave.
