> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Leader of the Stevedore Union.
> **Where:** Javan Town Docks
> **Status:** Alive

## What the Party Knows
- A burly man with a weathered face. Speaks for the dockworkers.
- Has a deep distrust of the Commonwealth. Has seen too many workers die in "accidents."
- Knows about the **dark port operations** — smuggling, weapons, people.
- Will not talk to strangers. Will talk to people who help his workers.

## What He Offers
- **Information** about smuggling routes and Commonwealth operations.
- **A safe passage** through the docks (if the party earns his trust).
- **Muscle** — his dockworkers will fight if needed.

## Current Goal
Protect his workers. Expose the Commonwealth's crimes.
