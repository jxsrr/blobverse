> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Owner of "Teritip Tua" diving shop.
> **Where:** Kota Juto, Distrik Pasang
> **Status:** Alive. Ancient (1000+ years old). Deaf.

## Appearance
An ancient Tortle. Slow-moving. Smells of rust, oil, and copper. Surrounded by antique diving helmets, harpoons, nets, and strange oxygen tanks made from giant nautilus shells.

## What the Party Knows
- He is deaf. You have to shout.
- He knew **Atlan Nouveau** personally.
- He has a copy of Atlan's children's book (the one about the sea).
- He can give the party a **map to Atlan's hidden base** if they ask the right question.
- He is not hostile. He is just old.

## Current Goal
Run his shop. Remember the sea.
