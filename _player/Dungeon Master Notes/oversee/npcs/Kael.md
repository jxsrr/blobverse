%% DM SECRETS %%

## Who He Is
- A **young student** at Universitas Tarakita. Quiet. Thin. Dark circles under his eyes.
- Appears to be an ordinary student — maybe 17–18 years old.
- **He is not ordinary.** He is a **Cycle Clan agent** — planted at the university to watch for HVS (Shin).

## What He Knows
- He knows about **HVS-01 and HVS-02**. He has been told the shells are dangerous.
- He has been told to **report any sighting** to his handler — a member of the Cycle Clan who reports directly to **Lei Firsa**.
- He knows **[[oversee/npcs/Professor Rubi]]** — but does not know she is also Cycle Clan. He believes she is a "regular professor."

## What He Offers
- **A Charm of Silence** — if the party helps him with his "demonic voices." (The voices are real — a side effect of a failed HVS experiment that touched him as a child. The Charm silences them for 24 hours.)
- **Information** about the university — but only if the party does not threaten him.
- **A warning to Shin:** *"You need to leave. Before they find you."*

## Personality
- Nervous. Jittery. Always looking over his shoulder.
- Does not want to be a spy. Was forced into the role by his family.
- Wants to defect — but is too afraid of Lei Firsa to try.

## His Secret Guilt
- He has already reported someone to his handler once.
- That person was an innocent student who mentioned "seeing a man with a strange mark" near the library.
- The student was found dead three days later. Kael has never forgiven himself.
- If the party befriends him, he may confess this. If they turn him in, he will be executed by the Cycle Clan.

## When Party Meets Them
- **Season 3 encounter** — at Tarakita University.
- Wrap his player-facing content in `::pf` markers when encountered.

## Naming Note
- **Not the same as [[Jenderal Kael]]** (the imprisoned Cipher at Benteng Atlan). Kael is a common name in Oversee — this is a coincidence.
- To avoid confusion in wikilinks: reference this one as `[[Kael (Student)|Kael]]` and the other as `[[Jenderal Kael|Jenderal Kael]]`.

## Open Questions
- Does he know about the **Living Failures** in the underground library? (Yes — but he is terrified of them.)
- Does he know about **HVS-03**? (Yes — he has seen the empty, cracked shell tube. He does not know what it means.)
- Will he defect to the party? (Yes, if they earn his trust.)
