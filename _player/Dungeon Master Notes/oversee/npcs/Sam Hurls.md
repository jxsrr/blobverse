> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** The bard of the Initial Six. Later, the Echo that haunted Ghuffghuff.
> **Where:** Ghuffghuff (formerly)
> **Status:** Deceased — Echo released by Swaith

## Living Form — The Bard
- One of the **Initial Six**. A chubby human man with wavy black hair, purple and red clothing embroidered with gold.
- Carried a lute. Played music that could open doors between dimensions.
- Collected stories from every corner of Oversee. Never finished his last song.
- Was **betrayed by Lei Alejandro** in 200 AV. His soul was fed to the Valla.
- Without **Safayer** (the blue Gemheart), his soul was not purified. He became an **Echo**.

## Echo Form — The Many-Headed Singer
- A pale, bloated creature. Multiple human heads rotate around a central mass. Each head is a fragment of someone Sam met in his travels.
- They all sing the same unfinished melody.
- Lured villagers of Ghuffghuff into an illusory world. Drained them dry.
- **HVS stabbed him with Swaith.** Released the trapped souls. He briefly returned to his true form.
- Said only a few words before **[[oversee/npcs/Accarus Grin|Accarus]] shot him with an arrow**.
- His body crumbled. His Echo returned to Simon.

## Personality
- Warm. Theatrical. Loves music.
- Deeply sad. His last words were an apology.
- Wanted to finish his song.
