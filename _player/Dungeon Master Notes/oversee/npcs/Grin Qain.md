## What the Party Knows
- A Grin elf. Eternal smile. Horns. Magic-deaf.
- Wielded **Swaith** (the soul-thirsting blade). Simon gave it to her.
- Her last words: *"Take care of yourself, Shin. You are the best friend I ever had."* (Whispered to Simon, not Shin — but the party misinterprets this.)
- Her death broke Simon.

## What She Offers (If an Echo is found)
- Closure for Shin (who carries Swaith).
- Closure for Husgus (who carries Gordon's legacy).
- The truth about Swaith's origin.

## Current State
- Dead. But her **Echo may still exist** somewhere.

## The Letter (from Simon's perspective)
> *"She smiled even as she died. That is the Grin curse. You cannot stop smiling. You cannot show pain. She died smiling. I have never forgiven myself for letting her."*
