> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Innkeeper at Karang Tidur
> **Where:** Kota Juto, Distrik Pasang
> **Status:** Alive

## What the Party Knows
- Old halfling woman. Warm smile. Seems kind.
- Runs the **Karang Tidur** inn — a former private residence given to adventurers.
- Told the party about a break-in. Gems were "stolen."
- Blamed the **Sea Temple cultists**.
- The party later discovered she may be lying.

## Current Goal
Run her inn. Stay out of trouble. (Or so she says.)
