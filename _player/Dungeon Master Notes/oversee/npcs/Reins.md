> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Ama's best friend from another world. Wife of Atlan. Mother of Mira.
> **Where:** The Gap (trapped)
> **Status:** Alive — trapped, waiting, watching.

## What the Party Knows
- Ama's best friend from their home world, **Aede**. Short pink hair. Moon-shaped staff. Purple robes.
- Empathetic, calm, wise — the one who understood people's emotions best.
- **Fell into Oversee** hundreds of years before Ama, near a lighthouse in Kota Juto.
- Met **Atlan Nouveau**. They fell in love. Had a daughter, **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Mira Nouveau|Mira]]**.
- Chose to stay in Oversee instead of returning to Aede to fight the Destroyer God.
- Her fate after Atlan's disappearance is unknown.

## Ama's Memory
- Ama believes Reins died in Aede. He does not know she fell into Oversee.
- **He has not yet learned that she lived, loved, and had a daughter here.**
- When he learns, it will change everything he believes about his quest.
