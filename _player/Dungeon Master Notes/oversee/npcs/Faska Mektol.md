## What the Party Knows
- Traveled to Ghuffghuff with her husband **Herman** and daughter **Cindy**.
- Kind. Warm. Held Cindy close during the journey.
- **Eaten by Echo Sam** on the first night in Ghuffghuff.
- Her dried corpse was found in Domido Forest — next to **Yahanda's corpse**.
- **Herman did not remember her.** When asked, he insisted only he and Cindy had come to Ghuffghuff.

## What the Party Remembers
- She was the first person they *watched* be forgotten.
- She gave the party dried fruit on the road. It was the last kindness before everything went wrong.
- **Her daughter Cindy survived.** Cindy does not remember her — but Cindy's mother loved her.
