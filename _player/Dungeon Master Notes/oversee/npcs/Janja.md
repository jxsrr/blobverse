> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Guanik's wife. Mother of Dontol, Huan, and Nila.
> **Where:** Died on Pulau Dima
> **Status:** Deceased (killed by Will of Grin)

## What the Party Knows
- A kind, quiet woman. Warm. Made the party tea when they arrived at her home.
- Lived with her husband **[[oversee/npcs/Guanik Smith|Guanik]]** and their three children.
- **Killed by [[oversee/factions/Will of Grin]]** when they attacked Pulau Dima in Session 2.
- Her home was burned. Her family scattered.
- The party was not there when she died.

## What the Party Remembers
- She welcomed strangers into her home without hesitation.
- She was the first person to show the party kindness after Greyland Castle.
- Her death was the party's first real failure — they couldn't save her.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Janja died protecting her youngest children, **Huan** and **Nila** (who escaped).
- Her eldest son **Dontol** was away fishing when the attack happened. He survived.
- **Dontol's whereabouts are unknown.** He may be searching for survivors. He may be hunting Will of Grin. He may be dead.
- Guanik (her husband) was killed in the same attack. He was found on the beach, still holding his fishing net.
- **Huan and Nila** (if saved) were adopted by a family in Kota Juto. They still have the shell collection the party recovered for them.
- Janja's grave is a small stone cairn on Pulau Dima's beach. If the party ever returns, they can find it — but no one has visited it since.
