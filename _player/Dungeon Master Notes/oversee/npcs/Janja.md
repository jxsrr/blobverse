## What the Party Knows
- A kind, quiet woman. Warm. Made the party tea when they arrived at her home.
- Lived with her husband **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Guanik%20Smith">Guanik</a>** and their three children.
- **Killed by <a href="/oversee/factions/Will%20of%20Grin">Will of Grin</a>** when they attacked Dima Island in Session 2.
- Her home was burned. Her family scattered.
- The party was not there when she died.

## What the Party Remembers
- She welcomed strangers into her home without hesitation.
- She was the first person to show the party kindness after Greyland Castle.
- Her death was the party's first real failure — they couldn't save her.
