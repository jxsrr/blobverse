> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** The owner of a small warung (food stall) in Ghuffghuff.
> **Where:** Ghuffghuff Market
> **Status:** Unknown — presumed deceased

## What the Party Knows
- An old woman. Sold maps and warm food from a tiny stall.
- The party bought maps from her on their first day in Ghuffghuff.
- She was one of the first people Echo Sam erased — the party never saw her again after the first night.

## What the Party Remembers
- She was kind. She charged less for the maps than the party expected.
- She did not remember Yahanda when asked about him — she thought the party had come alone.
