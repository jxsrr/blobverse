> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Ancient silver dragon, deity of Klan Frostscale, mother of Syl Frost
> **Where:** Spire of Whispering Ice (Pirizia North)
> **Status:** Alive, weakened

## What the Party Knows
- Gargantuan silver dragon. Legendary protector of Klan Frostscale.
- Disappeared 300 years ago. The clan believed she would return.
- Was actually poisoned by **Safayer** — the blue Gemheart she swallowed to protect her egg.
- **Syl Frost is her son.** The "Breathstone" the clan worshipped was Syl's egg.
- The egg was conceived through a conspiracy involving **Lei Watigi** — a dark, non-consensual act.
- Syl fed Vythrax his own blood. Vythrax recovered. **Returned the full Safayer** to the party.
- Flew back to her spire. Still weak.

## Current Goal
Recover. Protect Syl from a distance.
