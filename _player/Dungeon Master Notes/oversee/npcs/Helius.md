## What the Party Knows
- A simple man. Worshipped **Valla** in the simplest way — prayer before sleep, bread shared even when there wasn't much.
- Found Shin as a child on the shore of Elis. Took him in without asking questions.
- Taught him to read, to listen, to sit in silence without needing to fill it.
- Never asked Shin to remove his gloves, or to stop smiling.
- **Sent Shin to The Grace** when he was grown. Told him: *"You need a purpose. And the world needs someone like you."*

## Shin's Memory
- His kindness is why Shin still worships Valla (in his own way).
- His death — from old age — was the first real grief Shin ever felt.
- His grave is still in Elis, near the shore where he found Shin. No one has visited it in 690 years.
