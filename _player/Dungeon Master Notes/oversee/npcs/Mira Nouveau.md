## What the Party Knows
- Half-elf. Daughter of **Atlan Nouveau** and **Reins**.
- Witnessed her father's disappearance as a teenager.
- Founded **Keeper of Secret** to find the truth.
- Hired the party to find **Gustaero**.
- Knows more than she tells. Has a plan.
- Carries **Kkana** — the stone greatsword, one of the six blades.
- Is **friendly** to the party — but has not told them everything.

## Current Goal
Find the truth about the Initial Six. Protect the world from the Commonwealth. Free the Valla.
