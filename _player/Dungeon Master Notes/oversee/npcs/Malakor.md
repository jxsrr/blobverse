## What the Party Knows (from Husgus)
- A wizard. Old. Fanatical. Believed fried noodles were sacred.
- Was an **agent of the Commonwealth** — placed in Gudameng to keep the Gruffgud War going.
- Killed Gordon in 695 AV.
- Was killed by **Husgus** in his tower. Husgus burned the tower down.

## His Legacy
- The truth about Gunung Rayu.
- Husgus's transformation into a halfling.
- The burning of his tower (which destroyed most of his research).
