## What the Party Knows (from Lore)
- An ancient, wise demon from the Gap.
- Loved **Gavaevodata**. Married her. Fathered the Grin people.
- Was supposed to be transferred into **HVS-02** (Shin's shell) by Guanik Wiseman.
- Never made it. A fragment of his consciousness was trapped in the shell when it broke.

## What the Party Does NOT Know
- That Shin carries Zargoth.
- That Zargoth is still awake.
- That Zargoth is not hostile.
- That Zargoth is a father who has been separated from his children for 10,000 years.
- That Zargoth wants to meet Vya.
- That Zargoth wants to be released.
