> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Expert on Gemhearts and Cycle Clan history
> **Where:** Outer Ring, Padre City
> **Status:** Deceased (before party arrived)

## What the Party Knows
- Was Donta's colleague. Studied the Gemhearts.
- His house was found open. Coffee cold. No Howdy.
- Footprints (large) led away.
- Found the **Cycle Clan Research File** in his home after talking to a cockroach.
- Died of a "heart attack" the day before the party arrived.
- His death was not natural.

## Legacy
- The Cycle Clan Research File is one of the party's most important documents.
- It contains the truth about the Gemhearts and the ritual.
