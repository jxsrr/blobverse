## Who They Were
- The **Wakil Keadilan** of the One Voice Commonwealth at the time of the **Initial Six** (200 AV).
- He served alongside **Geldfre** (Kanselir Agung) and **Hildag Grin** (Wakil Pertahanan).

## What They Did
- **Built the current Rose Guard** — not the original. The original Rose Guard was a group of scholars and witnesses. Erard turned it into a **law enforcement arm** — soldiers, mages, spies. He did not do this out of malice. He did it because the Commonwealth needed "order." He was told the scholars were too slow, too soft, too weak.
- **Wrote the first draft of the public Edisi Law** — the version that enshrines Klan Lei supremacy, hides the truth about the Valla, and makes the twin-killing tradition "legal." He did not write it alone. He was guided. He was told it was necessary.
- **Established the court system** — the courts that enforce the public Edisi Law. He believed in law. He believed in order. He did not know the law was a lie.

## Why It Matters
- His name appears in `The Triase.md` as the previous Wakil Keadilan at the time of the Initial Six.
- **The party has no reason to learn this name** — unless they research the Triase's history, or unless they find records in the Bahtera Bawah Tanah.

## When Party Meets Them
- **Never in person** — dead for 500 years.
- **Possible reveal:** In the Bahtera Bawah Tanah, Erard's records may be found alongside Geldfre's. They tell the story of a man who thought he was doing the right thing — and who was used. His last entry: *"I built the courts to protect the innocent. I did not know they would be used to condemn them. Forgive me."*
