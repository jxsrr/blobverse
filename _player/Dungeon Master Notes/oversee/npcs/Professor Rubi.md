## Overview
A senior instructor of history and arcane artifacts at <a href="/oversee/locations/Tarakita%20University%20of%20Heroism">Tarakita University of Heroism</a>. Known across campus for her sharp gaze, half-moon spectacles, and neatly pinned grey hair, she maintains a cluttered study in the North Tower packed with antique maps, fractured wands, battered shields, and old relics.

## Reputation
- **Scholar of the Old World:** Students and fellow faculty regard her as one of the university's foremost authorities on pre-Commonwealth artifacts and forgotten historical records.
- **Quiet Demeanor:** Patient and soft-spoken in lectures, though notoriously selective about whom she allows to browse her private archives.
