> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Priest of the neglected Valla temple in Javan Town.
> **Where:** Javan Town
> **Status:** Alive

## What the Party Knows
- An old, tired priest. Coal dust on his robes.
- Spends more time **burying workers** than preaching.
- Knows about the dark port operations but stays silent.
- Not corrupt — broken. He has seen too much.

## What He Offers
- **Healing** (for free, if the party is kind).
- **Information** about the port's deaths.
- **A warning**: *"Do not trust the smile that does not reach the eyes."*

## Current Goal
Bury the dead. Keep his faith.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Father Tulus's faith is **shaken**. He has seen too many innocent people die. He does not know if Valla is real.
- He has a **hidden diary** in the temple. It contains the names of every worker who died in "accidents" at the port.
- If the party shows him proof that the Commonwealth is responsible, he will break down and cry. Then he will give them the diary.
- The diary is **evidence**. It can be used to turn the public against the Commonwealth.
- He is the only priest in Javan Town. If he dies, no one will bury the dead.
