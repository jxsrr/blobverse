> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** The dwarf who raised Husgus. The man who ended the Gruffgud War. The Noisy Rhino.
> **Where:** Died in Hutan Domido
> **Status:** Deceased (killed by Malakor)

## What the Party Knows (from Husgus)
- A dwarf ranger. Thick white beard. Big axe. Viking hat.
- Lived alone in Hutan Domido for centuries — his lifespan was extended by the Gustaero bow.
- Raised Husgus from childhood. Taught him to hunt, to track, to survive.
- **Ended the Gruffgud War** by convincing both sides to make peace. He was called **"Badak Berisik"** (Noisy Rhino) because he never stopped yelling about the truth.
- Was **killed by Malakor** in 695 AV. Husgus buried him under the old tree in Domido Forest.
- Taught Husgus to be skeptical of gods and rulers. Taught him that the true guardian is not the one who seeks fame, but the one who protects the community.

## His Legacy
- **The Gustaero bow** — given to him by Olaf, passed to Husgus.
- **The melody of Sam Hurls** — taught to him by Sam, passed to the villages.
- **The truth about Gunung Rayu** — he tried to expose the Commonwealth's secret excavation. No one listened.
- **Husgus** — his greatest legacy.

## Current State
- Dead. Buried in Hutan Domido.
