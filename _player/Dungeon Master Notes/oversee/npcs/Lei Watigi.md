> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** The water heir of Klan Lei. Syl's biological father. The man who raped Vythrax.
> **Where:** Deceased — died centuries ago
> **Status:** Dead

## What the Party Knows
- One of the four children of **Lei Firsa** and **Simon Greenbottle**.
- The water heir — the founder of the **Lei Watigi** line.
- Carried **Safayer** — the blue Gemheart — before it was swallowed by Vythrax.
- Was involved in the conception of **Syl Frost**.
- The player-facing history says he and Vythrax had a "forbidden love." This is a lie.

## What the Party Does NOT Know
- Lei Watigi **raped** Vythrax. He did not seduce her. He did not love her. He assaulted her with the help of the Frostscale priests.
- He is **not** a tragic romantic figure. He is a monster.
- He died centuries ago. He never faced justice. He never answered for what he did.
