## What the Party Knows
- Son of **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Guanik%20Smith">Guanik</a>** and **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Janja">Janja</a>**. Brother of **Nila** and **Dontol**.
- Asked the party for help recovering his and his sister's **shell collection** — stolen by a "giant crab monster" (actually a kobold).
- If the party saved the shells: he gave them a **spiral shell** as thanks.
- **Was on Dima Island when Will of Grin attacked.** Survived (per Session 2 recap).
- His current whereabouts are unknown, but he is believed to be alive.

## His Personality
- Shy. Curious. Spoke more to his sister than to the party.
- Loved collecting shells more than anything.
