> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** The "merchant wife" the party escorted to Padre City. A Keeper of Secret courier.
> **Where:** Javan Town (travels frequently)
> **Status:** Alive

## What the Party Knows
- The wife of **Pak Andreas** the blacksmith. Hired the party to guard her on the road to Padre City in Session 4.
- Paid 200 gp. Was cheerful, sharp, and quiet in equal measure.
- Carried more goods than a merchant should — crates, sealed boxes, careful to always sit beside them.
- Left the party at the Festival. Was not seen again.

## What the Party Remembers
- She argued with the Commonwealth soldiers **without fear** — a woman who knew she was untouchable.
- She watched the crowd carefully during the parade. Not like a merchant — like a **watcher**.
