> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Shin's closest friend in The Grace. Warm, loyal, never questioned his smile.
> **Where:** Died in Elis (690 AV)
> **Status:** Deceased

## What the Party Knows
- A member of **The Grace** — the demon-hunter order Shin belonged to before he woke up 690 years later.
- Befriended Shin on his first day. Sat beside him without being asked. Never treated him as strange.
- **Did not blame Shin for what happened in Elis.** She tried to save him.
- Died in **690 AV**, when The Grace fell.

## Shin's Memory
- Her last words to Shin, in a letter never delivered: *"Shin, I know you're hiding something. Don't run. We are your family, whatever you carry."*
- Shin still thinks she may have blamed him. He is wrong.
- The letter is in **Zundabun**, held by **Moro** the shadowless bartender.
