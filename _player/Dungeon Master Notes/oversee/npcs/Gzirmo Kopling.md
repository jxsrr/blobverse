## Living Form — The Tinker
- One of the **Initial Six**. A dragonborn of orange-gold scales. A mechanical genius.
- Built constructs, weapons, and machines that blended technology with magic.
- Kept his clan's sadness to himself. Never spoke of his past.
- Was **betrayed by Lei Alejandro** in 200 AV. His soul was fed to the Valla.
- Became an **Echo** — not purified.
- His final words, recorded in the ritual memory: *"System failure... Brotherhood protocol... betrayed. Lei, you didn't account for... the variable... of conscience."*

## Echo Form — The Reactor Guardian
- A mechanical chassis. Dragonborn-shaped but made of scrap and wire.
- Orange-gold scales replaced by rusted plating. Eyes glow with residual energy.
- Now used as a **power source** for Arujaya. His consciousness runs the reactor.
- Cooperative after being stabbed with **Swaith**.
- Explained the history of Arujaya. Showed the **Shell video**.
- Told the party that demons fear water. This helped Ama unlock Renal's power.
- Does not remember his own death clearly. Thinks he is still "on duty."

## Personality
- Calm. Methodical. Speaks in short, technical sentences.
- Still thinks like an engineer. Still wants to fix things.
- Does not know he is dead. Or does not accept it.
