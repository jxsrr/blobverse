> [!info] At a Glance
> **Name:** Oldrunn
> **Role:** The cabbage merchant who raised Syl Frost.
> **Where:** Frostheart Village market
> **Status:** Alive

## What the Party Knows
- An old dragonborn woman named **Oldrunn**. Runs a cabbage stall in Frostheart.
- **Adopted Syl** after he hatched from the "Breathstone."
- Cried when she saw Syl again in Session 5.
- Called him "my little ice shard."
- She is not a priest. She is not a warrior. She is a mother.

## What She Offers
- **Kindness.** Free cabbage for Syl and his friends.
- **Shelter** in Frostheart (a small hut behind her stall).
- **Stories** about Syl's childhood.
- **A listening ear.** She will not judge. She will not preach. She will simply listen.

## Current Goal
Live quietly. See Syl succeed. Help the clan heal.
