## What the Party Knows
- Oldest dragonborn in Frostheart. Scales nearly white and translucent. Horns spiral like a ram's.
- Voice is a whisper. Every word is treated as Vythrax's command.
- Led the ritual to hide Syl's identity — or so the party suspects.
- Knew the truth: **Syl is Vythrax's son**.
- After Vythrax was healed, bowed to Syl alongside the entire clan.
- Has named Syl as his successor.

## Current Goal
Heal the clan. Protect Syl. Atone before he dies.
