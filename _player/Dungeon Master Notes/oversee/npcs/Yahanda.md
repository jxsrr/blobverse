## What the Party Knows
- Traveled with the party from Javan Town to Ghuffghuff.
- Quiet, kind, and tired. Spent most of the journey reading.
- Assigned to the new temple in Ghuffghuff — but when the party arrived, the temple priests did not recognize him.
- **Eaten by Echo Sam** on the first night in Ghuffghuff.
- His dried corpse was found in Domido Forest the next morning.

## What the Party Remembers
- He was one of the first victims.
- **No one in the village remembered him** — not even the priests he was supposed to join.
- His death was the party's first clue that something was erasing memories in Ghuffghuff.
