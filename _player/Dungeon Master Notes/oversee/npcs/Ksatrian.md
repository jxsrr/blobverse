## Overview
A wandering knight from the era prior to 01 BV, remembered only in fragmented historical records and weapon lore as the previous bearer of [[oversee/items/Kkana — The Stone Greatsword]] and [[oversee/items/Terrde — The Patient Blade]].

## Historical Notes
- **Bearer of Two Blades:** According to surviving accounts of the legendary weapons, Ksatrian carried both swords during his final travels before entrusting them to another shortly before his death.
- **Forgotten Order:** Little else about his homeland, allegiance, or final resting place has survived in public archives.
