> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Kanselir Agung, leader of the Triase
> **Where:** Padre City / mobile
> **Status:** Alive

## What the Party Knows
- Beloved by the people. Warm smile. Diplomatic.
- Twin brother of **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Lei Darvin Thorne|Darvin]]**.
- Wants to "thicken the Gap" and merge it with Oversee.
- Extremely dangerous. Extremely careful.
- He was the one who ordered the burning of Chenalia — or so the party suspects.
- He is hunting Darvin.

## Current Goal
Claim the Topaju bloodline. Eliminate Darvin. Consolidate power.
