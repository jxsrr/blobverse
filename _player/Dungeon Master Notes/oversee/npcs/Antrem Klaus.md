> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Vya's father. [[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Accarus Grin|Accarus]]'s twin. Son of [[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Hildag Grin|Hildag]].
> **Where:** Maw Desert (with Will of Grin)
> **Status:** Alive

## What the Party Knows
- Twin brother of **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Accarus Grin|Accarus]]**. Both are descendants of **Lei Firage** (fire) through their father.
- Neither has full fire power. Antrem's is especially weak — dormant, almost silent.
- Was a simple shepherd in Elis. Married **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Vreina Arleschia|Vreina Arleschia]]**.
- Left Vya when she was 4 years old, called by [[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Accarus Grin|Accarus]] for a "greater purpose."
- Did not want to leave. Still loves Vya. Not ambitious, not clever — just kind.
- Alive. Living with Will of Grin. Not allowed to see Vya or write to her.

## Current Goal
Be useful to his brother. Survive. See Vya grow up — even if only from a distance.
