> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Owner of Dora's Rhino Stable in Javan Town.
> **Where:** Javan Town
> **Status:** Alive

## What the Party Knows
- A middle-aged dwarf woman. Practical. No-nonsense.
- Runs the largest rhino stable in Javan Town.
- Charges 200 gp for a ride to Padre City (40 gp per person).
- Trustworthy. Reliable. Not cheap.

## What She Offers
- **Rhino transport** to any major Harka city.
- **A safe route** through dangerous territory.
- **A warning** about the road ahead.

## Current Goal
Run her stable. Keep her rhinos safe.
