> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Eldest son of [[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Guanik Smith|Guanik]] and [[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Janja|Janja]]. Brother of [[Huan]] and [[Nila]].
> **Where:** Unknown
> **Status:** Alive — whereabouts unknown

## What the Party Knows
- The eldest of Guanik's three children. Was not at home when the party arrived at Pulau Dima — he was out fishing.
- **Was not on the island** when Will of Grin attacked.
- His siblings survived. His parents did not.
- The party has never met him.

## What the Party Heard
- Guanik spoke of him warmly — "my eldest, Dontol, he is the best fisherman in the family."
- Huan and Nila looked up to him. They have not seen him since the attack.
