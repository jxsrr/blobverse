### Who Geneva Is
- **Geneva Grin** is a Grin woman — a descendant of **Gavaevodata** and **Zargoth**. She carries the eternal smile. She is magic-deaf. She is a survivor.
- She is **not** Hildag's daughter. She is from a **different Grin family** — a smaller, less influential bloodline. She married into the leadership.
- **She was one of Hildag's experiments.** Hildag bred her — carefully, deliberately — as part of her decades-long research into the twin tradition, the Grin bloodline, and the possibility of a True Heir. Geneva does not know this. She thinks she is just a Grin woman who fell in love with a soldier.
- **She met Accarus Grin in Will of Grin.** They grew up together — not as children, but as young adults. They trained together. They fought together. They fell in love slowly, like a fire kindling from small embers.
- They married in a small ceremony, attended only by members of Will of Grin. **Hildag** — Accarus's mother — was there. She smiled. She always smiles.
- **Jarko** was born a few years later. He was their only child. He was not the True Heir. Hildag knew. Geneva did not. She loved him anyway.

### Jarko's Death
- Jarko was killed by **[[Iron Gort|Gort]]** during the raid on the Maw Desert hideout (Session 05). His skull was crushed in front of the party. His blood pooled on the stone floor.
- **Geneva was not there.** She was in another part of the compound, coordinating the evacuation. She did not see her son die. She heard about it afterward. She has not forgiven herself for not being there.
- **Hildag and Accarus knew Jarko was not the True Heir.** They used him as a decoy. They let him die. Geneva **did not know**. She does not know now. She believes her son died a hero — a Grin warrior defending his people. She does not know he was sacrificed by his own grandmother and father.
- If Geneva ever learns the truth — that Hildag and Accarus let Jarko die — she will break. She may hate them. She may forgive them. The choice is hers.

### Geneva and Hildag — The Experiment
- **Geneva does not know she was an experiment.** She thinks Hildag is her mother-in-law. She thinks Hildag accepted her into the family because she loved Accarus. She is wrong.
- **Hildag bred Geneva** as part of her research. She wanted to see if a specific combination of Grin bloodlines would produce a child with different traits. Geneva was the result. She was not the True Heir. Her son Jarko was not the True Heir. But Hildag learned from them. She learned what **didn't** work.
- **If Geneva ever learns the truth**, she will break. She will ask: *"Was any of it real? Did you love me? Did you love Jarko?"* Hildag will answer: *"I loved what you gave me. I loved Jarko. But you were always an experiment."* Geneva will not forgive her.

### Geneva's Role in Will of Grin
- Geneva is **co-leader of field operations** — the Smiling Hand. She works alongside Accarus. She leads missions. She makes tactical decisions. She is the brain; Accarus is the muscle.
- She is **not** the grand strategist. That is Hildag. Geneva executes. She does not design. She is the hand, not the weaver.
- She is **loyal to Accarus**. She loves him. She has loved him since they met in Will of Grin. She would die for him. She would kill for him. She has killed for him.
- She is **loyal to the Grin cause**. She believes in the fight. She believes in the truth. She believes the Grin deserve to be free. She will do whatever it takes.

### Geneva and Vya
- Geneva does not know that **Vya** is Hildag's magnum opus. She does not know that Vya is the True Heir of Lei Firage. She does not know that Hildag bred Vya after Jarko's death — to replace the son she sacrificed.
- Geneva will learn the truth in Season 3. She will be devastated. She will feel betrayed. She will ask: *"Did you know? Did you know she was the one? Did you know Jarko was not?"* The answer will break her.
- If the party tells Geneva the truth about Vya and Jarko, she will not believe them at first. She will deny it. She will call them liars. Then she will investigate. Then she will find the proof. Then she will break.

### Geneva and the Party
- Geneva is **friendly** to the party — to a point. She welcomed them to the Maw Desert hideout. She trained with them. She shared meals with them.
- She is **grateful** to them for trying to save Jarko — even though they failed. She does not blame them. She blames Gort. She blames the Commonwealth. She blames herself.
- She is **suspicious** of Vya. Not hostile. Suspicious. Vya is the new True Heir. Vya is the one Hildag has been watching. Vya is the one who will replace Jarko. Geneva does not know this — but she feels it. She feels that Vya has taken something from her. She is not wrong.
- If the party ever betrays Will of Grin, Geneva will be the first to hunt them. She is not cruel. She is a mother who has lost everything. She will not lose the cause too.

### Geneva's Fate
- Geneva is **not a villain**. She is a mother. She is a widow — in spirit, if not in fact. She is a woman who has lost her son and is trying to find a reason to keep fighting.
- **If she learns the truth about Jarko**, she may leave Will of Grin. She may defect. She may become an NPC ally — or an enemy.
- **If she never learns the truth**, she will fight alongside Accarus until the end. She will die for the cause. She will die for the son she thinks died a hero.
- **The choice is the party's.** They can tell her the truth. They can keep it hidden. They can use it as leverage. The DM should present the options and let the party decide.
