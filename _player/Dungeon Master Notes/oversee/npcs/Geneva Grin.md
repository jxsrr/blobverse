> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Co-leader of Will of Grin. [[oversee/npcs/Accarus Grin|Accarus]]'s wife. [[oversee/npcs/Jarko|Jarko]]'s mother (deceased).
> **Where:** Maw Desert
> **Status:** Alive

## What the Party Knows
- Elf with the eternal Grin smile. Kind but hardened.
- Wife of **[[oversee/npcs/Accarus Grin|Accarus]]**. Mother of **[[oversee/npcs/Jarko|Jarko]]** (deceased).
- Married Accarus after they met in Will of Grin.
- Lost her son to [[oversee/npcs/Lei Iron Gort|Lei Iron Gort]]. Has not recovered.

## Current Goal
Avenge [[oversee/npcs/Jarko|Jarko]]. Protect her people. Support [[oversee/npcs/Accarus Grin|Accarus]].
