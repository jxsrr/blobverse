## What the Party Knows
- A small, pitiful fish-man. Large eyes. Scaly skin.
- Stole food from the Karang Tidur inn because he was hungry.
- Not hostile. Just hungry.
- The party let him go. He is grateful.
- He lives in the **Kuil Lautan** — the Sea Temple in Juto Port City.
- He speaks in simple sentences. He does not remember much about his past.
- He knows a hidden path through the temple's underwater caves.

## What He Offers
- **Information** about the Sea Temple.
- **A friend** in Juto Port City.
- **A secret:** he knows a hidden path through the temple's underwater caves.
- **The way home** — if he can remember it.

## Current Goal
Eat. Survive. Maybe make friends. Maybe remember where he came from.
