## What the Party Knows
- Twin brother of **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Antrem%20Klaus">Antrem</a>** (Vya's father).
- Husband of **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Geneva%20Grin">Geneva</a>**. Father of **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Jarko">Jarko</a>** (deceased).
- Carried **Gustaero** — gave it to Husgus after the Frosthaven mission.
- Wants to collect the Gemhearts. Wants to avenge the Grin people. Wants to find the truth.
- Morally gray. Willing to sacrifice for the cause.
- Killed **Echo Sam** to prevent the party from learning too much.
- Offered the party a place in Will of Grin (Session 03). Gave them seven days to decide.

## Current Goal
Avenge Jarko. Protect the Grin people. Find the truth. Complete his mother's plan.
