> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Ama's adoptive father. Innkeeper at The Golden Sheaf.
> **Where:** Nesia Town
> **Status:** Alive

## What the Party Knows
- Farmer in **Nesia Town**. Adopted **Zure Ama** after he arrived in Oversee 6 years ago.
- Taught Ama to farm. Gave him a home. Loved him like a son.
- Owns **The Golden Sheaf** — an inn in Nesia.
- Married to **Siti**.

## What Happened
- In Session 6, Ama returned to Nesia seeking shelter.
- **Joni called the Commonwealth.** He saw Ama as a "terrorist" — brainwashed by Commonwealth propaganda.
- Held Ama in a controlling hug while waiting for soldiers to arrive.
- The party escaped. Joni was left behind.

## Current State
- Still runs The Golden Sheaf. Under Commonwealth surveillance.
