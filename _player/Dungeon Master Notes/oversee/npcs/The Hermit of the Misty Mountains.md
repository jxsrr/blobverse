## Overview
A solitary old man clad in weathered animal skins who is rumored among high-country trappers and travelers in [[oversee/locations/Elis]] to have lived alone in the upper valleys of the Misty Mountains (*Pegunungan Kabut*) for half a century. Few claim to have seen him from afar, and fewer still have spoken to him.

## Rumors & Lore
- **Guardian of the Peaks:** Locals in the foothills whisper that he is merely the latest in a long line of caretakers watching over a remnant from before the Rapture.
- **Reclusive Nature:** He avoids settlements entirely and reacts with quiet caution toward armed outsiders venturing too high into the mist.
