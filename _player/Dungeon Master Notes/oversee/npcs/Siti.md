> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Ama's adoptive mother. Innkeeper at The Golden Sheaf.
> **Where:** Nesia Town
> **Status:** Alive

## What the Party Knows
- Wife of **Joni**. Co-owner of The Golden Sheaf.
- Adopted **Zure Ama** alongside Joni.
- Kind. Gentle. Made Ama his favorite meals.
- In Session 6, she stood behind Joni while he called the Commonwealth.

## What Happened
- When Ama returned, Siti did not speak. She just watched.
- She did not stop Joni.
- She let them go.

## Current State
- Still runs The Golden Sheaf. Under Commonwealth surveillance.
