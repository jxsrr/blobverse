> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Vya's mother. Wife of [[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Antrem Klaus|Antrem]]. Daughter of the Arleschia family.
> **Where:** Unknown — presumed missing
> **Status:** Unknown — presumed missing

## What the Party Knows
- Minor noble of the **Arleschia** family in Elis.
- Married **[[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Antrem Klaus|Antrem]]**, a simple shepherd. Defied her family's expectations.
- Made a pact with **Kopling** to protect Vya from Will of Grin and from her own awakening fire.
- Warned Kopling: *"If the fire awakens before she is ready, she will burn from within."*
- Was not present when Chenalia was burned. Her fate is unknown.
- May have been taken by whoever actually burned Chenalia.

## Current Goal (Presumed)
Survive. Find Vya. Warn her about the fire.
