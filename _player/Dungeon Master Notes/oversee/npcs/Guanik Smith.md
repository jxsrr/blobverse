> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Old dwarf fisherman who rescued the party
> **Where:** Pulau Dima (home destroyed)
> **Status:** Deceased (killed by Will of Grin)

## What the Party Knows
- Found the party floating at sea after Greyland Castle collapsed.
- Took them to his home on **Pulau Dima**.
- Family: wife **Janja**, sons **Huan** and **Nila**, eldest **Dontol**.
- Told the party the Green Emerald was one of **four treasures of Lei**.
- Named the four heirs: **Wendigo, Firage, Watigi, Roiga**.
- Family was killed by **Will of Grin** (led by [[oversee/npcs/Jarko]]) when the party was away.
- His home was burned.

## Legacy
- His knowledge survives in the party's memory.
- His research was later found in Arujaya — the video of the Shell.
::/pf
