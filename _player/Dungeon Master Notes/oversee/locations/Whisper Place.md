> [!info] At a Glance
> **Part of:** West Harka (remote, near the mountains)
> **Notable People:** Erah (hermit)
> **Status:** Small, quiet, forgotten

## What Matters Here
- A small village in the western mountains. Not on most maps.
- **Janisha Cemetery** — old, overgrown. The names are mostly illegible.
- **Olaf's Hut** — a small, abandoned cabin. It was Olaf's home before he joined the Initial Six.
- **The Barter Market** — a small trading post. Barter only. No coin.
- **The Root and Berry** — an inn. Warm. Quiet. The innkeeper does not ask questions.
- **The Hermitage** — a cave in the mountains. Home of **Erah**, an old hermit. She knows the old stories.

## What the Party Knows
- They have not been here.
- It is mentioned in the source material as a location near Ghuffghuff.

## Current State
- Quiet. The village is dying. Young people leave. Old people stay.
