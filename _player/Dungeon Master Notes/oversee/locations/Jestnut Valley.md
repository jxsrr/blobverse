## What Matters Here
- A remote trading post on the border of the wasteland. Once the largest **Jestnut** (magic peanut) depot in the north.
- Now a **quarantine city** — locked from the inside. A magical blight has infected the population.
- **The Rusty Wheel Gate** — iron gate, scratched from the inside. Pollen in the air (CON save DC 12 or cough for 1 minute).
- **Muat Seratus Market** — mostly collapsed. Sells:
  - **Simple Filter Mask** (5 gp) — advantage on CON saves vs. pollen for 4 hours.
  - **Raw Jestnut Oil** (10 gp) — coat a weapon for +1d4 poison damage (20% chance to drop).
  - **Lucky Coin** (1 gp) — can be used in the Jackpot Machine.
- **The Last Smile Tavern** — dim, lit by green peanut candles. **Milo** (bartender, one hand wrapped in green bandages) knows about **Jestlord** and **Valerius's letter**.
- **Wheel Plaza** — a giant **Jackpot Machine** in the center. Infected people sit in stasis around it, lips moving silently.
- **The Sap Canal** — underground sewers filled with thick green sap. CON save DC 13 or laugh hysterically for 1 minute.
- **Red Polyp Warehouse** — the nest of **Jestlord** (CR 8). Contains:
  - **Greta** (Cycle Clan researcher, half-blind from spores).
  - **Greta's notebook** — reveals Valerius commissioned the Jestnut project as a "shell resistance test."
  - **Jestlord** — a 4-meter-tall peanut-flesh monster with the faces of workers appearing on its surface.
  - **Vaccine** — 1 ampoule (cures 5 people).
  - **Valerius's Letter** — instructions to "test shell resistance to spores."
