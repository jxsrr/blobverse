## What Matters Here
- One of the major continents of **Oversee**, home to sprawling urban centers such as **<a href="/oversee/locations/Padre%20Megalopolis">Padre City</a>**, **<a href="/oversee/locations/Yawning%20Town">Yawning Town</a>**, and **<a href="/oversee/locations/Javan%20Town">Javan Town</a>**.
- Borders the wild continent of **<a href="/oversee/locations/Elis">Elis</a>** along the towering **<a href="/oversee/locations/Arbor%27s%20Observatorium">Pegunungan Arbor</a>** mountain range.
- Strongly influenced by **<a href="/oversee/factions/One%20Voice%20Commonwealth">The Commonwealth</a>** and the noble houses of **<a href="/oversee/lore/Lei%20Bloodline">Klan Lei</a>**.

## What the Party Knows
- Mentioned in historical records and regional news as a seat of Commonwealth urban power and political indifference toward peripheral conflicts.
