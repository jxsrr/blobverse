## What Matters Here
- A twisted forest. Trees grow crooked. Roots reach like hands.
- Fog is constant. The sun never fully penetrates the canopy.
- Strange sounds. Distant laughter. The feeling of being watched.
- **Rayio** (the legendary longsword) was thrown into this forest by Lei Firsa.
- The forest is inhabited by **curious spirits** — not hostile, but dangerous.
