## What Matters Here
- The most prestigious magical university in Oversee.
- **Rector's Office** — Donta's office. Contains a painting of the **Initial Six** and hidden documents.
- **Main Library** — public archives. Useless for deep research (the Commonwealth censors it).
- **Navigation Archive** — locked. Contains old maps and star charts. The door is marked with a brass plaque: **"KANTOR KEPALA ARSIP"** (Head Archivist's Office).
- **The Rotunda of Knowledge** — a mechanical model of the solar system rotates slowly in the center.
- **Secret Basement** — beneath the Navigation Archive. Atlan's hidden base? No — that's in Kota Juto. This basement is **Donta's private collection**.

## What Happened Here
- The party met Donta ([[oversee/sessions/Session 04 — Territorial|Session 04]]).
- Donta showed them the painting of the Initial Six.
- Donta committed suicide here.
- The party was fined 200 gp for trespassing.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Donta's private collection contains a **fragment of the Edisi Law** and a **letter from [[oversee/npcs/Mira Nouveau|Mira]]** dated 12 years ago.
- The letter proves Donta was a Keeper of Secret informant.
- The Navigation Archive's locked door was pickable (DC 15). Inside: a **hidden staircase** leading down to Donta's collection.
- Donta's collection also contains a **map of the Bahtera Bawah Tanah** — a copy of the one Kael has. Donta was preparing to go there before he died.
