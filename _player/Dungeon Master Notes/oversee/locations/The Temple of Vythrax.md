> [!info] At a Glance
> **Part of:** Frostheart Village
> **Notable People:** Elder Jarl, the priests of [[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Vythrax|Vythrax]]
> **Status:** Active. Holy. Changed.

## What Matters Here
- The tallest and most intricate ice structure in Frostheart.
- The entrance is shaped like a dragon's open mouth. Every dragonborn who enters must bow.
- **Hall of the Breathstone** — the innermost chamber. The real Breathstone (Syl's egg) was kept here. It is now cracked and empty.
- **The Nest of Whispers** — a side chamber where young dragonborn meditate for 100 days in total silence.
- **The Frostscale Monolith** — a wall of carved ice listing the clan's lineage for 500 years. Syl's name was erased. It has now been re-carved.
- **The Frozen Legion** — outside the temple, on the path to the Spire. Ice statues of ancient dragonborn protectors. They move when unobserved.

## What Happened Here
- The party discovered the truth about Syl ([[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/sessions/Session 05 — Empty Husk|Session 05]]).
- [[_player/Dungeon Master Notes/oversee/npcs/Vythrax|Vythrax]] was healed here.
- The clan bowed to Syl.
- Syl's name was carved on the monolith.

## Current State
- The temple is in a state of quiet celebration. The clan has a new purpose.
- Elder Jarl is preparing to step down. He will name Syl as his successor.
