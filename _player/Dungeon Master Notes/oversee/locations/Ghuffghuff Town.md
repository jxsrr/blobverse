> [!info] At a Glance
> **Part of:** West Harka
> **Notable People:** Memeng, Cindy, Herman, Faska (deceased)

## What Matters Here
- Village of hunters and cooks. **Fried rice** is not just food — it is identity.
- Famous for the **Gruffgud War** — a centuries-long conflict with neighboring **Gudameng** over whether fried rice or fried noodles are superior.
- Has a **Wind Silo** (an old defense structure). Site of **Gustaero**'s final hiding place.
- Domido Forest borders it. Gordon's hut is nearby.

## What Happened Here
- Echo Sam Hurls terrorized the village ([[oversee/sessions/Session 03 — The Bard|Session 03]]).
- Yahanda, Faska, and many villagers were eaten.
- Agus killed Bud-eg here.
- [[oversee/npcs/Accarus Grin|Accarus]] appeared and made his offer.

## Current State
- Half-ruined. Rebuilding. Cindy (the Mektol orphan) runs the Legendary Noodle Temple in Gudameng now.
