## What Matters Here
- Working port town. Coal dust, forklifts, labor unions.
- **Serikat Bongkar Muat** — stevedore union. Led by **Pak Basir**.
- **Kuil Valla** — neglected. Father Tulus spends more time burying workers than preaching. He knows about dark port operations but stays silent.
- **The Docks** — run by **Sasimo**, a large, rough man with a mechanical hand. Won't talk to strangers unless they say "[[oversee/npcs/Accarus Grin|Accarus]]."
- **The Salty Gill** — tavern. **Junior** (bartender) says: *"People with weird smiles (Will of Grin) often smuggle weapons through here."*
- **Jiopa Cemetery** — the party slept here once.
- **Dora's Rhino Stable** — reliable transport. 200 gp to Padre City.

## What Happened Here
- The party arrived here on their way to Padre City ([[oversee/sessions/Session 04 — Territorial|Session 04]]).
- They learned about the Triase from Commonwealth soldiers.
- They met Pak Andreas here.
