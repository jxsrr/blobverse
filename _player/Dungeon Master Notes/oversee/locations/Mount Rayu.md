## What Matters Here
- A mountain in the Gruffgud region. Fog-covered. Rocky. Unremarkable.
- **The secret project.** In the source material, the Commonwealth was secretly excavating beneath Gunung Rayu.
- They were digging into an **ancient underground city** — a pre-Rapture ruin.
- They used the **Gruffgud War** as a distraction. They kept the war going for centuries to hide the excavation.
- **Gordon** was the only one who knew. He was called "Badak Berisik" (Noisy Rhino) for trying to warn people.

## What the Party Knows
- They learned about the excavation from the carving at Greyland Castle and from Gordon's backstory.
- They have not been to Gunung Rayu.

## Current State
- The excavation is still ongoing. The Commonwealth has sealed the mountain.
