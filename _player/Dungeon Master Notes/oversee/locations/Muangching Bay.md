## What Matters Here
- Fishing town. Wooden houses. Palm trees. Smell of salt and fish.
- **Muangching Market** — fish, nets, and lemonade. **Ibu Sari** runs the lemonade stand.
- **The Docks** — fishing boats and a few merchant ships.
- The party docked here briefly. Discovered there was **no milk** available.
- HVS's psychological need for milk was a major crisis. It made them seem strange to the locals.

## Current State
- Ordinary fishing town. Nothing special. That is why it is useful — the Commonwealth doesn't watch it.
