> [!info] At a Glance
> **Part of:** Above Arbor Mountains (Harka/Elis border)
> **Notable People:** Varek (last lucid resident)
> **Status:** Isolated. Fog-locked.

## What Matters Here
- A colony built on the peaks above the cloud layer.
- Isolated for **200 years** by a permanent fog bank that never moves.
- Originally a **Cycle Clan observatory**. The researchers tried to see **past the Gap**.
- They succeeded. They saw the **Overseer**. The Overseer saw them back.
- Since then, the fog has never lifted. It is a **curtain** they created — and a **prison** that traps them.
- **Varek** is the only one still lucid. He holds the **Kunci Kabut** (Cloud Key).

## What the Party Knows
- The colony is a failed experiment. The Cycle Clan tried to create a **second Valla** here.
- The fog is not weather. It is a magical blindfold.
- Varek warned: *"Valerius thinks he can thicken the Gap. He cannot. The Gap is not a wall. It is a wound."*
