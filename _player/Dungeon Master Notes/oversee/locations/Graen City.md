> [!info] At a Glance
> **Part of:** Rioquan Timur (coastal)
> **Notable People:** Kuur (mute beggar), Nenek Laksmi (blind code-breaker, hidden)
> **Status:** Small coastal town. Carnival atmosphere.

## What Matters Here
- A coastal town in Rioquan Timur. Warm. Tropical. Colorful.
- **Pantai Kocak** — a beach with strange, playful waves.
- **Karnival Serbuk Sari** — a carnival. Always running. Full of strange performances.
- **The Allergy of Art** — the main tent. A surrealist performance. **Suspect X** (a bad carpenter) is here.
- **The House of Mirrors "Reflection of Mortality"** — HVS sees a silhouette of young Shin and the shadow of Zargoyle's horns.
- **The Red Nose Tavern** — a former clown reveals the true identity of the carnival troupe: elite hunters from Elis in disguise.
- **The Bha-Kade Circus Tent** — Valerius is visiting. He meets the party here (briefly, politely).
- **The Silent Docks** — a strange dock where all transactions are done via chalkboards and hand signs. No one speaks. Those who speak are expelled.
  - **Ink & Salt** — sells waterproof chalk and salt-ink.
  - **The Dead Numbers Tavern** — a dim tavern full of former spies and code-breakers. **Ruslan** (bartender) serves.
- **Tears Square** — the central square.
- **The Canal of Forgetting** — a network of canals separating the districts. Only accessible by gondola.
  - Leads to **Nenek Laksmi's Mansion** without further puzzles.
- **Whisper Dam** — a massive concrete wall holding back the sea. The boundary of the city.

## What Happened Here
- The party shipwrecked here after the Abyssal Gorger attack (source material, Session 8).
- They met the carnival troupe. They found the **Vigenère cipher**.
- They met Nenek Laksmi.

## Current State
- Quiet. The carnival is still running. Nenek Laksmi is still alive.
