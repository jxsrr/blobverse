## What Matters Here
- Vertical port city built on cliffs and coral pillars.
- Two districts: **Distrik Pasang** (lower, floods twice daily) and **Tingkat Atas** (upper, wealthy).
- **Observatorium** — Center of Knowledge. Secretly run by Keeper of Secret.
- **Karang Tidur** — inn where the party stayed.
- **Teritip Tua** — diving shop run by Pops (ancient Tortle).
- **Mutiara** — elite tavern. Home of legends.

## What Happened Here
- The party joined Keeper of Secret (<a href="/oversee/sessions/Session%2002%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Wizard">Session 02</a>).
- Found Atlan's hidden base beneath the Observatorium.
