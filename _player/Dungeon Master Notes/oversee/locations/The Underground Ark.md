## What Matters Here
- A massive underground structure built by the **Cycle Clan**.
- It is an **ark** — a vault of knowledge, artifacts, and records.
- It holds the **original copy of the Edisi Law** — the promise that governs the world.
- It also holds the **Cycle Clan archives** — everything Guanik Wiseman ever learned.
- Access requires the **Peta Bahtera** (Map of the Ark). A copy is held by **Rivan** — in his basement in Zundabun. Another copy is hidden in the **Lyceum archives** — in Donta's private collection.
- The entrance is hidden in the roots of Pegunungan Arbor.

## What the Party Knows
- Rivan mentioned it. He has not yet given them the map.
- It is where the truth about the Valla is kept.
- It may also contain a way to **free Simon** without killing him.
