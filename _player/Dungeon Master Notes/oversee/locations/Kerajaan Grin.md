## What Matters Here
- The ancestral homeland of the **Grin bloodline** — a people marked by the **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Grin%20Curse">eternal smile</a>**.
- Following **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Great%20Disappearance">The Great Disappearance</a>**, as Atreus's name faded from public memory, the kingdom gradually fractured and collapsed.

## What the Party Knows
- Historical records from the Initial Six era and accounts from **<a href="/oversee/factions/Will%20of%20Grin">Will of Grin</a>** describe it as a marginalized realm abandoned after Atreus vanished.
