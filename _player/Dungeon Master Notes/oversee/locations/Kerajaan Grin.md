## What Matters Here
- Homeland of the **Grin bloodline** — those marked by the **eternal smile** of **[[oversee/lore/The Grin Curse]]**.
- **[[oversee/npcs/Atreus Grin]]** was a prince of this realm, and **[[oversee/npcs/Grin Qain]]** fled from here seeking a way to break her family's curse.
- Slowly collapsed in the decades following **[[oversee/lore/The Great Disappearance]]**.
