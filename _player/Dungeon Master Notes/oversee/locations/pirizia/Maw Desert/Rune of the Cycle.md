## What Matters Here
- A stone circle in the desert. Worn, ancient, half-buried in sand.
- A **resting place** for travelers. The magic here is old but gentle.
- The party rested here after escaping Arujaya with Livia as hostage.
- **HVS learned the meaning of his name here** (HVS = Human Vessel Shell).
- The runes on the stones tell the story of the Cycle Clan — the founding, the ritual, the replacement of the Valla.
- Anyone who sleeps here dreams of the Cycle.

## Current State
- Undisturbed. Waiting.
