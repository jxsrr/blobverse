## What Matters Here
- A **university** in Rioquan Timur Barat. Dedicated to arcane arts and history.
- **Built on top of the ancient Observatory of Ancient Lake** — the First Observatory, the birthplace of the Cycle Clan.
- The lake was drained. The tower was excavated. The university was built on top of it.
- **The Hall of Time** — a corridor of aging wooden lockers. Contains fragments of memory from former students.
  - **Locker #47 (Crest Muda)** — a blue Grace cloak, old notes, and a wooden sword inscribed "For my brother, Shin." Triggers an illusion of Crest training.
- **Tarakita Arena** — an open oval stadium with a magical safety barrier. No one can die here. Duels are common.
  - Professor Rubi can create an **illusion of Young Zargoyle** (CR 5) for HVS to train against.
- **The Underground Library "Ark of Forgetting"** — a massive underground library. Guarded by **Living Failures** (HVS experiments gone wrong).
  - Contains **Guanik Wiseman's journal**.
  - Contains **HVS-03** — an empty, cracked shell tube.
- **Professor Rubi's Office** — North Tower. Full of artifacts. Rubi knows about the **HVS contract**.
- **The Student Dormitory "Young Nest"** — a lively dorm. **Kael** (a student haunted by demonic voices) will give HVS a **Charm of Silence** if they share their burden.
- **The Graduate Tower – Hall of Memories** — a tower with names of alumni. **Edra and Crest's names** are here. Shin's name is **missing**.
  - A **Sheath of Memory** — inscribed "S.V.H." — gives bonus against demons.

## The Ancient Observatory (Ancient Lake) — Beneath the University
- **The Lake of Ancient Lake** — a dark blue lake that never moves. No wind touches it. It is the **impact crater** of the Shell.
- In the center: a **white stone tower** — the **First Observatory**, built by **Guanik Wiseman** in 1040 BV.
- This is where Wiseman first saw the **Shell** in the sky. This is where he received his vision from beyond the veil. This is where the **Cycle Clan was founded**. This is where the original **Edisi Law** was signed.
- The tower is half-sunken. The lower levels are flooded. The upper levels are intact.
- **The Altar of the Lake** — a black stone altar in the center of the lake, visible only when the water recedes.
- **The Well of the Lake** — beneath the altar. Leads to a submerged cave with:
  - **Wiseman's journal** — the full account of the Shell's descent, the founding of the Cycle Clan, and the creation of the HVS shells.
  - **A cracked Gemheart fragment** — a piece of Emelanica that broke off when the Gemhearts were formed. It can be fused with Emelanica to double its power.
  - **An ancient echo** — a fragment of primordial consciousness. It is tired. It is fading. It will answer one question truthfully.
  - **The first Cycle Clan sigil** — one of three. It resonates with the other two Observatories.

## The Three Observatories
There are **three Observatories** built by the Cycle Clan, each with a different purpose:

1. **The First Observatory — Ancient Lake (Optical/Stellar)**
   - Built by **Guanik Wiseman** in 1040 BV. Dedicated to studying the **stars** — the optical sky.
   - Where Wiseman recorded the Shell video. Where he received the vision. Where the Cycle Clan was founded.
   - **Ruined and forgotten.** Now the site of **Universitas Tarakita**.

2. **The Second Observatory — Juto Port City (High-Energy Astrophysics)**
   - Built centuries later in **Juto Port City** (Rioquan South). Dedicated to **high-energy astrophysics**.
   - Repurposed by **Atlan Nouveau** for **oceanographic research**.
   - Now the **current headquarters of the Keeper of Secret**.

3. **The Third Observatory — Pegunungan Arbor (Radio)**
   - Built in the **Pegunungan Arbor** (Arbor Mountains, Harka/Elis border). Dedicated to **radio astronomy**.
   - **Abandoned.** Connected to the **Cloud Prison**.
