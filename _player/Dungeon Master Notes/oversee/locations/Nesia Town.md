## What Matters Here
- Farming village. Rice fields. Golden wheat.
- **The Golden Sheaf** — the inn owned by **Joni and Siti**, Ama's adoptive parents. It was their home.
- **Hora's Rhino Stable** — runs a dairy farm. Sells "premium" rhino milk at extortionate prices.
- **Koperasi Petani** — farmer's cooperative.
- **Habara Cemetery** — old, quiet, overgrown.

## What Happened Here

- Vya tried to milk the male rhino "Steve" and was kicked for 22 damage.
- They stole the milk. Fled.
- **Joni and Siti betrayed Ama** to the Commonwealth.

## Current State
