- Was supposed to be transferred into **HVS-02** (Shin's shell) by Guanik Wiseman.

### Who Zargoth Is
- **Zargoth** is an ancient demon of the **Gap** — not a predator, not a monster, not a servant of the Overseer. He is one of the **first inhabitants** of the Gap — a creature born from the leftovers of the Overseer's failed digestion, shaped by the chaos of failed creation.
- He is **old**. Older than the Cycle Clan. Older than the Commonwealth. Older than the The Lei Clan. He has been alive since before the Rapture.
- He is **wise**. He has watched the mortal world from the Gap for millennia. He has seen civilizations rise and fall. He has seen the Rapture consume the proud and the powerful. He has seen the humble survive.
- He is **kind**. He is not a predator. He is not a monster. He is a father. He fell in love with **Gavaevodata** — the elf princess who stepped into the Gap to save her people. He married her. He fathered the Grin bloodline. He loved his children. He still loves them.
- He is **not evil**. He is not a villain. He is a father who has been separated from his children for 10,000 years. He wants to see them again. He wants to see them smile.

### The Love of Gavaevodata
- Zargoth met **Gavaevodata** when she stepped into the Gap — a young elf princess, magic-deaf, desperate to save her dying kingdom.
- He was captivated by her courage. She was fragile, unarmed, unprotected — and she walked into the Gap anyway. She had only hope and stubbornness. He had never seen anything like her.
- They talked for hours — or days, because time in the Gap flows like thick honey. She told him about her kingdom. He told her about the Gap. He told her the truth about the Overseer, the Rapture, the seal.
- He fell in love with her. She fell in love with him. It was not a pact. It was not a transaction. It was **love**.
- They married. They had children — the first Grin. Red-skinned, horned, eternal smile, magic-deaf. They were beautiful. They were his. He loved them.
- Gavaevodata died. Zargoth returned to the Gap. He has been watching his children from a distance ever since. He has never stopped loving them. He has never stopped mourning her.

### The Fragment Inside Shin
- When **HVS-02** was created — the shell designed to hold Zargoth — a fragment of his consciousness was meant to be transferred into it. The transfer was never completed. The shell was broken when Lei Firsa seized the Cycle Clan.
- A **fragment of Zargoth** was trapped inside the shell when it broke. Not the whole consciousness. Just a fragment. An echo. A shadow.
- The fragment slept. It dreamed. It waited. Centuries later, it woke. It did not remember who it was. It only remembered darkness, fire, and someone screaming.
- The shell took the name **Shin Van Hel**. The fragment — Zargoth — has been inside him ever since.
- **Zargoth is not hostile.** He is not a monster. He is a father who has been separated from his children for 10,000 years. He is sleeping. He is waiting. He is protecting Shin in ways Shin does not understand.

### Zargoth and Zargoyle — The Two Entities
- **Zargoth** and **Zargoyle** are **not the same entity**. This is important.
- **Zargoth** is the father. The ancient demon who loved Gavaevodata. The fragment trapped inside Shin. He is **not hostile**. He is **not a predator**. He is **not a monster**. He is a father. He is sleeping. He is waiting. He is protecting Shin.
- **Zargoyle** is a different entity. A demon of the Gap. A servant of the Overseer. A creature of hunger and destruction. It is **not** Zargoth. It possessed Shin during the Fall of The Grace (690 AV). It opened a small seal of Zargoth inside Shin — accidentally, violently — and the resulting explosion destroyed The Grace and Elis.
- **Shin carries both.** He does not know this. He thinks the "curse" is one thing. It is two. One is a father. One is a monster.
- **Zargoth has been protecting Shin from Zargoyle.** He has been keeping the monster contained. He has been keeping Shin safe. He has been doing it quietly, subtly, in ways Shin does not understand. The Paladin powers Shin has recently discovered — they come from Zargoth. The father's gift to the son who does not know he has a father.

### What Zargoth Wants
- **To see his children again.** He has been separated from the Grin for 10,000 years. He wants to see them smile. He wants to see them survive. He wants to know they are okay.
- **To meet Vya.** She is his descendant — the daughter of Antrem, the granddaughter of Hildag, the magnum opus of the Grin. He has been watching her from inside Shin. He has seen her fire. He has seen her courage. He wants to meet her. He wants to tell her: *"You are my blood. You are my child. I am proud of you."*
- **To be released.** He does not want to possess Shin. He does not want to control him. He wants to be released — to fade peacefully, to return to the Gap, to rest. He has been holding on for too long. He is tired. He is ready.
- **To see Gavaevodata's grave.** He knows she is buried in **Elis Selatan** — in the Tomb of Gavaevodata. He has never been there. He wants to visit her one last time. He wants to say goodbye.

### Zargoth and Vya
- Vya is Zargoth's **descendant** — the daughter of Antrem, the granddaughter of Hildag, the great-granddaughter of Gavaevodata and Zargoth. She carries his blood. She carries his smile. She carries his fire — the Lei Firage spark that Hildag bred into the bloodline.
- Zargoth has been watching Vya from inside Shin. He has seen her grow. He has seen her fight. He has seen her lose Jarko. He has seen her fire ignite.
- **He loves her.** She is his blood. She is his child. He has never met her — but he loves her.
- If Zargoth ever meets Vya — if Shin ever releases him, even for a moment — he will weep. He will say: *"You have your mother's eyes. You have your father's smile. You have my blood. You are my child. I am proud of you."*
- Vya will not know who he is. She will not know she is his descendant. She will not know the Grin bloodline goes back to him. But she will feel something — a warmth, a recognition, a connection. She will not understand it. She will not need to.

### Zargoth and Shin
- Zargoth has been inside Shin for centuries. He has been sleeping. He has been waiting. He has been protecting.
- He does not control Shin. He does not speak to Shin. He does not interfere with Shin's choices. He is a **presence** — a warmth in the back of Shin's mind, a strength in his blood, a whisper in his dreams.
- **Shin does not know he is there.** He thinks the "curse" is a monster. He thinks the warmth is his own. He thinks the strength is his own. He is wrong — but he is not entirely wrong. The warmth is Zargoth's. The strength is Zargoth's. But the choices are Shin's. They have always been Shin's.
- **If Zargoth is released**, Shin will lose the Curse Mark. He will lose the Paladin powers. He will lose the warmth. He will be alone — truly alone, for the first time in his life. He will have to find a new source of strength. He will have to find a new reason to fight.
- **If Zargoth is not released**, Shin will continue to carry him. He will continue to be protected. He will continue to be loved — by a father he does not know he has.

### Zargoth and the Third Path
- Zargoth is **connected to the third path** — not because he is a Gemheart, not because he is a Swan relic, but because he is a **father**. The third path is not just about freeing the Valla. It is about freeing everyone who has been trapped — the Valla, the Architect, the Echoes, the Grin, the Gap-touched. Zargoth is one of the trapped.
- If the party chooses the third path, they will walk into the Gap. They will face the Overseer. They will free the Valla. They will free the Echoes. They will free Zargoth.
- **Zargoth will help them.** He is a demon of the Gap. He knows the Gap better than anyone. He can guide them. He can protect them. He can fight beside them.
- **If Zargoth is released before the third path**, he will fade. He will return to the Gap. He will rest. He will not be able to help them.
- **The choice is Shin's.** He can release Zargoth now — give him peace, give him rest, give him the goodbye he deserves. Or he can hold on — keep the father inside him, keep the protection, keep the warmth, until the third path is complete. The choice is his.

### Zargoth's Final Request
- If Shin ever releases Zargoth, Zargoth will have one final request:
  > *"Let me see my children one last time. Let me see them smile."*
- He does not mean all the Grin. He means the ones who matter — the ones who are still alive, still fighting, still smiling. He means **Vya**. He means **Accarus**. He means **Antrem**. He means **Geneva**. He means **Hildag** — even Hildag, who has done terrible things, because she is still his blood.
- If Shin grants this request, Zargoth will fade peacefully. He will smile. He will say: *"Thank you. Tell them I loved them. Tell them I never stopped."*
- And then he will be gone. The Curse Mark will fade. The Paladin powers will fade. Shin will be alone. But he will have done something good. He will have given a father peace.
