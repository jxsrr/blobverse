## Living Form — The Druid
- One of the **Initial Six**. A Grin elf — eternal smile, small horns, magic-deaf.
- A druid. He could not cast spells like a wizard — but he could speak to the land, and the land listened.
- Wanted to prove to the world that the Grin were not monsters.
- Was **betrayed by Lei Alejandro** in 200 AV. His soul was fed to the Valla.
- His final words, recorded in the ritual memory: *"You think you win, Lei? This smile... this curse... will haunt you!"*

## His Letter
- Found by **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Hildag%20Grin">Hildag</a>** among his possessions after the betrayal.
- Addressed to *"whoever inherits this Smile."*
- Key line: *"Don't look for who. Look for what has been lost. All keys reside in Emelanica."*
- His last written line: *"Laugh for me. I will perform my last show."*

## What the Party Knows
- He was the first Grin to be accepted by the wider world.
- His disappearance is why the Grin Kingdom fell.
- His letter is the reason **Will of Grin** searches for the Gemhearts.

### Who Atreus Is
- **Atreus Grin** was the **druid of the Initial Six** — a Grin elf with the eternal smile, small horns, and magic-deafness. He was not a warrior. He was not a mage. He was a **druid** — a speaker to the land, a healer of the earth, a guardian of the wild.
- He was **beloved**. He was the first Grin to be accepted by the wider world — not as a curiosity, not as a monster, but as a **hero**. He proved that the eternal smile was not a curse. He proved that the Grin were not evil. He proved that the Gap's blood in his veins did not make him a demon.
- He was **kind**. He healed wounded earth. He called roots to protect villages. He replanted felled forests. He did not fight for glory. He fought for **life**.
- He was **not a fool**. He knew the world hated the Grin. He knew the world feared the smile. He knew the world would never fully accept him. But he believed that if he could just prove himself — if he could just save enough people, heal enough land, protect enough villages — the world would see the truth.
- **He was wrong.** The world did not see the truth. The world saw a Grin who was too powerful, too beloved, too dangerous. The world saw a threat. The world killed him.

### The Ritual — What Atreus Knew
- Atreus was **uneasy from the start**. He did not trust **Lei Alejandro**. He did not trust the ritual. He did not trust the "vortex of energy" that Alejandro was summoning.
- **He felt the roots move.** He felt the earth tremble. He felt something wrong beneath his feet. He told the others: *"Ah, the earth trembles so much! I can feel the roots moving — opening their mouths to welcome our sweet flesh!"*
- **He was laughing.** Not because he thought it was funny — because he was terrified. The Grin smile does not fade. He could not show his fear. He could only laugh.
- **He warned the others.** He told them something was wrong. He told them the roots were moving. He told them the land was hungry. He told them they were walking into a trap.
- **No one listened.** The ritual was already in motion. The circle was already closed. The trap was already sprung.
- **He died laughing.** He did not go quietly. He did not accept his fate. He laughed until the light consumed him. His final words — recorded in the ritual memory — were: *"You think you win, Lei? This smile... this curse... will haunt you! Let history remember me as a monster — but I am the one who laughs last!"*
- **He was right about everything.** He was the third one to see through Alejandro. He was the third one to try to stop it. He was the third one to die fighting.

### Atreus's Letter — The Words He Left Behind
- **Atreus wrote a letter** before the ritual. He did not know he would die. He did not know he would become an Echo. He only knew something was wrong.
- **He gave the letter to Hildag Grin** — his distant relative, the Weaver of Will of Grin. He told her: *"If I do not return, give this to whoever inherits my smile."*
- **Hildag kept the letter.** She gave it to the party in Session 05. It says:
  > *For whoever inherits this Smile.*
  > *We will not return home.*
  > *The stage is set, but our Puppeteer forgot the full script. He stands there with his arrogant green eyes, thinking he can calm the storm with nothing but Wind's Whisper.*
  > *How laughable! He thinks himself the Gatekeeper, when he is no more than a page serving the night's meal to its master.*
  > *And Sam... ah, poor Sam. He keeps humming beside him. His song is off-key, but somehow it feels so sweet.*
  > *Don't look for my body on this earth. I am no longer there. I have become roots, holding these cracked walls from ruin.*
  > *If you want to understand this bitter joke... if you want to know why the world hasn't collapsed today... or how to collapse it later...*
  > *Don't look for who. Look for what has been lost. All keys reside in Emelanica.*
  > *Laugh for me. I will perform my last show.*
  > *— A.G.*
- **The "Puppeteer" in the letter is Lei Firsa** — not Alejandro. The ambiguity is intentional. The party will learn the truth in Season 3.
- **The "arrogant green eyes" are Alejandro's.** Atreus saw through him. He knew Alejandro was the betrayer. He just could not prove it.
- **"All keys reside in Emelanica"** is a clue. Emelanica is the green Gemheart — the time stone. It is the key to the truth. It is the key to the third path.

### Echo Atreus — The Guardian of Chenalia
- **Echo Atreus** has not been met by the party. He is in the **burned ruins of Chenalia** — the same village where Vya was born.
- He is **protecting the village**. He does not know it is already dead. He thinks the burning just happened. He thinks there are still people to save. He thinks he can still make a difference.
- **He is not hostile.** He is not dangerous. He is **lost**. He is **grieving**. He is trying to do the only thing he knows how to do — protect the innocent.
- **Echo abilities** (if encountered):
  - **Eternal Smile:** His smile never fades. Creatures who can see him have disadvantage on Insight checks.
  - **Roots of the Forest:** Can move through plants and trees. Casts *Entangle* at will (DC 15).
  - **Verdant Wave (Recharge 5–6):** 30-ft cone, DC 15 Str save, 6d6 bludgeoning, restrained.
  - **Healing Bloom (1/day):** Heals all allies within 30 ft for 30 HP.
- **What his Echo is doing:** Protecting the burned village. He does not know it is already dead. He thinks the burning just happened.
- **When his Echo meets Vya:** He will recognize her as a Grin descendant. He will ask her to "help him save the village." He does not know the village is already gone. He does not know Vya is his distant relative. He only knows she carries the smile.
- **What his Echo will give the party** (if released with Swaith): he will thank them and fade. Before he fades: *"The Puppeteer is still alive. She is in the Ark. She is waiting."*
- **Reveal schedule:** Echo Atreus will appear in Season 3, when the party returns to Chenalia to find Vreina.

### Atreus and the Grin Curse
- Atreus did not want to break the Grin curse. He wanted to **prove it was not a curse**. He wanted the world to see the Grin as people — not as monsters, not as demons, not as something to be feared.
- **He failed.** The world did not see the truth. The world saw a threat. The world killed him.
- **His death is the reason the Grin Kingdom fell.** Without Atreus — without the hero who proved the Grin were not monsters — the world turned on them again. The Grin were driven out. The Grin were persecuted. The Grin were scattered.
- **His legacy is Will of Grin.** Hildag founded the organization to find the truth about his disappearance. Geneva and Accarus lead it now. They are still fighting for the world he believed in.
- **His Echo is still fighting.** He is still trying to protect the innocent. He is still trying to prove the Grin are not monsters. He is still smiling.
