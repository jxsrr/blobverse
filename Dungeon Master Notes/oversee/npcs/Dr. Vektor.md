- Performed Ama's surgery. Fused Renal into his arm.

- Is studying the **First Tree** for immortality.
- **Lost his daughter Kristine.** Her death broke him.
- Gruff, impatient, but not cruel.
- Warned the party about the toxic smog and sludge in The Depths.

## Current Goal
Solve death. Bring back Kristine. (Or at least, never lose anyone again.)
