## Overview
A former High Chancellor of the <a href="/oversee/factions/One%20Voice%20Commonwealth">One Voice Commonwealth</a> who governed from <a href="/oversee/locations/harka/Padre%20Megalopolis">Padre City</a> roughly five centuries ago, during the era around 200 AV.

## Historical Notes
- **Chancellor of the Old Era:** Surviving public archives list Geldfre among the early High Chancellors who succeeded the founders of the Commonwealth before its modern administrative structure took shape.
- **Faded Legacy:** Outside of official succession rolls in Padre City, little of his personal tenure remains widely known today.

## Who They Were
- The **Kanselir Agung** of the One Voice Commonwealth at the time of the **Initial Six** (200 AV).
- **Not the first Kanselir Agung.** That was **Guanik Wiseman**. Geldfre inherited the role from Guanik's legacy — likely through several generations of successors.
- The **last Kanselir Agung who remembered the original promise** — the promise Guanik made to the Architect. The last one who believed the Commonwealth was a search, not a throne.
- He welcomed the Initial Six to the Commonwealth. He trusted **Lei Alejandro**. He watched them disappear.

## What They Did
- **Welcomed the Initial Six** (200 AV) — greeted them, hosted them, supported their mission.
- **Trusted Lei Alejandro** — believed he was a hero, a descendant of Lei Wendigo, a man who wanted to save the world. He did not know Alejandro was planning to sacrifice his companions.
- **Watched the Great Disappearance** — the Six vanished. He did not know what happened. He suspected something was wrong. He never found proof.
- **Tried to keep the original Commonwealth alive** after the Disappearance. He failed. Lei Firsa's influence was too strong. The Triase was already forming in the shadows.
- **Died in office** — cause unknown. (You may decide: natural causes, assassinated by Lei Firsa's agents, or assassinated by a rival branch of the Klan.)

## Why It Matters
- Geldfre was the **last link to the original promise**. After him, the Commonwealth became the Triase's Commonwealth — and the Triase became Lei Firsa's.
- His death (or removal) was the moment the original One Voice Commonwealth ended. It was not a single event — it was a gradual corruption — but Geldfre's death was the turning point.
- The party has no reason to learn this name — unless they research the Triase's history, or unless they find records in the The Underground Ark.
- **His name appears in `The Triase.md`** as the previous Kanselir Agung at the time of the Initial Six.

## When Party Meets Them
- **Never in person** — dead for 500 years.
- **Possible reveal:** In the The Underground Ark, the original Edisi Law may bear his signature — or his testimony. If Geldfre left a record, it would say: *"I was the last one who remembered. I am sorry I could not hold the line."*
