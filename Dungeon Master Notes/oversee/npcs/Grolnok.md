## What the Party Knows
- A goblin king. Smarter than most goblins.
- Had a daughter who married **Lei Alejandro**.
- She died in childbirth with a stillborn child.
- **Emelanica** was meant for his heirs. He was guarding it.
- Was initially peaceful. Turned hostile when the party mentioned Alejandro.
- Died when the castle collapsed. His body was lost at sea.

## His Legacy
- The Green Emerald (Emelanica).
- The truth about Alejandro's love for a goblin queen.
- The carving in the sea cave.

## Current State
- Dead.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Grolnok did not die in the castle. He **escaped** through a hidden tunnel. He is alive.
- He is now the leader of a small goblin refugee community in the **Gunung Rayu** region. He has been watching the Commonwealth excavation.
- He knows more about Alejandro than anyone alive. He can tell the party the truth about the goblin queen.
- If the party finds him, he will be hostile at first — but he will listen if they mention Emelanica.
- His last words (if he dies): *"Tell Alejandro... I forgive him. He loved her. That is enough."*
