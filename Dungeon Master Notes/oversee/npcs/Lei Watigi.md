### Who Lei Watigi Is
- **Lei Watigi** is one of the four children of **Lei Firsa** and **Simon Greenbottle**. He is the **water heir** — the founder of the **Lei Watigi** line, the water branch of Klan Lei.
- He was born from Simon's stolen essence, manufactured by Lei Firsa to be a vessel for the water element of the Valla's blood. He is not a hero. He is not a good man. He is his mother's son.
- He was **proud**. He was **arrogant**. He was **ambitious**. He was everything Lei Firsa wanted him to be — and nothing Simon would have wanted him to be.
- He carried **Safayer** — the blue Gemheart — before it was swallowed by Vythrax. He was the guardian of the purification stone. He was supposed to protect it. He lost it. He lost it to his own arrogance.

### The Assault — What Really Happened
- Lei Watigi wanted something no one else could have. He wanted a child with a dragon. He wanted a being that combined the power of Klan Lei with the power of an ancient dragon. He wanted a weapon. He wanted a legacy. He wanted to prove he was more than his mother's son.
- He approached **Klan Frostscale** in secret. He offered wealth, influence, access to the Commonwealth's resources. He asked for one thing: their help to have a child with their deity.
- The priests — who had only ever been keepers of ritual without truly experiencing Vythrax's presence — were tempted. They conspired in a scheme that would destroy the sanctity of their own clan.
- They weakened Vythrax with rituals and potions. They made her vulnerable. **Lei Watigi raped her.**
- When Vythrax became pregnant, the priests panicked — not because of what had been done to their deity, but because of the **disgrace** it would bring to the clan.
- The "conspiracy" that is spoken of in the player-facing history is not about the assault. It is about **covering up the assault**. The priests who "leaked the plan" were not trying to save Vythrax — they were trying to expose Lei Watigi and save themselves.
- **Vythrax did not love Lei Watigi.** There was no love. There was no seduction. There was violation.
- The player-facing history says "forbidden love." That is the Cycle Clan's rewrite. The truth is darker. The truth is rape.

### The Safayer — What He Lost
- Lei Watigi carried **Safayer** — the blue Gemheart — before the assault. He was the guardian of the purification stone. He was supposed to protect it.
- During the assault — or shortly after — Vythrax swallowed the stone. She did not know what it was. She did not know what it would do. She only knew she needed to protect her egg. She only knew she needed to protect her child.
- Lei Watigi lost the stone. He lost his legacy. He lost his power. He never forgave himself. He never forgave Vythrax. He never forgave the child.
- **He died centuries ago.** He never faced justice. He never answered for what he did. He never met his son. He never knew what Syl became.

### Lei Watigi and Syl
- Lei Watigi is **Syl's biological father**. He created Syl through violence. He created Syl through rape. He is the reason Syl exists. He is the reason Syl carries the fire of Lei Firage. He is the reason Syl is the True Heir.
- **Syl does not know this.** He thinks Lei Watigi was a Lei descendant who loved his mother. He thinks he was a tragic figure. He thinks he was a "forbidden love." He is wrong.
- **If Syl ever learns the truth** — that his father was a monster, that his mother was a victim, that he was born from violence — he will break. He will weep. He will ask: *"Am I a monster too?"* Vythrax will hold him. Vythrax will say: *"You are not him. You are me. You are my son. You are not a monster."*
- **Lei Watigi is dead.** He died centuries ago. He will never face justice. He will never answer for what he did. Syl will have to find his own peace. He will have to decide what to do with the truth.

### Lei Watigi and Vythrax
- Vythrax has never spoken of Lei Watigi. She has never said his name. She has never told anyone what he did. She carries the shame. She carries the pain. She carries the silence.
- **If Vythrax ever speaks of him** — if she ever tells Syl the truth — she will not weep. She will not rage. She will simply say: *"He was a monster. He is dead. You are not him. You are my son."*
- She does not hate Lei Watigi. She does not have the energy for hate. She is tired. She is dying. She only wants to protect her son. She only wants him to know he is loved.

### Lei Watigi and the Klan Lei
- Lei Watigi is the **founder of the Lei Watigi line** — the water branch of Klan Lei. His descendants are the water mages of the Commonwealth. They do not know the truth about their founder. They believe he was a hero. They believe he was a tragic figure. They believe he loved a dragon and lost her.
- **The Lei Watigi line is tainted.** Not by blood — by the truth. Their founder was a rapist. Their founder was a monster. If the truth ever came out, the line would collapse. The water mages of the Commonwealth would lose their legitimacy. They would lose their power.
- **Lei Watigi's descendants are still alive.** They serve in the Commonwealth. They serve the Triase. They do not know their founder's crime. They may never know. The party may never tell them. The truth may be buried forever.
- **If the party exposes the truth**, it will damage the Lei Watigi line. It will damage the Commonwealth. It will damage the Klan Lei. It may be the spark that starts a revolution — or the match that burns the world.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Lei Watigi **raped** Vythrax. He did not seduce her. He did not love her.
- He is **not** a tragic romantic figure. He is a monster.
- He died centuries ago. He never faced justice. He never answered for what he did.
- Syl does **not** know the truth. He thinks his father was a "forbidden love." He is wrong.
- Vythrax has never spoken of him. She carries the shame alone.
- The Lei Watigi line is **tainted** by the truth. Their founder was a rapist. If the truth ever came out, the line would collapse.
