## What the Party Knows
- Half-elf. Daughter of **Atlan Nouveau** and **Reins**.
- Witnessed her father's disappearance as a teenager.
- Founded **Keeper of Secret** to find the truth.
- Hired the party to find **Gustaero**.
- Knows more than she tells. Has a plan.
- Carries **Kkana** — the stone greatsword, one of the six blades.
- Is **friendly** to the party — but has not told them everything.

## Current Goal
Find the truth about the Initial Six. Protect the world from the Commonwealth. Free the Valla.

### Who Mira Is
- **Mira Nouveau** is the daughter of **Atlan Nouveau** — the oceanographer of the Initial Six — and **Reins** — the woman from Aede who fell into Oversee. She is their only child. She is their legacy.
- She was **fourteen years old** when she witnessed the **Great Disappearance** (200 AV). She saw her father's soul torn from his body. She saw **Lei Alejandro** watch it happen. She has never forgotten.
- She founded the **Keeper of Secret** the year after. She was fifteen. She has spent her entire life building it. She is now in her forties.
- She is **not** a Cycle Clan member. She is not a Cipher. She is an independent agent who founded her own organization. She is not part of a larger structure. She is the structure.
- She carries **Kkana** — the stone greatsword, one of the six blades. She received it through a Keeper of Secret expedition 12 years ago. She has never used it in front of the party. It is the only thing keeping her safe from Commonwealth assassins.

### The Night of the Disappearance
- Mira was **fourteen**. She had followed her father to the altar. She had hidden behind a pillar. She had watched.
- She saw **Lei Alejandro** raise his hands. She saw the vortex of energy. She saw the souls of the Initial Six torn from their bodies. She saw her father scream: *"Tell Mira! This wasn't destiny — this was murder!"*
- She saw Alejandro's face. She saw the satisfaction. She saw the grief. She saw everything.
- She did not speak of it for a year. She could not. She was a child. She was alone. She was terrified.
- Then she founded the Keeper of Secret. She has been fighting ever since.

### The Keeper of Secret
- The Keeper of Secret is **not** a remnant of the Cycle Clan. It is an independent organization — born from Mira's grief, not from Guanik's promise.
- Some former Cycle Clan members **joined** the Keeper after the Cycle Clan was shattered by Lei Firsa. But they did not found it. They joined it. They are members, not leaders.
- Mira is the founder. Mira is the leader. Mira is the heart of the organization.
- The Keeper is a network of scholars, spies, and witnesses. It does not fight. It remembers. It preserves the truth. It waits for the right moment to reveal it.
- The Observatorium in **Juto Port City** is the headquarters. The public face is a center of knowledge. The secret face is the Keeper's archive. Beneath the Navigation Archive is **Atlan's abandoned base** — the eternal flame, the diary, the Twin Shell Amulet.

### What Mira Holds
- **Emelanica** — the green Gemheart, the time stone. She took it from the party after Session 2. She did not ask. She did not tell them. She believes she is protecting them.
- **Rubion** — the red Gemheart, the annihilation stone. She took it from the party after Session 2. Same reasons.
- **Kkana** — the stone greatsword, one of the six blades. She received it through a Keeper expedition 12 years ago.
- **The Twin Shell Amulet** — the key to the **The Underground Ark**. She found it in Atlan's base. She has not told the party. She is afraid of what is in the Ark. She is afraid of what she will find.
- **Atlan's Compass** — she gave it to the party. It points to lost things. She does not need it anymore. She has already found what she was looking for — or so she thinks.

### What Mira Knows
- She knows **Lei Alejandro betrayed the Initial Six**. She saw it happen.
- She knows the **Echoes** exist. She has seen them. She has tried to free them. She has failed.
- She knows the **Commonwealth is corrupt**. She knows the Triase is a lie. She knows the public Edisi Law is a fabrication.
- She knows about the **Gemhearts** — at least, she knows they exist. She holds two of them. She knows they are important. She does not know they are organs of the Architect.
- She knows about the **The Underground Ark**. She has the key. She has not gone there. She is afraid.
- She knows her mother **Reins** came from another world — **Aede**. She knows Reins loved Atlan. She knows Reins died with him. Or so she thinks.

### What Mira Does NOT Know
- She does **not** know that **Guanik Wiseman is alive**. She does not know that the Architect is inside him. She does not know that Guanik is the last living founder of the Cycle Clan.
- She does **not** know that **Lei Alejandro is alive** and hiding in the The Underground Ark. She thinks he is dead. She thinks he died in the ritual. She is wrong.
- She does **not** know that **Lei Firsa** corrupted the Commonwealth. She suspects it. She has no proof.
- She does **not** know that **Simon Greenbottle** is the Valla. She knows the Valla is a prisoner. She does not know his name. She does not know he was forced into the role.
- She does **not** know about the **Swan Trio** — Gustaero, Niette, Alnean. She knows Gustaero exists. She does not know it is part of a larger instrument. She does not know the third path requires it.
- She does **not** know that **Reins is alive** — trapped in the Gap, kept alive by a fragment of Safayer. She thinks her mother died with her father. She has been carrying the grief for 25 years.
- She does **not** know that **Ama** is from the same world as her mother. She does **not** know that Ama is Reins's best friend. She does **not** know that Ama is grieving the same woman she is.
- She does **not** know about **Zargoth**. She does **not** know about **Zargoyle**. She does **not** know about **HVS-01** or **HVS-02**. She does **not** know that Husgus is a shell. She does **not** know that Shin carries a father inside him.

### Mira's Plan
- **Step 1:** Collect the Gemhearts. She has Emelanica and Rubion. She wants Safayer (the party has it) and Topaju (she does not know where it is).
- **Step 2:** Expose Lei Alejandro. She wants the world to know what he did. She wants him to face justice. She does not know he is alive. She thinks he is dead. She wants to expose his memory.
- **Step 3:** Find a way to free the Valla. She does not know how yet. She believes the Gemhearts are the key. She does not know about the Swan Trio. She does not know about the override clause.
- **Step 4:** Dismantle the corrupted Commonwealth. She wants to replace it with something better — something that remembers the original promise. She does not know the original promise. She only knows the Commonwealth is a lie.
- **Step 5:** Protect the world from the Overseer. She knows the Rapture is real. She knows the seal is failing. She wants to find a way to stop it. She does not know the third path is possible.

### Mira and the Party
- Mira is **friendly** to the party. She recruited them in Session 2. She gave them a mission. She trusts them — to a point.
- Mira is **disappointed** that the party chose Will of Grin over Keeper of Secret (Session 4). She understands why. She does not hold it against them. But she is watching.
- Mira will **help** the party if they ask. She will give them information, resources, and shelter. She will not give them Emelanica or Rubion — not yet. She believes she is protecting them.
- Mira will **not** tell the party about the Twin Shell Amulet until she trusts them completely. She has been betrayed before. She is careful.
- Mira is **not** a villain. She is not a hero. She is a woman who saw her father murdered and has spent her life trying to make it right. She is tired. She is afraid. She is doing her best.

### Mira and Reins — The Mother She Lost
- Mira believes her mother **Reins** died with her father. She believes they were both killed in the ritual. She has been carrying the grief for 25 years.
- She does **not** know Reins is alive. She does **not** know Reins is trapped in the Gap. She does **not** know Reins has been watching her from a distance for 500 years.
- **If Mira ever learns the truth** — that her mother is alive, that she has been watching, that she never abandoned her — she will break. She will weep. She will ask: *"Why didn't she come back?"* The answer will be: *"Because she could not. Because she was trapped. Because she has been watching you every day."*
- **The reunion of Mira and Reins is a Season 3 beat.** It will be one of the most emotional moments of the campaign.

### Mira and Ama — The Connection They Do Not Know
- Mira does **not** know that **Ama** is from the same world as her mother. She does **not** know that Ama was Reins's best friend. She does **not** know that Ama is grieving the same woman she is.
- Ama does **not** know that Mira is Reins's daughter. He does **not** know that Reins lived a whole life in Oversee. He does **not** know that Reins had a daughter.
- **If they ever learn the truth** — that they are both grieving the same woman, that they are both connected to Aede, that they are both part of the same story — they will look at each other differently. They will see a reflection. They will see a friend.
- **The connection between Mira and Ama is a Season 3 reveal.** It will change everything.

### Mira's Role in Season 3
- **Session 8–10** — The party reaches the The Underground Ark. They find Lei Alejandro. They learn the truth about the ritual. They learn about Guanik. They learn about the third path.
- **Session 11** — The party confronts Mira. They ask her about Emelanica and Rubion. They ask her about the Twin Shell Amulet. They ask her why she kept secrets. She will answer honestly. She will say: *"Because I was afraid. Because I did not know if you could be trusted. Because I have been alone for 25 years."*
- **Session 11** — Mira learns the truth about her mother. She learns Reins is alive. She learns Reins is trapped in the Gap. She breaks. She weeps. She asks: *"Why didn't she come back?"*
- **Session 11** — Mira learns the truth about Ama. She learns he is from Aede. She learns he was Reins's best friend. She looks at him differently. She sees a reflection.
- **Endgame** — Mira must decide what to do with the Keeper of Secret. She can join the party. She can leave. She can dissolve the organization and start something new. The choice is hers.

### Mira's Fate
- If the party earns Mira's trust, she will give them Emelanica, Rubion, and the Twin Shell Amulet. She will join them. She will help them perform the override. She will help them free the Valla.
- If the party does not earn her trust, she will keep the Gemhearts. She will keep the Amulet. She will continue her own plan. She will fail. She will die. Or she will survive — but she will never know the truth about her mother.
- If the party betrays Mira, she will become an enemy. She will not forgive them. She has been betrayed before. She will not be betrayed again.
- **The choice is the party's.** The DM should present the options and let the party decide.
