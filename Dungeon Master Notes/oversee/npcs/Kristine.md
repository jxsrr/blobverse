## Overview
The late daughter of <a href="/oversee/npcs/Dr.%20Vektor">Dr. Vektor</a>, the renowned cybernetic surgeon of <a href="/Arujaya">Arujaya</a>.

## Background
- **Tragedy in Arujaya:** According to townsfolk who remember the doctor's earlier years, Kristine died as a teenager in a mining accident in Arujaya—a loss from which Dr. Vektor never truly recovered.

%% DM SECRETS %%

## Who She Was
- The **daughter of <a href="/oversee/npcs/Dr.%20Vektor">Dr. Vektor</a>** — the cybernetic surgeon of Arujaya.
- Died in a **mining accident** in Arujaya when she was a teenager.
- Her death broke Dr. Vektor. He has never recovered.

## Her Current State
- **Her body is preserved in a stasis tube** in Dr. Vektor's private lab.
- She is not alive. She is not dead. She is suspended — waiting for her father to find a way to bring her back.
- Dr. Vektor has been studying the **First Tree** for decades, trying to unlock immortality, so he can wake her.

## Why It Matters
- Kristine is **Dr. Vektor's motivation** — his entire arc, his research into the First Tree, his willingness to help the party.
- If the party ever offers to help bring her back, he will do **anything** for them.
- If they suggest letting her go, he will break. Possibly he will turn on them.

## What She Would Want (If She Could Speak)
- She would want her father to **let go**.
- She would want him to live.
- She would want him to stop chasing her ghost and start living his own life.
- But he cannot hear her. She cannot speak from inside the tube.

## When Party Meets Them
- **Never in person** — unless they find Dr. Vektor's lab and open the stasis tube. (If they do, she is still dead — the stasis only preserves her body, not her soul.)
- **Possible reveal:** If the party asks Dr. Vektor about his daughter, he will tell them the truth. This is a **Season 3 emotional beat**.
