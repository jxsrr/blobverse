## What the Party Knows
- An old farmer. Runs the rhino dairy.
- Sells "premium" milk at **extortionate prices** (35 gp/bottle).
- Was the victim of the party's theft in Session 6.
- Has a male rhino named **Steve**. (Steve cannot be milked. The party learned this the hard way.)
- Is **not** a villain. He is just greedy. Or so it seems.

## What He Offers
- **Rhino milk** (expensive).
- **Rhino transport** (150 gp to any nearby town).
- **A grudge** against the party.

## Current Goal
Run his farm. Recover his stolen milk. Survive the Commonwealth's taxes.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### Who Hora Is
- **Hora** is the owner of **Hora's Rhino Stable** — the only dairy farm in Nesia Town that produces rhino milk. He has been running the farm for decades. It was his father's farm. It was his grandfather's farm. It is the only thing he has ever known.
- He is **not a villain**. He is not evil. He is not cruel. He is **greedy** — but his greed is born from desperation, not malice. The Commonwealth taxes him heavily. The war with the Gruffgud villages hurt his trade. The rise of cheaper cow's milk from Harka has undercut his prices. He is barely keeping his head above water.
- He charges **35 gp per bottle** for "premium" rhino milk because he has no choice. He needs the money. He needs to keep the farm running. He needs to feed his family. He is not trying to cheat anyone. He is trying to survive.
- He is **not a bad man**. He is a tired man. He is a man who has been squeezed by the system for so long that he has forgotten what it feels like to breathe. He is a man who has given up on being liked. He is a man who has settled for being tolerated.

### The Farm
- **Hora's Rhino Stable** is a wooden compound on the edge of Nesia Town. It has a barn, a dairy, and a small pasture where the rhinos graze. It is not a large farm. It is not a wealthy farm. It is a **family farm** — the kind that has been passed down for generations, the kind that is slowly dying.
- **Steve** — the male rhino the party tried to milk — is Hora's favorite. He is not a dairy rhino. He is a **breeding rhino**. He is strong, stubborn, and has a temper. He is also, according to Hora, "the best rhino I have ever had." Steve kicked Vya for 22 damage in Session 06. Hora laughed when he heard about it. He is still laughing.
- **The dairy rhinos** — the females — are the ones that produce the milk. They are gentle, patient, and well-cared for. Hora treats them like family. He knows each one by name. He talks to them when he is sad. He sings to them when he is happy. They are the only things in his life that have never let him down.
- **The hidden cellar** — beneath the barn — is where Hora keeps his **stash of premium milk**. He sells it to wealthy clients in **Padre City** — nobles, merchants, and Commonwealth officials who pay handsomely for the rare delicacy. The cellar is hidden behind a false wall. Only Hora knows it is there. The party does not know. If they ever find it, they will find enough milk to feed a small army — and enough evidence to prove that Hora is not as poor as he pretends.

### The Tax Problem
- **Hora is taxed heavily by the Commonwealth.** The taxes are not fair. They are not reasonable. They are designed to squeeze small farmers like Hora out of business, to force them to sell their land to larger, wealthier farms owned by Klan Lei descendants.
- Hora has been fighting the taxes for years. He has written letters. He has hired lawyers. He has pleaded with the local magistrate. Nothing has worked. The taxes keep coming. The debts keep piling up.
- **He is in debt.** He owes money to the Commonwealth. He owes money to the local moneylender. He owes money to his suppliers. He is one bad harvest away from losing the farm. He is one bad month away from losing everything.
- **He does not tell anyone this.** He does not want pity. He does not want charity. He wants to be left alone to run his farm and pay his debts and maybe, one day, die in peace.
- **If the party ever helps him with his taxes** — by exposing the corruption, by finding a loophole, by paying off his debt — he will be **grateful**. He will not say it. He will not show it. But he will remember. He will help them if they ever need him.

### Hora and the Party
- Hora **does not like the party**. They stole his milk. They tried to milk his male rhino. They made a mess of his farm. They are, in his eyes, a bunch of entitled adventurers who think the world owes them a living.
- But he **does not hate them**. He is too tired to hate anyone. He is too busy trying to survive to hold a grudge. He will not help them unless they help him first. He will not forgive them unless they apologize — and pay for the stolen milk.
- **If the party apologizes and pays for the stolen milk**, Hora will forgive them. He will give them a **discount** on future purchases. He will tell them what he knows about the region. He will not become their friend. He will become their **acquaintance**. That is enough.
- **If the party ignores him**, he will continue to be a grumpy old farmer who charges too much for milk. He will not become their enemy. He will not seek revenge. He will simply be there — a reminder that the world is full of people who are just trying to survive.

### Hora's Son
- Hora has a **son** — a young man named **Aris**. Aris is a **Commonwealth soldier** — a private in the **Garda Besi**. He was conscripted into the army when he was eighteen. He has not written to his father in years.
- Hora does not talk about Aris. He does not say his name. He does not look at the old photo on the mantle — the one of a young man in a Commonwealth uniform, smiling, holding a sword. He does not cry. He has forgotten how to cry.
- **Aris is still alive.** He is stationed in **Padre City**. He is not a bad man. He is not a good man. He is a soldier — a cog in the machine. He does what he is told. He does not think about his father. He does not think about the farm. He does not think about anything.
- **If the party ever meets Aris**, they will see a young man who looks like Hora — the same stubborn jaw, the same tired eyes, the same silence. They will see a man who has forgotten where he came from. They will see a man who has become what the Commonwealth wanted him to become.
- **If Aris ever returns to Nesia**, Hora will not speak to him. He will not welcome him. He will not embrace him. He will simply look at him, nod, and go back to work. But he will also, quietly, put out a second bowl at dinner. He will also, quietly, make up a second bed. He will also, quietly, wait for his son to come home.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Hora is **not evil**. He is **greedy** because he is desperate. The Commonwealth taxes him heavily. He is in debt. He is one bad harvest away from losing everything.
- **The hidden cellar** beneath the barn contains a **stash of premium milk** — enough to feed a small army, enough to prove Hora is not as poor as he pretends.
- **His son Aris** is a Commonwealth soldier. Hora does not talk about him. He does not say his name. He is waiting for him to come home.
- **If the party helps him with his taxes**, he will be grateful. He will not say it. He will not show it. But he will remember. He will help them if they ever need him.
- **If the party apologizes and pays for the stolen milk**, he will forgive them. He will give them a discount. He will tell them what he knows. He will become their acquaintance. That is enough.
