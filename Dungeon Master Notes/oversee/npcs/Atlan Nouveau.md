## Living Form — The Oceanographer
- One of the **Initial Six**. A human man. No magic of his own — but the sharpest mind of the group.
- Studied the sea, the currents, the stars. Wrote the first reliable maps of Rioquan.
- Loved **Reins** deeply. She was from another world (Aede). Their daughter is **<a href="/Mira%20Nouveau">Mira</a>**.
- Made a pact with **Keeper of Secret** to protect Mira and her descendants.
- Was **betrayed by Lei Alejandro** in 200 AV. His soul was fed to the Valla.
- His final words, recorded in the ritual memory: *"Tell Mira! This wasn't destiny — this was murder!"*

## What the Party Has Found
- **Atlan's abandoned base** beneath the Observatorium in Kota Juto (Session 02).
- **Atlan's Diary** — four entries, revealing his origin from Aede, his love for Reins, and his pact with Keeper of Secret.
- **Atlan's Compass** — a magical compass that points to lost things. Passed to Mira, then to the party.
- **The eternal flame** in the base — a blue flame that never dies. Inscription: *"Burn here, forever in my heart, the one I love."*
- **The Twin Shell Amulet** — the key to the Bahtera Bawah Tanah. Mira holds it. She has not told the party.

## Personality (from the diary)
- Curious. Methodical. Kind.
- Loved the sea more than he loved most people — but he loved Reins and Mira more than the sea.
- Afraid of being forgotten.

### Who Atlan Is
- **Atlan Nouveau** was the **oceanographer of the Initial Six** — a human man with no magic, no combat skill, and no special powers. He was not a warrior. He was not a mage. He was a **scientist** — a man who studied the sea, the currents, the stars, and the patterns of the world.
- He was **curious**. He was **methodical**. He was **kind**. He was the one who kept the others grounded — the one who reminded them that the world was not just monsters and magic, but also tides and stars and the smell of salt in the air.
- He was **not a hero**. He was not a chosen one. He was a man who loved the sea and loved his family and tried to do the right thing. He died trying. That is enough.
- **He was from Oversee** — born and raised in **Rioquan**, in a coastal city always swept by sea winds. He was not from another world. **Reins was.** He was the one who found her on the beach. He was the one who loved her. He was the one who married her. He was the one who had a daughter with her.

### Atlan and Reins
- **Reins** fell into Oversee hundreds of years before Ama. She landed on the beach below the Observatorium in **Kota Juto**. Atlan found her — wet, confused, speaking a language no one recognized.
- **He took her in.** He gave her dry clothes. He taught her the Rioquan language. He showed her the beauty of this world — the waves, the stars, the warm laughter of fishermen in the port tavern.
- **They fell in love.** She chose to stay in Oversee. She chose Atlan over destiny. She chose love over duty. She knew she was abandoning Aede. She knew the Destroyer God was still out there. She did not care.
- **They married** in a small ceremony on the beach, attended by fishermen and Atlan's colleagues from the Observatorium. Years later, **Mira** was born — a child with her father's brown eyes and her mother's mysterious smile.
- **Atlan built a swing** beneath the Observatorium. He taught Mira the names of stars and the patterns of waves. He read her stories about the sea before sleep. For the first time in his life, he felt **whole**.
- **He did not know Reins was from another world** — not at first. She told him eventually. He did not care. He loved her anyway.

### The Pact with the Keeper of Secret
- Atlan knew the Initial Six were walking into danger. He did not trust **Lei Alejandro**. He did not trust the ritual. He made a **pact** with the **Keeper of Secret** — a secret organization of servants and witnesses who had seen the truth.
- **The pact was simple:** the Keeper would protect Mira and her descendants. In exchange, Atlan would serve as a sailor for them — mapping the world, charting the seas, gathering information. He would be their eyes on the water.
- **He kept his end of the bargain.** He served the Keeper for years. He mapped the coasts of Rioquan. He charted the currents of the Elis Ocean. He wrote the first reliable maps of the world. He was not a hero. He was a **cartographer**. He was a **father**. He was a **friend**.
- **He hid the Twin Shell Amulet** in his base beneath the Observatorium. He left instructions for his descendants: *"If you are my descendant and reading this, the pact still holds. The compass brought you here, as designed. Now, take the compass to the 'Locked Sea Archive.' Show it to the Keeper. They will give you the answers you seek."*
- **Mira found the base.** She found the amulet. She found the diary. She has been holding the amulet ever since. She has not told the party. She is afraid of what is in the Ark.

### The Ritual — What Atlan Knew
- Atlan was **uneasy from the start**. He did not trust **Lei Alejandro**. He did not trust the ritual. He did not trust the "vortex of energy" that Alejandro was summoning.
- **He felt the mana flow reverse.** He felt the energy shift. He realized — too late — that this was not a summoning. This was an **extraction**. Alejandro was draining their souls.
- **He warned the others.** He told them something was wrong. He told them the mana was reversing. He told them they were being drained.
- **No one listened.** The ritual was already in motion. The circle was already closed. The trap was already sprung.
- **He died screaming.** Not for himself — for **Mira**. His final words — recorded in the ritual memory — were: *"Tell Mira! This wasn't destiny — this was murder!"*
- **He was right about everything.** He was the fourth one to see through Alejandro. He was the fourth one to try to stop it. He was the fourth one to die fighting.
- **His last thought** was of Reins. He did not know she was already trapped in the Gap. He did not know she had been watching him from a distance for years. He only knew he loved her. He only knew he would never see her again.

### Echo Atlan — The Father Who Watches
- **Echo Atlan** has not been met by the party. He is in **Kota Juto** — near the Observatorium, or on the beach where he first found Reins.
- He is **wandering**. He is **looking for Mira**. He is **looking for Reins**. He does not know Reins is alive — trapped in the Gap. He does not know Mira is the leader of the Keeper of Secret. He only knows he lost them both.
- **He is not hostile.** He is not dangerous. He is **lost**. He is **grieving**. He is trying to find the family he lost.
- **Echo abilities** (if encountered):
  - **Echo of the Tide:** Can cast *Control Water* at will (DC 16).
  - **Navigator's Sight:** Can see through magical illusions and invisibility within 60 ft.
  - **Echo of the Father:** If reduced to 0 HP, becomes incorporeal, reforms in 1d4 hours.
  - **Healing Waters (1/day):** Heals all allies within 30 ft for 25 HP.
- **What his Echo wants:** To see Mira one last time. To tell her the truth about her mother. To apologize for not being there.
- **When his Echo meets Mira:** He will say: *"My daughter. You have grown so much. I am sorry I could not stay."*
- **What his Echo will give the party** (if released with Swaith): the location of the **Bahtera Bawah Tanah** (the Underground Ark), and confirmation that the **override clause exists**.
- **Reveal schedule:** Echo Atlan will appear in Season 3 — likely in Mira's dreams or in a trance at the Observatorium.

### Atlan and Mira
- Atlan **loved Mira** more than anything in the world. She was his daughter. She was his legacy. She was the only good thing to come out of his time in Oversee.
- **He did not know she would witness his death.** He did not know she would hide behind a pillar and watch. He did not know she would carry the trauma for the rest of her life.
- **If Atlan's Echo ever meets Mira**, he will break. He will weep. He will say: *"I am sorry you saw that. I am sorry you had to watch. I am sorry I could not protect you."*
- **Mira will forgive him.** She always forgives him. He is her father. He is the man who built her a swing. He is the man who taught her the names of stars.

### Atlan and Reins
- Atlan **loved Reins** deeply. She was his wife. She was his partner. She was the mother of his child.
- **He did not know she was trapped in the Gap.** He did not know she had swallowed a fragment of Safayer. He did not know she had been watching him from a distance for years. He only knew he loved her. He only knew he would never see her again.
- **If Atlan's Echo ever meets Reins** — if the party somehow brings them together — it will be a moment of reunion. Two souls who loved each other, separated by death and the Gap, meeting at last. Atlan will say: *"I am sorry I left you."* Reins will say: *"You did not leave. You were taken. I have been waiting."*
