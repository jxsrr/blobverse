## Overview
A wandering knight from the era prior to 01 BV, remembered only in fragmented historical records and weapon lore as the previous bearer of <a href="/oversee/items/Kkana%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Stone%20Greatsword">Kkana — The Stone Greatsword</a> and <a href="/oversee/items/Terrde%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Patient%20Blade">Terrde — The Patient Blade</a>.

## Historical Notes
- **Bearer of Two Blades:** According to surviving accounts of the legendary weapons, Ksatrian carried both swords during his final travels before entrusting them to another shortly before his death.
- **Forgotten Order:** Little else about his homeland, allegiance, or final resting place has survived in public archives.
::/pf

## Who He Was
- A wandering knight. Old. Dying of wounds he could not heal.
- Simon Greenbottle found him on the road. Ksatrian gave Simon two weapons before he died:
  - **<a href="/oversee/items/Kkana%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Stone%20Greatsword">Kkana — The Stone Greatsword</a>** — "for the one who protects."
  - **<a href="/oversee/items/Terrde%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Patient%20Blade">Terrde — The Patient Blade</a>** — "for the one who waits."
- He asked Simon for one thing: *"Do not use them to conquer. Use them to protect."*
- He died in Simon's arms.

## Why It Matters
- Ksatrian is the origin of **two** of the six legendary weapons.
- Simon kept his promise. He never used either sword to conquer.
- **If the party ever asks about the weapons' origins** (with the right NPC), they will hear Ksatrian's name.

## When Party Meets Them
- **Never in person** — he is long dead.
- **Possible reveal:** If the party finds Ksatrian's grave (location TBD by you — maybe near where Simon's story began), they can find his rusted armor and a small carving: *"I gave my swords to a man with kind eyes. I do not regret it."*
- If revealed, no `::pf` needed — it is historical lore the party can learn through investigation.
