## What the Party Knows (from Sam's Memory and Lore)
- He was the **leader of the Initial Six**. Wind mage. Descendant of Lei Wendigo.
- He wielded **Emelanica** (which he later hid in Greyland Castle).
- He loved **Ratu Ghaniba**, a goblin queen. She died in childbirth. Their child died with her.
- He gave **Emelanica** to the goblins in honor of Ghaniba.
- He **betrayed the Initial Six** in 200 AV. He sacrificed their souls to Simon to keep him alive.
- He did not use **Safayer** for purification. That is why the Echoes exist.
- He wrote a carving at Greyland Castle: *"The star has fallen. Six have left us..."*

## What the Party Does NOT Know
- Where he is.
- That he is still alive.
- That he adopted **Livia Vane**.
- That he holds **Topaju**.
- That he has spent 500 years punishing himself.
- That he is guarding the **Edisi Law** in the Bahtera Bawah Tanah.
- That he is waiting for someone worthy to come.

## Current Goal
Guard the Edisi Law. Wait for someone worthy. Atone.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### Who Lei Alejandro Is
- **Lei Alejandro** was born into the **Lei Wendigo** bloodline — the wind branch of Klan Lei. He was a prodigy. He was the strongest wind mage of his generation.
- He was chosen to lead the **Initial Six** — not because he was the most powerful, but because he was the most trusted. He was a hero. He was a descendant of Lei Firsa's line. He was supposed to be the one who saved the world.
- He fell in love with **Ratu Ghaniba** — the goblin queen, the daughter of King Grolnok. It was a forbidden love. The Klan Lei condemned it. He did not care.
- Ghaniba died in childbirth. Their child was stillborn. Alejandro was broken. He gave **Emelanica** to the goblins in her honor. He wrote the carving at Greyland Castle. He tried to move on.
- He was manipulated by **Lei Firsa**. She told him that the only way to save the world was to feed the Valla. She told him that the Initial Six were strong enough to survive. She told him it was necessary.
- He performed the ritual in 200 AV. He sacrificed the Initial Six to Simon. He did not use **Safayer** for purification — because he did not have it. The souls became **Echoes** instead of clean energy.
- He has spent 500 years punishing himself. He did not become a hero. He became a hermit.

### The Bahtera Bawah Tanah — His Prison and His Duty
- After the ritual, Alejandro fled. He could not face the world. He could not face what he had done.
- He found the **Bahtera Bawah Tanah** — the Underground Ark. The Cycle Clan had built it as a seed ship. It was sealed. It was forgotten.
- He entered the Ark. He found the **original Edisi Law** — the promise Guanik Wiseman made to the Architect. He read it. He understood what he had done.
- He has been guarding the Edisi Law ever since. He has been waiting for someone worthy to come. He has been waiting for a chance to atone.
- He holds **Topaju** — the yellow Gemheart. He found it centuries ago. He has kept it safe. He does not want it. He wants to give it to someone who can use it.
- **He is not the final antagonist.** He is a broken man who wants to help. He will offer the party everything he has — if they can prove they are worthy.

### Livia Vane — His Adopted Daughter
- **Livia Vane is not a twin.** She was alone from the start.
- Alejandro found her as a child. He adopted her. He raised her. He told her she was a twin who should have died — not to hurt her, but to protect her. He wanted her to understand why she was alone. He wanted her to know that she was special.
- He did not tell her the truth about himself. She does not know he was the one who betrayed the Initial Six. She does not know he is guarding the Edisi Law. She only knows he was an old man who raised her.
- **Livia believes she was a twin.** She believes her sister was killed. She carries the guilt. She does not know it is a lie.
- If Livia ever learns the truth — that Alejandro lied to her, that she was never a twin — she will break. She may hate him. She may forgive him. The choice is hers.

### Topaju — The Yellow Gemheart
- Alejandro holds **Topaju**. He found it centuries ago in the ruins of a Lei Roiga temple.
- He does not want it. He is not a Lei Roiga. He has no claim to it. He has kept it safe because he knew it was important.
- He will give it to the party if they prove they are worthy. The proof is not combat. The proof is **compassion**. If they can forgive him — or at least understand him — he will give them Topaju.
- Topaju is the yellow Gemheart. It is connected to structure, foundation, and stability. Without it, the continents would drift into the Gap. It is essential for the override.

### The Ritual — What Really Happened
- Alejandro did not perform the ritual to summon a new Valla. He performed it to **feed** Simon.
- He knew the ritual would fail. He knew the souls would not be purified. He did it anyway — because Lei Firsa told him it was the only way.
- He was pressured by the **Lei bloodline** to preserve pure magic. They wanted to keep the Klan strong. They wanted to keep Simon alive. They did not care about the cost.
- He has never forgiven himself. He has spent 500 years punishing himself. He will never forgive himself.

### The Puppeteer — Ambiguity
- Atreus's letter refers to the **Puppeteer**.
- The party assumes it is **Lei Alejandro**. It is not. It is **Lei Firsa**.
- The ambiguity is intentional. The party will learn the truth in Season 3.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Alejandro is **alive**. He is hiding in the Bahtera Bawah Tanah.
- He adopted **Livia Vane**. He is her father. He lied to her about her origin.
- He holds **Topaju**. He will give it to them if they prove they are worthy.
- He has been guarding the **Edisi Law** for 500 years.
- He is waiting for someone worthy. He is waiting for a chance to atone.
- He is not the final antagonist. He is a broken man who wants to help.
- The **Puppeteer** in Atreus's letter is **Lei Firsa**, not Alejandro.

### Season 3 Reveal Schedule
- **Session 8–10** — The party reaches the Bahtera Bawah Tanah. They find Alejandro. He does not attack them. He asks them: *"Do you know what I did?"*
- **Session 11** — Alejandro reveals the truth about the ritual, about Livia, about the Edisi Law, about Topaju. The party must decide what to do with him.
- **Endgame** — Alejandro offers them Topaju and the Edisi Law. He asks them to fulfill the promise. He asks them to atone for him.

### Alejandro's Role in the Endgame
- If the party accepts him, he will join them as an **NPC ally**. He is old, broken, and tired — but he knows more about the Valla than anyone alive. He can help them perform the override.
- If the party rejects him, he will not fight. He will simply fade. He will return to the shadows. He will wait for the next worthy person to come — if there ever is one.
- If the party kills him, Topaju will be lost. The Edisi Law will be lost. The override will be impossible. The third path will fail.
- **The choice is theirs.** The DM should present all three options clearly and let the party decide.
