## What the Party Knows
- A goblin queen. Daughter of **King Grolnok**.
- **Fell in love with Lei Alejandro** — a Lei descendant, one of the Initial Six.
- Married him against the will of many in the Commonwealth.
- **Died in childbirth**. The child was stillborn.
- Her death changed Alejandro. It is why he gave **Emelanica** (the Green Gemheart) to the goblins.
- **The gem was meant for his heirs** — Ghaniba's heirs. That is why Grolnok was guarding it.

## What the Party Heard
- From **Grolnok** (Session 1): *"My daughter married Alejandro. She died with her child. The gem was meant for her children, not for you."*
- From **Sam's memory** (Session 5): Alejandro's last words before the ritual were *"Forgive me..."* — but not to the Six. Perhaps to Ghaniba.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Ghaniba was not just a political marriage. **She genuinely loved Alejandro.** He loved her too.
- The twins tradition was invoked against their child — the Commonwealth forced the pregnancy to be "purified." This is why the child was stillborn.
- **Alejandro never forgave himself.** He gave the Gemheart to Grolnok as penance. He has spent 500 years trying to atone for Ghaniba's death.
- **Her grave** is in Greyland Castle — or it was, before the castle sank. Her body is now underwater.
- **Grolnok survived** — he escaped Greyland with his daughter's remains. He carries her ashes in a small urn.
- **Reveal schedule:** Season 3, if the party finds Grolnok in the Gunung Rayu region.
