## What the Party Knows
- The eldest of Guanik's three children. Was not at home when the party arrived at Dima Island — he was out fishing.
- **Was not on the island** when Will of Grin attacked.
- His siblings survived. His parents did not.
- The party has never met him.

## What the Party Heard
- Guanik spoke of him warmly — "my eldest, Dontol, he is the best fisherman in the family."
- Huan and Nila looked up to him. They have not seen him since the attack.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Dontol returned to Dima Island **two days after** the attack. He found the burned village, his parents' bodies, and no siblings.
- He buried his parents himself. **Janja's grave** and Guanik's grave are small cairns on the beach — he built them alone.
- He has spent the last six months **hunting Will of Grin**. He does not know about **Accarus**, or about the party, or about the Gemhearts.
- He is a fisherman, not a fighter — but he has learned. He has killed two Grin scouts.
- **His current location:** Following a lead into the **Gunung Rayu** region. He believes Will of Grin has a base there. (He is wrong — that is where Grolnok is hiding.)
- **When he meets the party:** He will not trust them at first. He will ask about his siblings. When he learns Huan and Nila are alive in Juto Port City, he will break down.
- **His role in Season 3:** He can become a **guide** to the Gunung Rayu region, or a **messenger** who carries word between Juto Port City and the party.
- **His grief:** He blames himself for not being there. He blames Guanik for befriending the party. He blames the party for bringing Will of Grin to Dima Island.
