## Living Form — The Ranger
- One of the **Initial Six**. A half-orc. Old, gruff, scarred.
- Believed in action over gods. Never trusted the divine — only trusted the people beside him.
- Trained **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Gordon">Gordon</a> "The Noisy Rhino"** as a young ranger. Gave Gordon the **<a href="/Gustaero">Gustaero</a>** bow.
- Was **betrayed by Lei Alejandro** in 200 AV. His soul was fed to the Valla.
- His final words, recorded in the ritual memory: *"You coward, Lei! You don't have the guts to face death, so you steal our breath!"*

## What the Party Knows (from Husgus)
- He was the mentor of Gordon.
- He carried the Gustaero bow before Gordon. It came from his grandfather **Gunnar Guirela** of Wind's Rest.
- His philosophy — "the true guardian is not the one who seeks fame, but the one who protects the community" — was passed to Gordon, then to Husgus.
- His atheism influenced Husgus's own atheism.

### Who Olaf Is
- **Olaf Guirela** was the **ranger of the Initial Six** — a half-orc, old and scarred, with a bow on his back and a chip on his shoulder. He was not the leader. He was not the smartest. He was not the most powerful. He was the **voice of reason** — the one who saw through the lies, the one who questioned the plan, the one who warned the others that something was wrong.
- He was **not a hero**. He was not a chosen one. He was a ranger — a hunter, a tracker, a survivor. He had spent his whole life in the wilderness, protecting villages from monsters, protecting travelers from bandits, protecting the innocent from the things that lurked in the dark. He did not do it for glory. He did not do it for gold. He did it because it was **right**.
- He was **an atheist**. He did not believe in gods. He believed in actions, hard work, and community. He believed in the people beside him. He believed in the bow in his hands. He did not believe in the divine — because he had seen too much suffering, too much injustice, too much cruelty to believe that anything was watching over them.
- He was **skeptical of rulers**. He had seen what kings and lords did to the common people. He had seen the wars they started, the taxes they levied, the lives they destroyed. He did not trust them. He did not serve them. He served the people — the farmers, the hunters, the villagers, the ones who could not protect themselves.
- He was **not a fool**. He knew the world was cruel. He knew the world was unfair. He knew the world was broken. He did not try to fix it. He tried to **protect** it — one village at a time, one person at a time, one arrow at a time.

### The Gustaero Bow — His Inheritance
- Olaf carried the **Gustaero bow** — the neck of the swan, one of the three ancient relics. It was not one of the six blades. It was something older, something separate, something that had been waiting for him.
- He inherited it from his **grandfather Gunnar Guirela** — a fisherman in **Wind's Rest** who found the bow in his fishing net one morning. Gunnar did not know what it was. He only knew it was special. He kept it safe. He gave it to Olaf when he was old enough.
- **Olaf carried it for decades.** He used it to protect the innocent, to hunt the guilty, to fight the darkness. He never used it to conquer. He never used it to kill for pleasure. He used it to protect.
- **He passed it to Gordon** — his student, his friend, his spiritual son. He saw something in Gordon — a kindness, a stubbornness, a refusal to give up. He gave him the bow. He told him: *"It was never mine. It was waiting for you."*
- **Gordon passed it to Husgus.** The chain continues. The bow remembers. The bow chooses.

### Olaf and Gordon
- Olaf met **Gordon** when Gordon was a child — around **190 AV**. Gordon's parents had died. He was living alone in the forest, surviving on roots and berries, hiding from the world.
- **Olaf and Sam Hurls found him.** They were traveling together — not for a great adventure, but for a moment of peace. They found the boy. They took him in. They taught him.
- **Olaf taught Gordon to hunt.** He taught him to read animal tracks. He taught him to set traps. He taught him to survive. He taught him his philosophy — the true guardian is not the one who seeks fame, but the one who protects the community.
- **Olaf gave Gordon the Gustaero bow.** He told him it was not for killing, but for protecting. He told him it had been passed down from his grandfather. He told him it was waiting for someone who understood that.
- **Olaf and Sam left.** They never returned. They died in the ritual. Gordon never saw them again.

### The Ritual — What Olaf Knew
- Olaf was **suspicious from the start**. He did not trust **Lei Alejandro**. He did not trust the ritual. He did not trust the circle. He did not trust the "vortex of energy" that Alejandro was summoning.
- **He warned the others.** He told them something was wrong. He told them the circle was not a shield — it was a **cattle pen**. He told them to draw their weapons. He told them to run.
- **No one listened.** The ritual was already in motion. The circle was already closed. The trap was already sprung.
- **He died fighting.** He did not go quietly. He did not accept his fate. He fought until the end. His final words — recorded in the ritual memory — were: *"You coward, Lei! You don't have the guts to face death, so you steal our breath!"*
- **He was right about everything.** He was the only one who saw through Alejandro. He was the only one who tried to stop it. He was the only one who died fighting.

### Olaf's Echo — The Guardian Who Waits
- **Olaf's Echo** has not been met by the party. He waits near **Gordon's grave** in Domido Forest.
- He is not hostile. He is not dangerous. He is **waiting**. He knows Husgus exists. He knows Gordon is dead. He is waiting for Husgus to come.
- **Echo abilities** (if encountered):
  - **Ghostly Bow:** Spectral bow deals force damage instead of piercing. Ignores cover.
  - **Hunter's Mark (1/day):** Marks one creature as his quarry. Advantage on all attacks against it for 24 hours.
  - **Piercing Shot (Recharge 5–6):** Fires an arrow that pierces all creatures in a 60-ft line. DC 15 Dex save, 6d8 force damage.
- **What his Echo is doing:** Waiting. He knows Husgus is coming. He has been waiting for a long time.
- **When his Echo meets Husgus:** He will say: *"You're Gordon's boy. You have his bow. You have his eyes."*
- **The Three Questions:** Olaf will test Husgus before giving him the boon:
  1. *"Why do you fight?"*
  2. *"Who do you protect?"*
  3. *"What would you sacrifice?"*
- **If Husgus answers truthfully:** Olaf will give him a **spectral arrow** that can pierce any armor once. It is not a legendary weapon. It is not a magic item. It is a gift. It is a father's gift to his son's son.
- **If Husgus lies:** Olaf will attack. He is not cruel. He is not unfair. He is a ranger. He tests those who would carry his legacy.
- **Before he fades (if released with Swaith):** *"Tell Gordon... I'm proud of him. Tell him... I'm sorry I left."*
- **Reveal schedule:** Echo Olaf will appear in Season 3, when Husgus visits Gordon's grave after learning about HVS-01.

### Olaf's Connection to the Swan Trio
- Olaf carried **Gustaero** — the neck of the swan. He did not know what it was. He did not know it was one of the three ancient relics. He only knew it was special. He only knew it was waiting.
- He passed it to Gordon. Gordon passed it to Husgus. The bow is still waiting. The swan is still incomplete. The third path is still possible.
- **If Olaf's Echo ever learns the truth about the Swan Trio** — that Gustaero is part of a larger instrument, that the third path requires all three relics — he will understand. He will say: *"I always knew it was more than a bow. I always knew it was waiting for something greater."*
