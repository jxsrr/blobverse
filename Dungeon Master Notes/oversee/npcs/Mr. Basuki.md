## Overview
A former dockworker in <a href="/oversee/locations/Javan%20Town">Javan Town</a> and the late younger brother of <a href="/Mr.%20Basir">Mr. Basir</a>, leader of the local Stevedore Union (*Dockworkers' Union*).

## Background
- **Harbor Accident:** Official port records list Basuki as having died in a workplace accident at the Javan Town docks prior to the party's arrival in the city, when a heavy cargo crate fell on him during unloading.
::/pf
%% DM SECRETS %%

## Who He Was
- The younger brother of **<a href="/Mr.%20Basir">Mr. Basir</a>** — the leader of the Stevedore Union in Javan Town.
- A dockworker. Not a fighter. A laborer.
- Killed by the Commonwealth for witnessing a **smuggling operation** — a Will of Grin weapons shipment passing through Javan Town.
- His death is why Mr. Basir hates the Commonwealth.

## What Happened
- Basuki saw a Commonwealth officer accepting a bribe from a Grin smuggler.
- He was going to testify. He never made it to court.
- His death was ruled an "accident" — a crate fell on him at the docks. (It did not.)
- Mr. Basir knows the truth. He has no proof.

## Why It Matters
- Basuki's death is **the emotional anchor** of Mr. Basir's character.
- If the party helps Mr. Basir expose the Commonwealth, they will be helping him **avenge his brother**.
- The **hidden ledger** in Mr. Basir's office has Basuki's name as the first entry.

## When Party Meets Them
- **Never in person** — dead before the campaign.
- **Possible reveal:** If the party investigates Basuki's death, they can find his grave in Javan Town's cemetery. The tombstone reads: *"He saw. He spoke. He was silenced. But we remember."*
