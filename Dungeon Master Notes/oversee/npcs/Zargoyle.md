## What the Party Knows (from Shin's Backstory)
- An entity that possessed Shin during the Fall of The Grace (690 AV).
- It destroyed The Grace. It destroyed Elis. It opened a Gap tear.
- It is called **Zargoyle** — the "curse" inside Shin.
- It is not the same as Zargoth. The party does not know this yet.

## What the Party Does NOT Know
- Zargoyle is **not** Zargoth. They are two different entities.
- Zargoyle is a **predator** — a demon of the Gap, a servant of the Overseer, a creature of hunger and destruction.
- Zargoyle is **contained** by Zargoth. Zargoth has been keeping the monster inside — keeping it from consuming Shin.
- Zargoyle wants to **escape**. It wants to consume. It wants to feed.
- If Zargoyle ever breaks free, it will destroy everything Shin loves. It will destroy the party. It will destroy the world.

### Who Zargoyle Is
- **Zargoyle** is a demon of the **Gap** — not a father, not a protector, not a prisoner. He is a **predator**.
- He was born from the leftovers of the Overseer's failed digestion — the same leftovers that birthed the Architect, the same chaos that shaped Zargoth. But Zargoyle is **not** Zargoth. Zargoyle is what Zargoth could have been if he had never loved. Zargoyle is hunger without kindness. Zargoyle is destruction without purpose.
- He is **old**. As old as Zargoth. As old as the Gap. He has been feeding on the crumbs of Ambitio-Ether that leak from Oversee for millennia. He has been waiting for a chance to cross over — to taste the world, to consume the world, to devour the world.
- He is **not intelligent** in the way Zargoth is. He is cunning. He is patient. But he is not wise. He is not kind. He is not a father. He is a **monster**.

### The Fall of The Grace — What Zargoyle Did
- **690 AV.** A massive Gap tear opened in **Elis** — not by accident. Zargoyle opened it. He had been waiting for centuries. He had been searching for a way through. The tear was his door.
- The Grace fought. They held the line. They died.
- **Shin was possessed by Zargoyle.** Zargoyle entered him through the curse mark — the fragment of Zargoth that had been sleeping inside him. Zargoyle did not know Zargoth was there. Zargoyle did not care. Zargoyle wanted a body. Zargoyle wanted to feed.
- Zargoyle used Shin's body to open a **small seal of Zargoth** — accidentally, violently. The seal was a lock on Zargoth's fragment. Zargoyle broke it. The resulting explosion destroyed The Grace. It destroyed Elis. It destroyed everything.
- **Shin was not responsible.** He was a victim. He was a weapon in the hands of a monster. He has spent 690 years believing he was the monster. He is not.
- **Zargoyle was driven out** — not by Shin, not by The Grace, but by **Zargoth**. The explosion of the seal woke Zargoth. The father fought the predator. Zargoth was stronger — not because he was more powerful, but because he had something to protect. He contained Zargoyle. He sealed him inside the curse mark. He has been keeping him contained ever since.

### Zargoyle and Zargoth — The Two Entities
- **Zargoth** is the father. The ancient demon who loved Gavaevodata. The fragment trapped inside Shin. He is **not hostile**. He is **not a predator**. He is **not a monster**. He is a father. He is sleeping. He is waiting. He is protecting Shin.
- **Zargoyle** is the predator. A demon of the Gap. A servant of the Overseer. A creature of hunger and destruction. It is **not** Zargoth. It possessed Shin during the Fall of The Grace. It opened a small seal of Zargoth inside Shin — accidentally, violently — and the resulting explosion destroyed The Grace and Elis.
- **Shin carries both.** He does not know this. He thinks the "curse" is one thing. It is two. One is a father. One is a monster.
- **Zargoth has been protecting Shin from Zargoyle.** He has been keeping the monster contained. He has been keeping Shin safe. He has been doing it quietly, subtly, in ways Shin does not understand.

### Zargoyle's Current State
- Zargoyle is **contained** — not destroyed, not gone. He is sealed inside the curse mark. He is held by Zargoth's will.
- He is **still hungry**. He is still waiting. He is still trying to break free.
- He whispers to Shin in his dreams. He shows him visions of destruction. He tries to tempt him, to weaken him, to find a crack in the seal.
- **He has not succeeded.** Zargoth is stronger. Zargoth is patient. Zargoth is a father. He will not let the monster out.
- **But the seal is weakening.** Zargoth is fading. The fragment inside Shin is not the whole demon — it is only a piece. The piece is tired. The piece is old. It cannot hold forever.
- **If Zargoth is released** — if Shin ever frees the father — Zargoyle will be released too. The seal will break. The monster will be free. Shin will have to find another way to contain him. Or he will have to destroy him.

### Zargoyle and the Third Path
- Zargoyle is **connected to the third path** — but not in the way Zargoth is. Zargoyle is the **monster** the third path must defeat.
- The third path is not just about freeing the Valla. It is about freeing the world from the Overseer. The Overseer is the ultimate predator. Zargoyle is a **miniature version** of the Overseer — a creature of hunger and destruction, a servant of the cosmic appetite.
- If the party chooses the third path, they will walk into the Gap. They will face the Overseer. They will defeat the cosmic hunger.
- **Zargoyle will try to stop them.** He will break free. He will try to consume Shin. He will try to consume the party. He will try to consume everything.
- **Shin will have to face him.** He will have to fight the monster inside him. He will have to destroy Zargoyle — or contain him forever.

### Zargoyle's Weaknesses
- **Swaith.** The soul-thirsting blade can absorb souls. It can release Echoes. It can **drain** a demon. If Shin ever uses Swaith on Zargoyle, he can weaken him. He can contain him. He can destroy him.
- **Safayer.** The blue Gemheart purifies souls. It strips memory, ego, identity. If Safayer is used on Zargoyle, it will **erase** him. It will turn him into nothing. It will destroy him completely.
- **Zargoth.** The father is stronger than the predator. If Zargoth is awake, if Zargoth is willing, he can contain Zargoyle. He can hold him. He can protect Shin.
- **Shin himself.** Shin is not a victim anymore. He is a Paladin. He is a Warlock. He is a man who has been fighting his whole life. He can fight Zargoyle. He can win.

### Zargoyle and the Party
- Zargoyle is **not** an NPC the party will meet. He is not a character with a personality. He is a **force** — a monster, a predator, a thing to be defeated.
- The party will **feel** Zargoyle's presence. They will see the curse mark glow. They will hear Shin mutter in his sleep. They will see the shadows move when Zargoyle whispers.
- They will not **meet** Zargoyle until the third path — until the final battle, until the Gap, until the moment when Shin must face the monster inside him.
- **When they meet him**, he will not speak. He will not negotiate. He will not bargain. He will simply **attack**. He will try to consume. He will try to destroy. He will try to feed.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Zargoyle is **not** Zargoth. They are two different entities.
- Zargoyle is **contained** by Zargoth. He is not destroyed. He is waiting.
- Zargoyle is **still hungry**. He is still trying to break free.
- Zargoyle is the **monster** the third path must defeat.
- If Zargoth is released, Zargoyle will be released too. The seal will break.
- Shin has been **protecting** them from Zargoyle. He has been fighting the monster in his sleep. He has been winning. But it is costing him.

### Season 3 Reveal Schedule
- **Session 8–10** — The party reaches the Bahtera Bawah Tanah. They learn about the two entities. They learn about Zargoyle. They learn he is contained by Zargoth.
- **Session 11** — Shin learns the truth about Zargoyle. He learns he was not responsible for The Grace. He learns he was a victim. He learns he has been fighting the monster his whole life — and winning.
- **Session 11** — Shin learns that if he releases Zargoth, Zargoyle will be released too. He must choose: keep the father, keep the monster contained, keep the pain — or release both, and face the monster alone.
- **Endgame** — Shin must face Zargoyle. He must destroy him. He must contain him. He must find a way to free himself — without freeing the monster.

### Zargoyle's Role in the Endgame
- If Shin releases Zargoth before the third path, Zargoyle will be released too. The monster will break free. Shin will have to contain him alone. He will have to find a new source of strength. He will have to fight the monster without the father's protection.
- If Shin holds on to Zargoth until the third path, Zargoth will keep Zargoyle contained. Shin will have the father's protection. He will be able to fight the Overseer without worrying about the monster inside him.
- If Shin releases Zargoth during the third path, Zargoyle will be released too. Shin will have to face him in the Gap. He will have to destroy him. He will have to use Swaith, Safayer, or his own strength. He will have to win.
- **The choice is Shin's.** The DM should present the options and let the player decide.

### Zargoyle's Final Fate
- If Shin destroys Zargoyle, the curse mark will fade. The Paladin powers will fade. The Warlock powers will fade. Shin will be alone — truly alone, for the first time in his life. He will have to find a new source of strength. He will have to find a new reason to fight.
- If Shin contains Zargoyle, the curse mark will remain. The Paladin powers will remain. The Warlock powers will remain. Shin will still carry the monster. He will still fight it every day. He will still win.
- If Shin frees Zargoyle, the monster will consume him. Shin will become the monster. The party will have to stop him. They will have to kill him. They will have to destroy the thing he became.
- **The choice is Shin's.** The DM should present the options and let the player decide.
