## Overview
A senior instructor of history and arcane artifacts at [[Tarakita University of Heroism]]. Known across campus for her sharp gaze, half-moon spectacles, and neatly pinned grey hair, she maintains a cluttered study in the North Tower packed with antique maps, fractured wands, battered shields, and old relics.

## Reputation
- **Scholar of the Old World:** Students and fellow faculty regard her as one of the university's foremost authorities on pre-Commonwealth artifacts and forgotten historical records.
- **Quiet Demeanor:** Patient and soft-spoken in lectures, though notoriously selective about whom she allows to browse her private archives.
::/pf

## Who They Are
- A professor at **[[Tarakita University of Heroism]]**. Specializes in history and arcane artifacts.
- An **older woman**. Sharp eyes. Glasses (half-moon lenses). Grey hair pinned back.
- Has a small office in the North Tower, filled with artifacts: maps, broken wands, old shields, a partial dragon skull.
- **Secretly a member of the Cycle Clan.** One of the few who survived Lei Firsa's purges.

## What They Know
- Everything about **HVS-01 and HVS-02** — she helped **Guanik Wiseman** document the project 500 years ago. She is one of the last living people who remembers the original shells being created.
- The **Echoes** — she knew some of them personally.
- The **location of the Bahtera Bawah Tanah** — though she has never been inside.
- **Lei Firsa's true identity** — she knows the name "Lei Firsa" and knows she is still alive.

## What They Offer
- **Information** about the HVS project.
- **Access** to the underground library beneath Tarakita (the "Ark of Forgetting").
- **A warning:** *"If you are what I think you are, you must never let Lei Firsa know."*
- If the party tells her about **Shin (HVS-02)** and **Husgus (HVS-01)**, she will break down and cry. Then she will tell them everything.
- She will give them a **Sheath of Memory** (a real magic item — +1 to attack rolls against demons, prevents the first hit from a demon each combat).

## Personality
- Kind. Patient. Tired. Has been waiting 500 years for someone to ask the right questions.
- Does not trust easily. Will test the party's intentions before revealing anything.

## When Party Meets Them
- **Season 3 encounter** — likely at Tarakita University.
- When encountered, wrap her player-facing content in `::pf` markers.
- She will be wary at first, but will open up if the party mentions **the Cycle Clan**, **the Echoes**, or **HVS**.

## Open Questions
- Is she the **last Cycle Clan member** alive? (Yes, or maybe one other — you decide.)
- Does she know **Jenderal Kael** personally? (Likely yes — they were peers.)
- Does she know **Jenderal Bisu**? (Likely yes.)
- Does she know **Varek**? (No — he was a later Cipher.)
- What is her real name? (Professor Rubi is her teaching name. Her true name — from the Cycle Clan — is something older.)
