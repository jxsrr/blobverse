## What the Party Knows
- A nervous, balding man. Runs the inn.
- Offered the party the "Signature Fried Rice Breakfast Package" — then whispered it was "not available" because "the ingredients... are difficult."
- Had a **black-and-white photo** in the lobby: "The Inter-Village Fried Rice Competition, 650 AV."
- The inn menu included "Fried Rice Night."

## What He Offers
- **Rooms** (5 sp/night).
- **Food** (when available).
- **A warning** about the strange things happening in Ghuffghuff.

## Current State
- Unknown. The inn was destroyed in Session 3. He may be dead. He may be in the illusion.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Mr. Bejir was **eaten by Echo Sam** early in the Ghuffghuff incident. He is dead.
- The party did not know him well enough to notice his absence.
- His inn is now a ruin. The photo is still there.
- The photo is a **clue**: "650 AV" refers to the year 650 AV — when the Gruffgud War ended. The "Inter-Village Fried Rice Competition" was the **peace treaty signing ceremony**.
- If the party investigates the photo, they will find a **hidden message** scratched on the back: *"The war is a lie. The mountain is the truth."*
