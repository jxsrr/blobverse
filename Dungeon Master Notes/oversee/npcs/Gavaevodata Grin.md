> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Founder of the Grin bloodline. The woman who married Zargoth.
> **Where:** Died in the Grin Kingdom
> **Status:** Deceased

## What the Party Knows (from Lore)
- An elf princess of the Grin Kingdom.
- Her kingdom was poor, magicless, and constantly attacked.
- She found a manuscript about **The Gap**. She stepped into it.
- She met **Zargoth** — an ancient demon. They fell in love.
- She returned to Grin and married him.
- Their children were the **first Grin** — red-skinned, horned, eternal smile, magic-deaf.

## The Curse
- Gavaevodata's children inherited the smile. It is the mark of the Gap.
- It also made them invisible to the Overseer.

## Current State
- Dead. Her legacy is the Grin people.


%% DM SECRETS %%
- Gavaevodata did not just marry Zargoth. She **made a pact** with him: her descendants would be protected from the Overseer, in exchange for being magic-deaf. This is the origin of the Grin curse.
- The pact is still active. It can be broken by **Vya's fire** — or strengthened.
- Her tomb is in **Elis Selatan** (the Grin Kingdom ruins). No one has found it.
- If Vya finds it, she will learn the truth about the fire inside her.
