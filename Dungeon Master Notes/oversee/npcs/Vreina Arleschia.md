- Minor noble of the **Arleschia** family in Elis.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### Who Vreina Is
- **Vreina Arleschia** is the **daughter of a minor noble family** in Elis — the Arleschia. Her family was not powerful. They were not rich. But they were noble — old blood, old name, old traditions.
- She was expected to marry a nobleman. She was expected to produce heirs. She was expected to be a proper lady.
- She did none of those things. She married **Antrem Klaus** — a simple shepherd. A man with no status, no wealth, no name. She defied her family. She chose love.
- She was **not a fool**. She knew what she was giving up. She knew her family would disown her. She knew her children would not inherit the Arleschia name. She did it anyway. She loved Antrem. She loved Vya. She would do it again.

### The Pact with Kopling
- Vreina knew her daughter was **different**. She saw the sparks in Vya's eyes when she was angry. She saw the flickers of fire when Vya cried. She knew her daughter carried something old — something inherited from the Lei Firage line that ran weakly through Antrem's blood.
- She did not know about Hildag's experiments. She did not know about the twin tradition. She did not know about the True Heir. She only knew that her daughter was in danger — from her own blood.
- She sought out **Kopling** — an old wanderer, dragonborn-blooded, someone her family had known for years. She trusted him. She asked him to protect Vya if she could not.
- She told him: *"If the fire awakens before she is ready, she will burn from within. I have seen it in my dreams."* She did not know if it was a premonition or a nightmare. She did not want to find out.
- Kopling promised. He took Vya. He raised her. He kept her safe. He has been keeping her safe ever since.

### The Burning of Chenalia
- Vreina was **not present** when Chenalia was burned. She was not in the village. She had been called away — perhaps to attend to a family matter, perhaps to meet someone, perhaps to find help for Vya. She does not know.
- When she returned, the village was ashes. Her husband was gone. Her daughter was gone. Her home was gone.
- She did not find them. She did not know if they were alive or dead. She has been searching ever since.
- **Who burned Chenalia is still unknown.** It may have been Will of Grin — but Will of Grin was not in Elis at the time. It may have been the Commonwealth — but why would the Commonwealth burn a remote village? It may have been **Lei Firsa's agents** — seeking to kill Vya before she could become the magnum opus. Or it may have been someone else entirely.
- **Vreina does not know.** She only knows that she lost everything. She has been searching for her daughter ever since.

### Her Current Whereabouts
- Vreina is **alive**. She is being held in a **secret Commonwealth facility in Elis**.
- The facility is the real reason **<a href="/Valerius%20Thorne">Valerius</a>** is "reconstructing" **Chenalia**. The village is a cover for an excavation. The Commonwealth is looking for something beneath the village — something ancient, something connected to the Lei bloodline.
- Vreina was captured by Commonwealth agents — not because she knew too much, but because she was Vya's mother. Valerius wanted a bargaining chip. He wanted leverage over Vya, over Antrem, over Will of Grin.
- She has been in the facility for years. She has been interrogated. She has been tested. She has not broken. She is Vya's mother. She is stronger than she looks.

### What Vreina Knows
- She knows the **Arleschia family** was not as powerless as it seemed. Her ancestors were connected to the Lei Roiga line — not directly, but through marriage, through alliances, through old bonds. She knows the Arleschia name carries a fragment of the earth element. She does not know about Topaju. She does not know that her bloodline is connected to the Gemhearts.
- She knows about **Hildag Grin**. She knows Hildag is the Weaver. She knows Hildag has been watching Vya. She does not know why. She suspects. She does not know the full truth.
- She knows about **Valerius**. She knows he is not what he seems. She knows he is afraid of something — something he does not name.
- She does **not** know about the twin tradition. She does **not** know about the True Heir. She does **not** know about Hildag's magnum opus. She only knows her daughter is in danger — and she has been searching for a way to save her.

### Vreina and Vya
- Vreina loves Vya **more than anything in the world**. She left her with Kopling because she believed it was the only way to keep her safe. She has regretted it every day since.
- She does not know Vya is alive. She does not know Vya is an adventurer. She does not know Vya is the True Heir. She only knows she lost her daughter — and she has been searching for her ever since.
- **If Vreina ever sees Vya again**, she will weep. She will hold her. She will say: *"You grew up. You grew up without me. I am sorry. I am so sorry."* She will not ask for forgiveness. She will not expect it. She will simply be glad her daughter is alive.
- **If Vya ever learns the truth** — that her mother did not abandon her, that she left to protect her, that she has been held captive by the Commonwealth for years — she will break. She will weep. She will ask: *"Why did you leave?"* Vreina will answer: *"Because I loved you. Because I could not let you burn."*

### Vreina and Antrem
- Vreina loved Antrem. She still loves him. She does not know he is alive. She does not know he is in the Maw Desert. She does not know he has been waiting for her — or for Vya.
- **If they ever meet again**, Antrem will weep. He will hold her. He will say: *"I should have stayed. I should have never left."* Vreina will answer: *"You came back for me. You always came back."*

### Vreina and Kopling
- Vreina trusts Kopling. He was the only one she could turn to. He was the only one who would protect Vya.
- She does not know Kopling is **Gzirmo Kopling's** distant relative. She does not know he carries dragonborn blood. She only knows he is kind, and he keeps his promises.
- If she ever sees Kopling again, she will thank him. She will ask: *"Is she safe?"* He will answer: *"She is alive. She is strong. She is your daughter."*

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Vreina is **alive**. She is being held in a secret Commonwealth facility in Elis.
- She made a pact with **Kopling** to protect Vya. She left Vya with him because she believed it was the only way to keep her safe.
- She was **not present** when Chenalia was burned. She did not abandon Vya. She was taken.
- She knows about **Hildag Grin**. She suspects Hildag has been watching Vya. She does not know why.
- She knows about **Valerius**. She knows he is afraid of something. She does not know what.
- She does **not** know about the twin tradition, the True Heir, or Hildag's magnum opus. She only knows her daughter is in danger.
