### Who Guanik Wiseman Is
- **Guanik Wiseman** was born in 1040 BV — or shortly before. He was an astrogeologist, a scholar, a man who watched the sky and asked questions no one else asked.
- He witnessed the **Shell** descending from the sky in 1040 BV. He recorded the video. He saw the hollow body. He understood that the world was not alone.
- The **Architect** spoke to him in dreams. But the dreams were not dreams. They were **direct consciousness transfer** — the Architect entering him, speaking through him, guiding his hand.
- Guanik was not chosen for his wisdom. He was chosen because he was **the only mortal at the time with the right combination of sensitivity, humility, and desperation.** Anyone else would have used the knowledge for power. Guanik wanted to save the world.
- He founded the **Cycle Clan** to maintain the seal and protect the truth.
- He excavated the **Gemhearts**.
- He founded the **One Voice Commonwealth** — not as a government, but as a **search** for the next shepherd, the next Valla, the next person willing to hold the seal.
- He was the **first Kanselir Agung**. He was not a politician. He was a shepherd — a man who had promised the Architect that he would find a way to save the world.
- He signed the **original Edisi Law** — the promise — with the Architect inside him, guiding his hand.

### The Architect Inside Him
- The Architect is **inside Guanik Wiseman**. This is how Guanik received his visions. This is how he knew the truth about the Shell. This is how he wrote the Edisi Law — the Architect guided his hand.
- The Architect is **not** an echo. It is not a fragment. It is the **Architect itself** — the same entity that became the first Valla. It split itself: part of it holds the seal at the threshold; part of it lives inside Guanik.
- This is why Guanik has lived for over a thousand years. The Architect sustains him. He is old, tired, and still holding the promise.
- **If Guanik dies, the Architect fragment inside him will be released.** It will return to the first Valla — the part of the Architect that holds the seal. This will strengthen the seal — briefly. But it will also mean the Architect can no longer guide anyone. Guanik's death is the end of the Architect's voice in the world.
- **The Architect does not control Guanik.** It speaks. It guides. It sustains. But Guanik's will is his own. He has spent a thousand years holding the promise. He has never broken it.

### The One Voice Commonwealth
- Founded by **Guanik Wiseman**. He was the first Kanselir Agung.
- It was originally a **search** — a group of people sworn to find the next shepherd, the next Valla, the next person willing to hold the seal.
- It was **not** a government. It was a **promise**.
- The original **Garda Mawar** was a group of scholars, historians, and witnesses — not law enforcement. They remembered the truth and preserved it.
- The original **Garda Besi** was a group of volunteers — not a military. They protected the search.
- At the time of the **Initial Six** (200 AV), **Geldfre** was the Kanselir Agung. He was not the first — he inherited the role from Guanik's legacy. He was the last Kanselir Agung who remembered the original promise.
- After the Great Disappearance, **Lei Firsa** had been pulling strings for centuries. She had been placing her descendants in key positions. She had been rewriting history. **Geldfre was the last Kanselir Agung who remembered the original promise.** After him, the Commonwealth became the Triase's Commonwealth — and the Triase became Lei Firsa's.

### The Edisi Law — Guanik's Promise
- The original Edisi Law is in the **Bahtera Bawah Tanah** — the Underground Ark.
- It was signed by **Guanik Wiseman**.
- It was written by Guanik and the Architect — the Architect inside him, speaking through his hand.
- It is not a legal code. It is a **promise** — Guanik's promise to the Architect that he would find a way to save Oversee, to find a shepherd, to make one voice.
- It contains the **override clause** — the Architect's Mercy.
- It is guarded by **Lei Alejandro** — alive, hiding in the Ark, waiting for someone worthy.

### Guanik's Current State
- Guanik is **alive**. He is not in the Bahtera Bawah Tanah — he is somewhere else, hidden, waiting. The party may meet him in Season 3.
- He is old. He is tired. He has been holding the promise for over a thousand years.
- He does not know that Lei Firsa corrupted the Commonwealth. He does not know that Simon is the Valla. He does not know that the Cycle Clan was shattered. He has been hidden, waiting, for someone to come.
- When the party meets him, he will ask them: *"Is the promise kept?"* If they say yes, he will smile. If they say no, he will nod. He has waited a long time for the answer.
- He will give them the **original Edisi Law** — if they can prove they are worthy. The proof is not combat. The proof is **compassion**. If they can show him that they understand the promise — that they want to save the world, not rule it — he will give them the Law.
- He will also give them the **Architect's consent** — the final requirement for the override. Without Guanik, the Architect's Mercy cannot be performed. Guanik is the voice of the Architect. He is the only one who can speak for it.
