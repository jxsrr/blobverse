## Overview
An elderly Grin woman who lives alone in a mountain cave known as the Hermitage near [[Whisper Place]]. Small in stature with grey hair, sharp eyes, and the permanent, unmoving smile characteristic of the Grin curse, she rarely descends toward settled roads.

## Reputation
- **The Hermit of Whisper Place:** Travelers passing through the region know of her mostly as a gruff, solitary figure who values her privacy and expects basic courtesy from anyone approaching her cave.
::/pf
%% DM SECRETS %%

## Who She Is
- An **old Grin woman**. Small. Grey-haired. A permanent smile (the Grin curse). Sharp eyes.
- Lives in the **Hermitage** — a cave in the mountains near **Whisper Place**.
- **Daughter of Grin Qain** — Simon Greenbottle's companion, the woman Lei Firsa sacrificed to make Simon the Valla.
- Erah was born shortly before her mother died. **She never met her mother.**
- She knows her mother's name because **Simon** told her — he visited her once, when she was a child. He told her: *"Your mother was the bravest person I ever knew."*

## What She Knows
- The truth about **[[Swaith — The Soul-Thirsting Blade]]** — that it was her mother's blade.
- The truth about **Grin Qain's death** — that she was sacrificed, not killed in battle.
- The truth about **Lei Firsa** — that she was the one who made Simon the Valla.
- The truth about **Simon** — that he is still the Valla, still alive, still suffering.
- She has been waiting her whole life for someone to ask about her mother.

## What She Offers
- **Information** — if the party mentions Grin Qain.
- **A hidden chest** in Olaf's Hut (in Whisper Place) — contains a **letter from Olaf to Gunnar**, a **map to the Wind Silo**, and a **lock of pink hair** (from Grin Qain, Olaf's old companion).
- **A message for Shin** — if the party mentions Swaith, she will tell them: *"My mother's blade was never meant to kill. It was meant to remember. When it is done with you, it will remember you."*

## Personality
- Gruff. Does not trust easily. Does not smile "because she is happy" (she cannot stop smiling — the Grin curse).
- Underneath: deeply sad. She has spent her whole life trying to understand who her mother was.
- If the party treats her with respect, she will become a **loyal friend**.

## When Party Meets Them
- **Season 3 encounter** — if the party travels to Whisper Place, or if they are hunting Grin Qain's remains.
- Wrap her player-facing content in `::pf` markers when encountered.

## Open Questions
- Did she ever try to find her mother's remains? (Yes — but she never found them.)
- Does she know about Simon's true fate? (Yes — Simon told her, when she was a child. She has never told anyone.)
- Is she actually **older** than she looks? (Yes — the Grin age slowly. She may be 200+ years old.)
