## What He Offers
- **Information.** The Dead Numbers is full of former spies. Ruslan knows how to connect people.
- **Code-breaking.** He was a Cycle Clan code-breaker before he retired. He can help with the Vigenère cipher.
- **A safe place** for people in trouble.

## Current Goal
Serve drinks. Stay out of the Commonwealth's reach.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Ruslan is a **former Cycle Clan agent**. He was one of Nenek Laksmi's students. He left the organization when Lei Firsa took over.
- He knows about **Nenek Laksmi's blindness**. He was there when she saw the Gap-touched message.
- He has a **hidden journal** under the bar. It contains the names of every Cycle Clan member who was killed by Lei Firsa.
- If the party asks about Nenek Laksmi, he will lead them to her.
- His real name is **Ruslan Vey**. He uses only his first name to avoid the Commonwealth.
