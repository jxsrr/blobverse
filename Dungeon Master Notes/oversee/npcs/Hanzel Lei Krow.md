## What the Party Knows
- Direct descendant of **Lei Wendigo** (wind element).
- Carries **Emelanica's twin** — no, wait, that's not right. Carries the **authority** of Emelanica, not the stone itself. The stone is with the party.
- Opened the Festival with a speech about the "Triase of the United Commonwealth."
- Political. Smooth. Well-dressed.
- Beloved by the crowds. Hated by Will of Grin.
- Was seen with [[Lei Iron Gort|Gort]] in private after the parade.

## Current Goal
Maintain power. Serve Valerius. Suppress dissent.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Hanzel knows about Valerius's plan. He is loyal but terrified.
- He secretly funds a small faction of Lei Wendigo loyalists who want to depose Valerius.
- He carries a **fragment of Emelanica's power** — the wind aspect. This is why he can control winds, but the Gemheart itself is with the party.
- If Valerius falls, Hanzel will try to take his place.
