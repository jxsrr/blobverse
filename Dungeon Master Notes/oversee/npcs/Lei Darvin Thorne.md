## What the Party Knows
- Twin brother of **[[Lei Valerius Thorne|Valerius]]** (Kanselir Agung).
- Appears senile, rambling about "Arumanehjaya" — a mechanical underground city with gears and automatic rifles.
- Hired the party for 500 gold to excavate the city. The city was real — **Arujaya**.
- Has genuine elemental earth magic. Much stronger than Valerius's.
- Was hidden from the world for over a decade. Seen as "disabled" by Klan Lei standards.
- He is **not crazy**. He is connected to the earth in a way no one else is.

## Current Goal
Find his underground city. Survive. (He does not fully understand the danger he is in.)

%% DM SECRETS %%

### Who Darvin Is
- **Darvin Thorne** is the **younger twin** of **[[Lei Valerius Thorne|Valerius Thorne]]**. He was hidden at birth — not killed. Valerius was kept. Darvin was hidden.
- He is the **True Heir of Lei Roiga** — by blood, not by stone. He carries the **claim** to Topaju. He does not carry Topaju itself.
- He is **not crazy**. His mind is partially fused with the **earth element** — the same element that Topaju governs. He can feel the earth. He can sense the stone. He is drawn to it. He is a living compass pointing to Topaju.
- He found **Arujaya** because he was drawn to it. The city is built on ancient earth-magic foundations. He could feel it. He could hear it. He followed it.
- He does not know he is the True Heir. He does not know Topaju exists. He only knows he can feel the earth — and that it has been calling to him his whole life.

### The Thorne Twins — What Really Happened
- Valerius and Darvin are the **surviving Thorne twins** — one kept, one hidden.
- Valerius was kept. He was raised in the light. He was groomed for power. He became Kanselir Agung.
- Darvin was hidden. He was raised in the shadows. He was told he was "disabled" — a burden, a secret, a shame. He was not told about his bloodline. He was not told about Topaju.
- **Valerius knows Darvin exists.** He has known for years. He has been hunting him. He wants him dead.
- **Why?** Because Valerius believes Darvin carries **Topaju** — the yellow Gemheart, the earth stone, the key to the Lei Roiga bloodline.
- **Valerius is wrong.** Darvin does not carry Topaju. **Lei Alejandro** holds Topaju. Valerius is hunting his brother for a lie.

### The Tragedy of Darvin
- Darvin is not a villain. He is not a hero. He is a man who has been hidden his whole life, told he was worthless, told he was a secret. He found something real — Arujaya — and he wanted to share it with someone. He hired the party because he wanted to prove he was not crazy.
- He is **connected to the earth**. He can feel the stone that is not his. He can sense the Gemheart that is held by another. He is a walking compass that points to something he will never hold.
- If Valerius kills Darvin, **nothing will happen**. Topaju will still be with Alejandro. The earth will not destabilize. Valerius will have killed his brother for nothing.
- **This is the tragedy of Darvin.** He dies for a lie. He is killed for a stone he never carried. He is a pawn in a game he does not understand.

### Darvin's Connection to the Earth
- Darvin is a **True Heir of Lei Roiga** — by blood. He can feel the earth. He can sense the stone. He can hear the deep.
- He cannot control the earth. He is not a trained mage. He is a man who has been hearing a song his whole life — and never learned how to sing it.
- If he ever met **Lei Alejandro**, he would feel the Topaju. He would know it was real. He would know he was not crazy. This is a Season 3 emotional beat.

### Darvin's Fate
- In the original timeline, Valerius kills Darvin in Session 8.
- **If Valerius kills Darvin**, nothing happens. Topaju is still with Alejandro. The earth does not destabilize. Valerius has killed his brother for nothing.
- **If the party saves Darvin**, he can be turned into an **NPC ally**. He is not a fighter. He is not a mage. But he can feel the earth. He can sense the Gemhearts. He can guide the party to places no one else can find.
- **If Darvin survives**, he will want to meet Lei Alejandro. He will want to see Topaju. He will want to know if the song he heard his whole life was real. Alejandro will show him. Darvin will weep. He will say: *"I thought I was crazy. I thought I was broken. I was neither. I was just… listening."*
- **The choice is the party's.** They can save him. They can let him die. They can use him. The DM should present the options and let the party decide.
