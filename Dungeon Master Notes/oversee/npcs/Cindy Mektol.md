## What the Party Knows
- A young girl. Orphaned by Echo Sam.
- Her parents (Herman and Faska) were eaten. She does not fully understand what happened.
- Was brought to Gudameng by Syl.
- Now lives in the Legendary Noodle Temple. The Guo family took her in.
- She is learning to cook. She is surprisingly good at it.

## What She Offers
- **A safe place to rest** in Gudameng.
- **Food** (always delicious).
- **Hope.** She is the only good thing to come out of the Ghuffghuff tragedy.

## Current Goal
Learn to cook. Honor her parents. Survive.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Cindy does not remember her parents. Echo Sam's song erased them from her memory.
- She remembers the party. She remembers Syl carrying her. She does not remember why.
- She has a **natural talent for magic** — specifically, fire magic. She does not know this yet. It will manifest when she is older.
- If the party returns to Gudameng, she will run to them and hug them. She will not let go.
- She has a **small box** under her bed. It contains a photo of her family, a shell from Dima Island, and a **note from Faska** — her mother's last words: *"Be brave, my little star."*
