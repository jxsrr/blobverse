## What the Party Knows
- Gargantuan silver dragon. Legendary protector of Klan Frostscale.
- Disappeared 300 years ago. The clan believed she would return.
- Was actually poisoned by **Safayer** — the blue Gemheart she swallowed to protect her egg.
- **Syl Frost is her son.** The "Breathstone" the clan worshipped was Syl's egg.
- The egg was conceived through a conspiracy involving **Lei Watigi** — a dark, non-consensual act.
- Syl fed Vythrax his own blood. Vythrax recovered. **Returned the full Safayer** to the party.
- Flew back to her spire. Still weak.

## Current Goal
Recover. Protect Syl from a distance.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### Who Vythrax Is
- **Vythrax** is one of the **three surviving ancient dragons** in Oversee. She is the **Silver Wind** — the last silver dragon, the guardian of the cold, the mother of Syl Frost.
- She is **ancient**. She has been alive since before the Rapture. She has seen civilizations rise and fall. She has seen the Cycle Clan form and shatter. She has seen the Klan Lei rise and corrupt.
- She is **not a god**. She is not divine. She is a dragon — powerful, long-lived, and wise — but she is not a deity. Klan Frostscale worships her because they do not know any better. She has never asked to be worshipped.
- She is **dying**. The Safayer poison has been in her veins for 300 years. It is not gone. The poison was slowed when Syl healed her — but it was not cured. She is still weak. She will not survive the campaign.

### The Assault — The Truth of Syl's Conception
- **Lei Watigi** — the water heir of Klan Lei, the son of Lei Firsa and Simon — wanted something no one else could have. He wanted a child with a dragon.
- He approached **Klan Frostscale** in secret. He offered wealth, influence, access to the Commonwealth's resources. He asked for one thing: their help to have a child with their deity.
- The priests — who had only ever been keepers of ritual without truly experiencing Vythrax's presence — were tempted. They conspired in a scheme that would destroy the sanctity of their own clan.
- They weakened Vythrax with rituals and potions. They made her vulnerable. **Lei Watigi raped her.**
- When Vythrax became pregnant, the priests panicked — not because of what had been done to their deity, but because of the **disgrace** it would bring to the clan.
- The "conspiracy" that is spoken of in the player-facing history is not about the assault. It is about **covering up the assault**. The priests who "leaked the plan" were not trying to save Vythrax — they were trying to expose Lei Watigi and save themselves.
- **Vythrax did not love Lei Watigi.** There was no love. There was no seduction. There was violation.
- The player-facing history says "forbidden love." That is the Cycle Clan's rewrite. The truth is darker. The truth is rape.

### The Safayer — What She Swallowed
- Vythrax swallowed the **full Safayer** — the blue Gemheart, the purification stone, one of the four organs of the Architect. It was not a fragment. It was the entire stone.
- She swallowed it to protect her egg. She knew the stone had extraordinary purifying power. She hoped it would create a magical resonance that would shield the embryo — an invisible barrier that would keep it safe and slow its development until the right time.
- She did not know it would poison her. She did not know the stone would burn in her veins for 300 years. She did not know it would nearly kill her.
- She also wanted to carry something that reminded her of what had happened. She wanted to punish herself. She swallowed the stone because she could not bear to let it go.

### The Poison in Syl's Blood
- When Syl hatched — hundreds of years later — the Safayer had been in Vythrax's body for centuries. The stone had poisoned Vythrax. The poison had passed into the egg. The poison had passed into Syl.
- Syl carries a **fragment of Safayer's power** in his blood — not the full stone, but a residue. It is what makes his blood a universal cure. It is also what is slowly killing him.
- Vythrax did not know this would happen. She thought the stone would protect the egg. She did not know it would poison her child.
- **She has never forgiven herself.** She swallowed the stone to protect her son. She gave her son a death sentence instead.

### The Return of Safayer
- In Session 05, Syl healed Vythrax by feeding her his own blood. The blood contained the Safayer residue — the fragment of the stone that had been killing Syl. It was enough to wake Vythrax, to clear the purple veins, to bring her back.
- **Vythrax returned the full Safayer to the party.** She coughed it up — a glowing blue stone, still pulsing with the Architect's memory. She did not want it anymore. She had carried it for 300 years. She was done.
- The party now carries the full Safayer. Vythrax carries only the scars — the memory of the poison, the memory of the assault, the memory of the son she could not protect.

### Vythrax's Current State
- Vythrax is **alive**. She is **weak**. She is **dying**.
- The Safayer poison has been slowed — but not cured. The stone was removed, but the damage was done. The poison has been in her veins for 300 years. It has scarred her. It has weakened her. It will kill her.
- She will not survive the campaign. She has months, perhaps a year. She does not know this. She suspects it. She has made her peace with it.
- **If the party calls on her**, she will fight. She will come. She will protect Syl. She will protect the party. But it will cost her. It will shorten her life. She will do it anyway. She is a mother. She will die for her son.

### Vythrax and Syl
- Vythrax **loves Syl** more than anything in the world. She did not raise him. She could not. She was poisoned, weakened, trapped in the Spire. But she watched him from a distance. She felt him through the Safayer resonance. She knew her son was alive. She knew her son was strong.
- **She does not blame Syl for what happened.** She does not blame him for the assault. She does not blame him for the poison. She blames herself. She swallowed the stone. She made the choice. She gave her son a death sentence.
- **If Syl ever needs her**, Vythrax will come. She will fly from the Spire. She will fight whatever threatens her son. She will die for him. She will die gladly.
- **If Syl ever asks about his father**, Vythrax will not speak. She will not tell him about Lei Watigi. She will not tell him about the assault. She will say only: *"You are my son. That is enough."*

### Vythrax and the Frozen Legion
- The **Frozen Legion** — hundreds of ice statues lining the path to the Spire — are **real dragonborn**, frozen by Vythrax 1,000 years ago to stop a Gap invasion.
- They are not dead. They are waiting. They will thaw when **Vythrax dies** or when **the Gap opens**.
- Vythrax knows this. She has been keeping them frozen. She has been keeping them safe. She does not want them to wake. They will be confused. They will be angry. They will not recognize the modern clan.
- **If Vythrax dies**, the Legion will thaw. The party will have to deal with them. They may be allies. They may be enemies. The choice will be theirs.

### Vythrax's Fate
- Vythrax **will not survive the campaign**. She is dying. The Safayer poison has done too much damage. She has months, perhaps a year.
- She will die protecting Syl. She will die protecting the party. She will die in the final battle — or before it. The DM should decide when.
- **Her death will be one of the most emotional moments of the campaign.** Syl will break. The party will break. Klan Frostscale will mourn. The Frozen Legion will thaw. The world will be different.
- **She will not be forgotten.** She is the Silver Wind. She is the mother of Syl Frost. She is the last silver dragon. She will be remembered.
