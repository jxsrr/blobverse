### Who Gordon Is
- **Gordon** was a **dwarf ranger** — a hunter, a tracker, a survivor. He was born in **~180 AV** in the mountains of Harka. He was not a hero. He was not a legend. He was a dwarf who loved the forest and the quiet.
- He was **over 500 years old** when he died. He was kept alive by the **Gustaero bow** — a side effect no one understood. The bow did not want its wielder to die. It held on. It protected. It slowed his aging to a crawl.
- He was a **former member of the Cycle Clan**. He left because he disagreed with the ritual of replacement. He believed the Valla should not be sacrificed. He believed there had to be another way. He was right — but he never found it.
- He was **not an atheist** — not exactly. He was **skeptical**. He believed in actions, hard work, and community. He believed in the people beside him. He believed in the forest. He did not believe in gods. He did not believe in rulers. He believed in **people**.
- He was **called "Badak Berisik"** — the Noisy Rhino — because he never stopped shouting about the truth. He never stopped trying to expose the Commonwealth's secret project at **Gunung Rayu**. He never stopped trying to warn the villages about the Gruffgud War. He never stopped being a pain in the ass to the people in power.
- **He was right about everything.** He was not mad. He was not demented. He was the only person in the region who saw the truth — and he was silenced, exiled, and mocked for it.

### The Childhood — Olaf and Sam
- When Gordon was about **ten years old** — around **190 AV** — his parents died in a hunting accident. He was left alone in the forest, living in a rundown hut, surviving on roots and berries and whatever he could catch.
- **Olaf Guirela** and **Sam Hurls** found him. They were traveling together — not for a great adventure, but for a moment of peace from the noise of the world. They arrived at **Domido Forest**, found the hut, found the boy.
- **Olaf** taught Gordon how to hunt, how to read animal tracks, how to set traps. He taught him his philosophy of life: **true strength does not come from magic or magical weapons, but from relentless training, discipline that never slackens, and a clean heart.** He also taught Gordon to be **skeptical of gods and rulers** — because they often had their own interests, interests that were not always good for the common people.
- **Sam** taught Gordon about **lighter things**. He taught him music. He taught him a particular melody — notes that were cheerful yet slightly melancholic, which were said to be able to open doors between dimensions. Sam laughed as he taught it, saying that if Gordon ever found himself in trouble, he could sing the melody and maybe someone would hear — even Sam himself, from wherever he was.
- **Gordon memorized the melody.** He sang it every night before sleep. He sang it when he was lonely. He sang it when he was afraid. He sang it when he was happy. He never forgot it.
- **Before leaving, Olaf gave Gordon the Gustaero bow.** He told him it was **not for killing, but for protecting**. He told him it had been passed down from his grandfather Gunnar, who found it in his fishing net. He told him it was waiting for someone who understood that.
- **Gordon accepted the bow with trembling hands.** He was ten years old. He was too small to use it. But he kept it. He kept it wrapped in cloth, hidden in his hut, waiting for the day he would be strong enough to use it.
- **Olaf and Sam left.** They never returned. Gordon never saw them again.

### The Gruffgud War — The Truth He Tried to Tell
- Gordon lived in Domido Forest for centuries. He watched the villages of **Ghuffghuff** and **Gudameng** grow. He watched them fight. He watched them die.
- The **Gruffgud War** — the centuries-long conflict between the two villages — was a lie. It was not about fried rice vs. fried noodles. It was not about food. It was about **Gunung Rayu**.
- The **Commonwealth** was secretly excavating beneath the mountain. They were digging into an **ancient underground city** — a pre-Rapture ruin. They were looking for something — artifacts, knowledge, power. They did not want anyone to know.
- They used the Gruffgud War as a distraction. They funded both sides. They kept the war going for centuries. They made sure the villages were too busy killing each other to notice what was happening at the mountain.
- **Gordon found out.** He saw the Commonwealth agents. He saw the weapons with the Commonwealth crest. He saw the secret camp near Gunung Rayu. He tried to warn the villages. He tried to expose the truth.
- **No one listened.** The Commonwealth destroyed his evidence. They silenced his witnesses. They called him mad. They exiled him. They called him **"Badak Berisik"** — the Noisy Rhino — and they laughed.
- **He never stopped shouting.** He kept trying. He kept warning. He kept being a pain in the ass. He kept singing Sam's melody. He kept waiting for someone to listen.

### The End of the War — 650 AV
- In **650 AV**, after nearly four centuries of bloodshed, the two villages finally ran out of strength. A new generation had been born and died without ever knowing what life without war felt like. The elders who remained were tired old people who no longer remembered why they were fighting.
- **Gordon descended from the mountain one final time.** His body was ancient. His hair was completely white. His beard hung to his chest. But his eyes still burned. And his voice still rumbled like thunder.
- He gathered the elders of both villages at the old fortress in the middle of **Domido Forest** — the same fortress where he had once seen the conspirators, where he had been wounded, where he had started all this. The fortress was weathered, its wood eaten by termites, its roof full of holes — but it still stood as a silent witness to history.
- **He spoke.** He told them the truth — about the Commonwealth, about Gunung Rayu, about the war they had been fighting for centuries. He told them they were puppets. He told them it was time to cut their strings.
- The meeting lasted three days and three nights. No one recorded it. No one wrote its history. But in the end, the elders signed a peace treaty. **The Gruffgud War ended.**
- **Gordon returned to his hut.** He did not stay to celebrate. He did not want to be a hero. He just wanted to be left alone. He had done what he could. He had ended the war. He had told the truth. He had kept his promise to Olaf and Sam — to be a guardian, not a conqueror.

### Husgus — The Child He Found
- **~690 AV.** Forty years after the war ended, Gordon was still living in his hut in Domido Forest. He was ancient — his age approaching five centuries. His body could barely move. But his eyes remained sharp. His days were spent sitting in front of the hut, watching the forest, occasionally singing the old melody Sam had taught him.
- One morning, when fog still covered the trees, Gordon saw something strange. Beneath the old tree where he usually sat, **a small body lay**.
- The figure was a **small child without clothes**. A tiny body, perhaps eight or nine years old, with pale skin and thin hair. No wounds. No signs of violence. The child simply lay there — as if just fallen from the sky.
- **Gordon dragged the child into his hut.** He was too old and weak to carry him. For days he cared for him — changing compresses, giving him water and finely mashed food. The child had no fever, no illness. He only slept deeply, like a statue.
- When the child finally woke, his eyes stared at Gordon emptily. Without curiosity. Without fear. Without anything. Like uncarved glass.
- With a hoarse voice — like someone speaking for the first time — he muttered about who he was. He shook his head when asked. He only remembered **darkness, fire, and someone screaming**. He did not know his name. Did not know where he came from. Did not know anything about this world. The only word he could say, over and over in his sleep, was **"Husgus."**
- **Gordon looked at the child for a long time.** In those empty eyes, he saw something familiar. Not as a human — but as a **mirror**. Like looking into himself as a child — orphaned, alone in this forest.
- Slowly, he said that the child might not know who he was, but he knew his name — and that was enough to start. With worn cloth he wrapped the small body, gave him the name **Husgus**, and said the child would stay here with him until he found his answer.
- **For the first time, there was a little light in Husgus's eyes** — perhaps a question, perhaps hope.
- **Gordon knew one thing:** this world was cruel, and this child needed someone to protect him — just as Olaf had once protected him.

### The Training — Five Years
- For the next **five years**, Gordon trained Husgus. His ancient body moved slowly, but his mind was still sharp. He taught many things: how to hunt, how to read animal tracks, how to set traps, how to use a bow. And the same philosophy Olaf had once taught him — about **protecting the weak**, about **standing tall even when the whole world thinks you are mad**.
- **Husgus learned with astonishing speed.** Within months, he could read tracks better than Gordon. Within a year, he could shoot a flying bird with terrifying accuracy. Within five years, he was better than Gordon had ever been.
- **The strangest thing: he never got sick.** And at night, when he slept, he often muttered in a language Gordon did not recognize — a language that sounded ancient, with a strange rhythm, like a mantra.
- **Gordon knew something was wrong.** This child was not an ordinary human. But he did not care. What mattered was that **Husgus had a good heart** — and that was worth more than any bloodline.
- One night by the campfire, Gordon asked if Husgus remembered anything about his past — about before he came here. Husgus shook his head. He only remembered darkness, someone screaming, and a blazing fire.
- **Gordon sighed.** He said the child might not be from this place — might be from somewhere far away. But that did not matter. What mattered was what he did now.
- Husgus asked what he should do. **Gordon answered with a bitter smile:** he should live, learn, become strong, and when the time came, choose for himself who he was. He was old and would not last much longer — but Husgus had time, and he should use it well.

### The Death of Gordon — 695 AV
- **Malakor** — one of the few people who still remembered Gordon — was a wizard who had sided with Gudameng during the war. A fanatic who believed fried noodles were sacred.
- But what few knew: **Malakor was an agent of the Commonwealth.** He had been placed in Gudameng to ensure the fires of war kept burning, to report every development, and to **silence anyone who tried to reveal the truth** — including the truth about the project at Gunung Rayu.
- During the war, Malakor had watched how Gordon nearly exposed all their plans. He had watched the Noisy Rhino gather evidence, speak with witnesses, and nearly tear open the veil that had been kept tight for centuries. Though the evidence had been destroyed and the witnesses silenced, Malakor knew: **Gordon was still alive, still knew, and as long as Gordon lived, the truth could never truly die**.
- One night in **695 AV**, while Gordon sat in front of his hut singing the old melody, **Malakor and his men attacked**. They came like shadows. Dozens of armed men.
- **Husgus** — who was hunting deer in another part of the forest — heard strange sounds. He ran home. But he was **too late**.
- **Gordon fought bravely.** The Gustaero bow in his hands loosed arrow after arrow, and several of Malakor's men fell. But there were too many. And when Gordon saw the faces of the attackers, his heart broke. They were people from both villages — people he had once saved, once spoken to, once looked in the eyes, once hoped could live in peace. Now they were **executioners**.
- **Malakor's dagger pierced Gordon's chest.**
- In his final moments, Gordon saw Husgus's silhouette appear from behind the trees. He wanted to scream at the child to run. But his voice was gone.
- **Husgus screamed** — not a scream of fear, but of **pure rage**. He grabbed the bow Gordon had given him and loosed an arrow at Malakor. The arrow struck the wizard's shoulder, making him stagger.
- Malakor turned furiously, asked who the child was, and raised his hand with magic gathering at his fingertips. **Husgus drew another arrow** — but Malakor was faster.
- The spell flew, striking **Husgus's small body**. And then **something happened**. **Husgus's body shone.** **Golden light radiated from within him.**
- For a moment, Malakor saw something that made him stagger back in terror — not a small child, but **something else**. Something that should not exist.
- Husgus fell to his knees, body trembling. He felt something **changing** inside him — not pain, but **transformation**. His bones cracked. His muscles contracted. His body **shrank** — smaller, lighter.
- When the light faded, he was still a child — but **smaller than before**. He was a **halfling**.
- In the chaos, **Husgus grabbed Gordon's body** and fled into the forest. Malakor and his men chased. But **Domido Forest was Husgus's home**. He knew every hollow, every hiding place, every secret path. He vanished like a ghost.
- Beneath the old tree where he had first been found, **Husgus buried Gordon**. His teacher's body felt heavy, though he was only a small child. With his own hands he dug the grave, tears falling. When the grave was finished, he sat beside it all night.
- In his hands, the **Gustaero bow** felt warm — as if the bow knew its owner had left, and was now **waiting for a new owner**.
- The next day, he began **tracking Malakor**. He found him in his tower. He killed him with Gordon's bow. He burned the tower. He walked away, leaving the ruins behind, heading toward a wider world.
- On the grip of Gordon's bow, he carved simple words:
  > *"Here lies Gordon, the Noisy Rhino. He saw the truth, and the world thought him mad. But he never stopped shouting. I will not forget you."*

### Gordon's Grave
- **Gordon's grave** is in **Domido Forest** — beneath the old tree where Husgus first found him. It is a simple mound, marked by the **Gustaero bow** planted in the ground like a headstone.
- The grave is **not just a grave**. It is a **Cycle Clan marker** — a ward against the Gap. Gordon knew what he was doing when he chose that spot. He chose it because it was a weak point in the veil. He chose it because he wanted to protect the forest even in death.
- **If the party ever visits the grave**, they will find a **hidden message** carved into the underside of the stone: *"The truth is in the Ark. The key is in the smile."*
- **The message is a clue.** It points to the **Bahtera Bawah Tanah** — the Underground Ark. It points to the **Grin** — the people with the eternal smile. It points to the truth that Gordon never stopped trying to tell.
