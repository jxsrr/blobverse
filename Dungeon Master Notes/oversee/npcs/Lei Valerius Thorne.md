- Beloved by the people. Warm smile. Diplomatic.
- Twin brother of **<a href="/Lei%20Darvin%20Thorne">Darvin</a>**.
- Wants to "thicken the Gap" and merge it with Oversee.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### Who Valerius Is
- **Valerius Thorne** is the **Kanselir Agung** of the Triase — the Chancellor, the highest office in the corrupted Commonwealth.
- He is a **descendant of Lei Wendigo** (wind element) — but his power is weak. He is not a true elemental heir. He is a politician, not a mage.
- He is the **older twin** of **Darvin Thorne**. He was kept. Darvin was hidden. Valerius has always known.
- He is **not the final antagonist**. He is a pawn. He is ambitious, dangerous, and smart — but he is not the one pulling the strings. **Lei Firsa** (or her legacy) is the true power. Valerius thinks he is in charge. He is not.

### The Twin — Darvin
- Valerius and Darvin are the **surviving Thorne twins** — one kept, one hidden.
- Valerius knows Darvin exists. He has been hunting him for years. He wants Darvin dead.
- Why? Because Darvin is the **True Heir of Lei Roiga** — the bloodline claim to **Topaju**. Valerius wants that claim. He wants to be the one who holds Topaju. He wants to be the one who controls the earth.
- **But Darvin does not carry Topaju.** **Lei Alejandro** holds Topaju. Valerius does not know this. He believes Darvin carries it. He is wrong.
- If Valerius kills Darvin, he will not gain Topaju. He will gain nothing. He will have killed his brother for a lie.
- **This is the tragedy of Valerius.** He is a pawn who thinks he is a player.

### Valerius and Livia
- Valerius knows Livia is **not** his sister. He knows she was adopted by **Lei Alejandro**. He knows she was never a twin.
- He has known for years. He has been waiting for the right moment to kill her.
- Why? Because she is a **loose end**. Because she was adopted by the man who betrayed the Initial Six. Because she knows too much about the Triase. Because she is a threat to his power.
- He will try to kill her if she defects. He will try to kill her if she stays. He will try to kill her no matter what she does.
- **He is not a good man.** He is not evil in the cosmic sense. He is a politician. He is a murderer. He is a coward. He is a pawn.

### Valerius and the Gap
- Valerius wants to **"thicken the Gap"** — to merge it with Oversee.
- He believes this will make the world safe from the Overseer. He believes it will make him a hero.
- **He is wrong.** The Gap is not a wall. It is a wound. Thickening it will not save the world. It will turn Oversee into a new Gap — a dimension of scavengers, not a world of mortals.
- He does not know this. He is being manipulated. **Lei Firsa** (or her legacy) is using him. He thinks he is the one in control.

### Valerius and Chenalia
- Valerius ordered the "reconstruction" of **Chenalia** — the village where Vya was born.
- It was not a reconstruction. It was an excavation. He was looking for something. The party does not know what.
- He also ordered the burning of Chenalia. Or so the party suspects. (DM decides: did he order it, or was it someone else? The truth is ambiguous. It may have been **Lei Firsa**'s agents, not Valerius. Or it may have been Valerius, following Firsa's orders.)
- **The truth about Chenalia is a Season 3 reveal.** The party may never know for sure who burned it.

### Valerius's Role in Season 3
- Valerius is **not the final boss**. He is the mid-boss. He is the one the party must defeat to reach the Bahtera Bawah Tanah, to reach Lei Alejandro, to reach the truth.
- If the party defeats Valerius, the Triase will fracture. The Commonwealth will descend into chaos. The party will have to decide what to do with the pieces.
- If the party kills Valerius, Darvin will be free. But Darvin will also be lost — he will not know what to do without his brother. He will wander. He will fade.
- If the party turns Valerius — if they can prove to him that he is a pawn — he will break. He will weep. He will ask: *"What have I done?"* He may join the party. He may not. The choice is his.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Valerius is **not the final antagonist**. He is a pawn of Lei Firsa (or her legacy).
- He knows Livia is not his sister. He has been waiting to kill her.
- He is hunting Darvin because he believes Darvin carries Topaju. **Darvin does not.** Lei Alejandro holds Topaju.
- He wants to "thicken the Gap." He does not know this will destroy the world.
- He ordered the "reconstruction" of Chenalia — but it was an excavation, not a reconstruction.
- He may or may not have ordered the burning of Chenalia. The truth is ambiguous.
