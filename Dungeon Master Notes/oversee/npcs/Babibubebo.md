## What the Party Knows
- A small, pitiful fish-man. Large eyes. Scaly skin.
- Stole food from the Karang Tidur inn because he was hungry.
- Not hostile. Just hungry.
- The party let him go. He is grateful.
- He lives in the **Kuil Lautan** — the Sea Temple in Juto Port City.
- He speaks in simple sentences. He does not remember much about his past.
- He knows a hidden path through the temple's underwater caves.

## What He Offers
- **Information** about the Sea Temple.
- **A friend** in Juto Port City.
- **A secret:** he knows a hidden path through the temple's underwater caves.
- **The way home** — if he can remember it.

## Current Goal
Eat. Survive. Maybe make friends. Maybe remember where he came from.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### Who Babibubebo Really Is
- Babibubebo is not a common kuo-toa. He is a **prince of the Kuo Lau Kingdom** — the underwater kingdom that guards **Alnean**, the breath of the swan.
- He was separated from his people as a child. He does not remember his home, his father, or his true name. He only remembers the smell of salt, the cold of the deep, and a song he cannot sing.
- The Kuo Lau King — his father — has been mourning him for years. He does not know Babi is alive.
- Babi adopted the name "Babibubebo" because it was the only sound he could make when he first arrived in Juto Port City. It is not his real name. His real name is a Kuo Lau word that sounds like **"Baelor"** — but he does not remember it.
- He is not a kuo-toa. He is a **Kuo Lau** — a more ancient, more noble race. He appears small and pitiful because he has been away from the deep for too long. The Kuo Lau need the pressure of the deep to thrive. Without it, they shrink.

### The Key to Alnean
- The only way to find the **Kuo Lau Kingdom** is through **Babibubebo**. He is the only one who knows the way — and even he does not remember it clearly.
- The party must help Babi remember. They must take him to the **Kuil Lautan** in Juto Port City and let him swim deep. The temple's underwater caves connect to the Kuo Lau's outer tunnels.
- Babi will lead them down. He will remember the way as he swims. The party will need **water-breathing magic** — or they will need to find a way to survive the deep.
- The Kuo Lau King will only give **Alnean** to someone who **brings Babibubebo home**. He has mourned his son for years. He will weep when he sees him.

### The Kuo Lau Kingdom
- The Kuo Lau Kingdom is an underwater city of coral, pearl, and bioluminescence, deep beneath the Elis Ocean.
- It is protected by a **pressure veil** — a magical barrier that keeps the water from crushing the city and keeps the surface world from finding it.
- The Kuo Lau are isolationist. They do not welcome visitors. They do not trust air-breathers. The party will have to earn their trust.
- The Kuo Lau King is old — older than most mortals. He has held Alnean for centuries. He knows what it is. He knows what it does. He has never used it.

### What Babi Does NOT Know
- He does not know he is a prince.
- He does not know his father is still alive.
- He does not know about Alnean.
- He does not know the Kuo Lau Kingdom is in danger — the Gap is thinning, the pressure veil is weakening, and the King has felt something stirring in the deep.
- He does not know that he is the key to the third path — because without Alnean, the party cannot enter the Gap.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Babi is a Kuo Lau prince, not a common fish-man.
- He is the key to finding Alnean — the breath of the swan.
- The Kuo Lau Kingdom exists. It is real. It is in danger.
- Babi's father has been waiting for him to come home.

### How to Use Babi
- **Season 3 encounter:** The party can return to Juto Port City and find Babi at the Kuil Lautan. He will be happy to see them. He will not remember much — but he will remember them.
- **The descent:** If the party asks Babi to lead them deep, he will be afraid. He does not remember the way. But he will try. He will swim, and the party will follow, and slowly — very slowly — he will remember.
- **The reunion:** When the Kuo Lau King sees Babi, he will weep. He will hold him. He will speak his true name — **Baelor** — and Babi will remember. This is a Season 3 emotional beat.
- **The gift:** If the party brings Babi home and passes the King's test, the King will give them **Alnean**. He will also give them a **Kuo Lau Pearl** — a small pearl that allows the bearer to breathe underwater for 1 hour per day. It is not Alnean. It is a gift. It is a thank you.
- **Babi's role after:** Babi will stay in the Kuo Lau Kingdom. He has been away too long. He has a people to lead. He has a father to love. He will not return to the surface. But he will remember the party. He will tell his children about the air-breathers who brought him home.

### Babi's True Name
- His real name is **Baelor**. He does not remember it. The Kuo Lau King will speak it when he sees him.
- "Babibubebo" is a nickname he gave himself as a child. It was the only sound he could make. It stuck. The party knows him by that name. His father will know him by his true name.
- If the party ever calls him "Babibubebo" in front of the King, the King will smile. He will say: *"That is not his name. But it is a good name. It is the name he gave himself. It is the name of a survivor."*
