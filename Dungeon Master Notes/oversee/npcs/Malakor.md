## What the Party Knows (from Husgus)
- A wizard. Old. Fanatical. Believed fried noodles were sacred.
- Was an **agent of the Commonwealth** — placed in Gudameng to keep the Gruffgud War going.
- Killed Gordon in 695 AV.
- Was killed by **Husgus** in his tower. Husgus burned the tower down.

## His Legacy
- The truth about Gunung Rayu.
- Husgus's transformation into a halfling.
- The burning of his tower (which destroyed most of his research).

### Who Malakor Is
- **Malakor** was a **Commonwealth agent** — a wizard, a fanatic, a man who had sold his soul for power and a steady supply of fried noodles. He was not a good man. He was not a bad man in the cosmic sense. He was a **tool** — a weapon in the hands of the Commonwealth, pointed at anyone who threatened their secrets.
- He was placed in **Gudameng** during the Gruffgud War. His official role was "advisor to the elders." His real role was **to keep the war going**. To ensure the fires of conflict never went out. To report every development to his handler — **Valerius Thorne**. To silence anyone who tried to reveal the truth about **Gunung Rayu**.
- He was **not the mastermind**. He was not the architect of the conspiracy. He was a **pawn** — a mid-level agent who did the dirty work. He believed he was important. He believed he was in control. He was wrong. He was a cog in a machine he did not understand.
- He was a **fanatic** — not about fried noodles (that was a cover, a persona, a way to fit in with the Gudameng locals), but about **order**. He believed the Commonwealth was the only thing standing between civilization and chaos. He believed the war was necessary. He believed the excavation at Gunung Rayu was necessary. He believed the ends justified the means — even if the means were murder.
- He was **not a powerful wizard**. He was competent. He was dangerous. He was not an archmage. He was a man who had learned enough magic to kill and to hide. He was a man who had learned enough cruelty to do what was asked of him.

### The Gruffgud War — His Role
- The Gruffgud War was a **lie**. It was not about fried rice vs. fried noodles. It was about **Gunung Rayu** — the ancient underground city beneath the mountain, the pre-Rapture ruin that the Commonwealth was secretly excavating.
- Malakor was one of the agents who kept the war going. He funded both sides. He spread rumors. He ensured the villages stayed at each other's throats so they would not notice what was happening at the mountain.
- He was **good at his job**. The war lasted for centuries. The villages destroyed themselves. The excavation continued. The Commonwealth got what it wanted.
- **Gordon was the only one who saw through it.** He saw the agents. He saw the weapons with the Commonwealth crest. He saw the secret camp near Gunung Rayu. He tried to warn the villages. He tried to expose the truth.
- **Malakor silenced him.** He destroyed his evidence. He killed his witnesses. He called him mad. He exiled him. He called him "Badak Berisik" — the Noisy Rhino — and he laughed.
- **He did not kill Gordon then.** He could have. He should have. But he did not. He thought the old dwarf was harmless. He thought the exile was enough. He was wrong.

### The Death of Gordon — 695 AV
- **695 AV.** Forty years after the war ended, Malakor was still in the region. He had not been reassigned. He had not been promoted. He was still watching. Still waiting. Still reporting.
- He learned that **Gordon was still alive**. He learned that the old dwarf was still in his hut in Domido Forest. He learned that the old dwarf had found a child — a strange child, a child with golden cracks, a child who might be something more.
- **He panicked.** He knew Gordon had been investigating Gunung Rayu. He knew Gordon had been gathering evidence. He knew Gordon had been trying to expose the truth. As long as Gordon lived, the truth could never truly die. As long as Gordon lived, the Commonwealth's secret was at risk.
- **He gathered his men.** Former warriors from both sides of the war — people who could not return to normal life, who still held grudges, who could be bought with promises of power or revenge. He told them Gordon was a traitor. He told them Gordon was a threat. He told them Gordon had to die.
- **They attacked the hut.** Dozens of armed men. Gordon fought bravely — the Gustaero bow loosed arrow after arrow — but he was old, and there were too many. Malakor's dagger pierced his chest.
- **Husgus arrived too late.** He saw Gordon fall. He saw Malakor standing over him. He screamed — not with fear, but with **rage**. He grabbed the bow. He loosed an arrow at Malakor. The arrow struck the wizard's shoulder.
- **Malakor cast a spell at Husgus.** The spell hit the child. And then **something happened**. Husgus's body **shone gold**. His body **shrank**. He became a **halfling**.
- For a moment, Malakor saw something that made him stagger back in terror — not a small child, but **something else**. Something that should not exist. He did not know what it was. He did not know what it meant. He only knew he had to kill it.
- **He did not get the chance.** Husgus grabbed Gordon's body and fled into the forest. Malakor and his men chased. But Domido Forest was Husgus's home. He knew every hollow, every hiding place, every secret path. He vanished.

### The Death of Malakor — 695 AV
- **Husgus did not forget.** He buried Gordon. He mourned. And then he **hunted**.
- He tracked Malakor to his tower — a tall, crooked spire at the edge of Domido Forest, near the ruins of the old fortress. The tower was surrounded by corpses — Malakor's followers who had failed in their tasks, or victims of the wizard's mad rituals. The stench stung Husgus's nose, but he did not flinch.
- **He slipped in through a crack in the stone wall.** He climbed the stairs. He found Malakor at the top — performing a ritual, surrounded by black candles, a book with a human skin cover open on a lectern.
- **Husgus drew Gordon's bow.** He aimed at Malakor's heart. But before he could release, the wizard turned. He laughed. He said: *"You are just a child. A tiny child. You cannot defeat me."*
- **Husgus loosed the arrow.** It flew straight into Malakor's chest — piercing his heart.
- **But Malakor's spell also reached Husgus.** Not as a direct attack — as a **wave of energy** that swept the room. Husgus's body **shone again** — brighter than before. The golden cracks flared. His body **shrank even smaller**. When the light faded, he was fully a halfling. The transformation was complete.
- **Malakor lay dead on the floor.** The arrow still in his chest.
- **Husgus burned the tower.** He did not take anything. He did not want anything. He just wanted it gone. The fire rose high, lighting the night sky. He walked away, leaving the ruins behind, heading toward a wider world.

### The Tower — What Survived
- **Husgus burned the tower.** But he did not burn everything. The fire consumed the upper levels — Malakor's quarters, his ritual chamber, his library. But the **lower levels survived**. The stone was too thick. The fire could not reach the basement.
- **The basement still holds Malakor's research.** His notes on Gunung Rayu. His reports to Valerius. His records of the Gruffgud War. His **spellbook** — a tome of dark magic, including a spell that can **detect HVS shells**.
- **The party can find the tower** — or what is left of it — in Season 3. It is a ruined spire at the edge of Domido Forest. The upper levels are ash. The lower levels are intact. The basement is a treasure trove of information — and a dangerous place. Malakor's wards may still be active. His traps may still be armed. His notes may still be guarded by something that should not exist.
- **Malakor's spellbook** is the most important item in the tower. It contains a spell that can **detect HVS shells** — the same spell Malakor used to sense that Husgus was different. If the party finds it, they can use it to find other shells — or to protect Shin. But using the spell may attract attention. It may attract **Valerius**. It may attract **Lei Firsa**. It may attract things that should not be attracted.

### Malakor and Valerius
- **Malakor was Valerius's agent.** He was not the mastermind. He was not the architect. He was a tool — a weapon in the hands of the Kanselir Agung. He reported to Valerius. He followed Valerius's orders. He believed he was important. He was wrong.
- **Valerius ordered Gordon's death.** He knew Gordon had been investigating Gunung Rayu. He knew Gordon was the last living witness to the conspiracy. He knew that as long as Gordon lived, the truth could resurface. He gave the order: *"Kill the old dwarf. Burn the hut. Destroy every trace of his investigation. And if the child survives... bring him to me."*
- **Malakor failed the last part.** He did not bring Husgus to Valerius. He tried to kill him instead. Valerius was not pleased. He did not punish Malakor — Malakor was already dead by the time he learned of the failure. But he noted it. He remembered. He began to watch for the child. He began to wonder what the child was.
- **Malakor's reports** — if the party finds them — will reveal Valerius's involvement. They will reveal the order to kill Gordon. They will reveal the excavation at Gunung Rayu. They will reveal the Commonwealth's conspiracy. They will reveal the truth.
