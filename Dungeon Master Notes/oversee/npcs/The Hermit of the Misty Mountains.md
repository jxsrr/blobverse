## Overview
A solitary old man clad in weathered animal skins who is rumored among high-country trappers and travelers in <a href="/oversee/locations/Elis">Elis</a> to have lived alone in the upper valleys of the Misty Mountains (*Pegunungan Kabut*) for half a century. Few claim to have seen him from afar, and fewer still have spoken to him.

## Rumors & Lore
- **Guardian of the Peaks:** Locals in the foothills whisper that he is merely the latest in a long line of caretakers watching over a remnant from before the Rapture.
- **Reclusive Nature:** He avoids settlements entirely and reacts with quiet caution toward armed outsiders venturing too high into the mist.
::/pf
%% DM SECRETS %%

## Who He Is
- An **old human man**. Weathered skin. Long grey beard. Rough hands. Wears animal skins.
- Has lived alone in the **Misty Mountains** for 50 years.
- His only companion is **the last Behemoth** — the dying ancient creature that lives in the high valleys.
- He has been caring for it for 50 years. He was not its first caretaker — he inherited the role from another hermit, who inherited it from another, going back 10 generations.

## What He Knows
- **Behemoths are real** — not legends.
- **The last one is dying** — it has been alive since before the Rapture, and its time is coming.
- **The Behemoth's Tear** — when the creature dies, it will shed a single tear. That tear is a **gemstone**. It can heal any wound once, including severed souls and death (within 1 minute).
- **The poison** — the Behemoth is not dying of age. It is dying of **Safayer residue** — the same poison that infected Vythrax. It is leaking from a buried Gemheart fragment in the mountains.
- If someone removes the poison, the Behemoth will give them the Tear before it dies.

## What He Offers
- **Access to the Behemoth** — but only to those who prove they mean no harm.
- **Information** about the Behemoth's history.
- **A warning:** *"It has been waiting a long time. Do not make it wait longer."*
- If the party heals the Behemoth, he will weep. Then he will walk into the mountains and not return. **His duty is done.**

## Personality
- Quiet. Patient. Deeply gentle.
- Does not speak much. Prefers silence to small talk.
- Has not spoken to another human in years — his voice is hoarse from disuse.

- **Season 3 encounter** — if the party travels to Pegunungan Kabut.
