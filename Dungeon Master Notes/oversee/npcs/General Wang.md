## Who He Is
- **General Wang** is a former general of the Commonwealth — one of the old guard, a man who served before the Triase consolidated its power.
- He was imprisoned in Cloud Prison — the fortress on the coast of Rioquan — after he refused to follow an order he believed was unjust.
- He is old. He is tired. He has been in prison for years. He does not know what has happened to the world outside.
- He is not a Cipher. He is not part of any four-Cipher structure. He is a prisoner.

## What He Knows
- He knows the **Commonwealth is corrupt**. He saw it happen. He refused to be part of it.
- He knows about **Valerius's plan** to thicken the Gap. He tried to stop it. He failed.
- He knows about the **Edisi Law** — the public version and the original. He knows the public version is a lie.
- He knows about the **HVS shells** — not the details, but enough to know they exist. He knows Klan Lei experimented with them.
- He knows about the **Bahtera Bawah Tanah**. He has never been there, but he knows it exists.
- He knows about **Lei Alejandro**. He does not know he is still alive. He thinks he is dead.
- He knows about **Lei Firsa** — or at least the legend of "Lei the Great." He suspects the legend is a lie.

## What He Offers
- **Information** about the Commonwealth's military, its weaknesses, its plans.
- **Information** about Valerius. He knows the Chancellor personally. He knows his fears, his ambitions, his secrets.
- **The location of the Bahtera Bawah Tanah** — or at least, the approximate location. He has never been there, but he knows where it is.
- **A map of the Commonwealth's defenses** — if the party can prove they are worthy of it.
- **A warning:** *"The Triase is not the enemy. The enemy is the woman behind the Triase. She has been pulling the strings for 700 years."*

## Personality
- Old. Tired. Sarcastic. He has lost everything. He has nothing left to lose.
- He does not trust easily. He has been betrayed before.
- He will test the party. He will ask them: *"Why do you want to stop the Commonwealth? To save the world? Or to rule it?"*
- If they answer truthfully, he will help them. If they lie, he will turn them away.

## When Party Meets Him
- **Season 3 encounter** — at Benteng Atlan. He is a prisoner. The party can free him, or they can speak to him through the bars.
- Wrap his player-facing content in `::pf` markers when encountered.

## Open Questions
- Is he guilty of a crime? (Yes — desertion. He refused an order. He believes he was right.)
- Does he have family? (A daughter, somewhere. He does not know if she is alive.)
- Will he join the party? (Yes, if they earn his trust. He is old, but he is still a soldier.)
