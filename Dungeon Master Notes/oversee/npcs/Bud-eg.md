## What the Party Knows
- A strange old man. Filthy. Smelly. **Deaf.**
- Blocked the party's cart with wood magic. Roots grabbed the wheels.
- Was trying to communicate something important.
- **Agus killed him** with an arrow before he could finish.
- The party later realized Bud-eg was trying to warn them about Echo Sam.

## His Warning
- Using sign language and frantic gestures, he told the party: **"Cover your ears when the singing starts."**
- This was the key to breaking Echo Sam's illusion.

## His Death
- Agus shot him in the forest. The party was too far away to stop it.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Bud-eg was **immune to Echo Sam's song** because he was deaf. He had been watching the monster for years.
- He lived alone in the forest, surviving on roots and berries, mapping Echo Sam's patterns.
- He had a **hidden journal** in his hut. It contains everything he learned about Echo Sam — including how to kill it.
- His real name was **Budegor**. He was once a ranger of the Yawning Guild. He left after his party died to Echo Sam.
- He had a **family** once. They died in Ghuffghuff years ago. He has been trying to avenge them ever since.
- If the party ever finds his hut, they will find his journal and a small wooden box with a lock of his daughter's hair.
