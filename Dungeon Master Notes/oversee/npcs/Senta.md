## What the Party Knows
- A middle-aged human woman. Practical. No-nonsense.
- Sells **adventuring gear** — rope, torches, rations, basic weapons.
- Has a soft spot for new adventurers. Gives a 10% discount to anyone with a fresh Guild badge.
- Vya bought a **flower** from her in Session 1.

## What She Offers
- **Standard gear** (PHB prices).
- **Local information.** She knows who's hiring, who's dangerous, and who's lying.
- **A flower.** If Vya ever returns, she will give her another one — free.

## Current Goal
Run her shop. Survive.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Senta's daughter was an adventurer. She died in Greyland Castle five years ago.
- She does not know the party's connection to Greyland. If she finds out, she will be devastated — and then furious.
- She has a **hidden stash of healing potions** in the back room. She will give them to the party if they promise to avenge her daughter.
- Her daughter's name was **Samira**. (Not the same Samira NOT <a href="/oversee/npcs/Mira%20Nouveau">Mira</a>. Different person. Common name.)
