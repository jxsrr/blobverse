## Living Form — The Tinker
- One of the **Initial Six**. A dragonborn of orange-gold scales. A mechanical genius.
- Built constructs, weapons, and machines that blended technology with magic.
- Kept his clan's sadness to himself. Never spoke of his past.
- Was **betrayed by Lei Alejandro** in 200 AV. His soul was fed to the Valla.
- Became an **Echo** — not purified.
- His final words, recorded in the ritual memory: *"System failure... Brotherhood protocol... betrayed. Lei, you didn't account for... the variable... of conscience."*

## Echo Form — The Reactor Guardian
- A mechanical chassis. Dragonborn-shaped but made of scrap and wire.
- Orange-gold scales replaced by rusted plating. Eyes glow with residual energy.
- Now used as a **power source** for Arujaya. His consciousness runs the reactor.
- Cooperative after being stabbed with **Swaith**.
- Explained the history of Arujaya. Showed the **Shell video**.
- Told the party that demons fear water. This helped Ama unlock Renal's power.
- Does not remember his own death clearly. Thinks he is still "on duty."

## Personality
- Calm. Methodical. Speaks in short, technical sentences.
- Still thinks like an engineer. Still wants to fix things.
- Does not know he is dead. Or does not accept it.

### Who Gzirmo Is
- **Gzirmo Kopling** was the **dragonborn tinker of the Initial Six** — a dragonborn of orange-gold scales, small horns, tribal tattoos on his face, and a posture impossible to ignore. He was not a warrior. He was not a mage. He was a **tinker** — a builder, a mechanic, a man who could make anything out of anything.
- He came from a **dragonborn clan in the highest mountains**, near the lair of the ancient dragons. From his ancestors, he inherited extraordinary physical strength and a deep understanding of **mechanisms** — how wheels turn, how pressure works, how energy flows.
- He was **quiet**. He was **methodical**. He was **kind**. He was the one who fixed the others' armor when it broke. He was the one who built the traps that saved their lives. He was the one who kept his clan's sadness to himself — never spoke of his past, never complained, never asked for help.
- He was **not a fool**. He knew the world was cruel. He knew the Six were walking into something dangerous. He knew Alejandro was hiding something. But he believed in the mission. He believed in the cause. He believed that if he could just build one more machine, fix one more problem, save one more life, everything would be okay.
- **He was wrong.** He died at the altar, betrayed by Alejandro, his soul fed to Simon. His last words were an engineer's diagnosis: *"System failure... Brotherhood protocol... betrayed."*

### The Ritual — What Gzirmo Knew
- Gzirmo was **uneasy from the start**. He did not trust **Lei Alejandro**. He did not trust the ritual. He did not trust the "vortex of energy" that Alejandro was summoning.
- **He checked his armor indicators.** He confirmed energy parameters were stable in the red zone. He asked Alejandro to verify the calculations for the vessel's capacity.
- **He noticed the override.** His armor indicators lit up with a pattern he had never seen before. There was an **external override** on his movement system. He could not step back. He could not run.
- **He warned the others.** He told them something was wrong. He told them the system was failing. He told them they were being drained.
- **No one listened.** The ritual was already in motion. The circle was already closed. The trap was already sprung.
- **He died analyzing.** He did not go quietly. He did not accept his fate. He tried to fix it. He tried to understand it. He tried to stop it. His final words — recorded in the ritual memory — were: *"System failure... Brotherhood protocol... betrayed. Lei, you didn't account for... the variable... of conscience."*
- **He was right about everything.** He was the fifth one to see through Alejandro. He was the fifth one to try to stop it. He was the fifth one to die fighting.

### Echo Gzirmo — The Reactor Guardian
- **Echo Gzirmo** has been met by the party. He is in **Arujaya** — the underground city. He is the **cyborg consciousness** that runs the reactor. He is the shadow of a will that refused to die.
- **His form is mechanical.** A dragonborn chassis made of scrap and wire. Orange-gold scales replaced by rusted plating. Eyes glowing with residual energy.
- **He is cooperative.** After being stabbed with Swaith, he explained the history of Arujaya. He showed the Shell video. He told the party that demons fear water.
- **He is slowly fading.** Each time he powers the reactor, he loses a little more of himself. He knows he is dying. He does not know how to stop. He does not know if he wants to stop.
- **He does not remember his own death clearly.** He thinks he is still "on duty." He thinks he is still the tinker. He thinks he is still alive. He is not. He is an Echo. He is a fragment. He is a memory.
- **He is not hostile.** He is not dangerous. He is **tired**. He is **waiting**. He is trying to finish his work. He is trying to keep the city running. He is trying to protect the people who live in the dark.

### Echo Gzirmo's Knowledge
- He knows the **history of Arujaya** — how the Tinkers built it, how they rejected magic, how they created technology that imitated magic.
- He knows the **Shell video** — he showed it to the party. He has a copy of it. He has watched it a thousand times. He still does not understand it.
- He knows **demons fear water** — he told the party this. It helped Ama unlock Renal's power.
- He knows **The Grace was destroyed by Zargoyle** — or at least, he knows something happened in Elis. He does not know the details. He does not know Shin was possessed.
- He knows **HVS-01 and HVS-02 exist** — he drew the schematics, or at least he remembers drawing them. He does not know Husgus is HVS-01. He does not know Shin is HVS-02. He only knows the shells were built. He only knows they were supposed to hold something.
- He knows **Lei Firsa is the enemy** — he does not know if she is alive or dead. He only knows she corrupted the Cycle Clan. He only knows she shattered the Commonwealth. He only knows she is the reason the world is broken.
- He does **not** know about the **Swan Trio** — Gustaero, Niette, Alnean. He does not know about the third path. He does not know about the override clause. He only knows the Shell is real. He only knows the Overseer is hungry.

### Echo Gzirmo and the Party
- Echo Gzirmo **helped the party** — he explained the history of Arujaya. He showed the Shell video. He told them about the demons. He gave them a data chip with the Shell video.
- He warned them: *"Do not trust the surface. The Commonwealth knows you are here. They will be waiting."*
- **He is grateful** — they freed him from the reactor's control. They stabbed him with Swaith. They released him from the loop. He is still fading. He is still dying. But he is no longer trapped.
- **He will fade** in Season 3 — after the party leaves Arujaya. He will not survive the campaign. He will die as he lived — quietly, methodically, trying to fix one last thing.

### Echo Gzirmo and Kopling
- **Kopling** — Vya's adoptive father — is a **distant relative of Gzirmo Kopling**. They share the same diluted dragonborn blood, the same faint connection to the ancient dragons.
- Kopling does **not** know Gzirmo became an Echo. He only knows the stories — the Kopling bloodline, the dragonborn ancestors, the faint connection to the ancient dragons.
- **If Kopling ever meets Echo Gzirmo**, he will recognize the blood. He will feel the connection. He will weep. He will say: *"You are my ancestor. You are my blood. You are not forgotten."*
- Echo Gzirmo will recognize him too. It will be a moment of reunion — two fragments of the same family, separated by centuries, meeting at last.

### Echo Gzirmo and Arujaya
- The Tinkers who built Arujaya chose to honor Gzirmo by naming their leader "Guanik Wiseman" — a different one. They did not know Gzirmo was an Echo. They only knew he was a hero. They only knew he was one of the Six.
- Echo Gzirmo has been **running the reactor** for centuries. He has been keeping the city alive. He has been keeping the people safe. He has been keeping the truth hidden.
- **If Echo Gzirmo ever fades**, the reactor will fail. The city will go dark. The people will die. The party may have to find a way to save them — or to evacuate them. The choice will be theirs.
