## What the Party Knows
- Young Grin warrior. Eager to prove himself. Kind. Hopeful.
- Trained with the party in the Maw Desert hideout. Welcomed them as family.
- Was the first to welcome Vya as family — he called her "cousin" before anyone else did.
- **Killed by <a href="/Iron%20Gort">Gort</a>** during the raid — his skull crushed in front of the party.
- His death ignited Vya's fire.
- His mother Geneva has not recovered. His father Accarus does not speak of it.

## What the Party Does NOT Know
- Jarko was **not the True Heir**. Hildag and Accarus knew. They used him as a decoy.
- Jarko did not know he was a decoy. He believed he was the True Heir. He believed he was the hope of the Grin.
- Geneva did not know. She still believes he died a hero.

### Who Jarko Was
- **Jarko Grin** was the son of **Accarus Grin** and **Geneva Grin**. He was the grandson of **Hildag Grin** — the Weaver, the founder of Will of Grin.
- He was a Grin — eternal smile, magic-deaf, horns. He was young — barely into his twenties when he died. He was eager. He was kind. He was hopeful.
- He was **not the True Heir**. Hildag knew this from the moment he was born. Accarus knew this from the moment he was born. They tested him. They measured his blood. They found no spark. He was a Grin warrior. He was not a Lei heir.
- **They did not tell him.** They let him believe he was the True Heir. They let him train. They let him hope. They let him believe he was the hope of his people.
- He was a **decoy** — a false heir to distract the Commonwealth, to draw attention away from Vya, to buy time. He did not know. He died believing he was a hero.

### Jarko and Vya
- Jarko met **Vya** at the Maw Desert hideout (Session 05). He was the first to welcome her. He called her "cousin" before anyone else did — a joke, an affection, a truth he did not fully understand.
- He did not know Vya was the True Heir. He did not know she was his actual cousin — the daughter of his father's twin brother, Antrem. He did not know she was the one Hildag had been waiting for.
- He saw Vya as family. He saw her as a fellow Grin — a fellow survivor. He saw her as someone who understood what it was like to carry a smile that never faded.
- **If Jarko had lived**, he would have been Vya's closest friend. He would have been her guide to the Grin world. He would have been her cousin in truth.
- He did not live. He died on the stone floor of the Maw Desert hideout. His blood pooled around him. His smile did not fade. It never fades.

### The Death of Jarko
- **Iron Gort** attacked the Maw Desert hideout (Session 05). He was hunting the party. He was hunting the Grin. He was hunting anyone who opposed the Triase.
- Hildag and Accarus made the call. They left Jarko behind. They teleported away with an emergency scroll. They did not tell Geneva. They did not tell the party. They did not tell Jarko.
- Jarko tried to run. He threw a fire punch at Gort — it landed, and it did nothing. Gort grabbed him by the head. He slammed his knee into Jarko's face — once, twice, three times. And then he crushed Jarko's skull against the floor.
- The party watched. They could not save him. They were too far. They were too weak. They were too slow.
- **Jarko died believing he was a hero.** He died believing he was the True Heir. He died believing his death meant something. He was wrong. He was a decoy. He died for nothing.

### Geneva and Jarko
- **Geneva did not know.** She did not know Jarko was not the True Heir. She did not know he was a decoy. She did not know Hildag and Accarus let him die.
- She was in another part of the compound when Jarko died. She was coordinating the evacuation. She heard about it afterward. She has not forgiven herself for not being there.
- She believes Jarko died a hero. She believes he died defending his people. She believes his death meant something. She is wrong — but she does not know. She may never know.
- **If Geneva learns the truth**, she will break. She will ask: *"Did you know? Did you know he was not the True Heir? Did you know Vya was the one?"* She will not believe the answer at first. She will deny it. Then she will investigate. Then she will find the proof. Then she will break.
- She may hate Hildag. She may hate Accarus. She may leave Will of Grin. She may never forgive them. The choice is hers.

### Accarus and Jarko
- **Accarus knew.** He knew Jarko was not the True Heir. He knew Jarko was a decoy. He agreed to the plan. He let his son die.
- He has not forgiven himself. He does not speak of it. He does not weep. He smiles. He always smiles. It is the Grin curse. But inside, he is broken.
- He does not talk about Jarko. He does not say his name. He does not visit his grave. He does not mourn. He carries the guilt in silence.
- **If the party confronts Accarus about Jarko**, he will not deny it. He will say: *"I did what I had to do. You would have done the same."* He will not apologize. He will not weep. He will not show emotion. He is his mother's son.

### Hildag and Jarko
- **Hildag knew.** She knew Jarko was not the True Heir from the moment he was born. She tested him. She measured his blood. She found no spark.
- She made the call. She let him die. She did not weep. She did not mourn. She smiled. She always smiles. It is the Grin curse.
- She does not speak of Jarko. She does not say his name. She does not visit his grave. She carries the guilt in silence — if she carries it at all.
- **If the party confronts Hildag about Jarko**, she will not deny it. She will say: *"I did what I had to do. The Grin need a True Heir. Jarko was not it. Vya is. I would do it again."* She will not apologize. She will not weep. She will not show emotion. She is the Weaver.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Jarko was **not the True Heir**. Hildag and Accarus knew. They used him as a decoy.
- Jarko did not know. He believed he was the True Heir. He believed he was the hope of his people.
- Geneva did not know. She still believes he died a hero.
- Jarko was Vya's actual cousin — the son of Antrem's twin brother, Accarus. He did not know. Vya did not know.
- Jarko's death was the trigger for Vya's fire. When he died, something inside Vya ignited. She did not know what it was. She still does not know what it is.
- Jarko's death was the moment Hildag knew Vya was the magnum opus. She had suspected it before. She knew it then. She watched Vya's fire ignite. She smiled. She always smiles.

### Jarko's Legacy
- Jarko is **dead**. He cannot be brought back. He cannot be saved. He cannot be avenged.
- But his death **matters**. It was the trigger for Vya's fire. It was the moment Hildag knew. It was the moment Accarus broke. It was the moment Geneva lost everything.
- **If the party honors Jarko's memory**, they will carry him with them. They will remember the boy who called Vya "cousin" before anyone else did. They will remember the boy who believed he was a hero. They will remember the boy who died for nothing — and they will make sure his death was not in vain.
- **If the party forgets Jarko**, they will become like Hildag and Accarus. They will become the kind of people who use children as decoys. They will become the kind of people who let the innocent die for the cause. They will become the kind of people who smile while their hearts are breaking.
