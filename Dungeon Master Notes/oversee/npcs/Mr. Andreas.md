## What the Party Knows
- A large, muscular man. Soot-stained. Kind eyes.
- Sells weapons and armor. Good quality. Fair prices.
- Hired the party to guard his wife on her way to **Padre City** (Session 4).
- Paid 200 gp.

## What He Offers
- **Weapons and armor** (PHB prices, 10% discount for the party).
- **Custom work.** He can forge a custom weapon if given time and materials.
- **A favor.** He owes the party for protecting his wife.

## Current Goal
Run his forge. Protect his family.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Pak Andreas's wife is not just a merchant. She is a **Keeper of Secret courier**. She carries messages between cities.
- He knows this. He does not approve. But he loves her.
- He has a **hidden forge** in the basement. It is where he makes weapons for the Keeper of Secret. Some of them are magical.
- If the party brings him **rare materials** (like Frostfang's ice shards or Behemoth bone), he can forge a **legendary weapon**.
- His wife's name is **Ibu Ratna**.
