## Appearance
An ancient Tortle. Slow-moving. Smells of rust, oil, and copper. Surrounded by antique diving helmets, harpoons, nets, and strange oxygen tanks made from giant nautilus shells.

## What the Party Knows
- He is deaf. You have to shout.
- He knew **Atlan Nouveau** personally.
- He has a copy of Atlan's children's book (the one about the sea).
- He can give the party a **map to Atlan's hidden base** if they ask the right question.
- He is not hostile. He is just old.

## Current Goal
Run his shop. Remember the sea.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Pops is **not a Tortle**. He is a **Gap-touched creature** who took the form of a Tortle centuries ago. He was once a demon from the Gap. He chose to stay in Oversee.
- He knows Atlan because Atlan helped him adapt to the mortal world.
- He is the one who told Atlan about the **Locked Sea Archive**.
- He has a **hidden room** in the back of his shop. It contains Atlan's original research, including the location of the **Bahtera Bawah Tanah**.
- He will only share this with someone who asks about Atlan "as a friend, not a scholar."
- His real name is **Oos** — a demon name. He does not use it.
