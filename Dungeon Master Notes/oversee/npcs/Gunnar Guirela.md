## What the Party Knows
- A simple fisherman. Lived in **Wind's Rest** his whole life.
- Found **[[Gustaero — The Swan Bow]]** in his net one morning. Did not know what it was.
- Kept it safe in his attic. Gave it to **Olaf** when he was old enough.
- His role in the bow's story is mentioned in **Will of Grin** and **Wind's Rest** files.
- Also appears in the **legend** of the legendary weapons — the bow was thrown into the sea by Lei Firsa, and Gunnar found it.

## His Legacy
- The bow passed from Gunnar → Olaf → **[[Gordon]]** → **[[Husgus]]**.
- The current wielder, Husgus, does not know Gunnar's name.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Gunnar was **not a fighter**. He was a fisherman. He never used the bow.
- He had a daughter — **Olaf's mother**. She died young. Gunnar raised Olaf alone.
- **His old house** is still in Wind's Rest. The attic still has a small chest where the bow was kept.
- **His daughter (Olaf's aunt)** still lives in Wind's Rest. She is old now. She remembers Olaf leaving with the bow.
- **Reveal schedule:** Season 3, if the party visits Wind's Rest.
