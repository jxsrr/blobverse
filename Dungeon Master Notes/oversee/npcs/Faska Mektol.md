> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Mother of the Mektol family. Wife of Herman. Mother of Cindy.
> **Where:** Died in Ghuffghuff
> **Status:** Deceased (eaten by Echo Sam)

## What the Party Knows
- Traveled to Ghuffghuff with her husband **Herman** and daughter **Cindy**.
- Kind. Warm. Held Cindy close during the journey.
- **Eaten by Echo Sam** on the first night in Ghuffghuff.
- Her dried corpse was found in Domido Forest — next to **Yahanda's corpse**.
- **Herman did not remember her.** When asked, he insisted only he and Cindy had come to Ghuffghuff.

## What the Party Remembers
- She was the first person they *watched* be forgotten.
- She gave the party dried fruit on the road. It was the last kindness before everything went wrong.
- **Her daughter Cindy survived.** Cindy does not remember her — but Cindy's mother loved her.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Faska was eaten on the first night. She was the second victim, after Yahanda.
- Her body was found the next morning beside Yahanda's. The party realized the pattern: Echo Sam eats, memory vanishes.
- **Her last words** — if the party ever finds a fragment of Echo Sam's memory or a Vision of the dead — were to Cindy: *"Be brave, my little star."*
- **Cindy has her mother's note.** It is in Cindy's small wooden box under her bed. The party has not found it yet.
- Faska's death is the emotional anchor for **Cindy's Season 3 arc**. If Cindy ever learns the truth, she may want revenge — or she may want to make sure the song that killed her mother is **never sung again**.
