%% DM SECRETS %%

### Who Livia Vane Is
- **Livia Vane is not a twin.** She was alone from the start.
- She was adopted as a child by an old man — who was later revealed to be **Lei Alejandro**, the betrayer of the Initial Six.
- Alejandro told her she was a twin who should have died — not to hurt her, but to protect her. He wanted her to understand why she was alone. He wanted her to know she was special. He was wrong. He lied. She believes it.
- She carries the guilt of a sister she never had. She has spent her whole life trying to be worthy of a life she thinks she stole.
- She is **not evil**. She is broken. She was raised by a man who lied to her. She was placed in a position she did not earn. She is trying to survive.

### The Twin-Killing Tradition — The Lie
- Livia believes she was the **younger twin** — the one who should have been killed.
- She believes her mother killed her sister instead, and hid her away. She believes she is living a life that was not meant for her.
- **None of this is true.** She was never a twin. She was an orphan. Alejandro found her. He made up the story. He thought it would give her purpose.
- She carries the guilt of a dead sister who never existed. She has never told anyone this. She has never questioned it.
- If she ever learns the truth — that Alejandro lied to her, that she was never a twin — she will break. She may hate him. She may forgive him. The choice is hers.

### Her Role in the Current Commonwealth
- Livia is the **Wakil Keadilan** — the Justice of the Triase. She leads the **Garda Mawar**.
- She is **not loyal to Valerius**. She knows he is waiting for the right moment to kill her. She does not know why.
- She is loyal to the **Commonwealth** — or at least, to the idea of it. She believes in order. She believes in law. She believes in the public Edisi Law (she does not know it is a lie).
- She is **terrified of Valerius**. She knows he is powerful. She knows he is patient. She knows he will kill her eventually. She does not know when.
- She is a **hostage of the party** (as of Session 07). She is not a prisoner in the traditional sense. She is a guest who cannot leave. She is trying to figure out who to trust.

### Valerius and Livia
- Valerius knows Livia is **not** his sister. He knows she was adopted by Lei Alejandro. He knows she was never a twin.
- He has known for years. He has been waiting for the right moment to kill her.
- Why? Because she is a **loose end**. Because she was adopted by the man who betrayed the Initial Six. Because she knows too much about the Triase. Because she is a threat to his power.
- He will try to kill her if she defects. He will try to kill her if she stays. He will try to kill her no matter what she does. She knows this. She does not know why.

### Livia's Role in Season 3
- If the party earns Livia's trust, she can be turned into an **ally**. She is not evil. She is broken. She wants to be good.
- If the party turns Livia, she will bring her **loyalists** with her. The Garda Mawar will fracture. Valerius will lose control of the Rose Guard.
- If the party turns Livia, she will ask them to help her find the truth about her past. She will want to know who she really is. She will want to meet Lei Alejandro.
- If the party helps her meet Alejandro, she will learn the truth — that she was never a twin, that Alejandro lied to her. She will break. She will weep. She will ask: *"Why did you lie to me?"* Alejandro will answer: *"Because I wanted you to be strong."*
- **Livia's fate is in the party's hands.** They can turn her. They can leave her. They can kill her. The choice is theirs.

### Livia and Ama
- Livia amputated Ama's arm in Chenalia. It was not personal. It was duty. She was following orders.
- She does not hate Ama. She does not even know him. She was doing her job.
- If the party confronts her about it, she will not apologize. She will say: *"I was doing my duty. You were in the way."* She is not cruel. She is a soldier.
- If Ama ever forgives her, she will be surprised. She will not know what to say. She will not understand why he would forgive her. She has never been forgiven for anything.
