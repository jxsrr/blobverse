## What the Party Knows
- An old man who raised **Vya** for 10 years after her mother **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Vreina%20Arleschia">Vreina</a>** disappeared.
- A wanderer. Not from Chenalia. Not from anywhere the party has heard of.
- Has **dragonborn blood** — faint scales on some parts of his skin, sharp eyes, a resilience that is not entirely human.
- Told Vya stories about the world. Taught her to survive. Taught her to hunt, to track, to read the land.
- Was the one who told Vya that her fire was not a curse — it was a gift. He did not know if that was true. He said it anyway.
- Disappeared when Vya left to join the Guild. She does not know where he went.

### Who Kopling Is
- **Kopling** is a **dragonborn-blooded wanderer** — not a full dragonborn, but not entirely human either. His skin has faint scales. His eyes are too sharp. He is older than he looks.
- He is a **distant relative of Gzirmo Kopling** — the dragonborn tinker of the Initial Six, the man who became Echo Gzirmo, the consciousness that runs Arujaya. They share the same diluted blood, the same faint connection to the ancient dragons.
- He has been wandering Oversee for decades. He has seen more of the world than most. He has learned to survive. He has learned to keep his promises.
- He is **not a hero**. He is not a villain. He is a man who has lived too long and seen too much — and who chose to spend his last years protecting a child who was not his own.

### The Pact with Vreina
- **Vreina Arleschia** sought Kopling out because her family had known him for years. He was the only one she could trust. She was a noblewoman. He was a wanderer. But he was kind. He kept his promises.
- Vreina told him about Vya. She told him about the fire. She told him about her dreams — the fire burning the village, Vya at its center, not as a victim but as its source. She did not know if it was a premonition or a nightmare. She did not want to find out.
- She asked him to protect Vya. She asked him to keep her safe until she was old enough to choose her own path. She asked him to keep the fire from consuming her.
- Kopling promised. He took Vya. He raised her. He taught her to survive. He taught her to fight. He taught her to love the world — even the parts of it that hated her.
- He kept his promise. He has not broken it. He does not intend to.

### The Raising of Vya
- Kopling raised Vya for **10 years** — from age 4 to age 14. He was not her father. He was not her grandfather. He was her guardian, her teacher, her friend.
- He taught her how to hunt. How to track. How to read the land. How to survive in the wilderness of Elis.
- He taught her about the **Grin** — the people with the eternal smile, the magic-deaf, the survivors. He taught her that the smile was not a curse. It was a shield.
- He taught her about the **fire**. He told her it was not a curse. It was a gift. He did not know if that was true. He said it anyway. He believed it.
- He told her stories about the world — about the Klan Lei, about the Commonwealth, about the Cycle Clan. He did not tell her the truth. He did not know the truth. He only knew what a wanderer knows — fragments, rumors, old songs.
- He taught her to be kind. He taught her to be strong. He taught her to be herself — not a noblewoman, not a Grin, not a Lei heir. Just Vya.

### The Disappearance
- Kopling disappeared when Vya left to join the Guild. He did not say goodbye. He did not say where he was going. He simply left.
- Vya does not know why. She does not know where he is. She does not know if he is alive.
- **He is alive.** He is wandering. He is watching. He is waiting.
- He knows Vya is with the party. He knows she is the True Heir. He knows she is in danger. He has been following her from a distance — not to interfere, but to protect. He promised Vreina. He keeps his promises.

### Kopling's Connection to Gzirmo
- Kopling and **Gzirmo Kopling** are **distant relatives** — from the same dragonborn bloodline that produced the tinker of the Initial Six.
- Gzirmo was the tinker of the Initial Six. He was betrayed by Lei Alejandro. He became the Echo that runs Arujaya. He is still there, still fading, still waiting.
- Kopling does not know this. He does not know Gzirmo became an Echo. He only knows the stories — the Kopling bloodline, the dragonborn ancestors, the faint connection to the ancient dragons.
- **If Kopling ever met Echo Gzirmo**, he would recognize the blood. He would feel the connection. He would weep. He would say: *"You are my ancestor. You are my blood. You are not forgotten."*
- Echo Gzirmo would recognize him too. It would be a moment of reunion — two fragments of the same family, separated by centuries, meeting at last.

### Kopling's Knowledge
- Kopling knows about **HVS shells**. He has heard the stories. He has seen the ruins of old Cycle Clan outposts. He has wondered if the shells were real.
- He does **not** know that **Husgus is HVS-01**. He has not met Husgus. He does not know Vya is traveling with a shell.
- He does **not** know that **Shin is HVS-02**. He has not met Shin. He does not know the party carries a shell.
- He suspects. He has been watching. He has been waiting. He is a wanderer. He notices things.
- If the party ever meets him, he will ask about the golden cracks. He will ask about the curse mark. He will ask about the smiles that do not fade. He will know more than he says.

### Kopling and Vya
- Kopling loves Vya. She is not his daughter. She is not his blood. But she is his child. He raised her. He taught her. He protected her. He would die for her.
- He has not seen her since she left. He has missed her every day. He has followed her from a distance. He has kept his promise.
- **If Vya ever sees Kopling again**, she will weep. She will hold him. She will say: *"You left. You did not say goodbye."* He will answer: *"I had to. You had to find your own path. I was always watching. I was always there."*
- She will forgive him. She always forgives him. He is her father — in every way that matters.

### Kopling and Vreina
- Kopling made a **pact** with Vreina. He promised to protect Vya. He promised to keep her safe. He promised to keep the fire from consuming her.
- He does not know Vreina is alive. He does not know she is being held in a secret Commonwealth facility in Elis. He thinks she is dead. He has mourned her.
- **If Kopling ever learns Vreina is alive**, he will not rest until he finds her. He will join the party. He will help them rescue her. He will keep his promise — not just to Vya, but to Vreina too.
- **If Kopling ever meets Vreina again**, he will not speak. He will simply nod. She will nod back. They will not need words. They made a pact. They kept it.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Kopling is a **distant relative of Gzirmo Kopling** — the dragonborn tinker of the Initial Six.
- Kopling made a **pact** with Vreina to protect Vya.
- Kopling **knows about the HVS shells**. He suspects something. He has been watching.
- Kopling is **alive**. He is wandering. He is following Vya. He is keeping his promise.
- Kopling is **not a fighter**. He is not a mage. He is a man who keeps his promises.

### Season 3 Reveal Schedule
- **Session 8–10** — The party learns about Kopling's connection to Gzirmo. They learn about the pact with Vreina. They learn he has been following them.
- **Session 11** — The party meets Kopling. He reveals what he knows. He asks about the golden cracks. He asks about the curse mark. He asks about the smiles that do not fade.
- **Session 11** — The party learns Vreina is alive. Kopling learns Vreina is alive. He asks to join them. He asks to help rescue her.
- **Endgame** — Kopling helps rescue Vreina. The reunion of Vreina and Vya. The reunion of Vreina and Kopling. It will be one of the most emotional moments of the campaign.

### Kopling's Fate
- If the party accepts Kopling, he will join them as an **NPC ally**. He is not a fighter. He is a tracker. He is a guide. He is a father. He will do whatever it takes to protect Vya.
- If the party rejects him, he will not fight. He will simply fade back into the shadows. He will continue to follow. He will continue to protect. He will keep his promise.
- If the party kills him, Vya will never forgive them. He is her father. He is the man who raised her. He is the man who kept his promise. He is the only one who never lied to her.
- **The choice is the party's.** The DM should present the options and let the party decide.
