## What the Party Knows
- Twin brother of **<a href="/Accarus%20Grin">Accarus</a>**. Both are descendants of **Lei Firage** (fire) through their father.
- Neither has full fire power. Antrem's is especially weak — dormant, almost silent.
- Was a simple shepherd in Elis. Married **<a href="/Vreina%20Arleschia">Vreina Arleschia</a>**.
- Left Vya when she was 4 years old, called by <a href="/Accarus%20Grin">Accarus</a> for a "greater purpose."
- Did not want to leave. Still loves Vya. Not ambitious, not clever — just kind.
- Alive. Living with Will of Grin. Not allowed to see Vya or write to her.

## Current Goal
Be useful to his brother. Survive. See Vya grow up — even if only from a distance.

### Who Antrem Is
- **Antrem Klaus** is the **twin brother of Accarus Grin**. He is the **son of Hildag Grin** and a **Lei Firage descendant**. He is the **father of Vya Arleschia**.
- He is the **younger twin** — born a few minutes after Accarus. Hildag hid them both. She protected them both. They were never registered. They were never separated — until the Great Strike.
- He carries the **eternal Grin smile**. He is magic-deaf. He is a survivor.
- He also carries **dormant Lei Firage fire** — a weak spark, barely alive. He cannot use it. He does not know it is there. It is the reason Hildag chose to breed with a Lei Firage descendant in the first place.

### The Great Strike — The Twin Who Was Lost
- Antrem and Accarus were born in Rioquan — the sons of Hildag and a Lei Firage descendant. Hildag had left them with a foster family to hide them from the Klan. She was in Pirizia, building Will of Grin.
- **The Great Strike** (674 AV) destroyed their home. The continent split. The sea raged. The sky burned.
- **Antrem was in the fields when it happened.** He was helping the foster father harvest wheat. The ground opened beneath him. He fell. He was swept away by the wave — carried out to sea, then washed ashore on the eastern coast of Rioquan.
- **He survived.** He did not know how. He did not know why. He only knew he was alive — and his brother was not.
- **He searched for Accarus for months.** He walked to refugee camps. He asked everyone he met. He showed Accarus's features to anyone who would listen. But no one had ever seen a young man with a face like his.
- **He stopped searching.** Not because he had forgotten Accarus, but because he had to live. He became a shepherd. He found peace.

### His Simplicity — Genuine, Not Drugged
- Antrem is **genuinely simple**. He is not pretending. He is not drugged. He is not hiding a sharper mind beneath a dull exterior.
- His simplicity is the result of three things:
  - **Hildag's experiments.** She bred him for anti-magic and Lei fire. She did not breed for cognitive sharpness. He was the rough draft — Accarus was the sharper one, Antrem the kinder one.
  - **The Great Strike.** The disaster that split Rioquan and separated him from his twin broke something in him. He was never the same after.
  - **The separation from Accarus.** Twins are not meant to be apart. The Grin twins especially. Antrem has spent decades without his other half. It has left him quieter, slower, more lost.
- He is not stupid. He is not broken. He is **simple** — in the way a quiet man is simple. He is kind. He is patient. He is gentle. He is everything Hildag did not want him to be.

### Antrem and Vreina — The Life He Built
- After the Great Strike, Antrem settled in **Nesia Town** — a farming village in Harka. He became a shepherd. He lived quietly. He did not speak of his past. He did not speak of his brother. He did not speak of the Great Strike.
- Years later, fate brought him to **Elis**. A sheep merchant he knew offered him work on the other continent. Antrem — who had no ties left in Rioquan — accepted.
- In Elis, he met **<a href="/Vreina%20Arleschia">Vreina Arleschia</a>** — a woman from a minor noble family who was not particularly ambitious.
- Vreina was drawn to this quiet young man — not for his wealth or his looks, but for his **simplicity**. In her eyes, Antrem was unlike other men who only wanted to raise their status by marrying a noble. He only wanted to live quietly, tend sheep, and perhaps one day have a family.
- They married in a small ceremony attended only by Vreina's family and a few of Antrem's neighbors. Antrem knew nothing about dowries or noble traditions — he came with the best clothes he had, a bunch of wildflowers, and a **sheep** as a gift.
- Vreina's family — already tired of noble pretense — liked this sincerity.
- Years later, **<a href="/Vya%20Arleschia">Vya</a>** was born — their daughter, a child with distinctive **pink hair**, sharp eyes, and a smile that never faded even though she was not a Grin descendant.
- **Or perhaps, between the blood of a simple shepherd and the blood of a minor noble, something older had awakened** — something connected to the **Lei Firage** bloodline, to the fire that no longer burned in Antrem, to a destiny not yet revealed.

### The Summons — Why He Left
- Years later — when Vya was 4 years old — **Accarus came to Chenalia**. He found Antrem. He told him he was needed. He told him it was for a "greater purpose." He told him the Grin needed him.
- Antrem did not want to go. He did not want to leave Vya. He did not want to leave Vreina. But Accarus was his brother. Accarus was his other half. Accarus had found him after decades of separation. He could not say no.
- He left. He promised Vya he would return. He did not return.
- **He has been in the Maw Desert ever since.** He is not a prisoner. He is a guest who cannot leave. He is not allowed to see Vya. He is not allowed to write to her. He is not allowed to know what his blood is being used for.
- **He does not know why he is there.** He does not know that Hildag and Accarus are using his blood — his dormant Lei Firage fire — to try to breed a new heir. He does not know about Vya's potential. He does not know about Hildag's magnum opus. He only knows he is helping his brother. He only knows he is waiting.

### Antrem and Vya
- Antrem **loves Vya** more than anything in the world. He left her when she was 4. He has not seen her since. He thinks about her every day.
- He does **not** know Vya is the True Heir of Lei Firage. He does **not** know Hildag has been watching her. He does **not** know she is the magnum opus. He only knows she is his daughter — the little girl who chased butterflies in the meadows of Chenalia.
- If Antrem ever sees Vya again, he will weep. He will hold her. He will say: *"You grew up. You grew up without me. I am sorry."* He will not ask for forgiveness. He will not expect it. He will simply be glad she is alive.
- **If Vya ever learns the truth about her father** — that he left her because Accarus called him, that he has been waiting for her in the Maw Desert, that he never stopped loving her — she will break. She will weep. She will ask: *"Why did you leave?"* He will answer: *"Because I could not say no to my brother. I should have. I should have stayed. I am sorry."*

### Antrem and Accarus
- Accarus and Antrem are **twins**. They were separated by The Great Strike. Accarus survived. Antrem was swept away. They found each other again — decades later — through Will of Grin's network.
- Accarus is the leader. Antrem is the guest. Accarus is the one who makes the choices. Antrem is the one who follows.
- Accarus loves his brother. He does not know how to tell him the truth. He does not know how to apologize. He does not know how to tell him that his blood is being used, that his daughter is the True Heir, that Jarko is dead because of the plan.
- **If Antrem ever learns the truth** — that Accarus let Jarko die, that Hildag used him, that they are using his blood — he will not be angry. He will be **sad**. He will say: *"I would have helped. You did not have to lie."* He will forgive them. He will always forgive them. He is too kind for the world.

### Antrem and Hildag
- Antrem does **not** know Hildag is his mother. He was raised by a foster family after The Great Strike. He does not remember his birth parents. Accarus told him their parents died in the disaster. Accarus did not tell him Hildag was still alive. Accarus did not tell him Hildag was the Weaver.
- If Antrem ever learns Hildag is his mother — the woman who bred him, the woman who used him, the woman who let Jarko die — he will not be angry. He will be **confused**. He will say: *"I have a mother?"* He will not understand. He will not know how to feel. He is too simple for hatred.
- **Hildag does not visit him.** She does not speak to him. She does not look at him. She sees him as a resource — a bloodline to be used. She does not see him as a son. She has not seen him as a son since the day she bred him.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Antrem is the **son of Hildag Grin** — not just a Grin shepherd. He is her son.
- Antrem is the **twin brother of Accarus** — Vya's uncle is also her father's brother. He is Vya's father.
- **Antrem was the twin who was lost.** Accarus survived the Great Strike. Antrem was swept away.
- Antrem is **genuinely simple**. He is not drugged. He is not pretending. He is a kind, quiet man who has been used his whole life.
- Antrem does **not** know his blood is being used. He does not know Hildag is his mother. He does not know Jarko is dead. He does not know Vya is the True Heir.
- Antrem has been waiting in the Maw Desert for years, hoping to see Vya again. He has not been allowed.

### Season 3 Reveal Schedule
- **Session 8–10** — The party learns about Hildag's role as Wakil Pertahanan. They learn about the twins — Accarus and Antrem. They learn about Jarko's sacrifice. They learn that Antrem is Vya's father. They learn that Antrem was the twin who was lost.
- **Session 11** — The party confronts Hildag and Accarus about Antrem. They learn he has been used. They learn he has been waiting. The party must decide what to do with him.
- **Endgame** — If the party reunites Antrem and Vya, it will be one of the most emotional moments of the campaign. He will weep. She will weep. They will hold each other. They will not speak. They will not need to.

### Antrem's Fate
- If the party reunites him with Vya, he will stay with her. He will follow her. He will protect her. He will die for her if he has to.
- If the party leaves him in the Maw Desert, he will stay. He will wait. He will never stop waiting.
- If the party tells him the truth about Jarko and Hildag, he will forgive them. He will always forgive them. He is too kind for the world.
- **The choice is the party's.** The DM should present the options and let the party decide.
