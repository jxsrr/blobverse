### Who Edra Is
- **Edra** was a member of **The Grace** — the independent demon-hunter order based in Elis. She was not the leader. She was not the strongest. She was not the smartest. She was the **heart**.
- She was **warm**. She was **loyal**. She was **kind**. She was the one who welcomed new recruits. She was the one who remembered their names. She was the one who made sure everyone had someone to sit beside at dinner.
- She met **Shin** on his first day. He was standing in the corner, alone, wearing gloves, smiling a smile that did not reach his eyes. She walked up to him, sat down beside him, and said: *"You look like you need a friend."*
- She never asked about the gloves. She never asked about the smile. She never asked about his past. She did not care. He was her friend. That was enough.

### Edra and Shin
- Edra was **Shin's best friend**. She was the first person who treated him as a person — not as a weapon, not as a mystery, not as a curse. She was the first person who made him feel like he belonged.
- She taught him to fight. She taught him to track. She taught him to survive. But more importantly, she taught him to **laugh**. She taught him to **trust**. She taught him to **love**.
- **She knew he carried something.** She saw the curse mark glow in the dark. She saw the shadows move when he was angry. She saw the way he flinched when someone said the word "demon."
- **She did not care.** She did not ask. She did not judge. She simply said: *"Whatever you carry, you carry it with me."*
- **She wrote him a letter** before she died. She never had the chance to deliver it. It is in **Zundabun**, held by **Moro** the shadowless bartender. It says: *"Shin, I know you're hiding something. Don't run. We are your family, whatever you carry."*

### The Fall of The Grace — 690 AV
- **690 AV.** A massive Gap tear opened in Elis. Demons poured through. The Grace fought. They held the line. They died.
- **Edra fought beside Shin.** She did not run. She did not hide. She stood with him. She died with him — or so she thought.
- **Shin was possessed by Zargoyle.** He did not choose it. He did not want it. It happened in the chaos of the tear.
- **Zargoyle opened a small seal of Zargoth inside Shin** — accidentally, violently. The resulting explosion destroyed The Grace. It destroyed Elis.
- **Edra died in the explosion.** She did not blame Shin. She did not have time to blame anyone. She died trying to save him. She died trying to reach him. She died believing he was still in there.
- **Her body was never found.** The explosion destroyed everything. The ruins of The Grace are still in Elis — a stone keep built into the side of a mountain, hidden from the world, protected by old wards. Her body may still be there. Or it may have been vaporized. No one knows.

### Edra and Crest
- **Crest** was Edra's closest friend in The Grace. They were opposites — she was warm, he was cold. She was impulsive, he was disciplined. She was the heart, he was the brain. They balanced each other.
- **They both loved Shin.** Not in the same way — Edra loved him as a brother, Crest loved him as a comrade — but they both loved him. They both tried to save him. They both died trying.
- **Edra's letter** — the one she wrote before she died — was written for Shin. **Crest's notes** — the ones he kept on Shin — were written for himself. Both of them were trying to understand him. Both of them were trying to protect him. Both of them failed.
- **If the party ever finds Edra's diary** — a full account of her time in The Grace — they will find entries about Shin. They will find entries about Crest. They will find entries about the Gap. They will find the truth about the fall — from her perspective. It is not the truth of the victors. It is the truth of a woman who loved her friends and died trying to save them.

### Edra's Letter — The Words She Never Said
- **The letter is in Zundabun.** It is held by **Moro** the shadowless bartender. He has been carrying it for 690 years. He promised Edra he would deliver it. He has been waiting for Shin to walk into his shop.
- **The letter says:** *"Shin, I know you're hiding something. Don't run. We are your family, whatever you carry. Whatever you are. Whatever you become. You are not alone. I will find you. I will always find you. — Edra."*
- **Shin has never read it.** He does not know it exists. He thinks she blamed him. He thinks she died hating him. He is wrong.
- **If Shin ever reads the letter**, he will break. He will weep. He will smile. He always smiles. But for the first time in 690 years, the smile will reach his eyes. He will know he was loved. He will know he was not alone.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Edra **did not blame Shin**. She tried to save him. She died trying to reach him.
- **Edra wrote him a letter.** It is in Zundabun. He has never read it.
- **Edra's body was never found.** It may still be in the ruins of The Grace. It may have been vaporized.
- **Edra's diary** is still in the ruins of The Grace. It contains her full account of the fall — from her perspective.
- **Edra and Crest both loved Shin.** They both tried to save him. They both died trying.
- **Edra's last words** — whispered to Crest as the explosion consumed them — were: *"Tell him it wasn't his fault. Tell him we loved him. Tell him to smile."*
