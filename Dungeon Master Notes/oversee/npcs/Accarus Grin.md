## What the Party Knows
- Twin brother of **[[Antrem Klaus|Antrem]]** (Vya's father).
- Husband of **[[Geneva Grin|Geneva]]**. Father of **[[Jarko]]** (deceased).
- Carried **Gustaero** — gave it to Husgus after the Frosthaven mission.
- Wants to collect the Gemhearts. Wants to avenge the Grin people. Wants to find the truth.
- Morally gray. Willing to sacrifice for the cause.
- Killed **Echo Sam** to prevent the party from learning too much.
- Offered the party a place in Will of Grin (Session 03). Gave them seven days to decide.

Avenge Jarko. Protect the Grin people. Find the truth. Complete his mother's plan.

### Who Accarus Is
- **Accarus Grin** is the **son of Hildag Grin** — the founder of Will of Grin, the Weaver, the former Wakil Pertahanan of the original Commonwealth.
- He is the **twin brother of Antrem Klaus** — Vya's father. They are the only surviving twins of the Klan Lei's twin-killing tradition. Hildag hid them. Hildag protected them. They were never registered. They were never separated — until the Great Strike.
- His father was a **Lei Firage descendant** — a man Hildag chose to breed with, to combine the Grin anti-magic with the Lei fire. Accarus and Antrem were the accidental success. Vya was the magnum opus.
- He carries the **eternal Grin smile**. He is magic-deaf. He is a survivor.
- He is **not a villain**. He is a morally gray leader who has made terrible choices for a good cause. He is his mother's son.

### The Great Strike — The Twin Who Stayed
- Accarus and Antrem were born in Rioquan — the sons of Hildag and a Lei Firage descendant. Hildag had left them with a foster family to hide them from the Klan. She was in Pirizia, building Will of Grin.
- **The Great Strike** (674 AV) destroyed their home. The continent split. The sea raged. The sky burned.
- **Accarus survived.** He was in the cellar when the earthquake hit — the foster family's root cellar, where they stored grain. The stone walls held. The earth split around the house, but the cellar remained. He was buried for three days. When he crawled out, the world was broken — but he was alive.
- **Antrem was not so lucky.** Antrem was in the fields when the strike hit. The ground opened beneath him. He was swept away by the wave. Accarus searched for him for weeks. He never found him. He believed Antrem was dead.
- **Hildag found Accarus** — not immediately, but weeks later. She had felt the Great Strike from Pirizia. She had felt the world break. She came to Rioquan to find her sons. She found one. She buried the foster family. She took Accarus with her.
- **Accarus grew up in Will of Grin.** He was raised by his mother — though he did not know she was his mother until years later. He was raised by the Grin. He was raised by the cause.

### Hildag's Revelation — The Truth About His Mother
- For years, Accarus thought Hildag was just the leader of Will of Grin — a woman who had taken him in out of kindness. He did not know she was his mother. He did not know she had bred him. He did not know she had hidden him from the Klan.
- **She told him the truth** — eventually. After Jarko was born. After he had proven himself. She told him: *"I am your mother. I hid you from the Klan. I hid you from the world. I am sorry."*
- He forgave her. He always forgives her. She is his mother. She is the only family he has left — besides Antrem, who he believes is dead.

### Geneva — The Woman Who Loved Him
- **Geneva** was not the woman who rescued Accarus from the sea. That was a story the party heard — a story that was wrong. Geneva did not find Accarus floating on a piece of wood. Accarus was never swept to sea.
- **Geneva was a Grin woman from a different family** — a smaller, less influential bloodline. She was one of Hildag's experiments — bred for a specific combination of Grin blood. She did not know it. She still does not know it.
- **She met Accarus in Will of Grin.** They grew up together — not as children, but as young adults. They trained together. They fought together. They fell in love slowly, like a fire kindling from small embers.
- **Geneva saw in Accarus a steadfastness** she had been looking for — not the steadfastness of a warrior, but the steadfastness of someone who had lost everything yet still endured.
- **Accarus saw in Geneva's smile** something he had never seen before: **acceptance without conditions**.
- They married in a small ceremony attended only by members of Will of Grin. Hildag was there. She smiled. She always smiles.

### Jarko's Death — The Sacrifice
- **Jarko was not the True Heir.** Hildag knew this. Accarus knew this.
- They used Jarko as a **decoy** — a false heir to distract the Commonwealth, to draw attention away from Vya, to buy time.
- When **Iron Gort** attacked the Maw Desert hideout (Session 05), Hildag and Accarus made the call. They left Jarko behind. They teleported away with an emergency scroll. They did not tell Geneva.
- Jarko died in front of the party. His skull was crushed. His blood pooled on the stone floor.
- **Accarus has not forgiven himself.** He does not speak of it. He does not weep. He smiles. He always smiles. It is the Grin curse. But inside, he is broken.
- **Geneva does not know.** She believes Jarko died a hero. She does not know he was sacrificed. If she ever learns the truth, she will break. She may hate Accarus. She may leave him. She may never forgive him.

### Gustaero — The Bow He Carried
- Accarus carried **Gustaero** — the Swan Bow, the neck of the swan, one of the three ancient relics.
- He received it from Hildag, who had kept it hidden for centuries. She gave it to him when he became co-leader of field operations. She told him: *"This is not a weapon. It is a promise. Use it to protect our people."*
- He carried it for decades. He never used it to kill. He used it to protect.
- After the Frosthaven mission (Session 05), he gave it to **Husgus**. He saw something in the halfling — a kindness, a strength, a connection to the bow that he himself had never had. He said: *"It was never mine. It was waiting for you."*
- **Accarus never carried Rubion.** Rubion was taken by the party from Jarko in Session 1, and later given to Mira. Accarus has never held it. He does not know where it is.

### The Killing of Echo Sam
- **Accarus killed Echo Sam** (Session 03) — not out of cruelty, but out of necessity.
- Echo Sam was about to reveal the truth about the **Initial Six** — the betrayal, the sacrifice, the murder. Accarus was not ready for the party to know. He needed them to trust Will of Grin. He needed them to join him. He could not let Echo Sam speak.
- He shot Echo Sam with an arrow. He watched him fall. He did not explain. He simply said: *"You did well. Better than I expected."*
- **He has never apologized.** He does not think he was wrong. He thinks he was protecting the cause. He is his mother's son.

### Accarus and Vya
- Accarus knows **Vya is the True Heir**. Hildag told him. He has known since before Jarko's death. He has been watching her. He has been waiting for her.
- He does **not** know Vya is his **niece**. Hildag did not tell him about Antrem's child. She did not tell him that Vya is the daughter of his twin brother. He will learn this in Season 3. It will break him.
- If Accarus learns Vya is his niece, he will not know what to say. He will look at her and see Antrem. He will see the brother he lost. He will see the daughter he never knew. He will weep. He will smile. He will say: *"You have your father's eyes."*

### Accarus and Antrem
- Accarus and Antrem are **twins**. They were separated by The Great Strike. Accarus survived. Antrem was swept away. Accarus believed Antrem was dead.
- Accarus found Antrem years later — through Will of Grin's network. He brought him to the Maw Desert. He told him it was for a "greater purpose." He did not tell him the full truth. He did not tell him about Vya. He did not tell him about Hildag's plan.
- **Antrem is not a prisoner.** He is a guest who cannot leave. He is not allowed to see Vya. He is not allowed to write to her. He is not allowed to know what his blood is being used for.
- Accarus loves his brother. He does not know how to tell him the truth. He does not know how to apologize. He is his mother's son.

### Season 3 Reveal Schedule
- **Session 8–10** — The party learns about Hildag's role as Wakil Pertahanan. They learn about the original promise. They learn about the twins — Accarus and Antrem. They learn about Jarko's sacrifice. They learn that Accarus survived the Great Strike — and Antrem was the one who was lost.
- **Session 11** — The party confronts Accarus about Jarko. He does not deny it. He says: *"I did what I had to do. You would have done the same."* The party must decide what to do with him.
- **Session 11** — Accarus learns Vya is his niece. He breaks. He weeps. He smiles. He says: *"You have your father's eyes."*
- **Endgame** — Accarus must decide what to do with his life. He can stay with Will of Grin. He can leave. He can join the party. He can try to make amends with Geneva. The choice is his.

### Accarus's Fate
- If the party tells Geneva the truth about Jarko, Accarus will lose her. She will leave him. She will never forgive him. He will be alone.
- If the party keeps the secret, Accarus will stay with Geneva. They will fight together. They will die together. They will never speak of Jarko again.
- If the party helps Accarus make amends, he may find a way to forgive himself. He may find a way to be the man his mother wanted him to be — or the man his brother thought he was.
- **The choice is the party's.** The DM should present the options and let the party decide.
