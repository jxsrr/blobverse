<div class="bv-intro-box">
  <p>Five strangers registered together at the Adventurer's Guild in [[oversee/locations/Yawning Town|Yawning Town]] under the name <strong>The Five Sticks</strong>. What began as a simple relic retrieval in [[oversee/locations/Greyland Castle|Greyland Castle]] spiraled into a continent-spanning conflict between the [[oversee/factions/One Voice Commonwealth|One Voice Commonwealth]], the [[oversee/factions/Will of Grin|Will of Grin]], and the [[oversee/factions/Keeper of Secret|Keeper of Secret]].</p>
  <p>Across the four continents of Harka, Pirizia, Rioquan, and Elis, ancient seals are cracking — and the truth behind the Gemhearts and the Human Vessel Shell project is coming to light.</p>
