## What Matters Here
- An underwater kingdom of coral, pearl, and bioluminescence, deep beneath the Elis Ocean.
- Protected by a **pressure veil** — a magical barrier that keeps the water from crushing the city and keeps the surface world from finding it.
- Inhabited by the **Kuo Lau** — an ancient, noble race of the deep. Not kuo-toa. Older. Prouder.
- The Kuo Lau do not trade with the surface. They do not welcome visitors. They do not trust air-breathers.
- The **Kuo Lau King** rules here. He is old — older than most mortals. He has held **Alnean** — the breath of the swan — for centuries.
- The kingdom is in danger. The Gap is thinning. The pressure veil is weakening. The King has felt something stirring in the deep.

## The Layout
- **The Pressure Veil** — the outer barrier. A shimmering dome of compressed water and old magic. It keeps the deep out and the city in. It is weakening.
- **The Coral Gates** — the entrance to the city. Guarded by Kuo Lau sentinels. Only those who carry Kuo Lau blood — or who are escorted by a Kuo Lau — may pass.
- **The Pearl Market** — the central square. Bioluminescent coral stalls. Trade in shells, pearls, and deep-sea relics. No surface coin.
- **The King's Spire** — the tallest coral tower. The Kuo Lau King's throne room. Alnean is kept here.
- **The Deep Tunnels** — the network of caves that connects the Kuo Lau Kingdom to the surface — specifically to the **Kuil Lautan** in Juto Port City. This is the path Babibubebo can lead the party through.
- **The Singing Reef** — a sacred grove of coral where the Kuo Lau sing to the deep. They believe the song keeps the pressure veil alive. They may be right.

## What the Party Knows
- They have never been here.
- They met **Babibubebo** — a small fish-man who lives in the Kuil Lautan in Juto Port City. They do not know he is from here.
- They have never heard of Alnean.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### The Kuo Lau Kingdom — Full Canon
- The Kuo Lau Kingdom is **not a myth**. It is real. It is hidden. It is in danger.
- The Kuo Lau are an **ancient race** — older than the Commonwealth, older than the Cycle Clan. They have lived in the deep since before the Rapture.
- They are **isolationist**. They have seen what the surface world does to the sea. They have chosen to stay below.
- They are **not hostile**. They are **wary**. They will not attack the party unless the party attacks first. But they will not welcome them either.

### The Kuo Lau King
- The **Kuo Lau King** is old — older than most mortals. He has ruled the deep for centuries.
- He is **Babibubebo's father**. He has been mourning his lost son for years. He does not know Babi is alive.
- He holds **Alnean** — the breath of the swan. He knows what it is. He knows what it does. He has never used it.
- He will only give Alnean to someone who **brings Babibubebo home**. He will weep when he sees his son. He will hold him. He will speak his true name — **Baelor**.
- He will test the party before giving them Alnean. The test is not combat. It is a **question**: *"Why do you want to breathe where you were not meant to breathe?"*
- If the party answers truthfully — if they say they want to survive so they can save someone else — the King will let them take Alnean.
- If they lie, the King will not attack. He will simply **close the gates**. Alnean will be lost to them.

### The Pressure Veil
- The pressure veil is a **magical barrier** that keeps the water from crushing the city and keeps the surface world from finding it.
- It is **weakening**. The Gap is thinning. The deep is stirring. The King has felt something moving in the dark.
- If the pressure veil fails, the Kuo Lau Kingdom will be exposed — to the surface, to the Commonwealth, and to whatever is stirring in the deep.
- The **Singing Reef** is the key to the pressure veil. The Kuo Lau sing to the deep, and the deep sings back. The song keeps the veil alive. If the singing stops, the veil will fall.

### The Deep Tunnels
- The Deep Tunnels connect the Kuo Lau Kingdom to the **Kuil Lautan** in Juto Port City.
- They are dark, cold, and long. They are guarded by **deep creatures** — not hostile, but territorial.
- Only a Kuo Lau can navigate them. **Babibubebo** is the only Kuo Lau who knows the way — and even he does not remember it clearly.
- The party will need **water-breathing magic** to survive the descent. Alnean would solve this — but they do not have Alnean yet. This is a chicken-and-egg problem. The DM must decide how the party survives the descent. Options:
  - **Potions of Water Breathing** — expensive, but available in Juto Port City.
  - **Syl's blood** — his blood is a universal cure. It might allow the party to breathe water. (DM decides if this works.)
  - **A favor from Vythrax** — the silver dragon can grant water-breathing for a short time.
  - **A Kuo Lau Pearl** — Babi might have one. He does not remember. If the party searches his belongings, they might find a small pearl that glows faintly. It is a Kuo Lau Pearl. It allows the bearer to breathe underwater for 1 hour per day.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- The Kuo Lau Kingdom is real. It is in danger. The pressure veil is weakening.
- Babibubebo is a prince. His father is the King. He has been mourning him for years.
- Alnean is here. It is the breath of the swan. Without it, the third path is impossible.
- The **Singing Reef** is the key to the pressure veil. If the singing stops, the veil will fall.
- The Kuo Lau are not hostile. They are wary. They can be allies — if the party earns their trust.
- The **Kuo Lau Pearl** — if the party finds one — allows the bearer to breathe underwater for 1 hour per day. It is not Alnean. It is a gift. It is a thank you.

### The Reunion
- When the Kuo Lau King sees Babibubebo, he will weep. He will hold him. He will speak his true name — **Baelor** — and Babi will remember.
- This is a **Season 3 emotional beat**. The party brought a lost son home.
- The King will give them **Alnean** — if they pass the test.
- He will also give them a **Kuo Lau Pearl** — a small pearl that allows the bearer to breathe underwater for 1 hour per day.
- He will ask the party to **help save the Kuo Lau Kingdom** — the pressure veil is weakening, and the deep is stirring. This is a **Season 3 side quest**.

### The Kuo Lau Kingdom in the Endgame
- If the party helps save the Kuo Lau Kingdom, the Kuo Lau will **join the final battle**. They are not many, but they are fierce. They can fight in the water, in the Gap, in the void.
- If the party ignores the Kuo Lau Kingdom, the pressure veil will fail. The Kuo Lau will be scattered. The deep will be exposed. The Gap will spread.
- The Kuo Lau Kingdom is **not optional** for the third path. The party needs Alnean. They need the Kuo Lau's help.
