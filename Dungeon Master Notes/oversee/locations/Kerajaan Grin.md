## What Matters Here
- The ancestral homeland of the **Grin bloodline** — a people marked by the **[[oversee/lore/The Grin Curse|eternal smile]]**.
- Birthplace of **[[oversee/npcs/Grin Qain|Grin Qain]]**, who left her homeland to seek a cure for her family's curse, and Prince **[[oversee/npcs/Atreus Grin|Atreus Grin]]**, hero of the marginalized and member of **[[oversee/lore/The Initial Six|The Initial Six]]**.
- Following **[[oversee/lore/The Great Disappearance|The Great Disappearance]]**, as Atreus's name faded from public memory, the kingdom gradually fractured and collapsed.

## What the Party Knows
- Historical records from the Initial Six era and accounts from **[[oversee/factions/Will of Grin|Will of Grin]]** describe it as a marginalized realm abandoned after Atreus vanished.
