## What Matters Here
- The ancestral homeland of the **Grin bloodline** — a people marked by the **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Grin%20Curse">eternal smile</a>**.
- Birthplace of **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Grin%20Qain">Grin Qain</a>**, who left her homeland to seek a cure for her family's curse, and Prince **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Atreus%20Grin">Atreus Grin</a>**, hero of the marginalized and member of **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Initial%20Six">The Initial Six</a>**.
- Following **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Great%20Disappearance">The Great Disappearance</a>**, as Atreus's name faded from public memory, the kingdom gradually fractured and collapsed.

## What the Party Knows
- Historical records from the Initial Six era and accounts from **<a href="/oversee/factions/Will%20of%20Grin">Will of Grin</a>** describe it as a marginalized realm abandoned after Atreus vanished.
::/pf

- The fall of Kerajaan Grin was accelerated by **Klan Lei** and **The Commonwealth**, who erased Atreus's legacy and hunted surviving Grin bloodlines to prevent anti-magic lineages from uniting.
