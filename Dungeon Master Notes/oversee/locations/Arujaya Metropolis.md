## What Matters Here
- Underground cyberpunk city. Built from scrap, steam, and neon.
- Inhabited by short, dirty, defensive people.
- **The Core** — middle layer. Markets, homes, mesh network.
- **The Depths** — bottom layer. Toxic smog, Ambitio-Ether sludge, the reactor.
- **Mnemonic Archive** — data repository. Echo Gzirmo is here.
- **Modification Parlor** — Dr. Vektor's cybernetic clinic.
