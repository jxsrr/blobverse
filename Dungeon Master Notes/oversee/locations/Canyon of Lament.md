## What Matters Here
- A canyon so deep no one has ever reached the bottom and returned.
- Legend: the gods wept here, and their tears carved the canyon.
- **Zenahhi** (the legendary longsword) was thrown into this canyon by Lei Firsa.
- The canyon walls are covered in **old carvings** — prayers, names, warnings.
- The deeper you go, the darker and hotter it gets. The air smells of iron.

## Current State
- Unmapped. Unclaimed. Extremely dangerous.


%% DM SECRETS %%
- The canyon is not bottomless. It leads to a **Cycle Clan ruin** and a cult that worships the tears.
- Zenahhi is held by a **Teary Knight** — a spirit bound to the blade.
- The cult believes the sword is a divine gift. They will not give it up easily.
- The sword glows brighter the closer it is to its true wielder. It is waiting.
