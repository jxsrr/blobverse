## What Matters Here
- A vast, untamed continent across the sea from **[[oversee/locations/Harka|Harka]]**, where primordial nature remains largely untouched by modern cities.
- Home to the last surviving ancient dragons — **[[oversee/lore/The Three Surviving Dragons|Verdantus]]** beneath the *Kanopi Tanpa Ujung* in the Eastern Wilds, and **[[oversee/lore/The Three Surviving Dragons|Aurelia]]** in the depths of *Danau Ajaib*.
- Homeland of **[[oversee/npcs/Gzirmo Kopling|Gzirmo]]**, the quietest warrior of **[[oversee/lore/The Initial Six|The Initial Six]]**.

## What the Party Knows
- Known through historical chronicles as an ancient, perilous wilderness beyond the main Commonwealth territories.
