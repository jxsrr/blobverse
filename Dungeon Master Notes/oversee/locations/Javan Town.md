## What Matters Here
- Working port town. Coal dust, forklifts, labor unions.
- **Dockworkers' Union** — stevedore union. Led by **Mr. Basir**.
- **Kuil Valla** — neglected. Father Tulus spends more time burying workers than preaching. He knows about dark port operations but stays silent.
- **The Docks** — run by **Sasimo**, a large, rough man with a mechanical hand. Won't talk to strangers unless they say "<a href="/oversee/npcs/Accarus%20Grin">Accarus</a>."
- **The Salty Gill** — tavern. **Junior** (bartender) says: *"People with weird smiles (Will of Grin) often smuggle weapons through here."*
- **Jiopa Cemetery** — the party slept here once.
- **Dora's Rhino Stable** — reliable transport. 200 gp to Padre City.

## What Happened Here
- The party arrived here on their way to Padre City (<a href="/oversee/sessions/Session%2004%20%E2%80%94%20Territorial">Session 04</a>).
- They learned about the Triase from Commonwealth soldiers.
- They met Mr. Andreas here.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Javan Town is a major smuggling hub for Will of Grin. Sasimo is a Grin agent.
- Father Tulus is not corrupt — he is just broken. He knows who killed the port workers but can't prove it.
- The port tunnels connect to an underground safehouse used by Keeper of Secret.
