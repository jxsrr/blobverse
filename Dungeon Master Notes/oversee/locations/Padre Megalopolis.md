## What Matters Here
- Built on a mesa. White granite walls 15 meters high.
- Streets absorb sunlight and glow softly at night. **The darkness never touches the Golden Ring.**
- Home of the **Governor's Palace**, **Tower of Time**, **Fortress of Justice**.
- The **Festival of the Great Lei** is held here every year.
- The **Lyceum University of Arcane Arts** is here. Professor Donta taught here.

## What Happened Here
- The Five Sticks watched the Festival parade (<a href="/oversee/sessions/Session%2004%20%E2%80%94%20Territorial">Session 04</a>).
- Met the Triase for the first time.
- Donta committed suicide after the party confronted him.
- Howdy was found dead.

## Current State
- Tense. Valerius is consolidating power. Livia is missing (hostage).

- Padre City is the political heart of the Commonwealth.
- The Tower of Time is where the Calendar was set. Its clock runs backwards — no one knows why.
