## What Matters Here
- A colossal ice stalagmite. Taller than the clouds.
- Surrounded by a perpetual storm. No one can climb it without protection.
- **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a>'s lair.** The silver dragon lives at the top.
- The air inside the spire is so cold it deals cold damage every round.
- Dozens of **ice statues** line the entrance — not adventurers frozen in place, but monsters and ancient dragonborn. <a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a> froze them.
- Deep inside: **Frostfang** is buried in ice. Waiting.

## Current State
- Open. <a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a> is alive. The storm has weakened slightly.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- The ice statues are alive. They are not dead. If <a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a> dies, they thaw.
- Frostfang is not just in the ice — it is **part** of the ice. Taking it will cause a minor collapse.
- The Spire is the tallest point in Pirizia. From the top, you can see the Shell in the sky.
