## What Matters Here
- Starting town. Home of **The Yawning Guild**.
- Surrounded by green forests and rice fields.
- Great stone temple to **Lei the Great** at the center.
- The Guild is where adventurers register and get jobs.

## What Happened Here
- The Five Sticks formed here (<a href="/oversee/sessions/Session%2001%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Sorcerer">Session 01</a>).
