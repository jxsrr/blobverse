## What Matters Here

### Zundabun
- **Hourglass Square** — a central monument: a giant hourglass. At its base: a half-erased inscription: *"We built to forget, but stone always remembers."*
- **The Eternal Scaffold District** — permanently under construction. The buildings are never finished.
- **The Coffee Shop "Almost"** — a small coffee shop. **Moro** (the shadowless bartender) serves.
  - Contains the **Undelivered Letter** — an old envelope with a blue-silver seal (The Grace symbol). It is a letter from Edra to Shin, never delivered: *"Shin, I know you're hiding something. Don't run. We are your family, whatever you carry."*
- **The Half House** — a house that is only half-built. **Rivan** (a middle-aged man with tired eyes) lives here. He believes if he finishes the house, he will forget his lost wife and children.
  - Rivan drew the **original sketches of HVS-01 and HVS-02**.
- **The Memory Corridor** — a corridor with names carved into the walls. Names of people long dead.
- **The Vanished Archive Building** — an archive that no longer exists.

## What the Party Knows
- They have not been here yet.
- It is connected to Graen via the canal network.
- Rivan drew the HVS sketches.

## Current State
- Active. Under Commonwealth surveillance (subtle).

%% DM SECRETS %%
- **Rivan** is not crazy. He is a **former Cycle Clan artist** who drew the original HVS schematics. He knows what HVS-01 and HVS-02 are.
- **Moro** (the shadowless bartender) is not human. He is a **Gap-touched entity** who lost his shadow when he crossed over. He is friendly.
- The **Undelivered Letter** from Edra to Shin is a **key emotional item** for Shin's arc. If he reads it, he gains a permanent +1 to Wisdom saves.
- **The Half House** has a hidden basement. In it: Rivan's original sketches, a map of the The Underground Ark, and a **fragment of the Edisi Law**.
- This location is a **Season 3 hub**. The party can rest, gather information, and find Rivan here.
