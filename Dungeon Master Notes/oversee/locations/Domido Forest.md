## What Matters Here
- Dense, ancient forest. Home of **Gordon "The Noisy Rhino."**
- Gordon's hut is here — a small, moss-covered cabin. Now empty.
- **Gordon's grave** — a simple mound under an old tree. Husgus buried him here.
- **Echo Sam Hurls** was defeated here (<a href="/oversee/sessions/Session%2003%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Bard">Session 03</a>).
- **The False Hydra's nest** was here. The creature is dead.
- The forest connects Ghuffghuff and Gudameng. It is a shortcut — but a dangerous one.
- **Echo Olaf Guirela** is here. He waits near Gordon's grave.

## What Happened Here
- Bud-eg the deaf old man lived here. He was killed by Agus.
- Husgus fought Malakor here. Malakor's tower was nearby.
- The party tracked Agus into this forest in Session 3.
- Echo Sam was fought here.

## Current State
- Quiet. The birds have returned. The forest is healing.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Gordon's hut is still standing. Inside: a few old hunting trophies, a worn-out bedroll, and a hidden compartment under the floorboards containing a **letter from Olaf** (never delivered) and a **map to the Wind Silo**.
- Echo Olaf waits here. He is not hostile. He is waiting for Husgus.
- The forest is **Gap-touched** in a small way. Sometimes the trees whisper in Sam's voice.
