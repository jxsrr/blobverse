> [!info] At a Glance
> **Part of:** Tropical island between Harka and Pirizia
> **Notable People:** [[Guanik Smith|Guanik]] (deceased), Janja (deceased), Dontol, Huan, Nila

## What Matters Here
- Beautiful tropical island. White sand. Coconut trees.
- **[[Guanik Smith|Guanik]]'s family home** — gnome-style architecture, warm yellow windows.
- The party was given a guesthouse here.
- **Huan and Nila's sub-mission** — recovering their shell collection from a "giant crab monster" (a kobold in disguise).

## What Happened Here
- The party recovered from Greyland Castle.
- Will of Grin (led by [[Jarko]]) attacked and burned the village.
- Guanik and Janja were killed. Huan and Nila survived (if the party saved them).
- The party defeated [[Jarko]] and took the **ruby (Rubion?)**.

## Current State
- Abandoned. Burned. A memorial to the party's first failure.
