## What Matters Here
- A vast, untamed continent across the sea from **<a href="/oversee/locations/harka">Harka</a>**, where primordial nature remains largely untouched by modern cities.
- Home to the last surviving ancient dragons — **Verdantus** beneath the *Kanopi Tanpa Ujung* in the Eastern Wilds, and **Aurelia** in the depths of *Danau Ajaib*.
- Homeland of **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Gzirmo%20Kopling">Gzirmo</a>**, the quietest warrior of **<a href="/oversee/lore/The%20Initial%20Six">The Initial Six</a>**.

## What the Party Knows
- Known through historical chronicles as an ancient, perilous wilderness beyond the main Commonwealth territories.
::/pf
