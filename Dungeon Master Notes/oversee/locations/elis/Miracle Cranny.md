## What Matters Here
- A narrow gap between two granite cliffs. Hundreds of swords and spears are embedded in the walls.
- This is where the **Hundred Days War** ended — not by victory, but by **miracle**.
- A hot spring burst from the ground. The soldiers stopped fighting. They drank together.
- The spring water is **tinted with Ambitio-Ether** — a leak from a buried Gemheart (Topaju).
- **Jenderal Bisu** runs a tea shop here. He serves **Teh Terakhir** (The Last Tea).
- The tea opens the door to the **Sumur Keajaiban** (Well of Wonders).
- **Mak Sari** sells relics and tea leaves from a small stall.

## What the Party Knows
- Not yet visited. Jenderal Bisu holds the **Clay Seal** that grants access to the Well.
- The water of the spring can grant visions of the future or the past.
- The swords in the walls are memorials. Some still hum with old magic.

## The Well of Wonders
- Beneath the tea shop: a stone well filled with glowing water.
- Drinking from the well grants one of six effects (roll 1d6):
  1. **Open Wound** — lose 1d8 HP, see a vision of your greatest mistake.
  2. **Healing** — regain 2d8 HP, remove 1 level of exhaustion.
  3. **Truth** — advantage on Insight checks for 1 hour.
  4. **Pure Sorrow** — cry for 1 minute (incapacitated), then advantage on Wis saves for 1 hour.
  5. **Wonder** — gain 3d10 temporary HP, see a vision of another Gemheart's location.
  6. **Grand Edition** — ask the DM one question about the Gap or the Overseer. The DM must answer truthfully.
- The Well is guarded by the **Well Guardian** (CR 6, golden water elemental). It only attacks if the party takes more water than they need.
%% DM SECRETS %%
- Jenderal Bisu is the last person alive who knew **Olaf Guirela** personally. He will recognize Husgus as Olaf's spiritual successor.
- The Well of Wonders is a direct leak from **Topaju** (with Lei Alejandro). Drinking from it creates a temporary connection to the Gemheart.
- If Darvin is nearby, the Well will pulse in resonance.
- The swords in the walls are not just memorials. They are **seals**. Each one holds a fragment of a soldier's soul. Removing them would release the souls — and weaken the seal on the Well.
- Jenderal Bisu's voice was taken by the **Overseer** itself when he screamed for peace. The entity heard him and silenced him permanently.
- **Jenderal Bisu is not a Cipher.** He is not part of any four-Cipher structure. He is a former general who runs a tea shop. He is a witness to history — not a guardian of a map.
