## What Matters Here
- **Rival village** to Ghuffghuff. The Gruffgud War was fought over fried rice vs. fried noodles.
- Now allies with Ghuffghuff. The war ended in 650 AV.
- **The Great Wok Gate** — giant wok-shaped gate. Guarded 24 hours by the Stirring Guard (armed with iron spatulas).
- **Wok Plaza** — mosaic noodle square. Annual Noodle Contest stage.
- **The Legendary Noodle Temple** — giant noodle bowl-shaped temple. Holds:
  - **The Holy Wok** — 300 years old.
  - **The Recipe Library** — 7 generations of recipes (guarded by the Guo family).
  - **The Cook's Trial Chamber** — 7 furnaces.
- **Culinary District** — Gang Wajan (Wok Alley). Specialized noodle shops.
- **Traditional Noodle Factory** — milling, kneading, drying.

## What Happened Here
- The party came here in <a href="/oversee/sessions/Session%2003%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Bard">Session 03</a> and found it normal — no singing, no missing people.
- **Cindy** (the Mektol orphan) was brought here for safety. She now runs the Legendary Noodle Temple.
- The party slept here after leaving Ghuffghuff.

## Current State
- Peaceful. Prosperous. The noodle temple is a place of refuge.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- The Holy Wok is a **Cycle Clan artifact**. It was used to cook ritual meals in the First Age. It has minor magical properties (food cooked in it heals 1d4 extra HP).
- Cindy is not just a survivor — she has a **natural talent for cooking** that borders on the supernatural. She may become a great cook, or a great mage.
- The Guo family (guardians of the Recipe Library) know more about the Cycle Clan than they let on.
