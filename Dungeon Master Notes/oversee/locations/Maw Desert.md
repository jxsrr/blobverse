## What Matters Here
- Harsh eastern desert. Hidden behind optical illusions and sand.
- **Will of Grin** headquarters — underground, three basement floors.
- **The Gaping Maw** — entrance. Grate trapdoor. Sentry gate.
- **The Board of Truth** — mission board for sabotage and intelligence.
- **The Training Dojo** — magically shifting cave dungeon for new recruits.
- **The Alchemy Exchange** — trade dungeon loot for Grin gear.

## What Happened Here
- The party trained and rested here ([[Session 05 — Empty Husk|Session 05]]).
- [[Jarko]] was killed here.
- [[Lei Iron Gort|Gort]]'s raid destroyed the upper floors.
- Vya reunited with Antrem here.

## Current State
- Damaged but functional. The Grin survivors are scattered.
