## Appearance
Dragonborn of Klan Frostscale. Metallic scales with natural frost patterns. Eyes like ice. Small horns at his temples. Silver-white hair. Pale as ice. He carries himself like someone who has spent his whole life being told he does not belong — because he has.

## Known Backstory
- From **Frosthaven Peak** (northern Pirizia), home of Klan Frostscale.
- Was the clan's scout. Secretly self-taught in magic — taboo in a clan that only wields Ice Breath.
- Ostracized and called **"anak haram"** (bastard child). The clan believed he was the result of a forbidden relationship between his adoptive mother and a foreigner.
- In Session 5, learned the truth: he is the son of **<a href="/Vythrax">Vythrax</a>**, the silver dragon, and **<a href="/Lei%20Watigi">Lei Watigi</a>** of Klan Lei.
- The "Breathstone" the clan worshipped was actually his egg.
- <a href="/Vythrax">Vythrax</a> swallowed the **full Safayer** to protect the egg; the poison passed into Syl's blood. Syl carries a **fragment of Safayer's power** — it is what makes his blood a universal cure. It is also what is slowly killing him.
- Syl healed <a href="/Vythrax">Vythrax</a> by feeding her his own blood. <a href="/Vythrax">Vythrax</a> recovered and returned the full Safayer to the party.
- The clan now bows to him. His name is carved on the family tree.

## Quirks
- Cursed by The Magician: 50% chance any magic item or healing spell fails and deals 1d6 Force damage instead.
- Blessed by Temperance: immune to poison and disease. His blood is a universal cure.
- Drinks blood? No — but his blood can heal. He has not fully tested its limits. He is afraid to.
- He is **slowly dying**. The Safayer fragment in his blood is a poison. It is a gift. It is a curse. It is both.

## Fate Card
**The Magician & Temperance** — magic is unstable around him, but his blood is pure.

### Who Syl Is
- Syl is the **son of Vythrax** — the silver dragon, the Silver Wind, the last of the ancient silver dragons — and **Lei Watigi** — the water heir of Klan Lei, the son of Lei Firsa and Simon.
- He is **not** the product of love. He is the product of **rape**. Lei Watigi assaulted Vythrax. The priests of Klan Frostscale helped. They weakened Vythrax with rituals and potions. They made her vulnerable. Lei Watigi used that vulnerability to force himself on her.
- When Vythrax became pregnant, the priests panicked. They could not let anyone know their deity had been violated. They could not let anyone know the child was a mixed blood — a dragon and a Lei. They created a lie. They told the clan the egg was the **Breathstone** — a sacred heirloom, a gift from Vythrax. They built an altar around it. They commanded the clan to worship it.
- The egg hatched in **700 AV** — the present era. Syl emerged. Not a dragon. Not entirely human. A child with pale skin, silver-white hair, faint horns, and eyes like ice.
- The priests panicked again. They could not let anyone know the truth. They gave the child to **Oldrunn** — a kind, infertile dragonborn woman who had always wanted a child. They told her to raise him in secrecy. They told her to keep him hidden. They told her he was "special."
- Oldrunn named him **Syl Frost**. She raised him with love. She did not know he was Vythrax's son. She only knew he was hers.

### The Ostracism
- Syl grew up in isolation. He knew only his adoptive mother and the priests who occasionally came to check on him. He did not know he was different — not at first.
- As he grew, he began to show strange, powerful magical abilities. He could control ice in ways no Frostscale child should. He could create snowstorms in closed rooms. He could sometimes speak in ancient languages no one had taught him.
- In Frostheart society — where magic was viewed with suspicion and often considered taboo — Syl's talent became a problem. Neighbors began to whisper. Rumors began to spread. His adoptive mother could no longer hide the reality that her child was different.
- The priests — who initially thought they could control the situation — began to panic. The secret could not last forever. If the truth about Syl's origin were exposed — that he was the child of Vythrax and Lei Watigi — the entire lie about the sanctity of their deity would collapse. So they made a new decision: instead of protecting Syl, they would **ostracize** him.
- They spread rumors that Syl was a **bastard child** — the result of a forbidden relationship between his adoptive mother and a foreigner. They created a narrative that Syl's strange magic was proof of "dirty blood" flowing in his veins. That way, public attention would be diverted from the deeper truth.
- Syl grew up in that ostracism. He learned that he was different. He learned that people looked at him with hatred and fear. He learned that he would never be accepted as part of the clan.
- **He never knew why.** He did not know he was Vythrax's son. He did not know about Lei Watigi. He did not know about the assault. He only knew he was hated — and he did not know what he had done to deserve it.

### The Safayer Fragment — The Gift and the Curse
- Vythrax swallowed the **full Safayer** — the blue Gemheart, the purification stone, one of the four organs of the Architect. She swallowed it to protect her egg. She hoped it would create a magical resonance that would shield the embryo — an invisible barrier that would keep it safe and slow its development until the right time.
- She did not know it would poison her. She did not know it would burn in her veins for 300 years. She did not know it would pass into the egg.
- When Syl hatched — hundreds of years later — the Safayer had been in Vythrax's body for centuries. The stone had poisoned Vythrax. The poison had passed into the egg. The poison had passed into Syl.
- Syl carries a **fragment of Safayer's power** in his blood — not the full stone, but a residue. It is what makes his blood a universal cure. It can heal any wound, cure any disease, neutralize any poison. It is a gift.
- It is also what is **slowly killing him**. The Safayer fragment is a poison. It is too pure. It is too powerful. Syl's body was not designed to hold it. It is burning him from within. He has months, perhaps a year. He does not know this. He suspects it. He has made his peace with it.
- **The Behemoth's Tear can save him.** A single tear from the last Behemoth can heal any wound — including the poison in Syl's blood. The party will need to find the Behemoth in Season 3. If they do, Syl can be saved. If they do not, he will die.

### The Healing of Vythrax
- In Session 05, Syl healed Vythrax by feeding her his own blood. The blood contained the Safayer residue — the fragment of the stone that had been killing Syl. It was enough to wake Vythrax, to clear the purple veins, to bring her back.
- **Vythrax returned the full Safayer to the party.** She coughed it up — a glowing blue stone, still pulsing with the Architect's memory. She did not want it anymore. She had carried it for 300 years. She was done.
- The party now carries the full Safayer. Syl carries only the fragment in his blood. The fragment is still killing him. The full stone did not cure him. It only stopped the progression. He is still dying.

### Syl and the Frostscale Clan
- The clan now **bows to Syl**. His name is carved on the family tree. He is no longer the "anak haram." He is the son of Vythrax. He is the heir to the clan.
- **Elder Jarl** — the leader of the Council of Elders — has stepped down. He has named Syl as his successor. He does not want the role. He does not know how to lead. He is learning.
- **Oldrunn** — his adoptive mother — wept when she saw him again. She called him "my little ice shard." She did not know he was Vythrax's son. She only knew he was hers. Syl still calls her "Mother." He always will.
- The clan is **divided**. Some still resent Syl. Some worship him. Some are afraid of him. Some are grateful to him. He has to navigate the politics of a clan that hated him — and now depends on him.

### Syl and Vythrax
- Syl loves his mother. He did not know her for most of his life. He only felt her presence in dreams — every time he hurt himself, every time he bled, he saw the silver dragon. He did not know what it meant. He knows now.
- Vythrax loves Syl more than anything in the world. She did not raise him. She could not. She was poisoned, weakened, trapped in the Spire. But she watched him from a distance. She felt him through the Safayer resonance. She knew her son was alive. She knew her son was strong.
- **Vythrax is dying.** The Safayer poison has been in her veins for 300 years. It was slowed when Syl healed her — but not cured. She has months, perhaps a year. She will not survive the campaign. She will die protecting Syl. She will die gladly.
- **Syl does not know Vythrax is dying.** He thinks his mother is recovering. He thinks the healing worked. He does not know the poison is still there. He will learn the truth in Season 3. He will break. He will weep. He will ask: *"Why didn't you tell me?"* Vythrax will answer: *"Because I wanted you to be happy. Because I wanted you to be free."*

### Syl and Lei Watigi — The Father He Never Knew
- **Lei Watigi** is Syl's biological father. He is the man who raped Vythrax. He is the man who created Syl through violence.
- Syl does not know this. He knows Lei Watigi was a Lei descendant. He knows he was involved in his conception. He does not know it was rape. He thinks it was "forbidden love" — the Cycle Clan's rewrite.
- **If Syl ever learns the truth** — that his father was a monster, that his mother was a victim, that he was born from violence — he will break. He will weep. He will ask: *"Am I a monster too?"* Vythrax will hold him. Vythrax will say: *"You are not him. You are me. You are my son. You are not a monster."*
- **Lei Watigi is dead.** He died centuries ago. He will never face justice. He will never answer for what he did. Syl will have to find his own peace. He will have to decide what to do with the truth.

### Syl's Role in the Endgame
- Syl is **not** the Valla. He is not the Architect. He is not a god. He is a dragonborn sorcerer with a dying mother and a blood that can heal anything but himself.
- But he is **connected** to Safayer. He is **connected** to the Swan Trio. He is **connected** to the third path.
- If the party chooses the third path, Syl will be one of the ones who walks into the Gap. He will carry the Safayer fragment in his blood. He will heal the wounded. He will purify the corrupted. He will be the light in the dark.
- **He does not know this yet.** He will learn. He will accept. He will do what he always does — protect the people he loves.
- **He is the healer of the party.** He is the one who mends the wounds, who cures the diseases, who brings back the dying. He is the one who will give everything — his blood, his life, his future — to save his friends.
- **He is dying.** He knows this. He has known for months. He has not told the party. He does not want them to worry. He does not want them to treat him differently. He wants to be Syl — the ice anomaly, the healer, the friend. He wants to live the time he has left.
