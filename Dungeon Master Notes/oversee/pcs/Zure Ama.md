## Appearance
Man from another world (Aede). White hair, sharp blue eyes. White-and-blue robes with sun-motif arm guards. People mistake him for a destined hero — a title he never asked for. His left arm is not his own — it is a prosthetic fused with an Imp, still soft and jelly-like, still waking up. He keeps it covered when he can. He does not like people to see it.

## Known Backstory
- Came from **Aede**, another world, over 40 years ago.
- Lost his best friend **Reins** to the **Destroyer God**. Reins was empathetic, calm, wise — the one who understood people's emotions best. She sacrificed herself. Ama could not save her.
- Stranded in **Nesia Village** (Harka). Adopted by farmers **Joni and Siti**. Learned farming. Found peace.
- Left to adventure because the world still needs saving, and Reins is waiting — somewhere, somehow.
- **Atheist.** After seeing Reins's human sacrifice, he trusts only his own actions.
- Obsessed with saving everyone — it is penance for failing Reins.
- Shares snacks to bring small smiles. It is the only thing he knows how to do when words fail.

## Recent Developments
- Left arm was amputated by **<a href="/Livia%20Vane">Livia</a>**'s wind attack in Chenalia (Session 06).
- Underwent cybernetic surgery in **Arujaya** — his Imp **Renal** was fused into the new arm (Session 06).
- The arm is still soft/jelly-like and not yet fully "awake." It is slowly solidifying.
- Gained "limitless" strength by consuming residual Ambitio-Ether — at a cost to his HP and stamina (Session 07).
- **Joni and Siti betrayed him** at The Golden Sheaf (Session 06). They called the Commonwealth. They believed he was a terrorist. He has not recovered.

## Fate Card
**The Hanged Man & The Devil** — eternal frozen smile (disadvantage on Persuasion/Insight; NPCs think he is mad). Bound to the Imp Renal (now fused into his arm).

### Who Ama Is
- **Zure Ama** is a man from **Aede** — another world, another dimension, another story. He came to Oversee over 40 years ago. He does not know how. He does not know why. He only knows he is here, and Reins is not.
- He was a **warrior** in Aede. He fought the **Destroyer God** — the entity that consumed his world. He lost. Reins sacrificed herself to save him. He has been running from that failure ever since.
- He landed in **Nesia Village** — a farming community in Harka. He was found by **Joni** and **Siti** — a kind couple who adopted him and taught him to farm. He found peace. He found purpose. He found a family.
- He left to adventure because he could not sit still. He could not forget Reins. He could not stop trying to save people — because saving people is the only thing that makes him feel like he is not a failure.
- He is **not a hero**. He is not a chosen one. He is a man who lost everything and is trying to make up for it. He is trying to save everyone because he could not save the one person who mattered most.

### The Lost Friend — Reins
- **Reins** was Ama's best friend in Aede. She was empathetic, calm, wise — the one who understood people's emotions best. She was the one who kept Ama grounded. She was the one who believed in him.
- She sacrificed herself to save him from the **Destroyer God**. She gave her life so he could live. He has never forgiven himself.
- **Ama believes Reins died in Aede.** He does not know she fell into Oversee. He does not know she met **Atlan Nouveau**. He does not know she married him. He does not know she had a daughter — **Mira Nouveau**. He does not know she lived a whole life here, in this world, while he was grieving her.
- **Reins is not dead.** She is trapped in the Gap — kept alive by a fragment of Safayer she swallowed on the day she fell. She has been watching from the Gap for 500 years. She speaks to Ama in dreams — he thinks they are memories, not messages.
- **The reunion is a Season 3 beat.** When Ama enters the Gap, he will find Reins. She is old, translucent, and smiling. She will say: *"You took your time."* He will break. He will weep. He will ask: *"Why didn't you tell me?"* She will answer: *"Because I wanted you to live."*

### The Betrayal — Joni and Siti
- **Joni and Siti** were Ama's adoptive parents. They raised him. They loved him. They taught him to farm. They gave him a home.
- In Session 06, Ama returned to Nesia seeking shelter. He went to **The Golden Sheaf** — the inn Joni and Siti owned. He knocked on the door. Joni opened it.
- Joni did not welcome him. He called the Commonwealth. He believed Ama was a terrorist — brainwashed by Commonwealth propaganda. He held Ama in a controlling hug while waiting for the soldiers to arrive. Siti stood behind him. She did not speak. She did not stop him. She just watched.
- The party escaped. Ama did not speak for hours. When he did, his voice was very quiet: *"They were my parents. And they gave me up for a lie."*
- **Joni and Siti were not evil.** They were scared. The Commonwealth threatened to burn Nesia if they did not cooperate. They believed Ama was a terrorist. They were wrong — but they were not malicious.
- **Joni whispered something as he held Ama** — something the party did not hear. He said: *"I am sorry. I did not know what else to do. They said they would burn the village."* Ama did not hear this. He will learn it in Season 3 if he returns.
- **The Golden Sheaf hides a secret** — a compartment under the bar containing Ama's childhood belongings: a wooden toy, a letter Joni never sent, and a photo of the three of them. If the party ever returns, Siti will give it to them.

### The Arm — Renal
- Ama's left arm was amputated by **Livia Vane** in Chenalia (Session 06). The wound did not bleed like a normal wound — Livia's wind curse infected it. Magic could not heal it.
- **Dr. Vektor** in Arujaya performed the surgery. He fused **Renal** — the Imp bound to Ama by the Devil Fate Card — into the new arm. The procedure took seven days. It cost 500,000 gold — a lifetime debt.
- The arm is **soft, grey, jelly-like**. It is slowly solidifying. It cannot yet wield weapons. It can consume Ambitio-Ether — the sludge in Arujaya's Depths — and grant Ama "limitless" strength for short bursts. It costs him HP and stamina. He cannot do it indefinitely.
- **Renal is not just an Imp.** It was originally **Qain** — a small imp bound to Ama's Devil Fate Card. The name "Renal" is a shortening. It has fragments of memories that are not its own. It remembers a desert. It remembers a woman with pink hair. These are echoes of **Grin Qain** — the woman who was sacrificed to make Simon the Valla. The imp was bound to the blade **Swaith** before it was bound to Ama. It is slowly becoming more than an imp. It might become a soul.
- **Renal is sarcastic.** It complains. It speaks telepathically to Ama. It is not a servant — it is a companion. It has been with Ama since the Fate Card was drawn. It has been with him through everything. It will not leave him.

### Ama's Atheism
- Ama does not believe in gods. He saw Reins sacrifice herself to the Destroyer God. He saw the Destroyer God consume his world. He saw no divine intervention. He saw no salvation. He saw only death.
- He believes in **actions**. He believes in **people**. He believes in the people beside him — the party, the friends he has made, the strangers he has saved. He believes in the snacks he shares, the hands he holds, the wounds he mends.
- **He is not a zealot.** He does not preach. He does not judge. He simply does not pray. He does not expect anything from the gods. He does not expect anything from the universe. He expects only what he can do himself.
- **He has been touched by the divine.** He does not know it. The Imp Renal is a Gap-creature. The Fate Card is a fragment of the Architect's tarot. The party is connected to the Valla, the Swan, the third path. He is part of a story older than the gods he does not believe in.
- **He will learn the truth.** He will learn that Reins is alive — trapped in the Gap, kept alive by a fragment of Safayer. He will learn that the Destroyer God is the Overseer. He will learn that the world is a seal, and the Valla is a prisoner. He will not start believing in gods. He will simply understand that the divine is not something to be worshipped — it is something to be survived. He has been surviving it his whole life. He will continue.

### Ama and the Party
- Ama is **loyal** to the party. He is the frontline tank, the relentless protector, the one who stands between his friends and danger. He is the one who will take the hit so someone else does not have to. He is the one who will carry the wounded. He is the one who will not stop.
- He is **Frightened** when he is more than 30 ft from a randomly chosen party member (Fate Card). He does not know why. He does not question it. He simply stays close.
- His **frozen smile** (Hanged Man) makes people uncomfortable. NPCs think he is mad or a cultist. He has disadvantage on Persuasion and Insight. He has learned to live with it. He has learned to let his actions speak for him.
- He is **the heart of the party** — not in the way Husgus is (quiet, steady, reliable), but in the way a fire is the heart of a camp. He is the one who reminds them why they fight. He is the one who shares his snacks. He is the one who will not let them give up.

### Ama's Role in the Endgame
- Ama is **not** the Valla. He is not the Architect. He is not a god. He is a fighter with a frozen smile and an Imp for an arm.
- But he is **connected** to the Gap. He is **connected** to Renal. He is **connected** to the third path.
- If the party chooses the third path, Ama will be one of the ones who walks into the Gap. He will carry Renal. He will consume the sludge. He will be the shield that protects the others.
- **He does not know this yet.** He will learn. He will accept. He will do what he always does — protect the people beside him.
- **He is the shield of the party.** He is the one who takes the hit, who holds the line, who will not let anyone die. He is the one who will give everything — his arm, his blood, his life — to save his friends.
- **He is not a hero.** He is a man who lost everything and is trying to make up for it. He is trying to save everyone because he could not save the one person who mattered most. He will learn that he cannot save everyone. He will learn that it is enough to save the ones beside him.

- Reins is **Mira Nouveau's mother**. She married Atlan Nouveau. She had a daughter. She lived a whole life in Oversee — while Ama was grieving her.
- Joni and Siti were **not evil**. They were scared. The Commonwealth threatened to burn Nesia if they did not cooperate.
- Joni whispered an apology as he held Ama. Ama did not hear it. He will learn it in Season 3.
- The **Golden Sheaf** hides a compartment with Ama's childhood belongings — a wooden toy, a letter, a photo.
- Renal is **not just an Imp**. It was bound to Swaith before it was bound to Ama. It has fragments of Grin Qain's memories. It is slowly becoming more than an imp.
- Ama's **limitless strength** comes at a cost. He cannot use it indefinitely. It drains his HP and stamina.
- The **Destroyer God** is the **Overseer**. Ama does not know this. He will learn it in Season 3.
