## The Two Primordial Entities

Before Oversee. Before the Gap. Before the sky was a sky.

There were two.

They were not gods. They were not creators. They were not even, strictly speaking, **beings** — at least not in the way mortals understand the word. But the scholars of the Cycle Clan, who have studied the oldest texts and the oldest dreams, gave them names:

**The Overseer** — the Mouth of Hunger.

**The Architect** — the Hand of Making.

## The Overseer

The **[[The Overseer & The Architect|Overseer]]** is not a god. It does not create. It does not nurture. It does not protect.

It **eats**.

It is a **cosmic hunger** — a singularity of appetite, drifting in the void that preceded existence. It consumes **[[Ambitio-Ether]]** — the magic of the universe, the residue of will and ambition and life. It is always hungry. It has never been satisfied. It never will be.

When the Overseer turns its attention to a civilization, that civilization **ends**. Not through war. Not through plague. Through a slow, silent dissolution — a **[[The Rapture|Rapture]]** in which every creature with magic is drawn into the sky and unmade.

The Overseer does not hate. It does not judge. It simply **eats**.

## The Architect

The **[[The Overseer & The Architect|Architect]]** was born from what the Overseer left behind.

When the Overseer consumes, it does not consume perfectly. Fragments remain — matter that was partially digested, energy that was never fully absorbed. These fragments, drifting in the void for eons, **crystallized**. They became aware.

The Architect is **preservation**. Where the Overseer eats, the Architect builds. Where the Overseer destroys, the Architect **remembers**. It is not good in the moral sense. It is not kind. It simply **refuses to let everything end**.

It made a decision — one that would shape all of reality — to sacrifice part of itself. It would create a place where mortal life could exist. A place where the Overseer's hunger could be **filtered**, **slowed**, **survived**.

That place became **Oversee**.

## The Cosmology of Oversee

The scholars of the Cycle Clan describe the universe as a **hierarchy of existence** — layers stacked from the outermost void to the innermost world.

### The Outermost Layer — The Overseer

At the top of the hierarchy — beyond all dimensions, beyond all known and unknown space — sits the **Overseer**. It is always there. It is always watching. It is always hungry.

Its attention is **annihilation**.

### The Second Layer — The Gap

Below the Overseer, but still far beyond the mortal world, lies **[[The Gap]]** — a dimension of failed creation. It is made of the **leftover matter** of the Overseer's digestion — fragments that were partially consumed but never fully became the Architect.

The Gap is **chaos**. It is neither fully real nor fully unreal. It is the space between what was eaten and what was made.

Within the Gap live the **devils and demons** — the red-skinned races, the tieflings, the imps. They are not evil in the way mortals understand evil. They are **scavengers**. They eat the crumbs of [[Ambitio-Ether]] that leak from Oversee. They need Oversee to survive. If the Overseer ever consumes everything, the demons will starve too.

One of them — **[[Zargoth]]** — is more than a scavenger. He fell in love with an elf princess. He fathered the **[[The Grin Curse|Grin bloodline]]**. He is the reason the Grin exist — the reason they smile, the reason they are magic-deaf, the reason they can survive the Rapture.

### The Third Layer — The Blue Sky

Above the mortal world stretches the **Blue Sky** — the canopy that separates the living from the void. But the wise know that the sky is not a boundary. It is a **veil** — a thin membrane that separates mortal consciousness from the terrible truth beyond.

Sometimes, through the veil, the truth leaks through. A comet that is not a comet. A star that is not a star. A shadow that moves against the wind.

In 1040 BV, the veil tore — and something fell through. Something **[[The Shell|hollow]]**. Something that was not alive.

### The Fourth Layer — Oversee

At the center of the hierarchy — suspended between the hunger above and the remnants below — lies **Oversee** itself.

It is the world of mortals. It is the world of the **[[The Inbu|Inbu]]** and their descendants. It is the world where kingdoms rise and fall under the gaze of powers far greater than themselves.

It is a world built on the ashes of civilizations that came before. A world where each civilization is destroyed in turn by forces it cannot understand, cannot fight, cannot stop.

And yet — the mortals **persist**.

They build. They love. They fight. They write. They remember.

They do not know they are being watched. They do not know that the sky is a veil, that the Gap is a wound, that the Overseer is real.

But they are.

And they are **not alone**.

## What This Means

The world is not a gift. It is a **refuge**.

The sky is not a roof. It is a **shield**.

The gods are not watching. The **Overseer** is.

But so is the **Architect** — the one who built the shield, who made the refuge, who **refused to let everything end**.

And the mortals of Oversee — the Inbu, the Lei, the Grin, the farmers, the wizards, the heroes — they are the **reason** the Architect built it all.

They are the **reason** the shield holds.

They are the **reason** the Overseer has not yet consumed everything.

## The Truth Beyond the Player-Facing Text

The player-facing text above is **the cosmology as the Cycle Clan presents it** — a scholarly interpretation of ancient texts and dreams. It is mostly accurate, but it omits the darkest parts.

### What the Players Do Not Know Yet

- **The Overseer is real.** It is not a myth. It is not a metaphor. It is an entity that exists beyond all dimensions, and it is hungry.
- **The Architect is not dead.** It became the **first Valla** — a living seal stretched across the threshold between Oversee and the Overseer. It is still there. It is still holding. It is **failing**.
- **The Valla is a filter.** Every Valla since the Architect has been a mortal soul, stretched and burned, filtering **[[Ambitio-Ether]]** so the Overseer does not taste the world. The current Valla is **[[Simon Greenbottle]]** — a halfling shepherd who was manipulated into the role 700 years ago.
- **The Gap is not just a place.** It is a **wound** in reality. It was made to hide Oversee from the Overseer, but it is thinning. When it fails, the Overseer will see everything.
- **The devils are not evil.** They are scavengers. They are the neighbors of the world, not its enemies. **[[Zargoth]]** — the father of the Grin — is the proof that they can love, sacrifice, and protect.
- **The sky is a veil.** It is the first layer of the seal. It is the reason the Rapture does not happen every day.
- **The Shell in the sky is not the Overseer.** It is the **Architect's shed skin** — the body it wore before it became the Valla. The Shell is hollow. The Overseer is what the Shell was before the Architect wore it.
- **The Cycle Clan was founded to protect this truth.** And it has been failing for 700 years.

### When the Party Learns the Truth

The full truth will be revealed in stages:

1. **Session 6** — The Shell video. The party learns the Shell is hollow.
2. **Session 7** — Syl sees the Overseer but does not remember.
3. **Session 8–10** — The party travels to the **[[The Underground Ark]]** and reads the **[[The Edisi Law|Edisi Law]]**. The full cosmology becomes known.
4. **Session 11** — The party faces **[[Lei Firsa]]** and the truth about the Valla.
5. **Endgame** — The party chooses: sacrifice, break the cycle, or become the new Valla.

### What This Document Is

This document is **the version the party could find in a library**. It is the version the **[[The Cycle Clan|Cycle Clan]]** would teach. It is the version the **[[Keeper of Secret]]** would guard.

The truth is in the DM secrets. The myth is in the player-facing page.

The party will have to decide which to believe.
