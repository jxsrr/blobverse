> [!info] The Age of the Guardian
> When the Cycle Clan found its greatest champion — and lost him to a woman with a plan.

## The Fragmentary Record

What follows is assembled from the **Berkas Penelitian Cycle Clan**, from oral histories preserved by the **[[Keeper of Secret]]**, and from fragments of the **[[The Edisi Law|Edisi Law]]** that the party has recovered. It is incomplete. It is sanitized. The Cycle Clan's official record was written by the victors — and the victors did not want the truth remembered.

This is the story as the Cycle Clan wants it told.

The story of **Lei the Great** — the first Guardian of the world, the wizard who gave his life to become the Valla.

It is not the full story.

## Lei the Great

According to the Cycle Clan's record, **Lei the Great** was born to a merchant family in **Harka** — though the record does not name the family, and the record does not mention the mother. The record says only that Lei was a **gifted ranger**, skilled with bow and blade, and that he traveled widely before finding his destiny.

The record says Lei was **selfless**. That he wanted only to protect the world.

The record says Lei became the **Valla** through a ritual of voluntary sacrifice, and that he has held the seal ever since — the first Guardian of Oversee, the eternal protector of the world.

Every city in Oversee has a statue of him. Every temple has a hymn. Every child learns his name before they learn to read.

## Simon Greenbottle

Before he was "Lei the Great," the Guardian was a **Halfling shepherd named Simon Greenbottle**.

He was born in **Greenvale**, a small farming village in Harka, in **~60 BV**. He was three feet tall, with curly brown hair and a round belly from eating too many of his mother's pastries. He had no magic. He had no ambition. He had only one unusual quality: **he could not say no to anyone who needed help.**

He was in his twenties when he received the first of what would become the **six legendary blades** — the blade **[[Zenahhi — The Loyal Blade|Zenahhi]]**, given to him by a dying knight named **[[Ksatrian]]** in the Misty Forest. He was in his thirties when he received the **[[Terrde — The Patient Blade|Terrde]]** sword — also from Ksatrian. He was in his thirties when he found the **[[Swaith — The Soul-Thirsting Blade|Swaith]]** dagger in the temple of **Dur-Khazim**. He was in his thirties when he received the **[[Frostfang — The Ice Fang|Frostfang]]** axe from **Lyra Frostweaver**. He was in his forties when he received the **[[Kkana — The Stone Greatsword|Kkana]]** greatsword from the stone giant **Grakkar**. And he received the **[[Rayio — The Wind Blade|Rayio]]** sword — the sixth blade — from a stranger whose name is not recorded.

But he also carried something else — not a blade, but a **relic**. The **[[Gustaero — The Swan Bow|Gustaero]]** bow, given to him by the old woman **Edna Gustaero** in **Wind's Rest**. Gustaero was not one of the six blades. It was one of the three ancient relics — the neck of the swan. But the Cycle Clan's records do not distinguish between them. They list all seven as "legendary weapons" — a simplification that has confused scholars for centuries.

Six blades. Six dying hands. Six last requests: *"Do not use them to conquer. Use them to protect."*

Simon kept them all. He never used them for himself. He carried them, and he protected them, and he never asked for anything in return.

He was, according to every surviving account, **the kindest person who ever lived.**

## The Two Companions

Simon did not travel alone.

The first companion was **Lei Firsa** — a human woman in her twenties, a ranger from the border forests. She was skilled with bow and blade, and she had a sharpness to her that Simon lacked. She saw his kindness and decided to walk beside him.

The second was **[[Grin Qain]]** — an elf from the **[[Kerajaan Grin|Grin Kingdom]]**, a rogue who had fled her homeland to seek something that could break her family's curse. She carried the **eternal smile** of the Grin bloodline. She carried **[[Swaith — The Soul-Thirsting Blade|Swaith]]** — Simon gave it to her, saying that a rogue should carry a blade, and a friend should be allowed to protect herself.

Three companions. Simon with Zenahhi at his hip. Lei Firsa with a bow on her back. Grin Qain with her eternal smile that never faded, even when her heart was breaking.

They walked together for twenty years.

## The Vision of the Architect

**~870 BV**

Deep beneath the **[[Observatorium Danau Kuno]]**, in a chamber that had never seen sunlight, **[[Guanik Wiseman]]** sat alone.

Every night, when the other Cycle Clan members slept, he waited for the voice of **[[The Overseer & The Architect|the Architect]]** to speak.

Wiseman had spent his life serving the Cycle Clan. He had led the excavation of the **[[The Gemhearts|Gemhearts]]**. He had performed the **[[The Cycle Ritual|maintenance rituals]]** dozens of times. But he was never at peace.

One night, he asked the Architect: *"Why must there always be a sacrifice? Why must a soul be stretched into a seal and burned for centuries?"*

The voice answered — not in words, but in direct understanding. There was no other container strong enough. A mortal body would shatter. Only a soul could hold the seal.

Wiseman asked: *"Then why not create a body that can hold it? A vessel, built from the beginning to bear the weight?"*

There was a long silence. And then — for the first time in his life — Wiseman sensed something like **surprise** from the entity on the other side.

The voice told him this was a new idea. It had never been tried. But it was possible. If a body could be built from the blueprint of the greatest biological creation — **[[The Inbu|the Inbu]]** — it might hold the Architect's consciousness without shattering.

Wiseman spent decades studying the ancient records of the Inbu. He learned how their cells stored energy, how their tissue channeled **[[Ambitio-Ether]]**, how their organs functioned for thousands of years without decay. He wrote it all down — a series of manuscripts that became known as the **Berkas Penelitian Cycle Clan**: the Cycle Clan Research File.

And then he built the **shells**.

## The Two Shells

**HVS-01** — Human Vessel Shell Unit 01 — was designed to hold **the Architect**. It was grown in a giant glass tube, submerged in a clear liquid rich in Ambitio-Ether. Cell by cell, tissue by tissue, a body formed. It had no consciousness. It had no soul. It was only a vessel — an empty shell waiting to be filled.

Wiseman planned to transfer the Architect's consciousness into HVS-01 during the next maintenance ritual. The Architect would no longer be stretched across the void. It would live in a body — a body that could walk, feel, laugh, and experience what it had given everything to protect.

**HVS-02** — Human Vessel Shell Unit 02 — was designed to hold **[[Zargoth]]**, the ancient demon of the Gap. Zargoth had loved **[[Gavaevodata Grin]]**, fathered the Grin bloodline, and been forced to return to the Gap when his love died. He had spent thousands of years watching the mortal world from a distance, longing for family he could not touch. The Architect spoke to Wiseman about Zargoth's sorrow. It asked Wiseman to build HVS-02 — a shell that could hold the demon, so that he could walk with his children one last time.

Wiseman agreed. He built both shells. He waited.

## The Meeting with the Cycle Clan

**~01 BV**

Simon was in his forties when he arrived at **[[Universitas Tarakita|Danau Kuno]]** — the ancient lake where the First Observatory stood.

He had been guided there by dreams. Not his own dreams — the dreams of the Architect, who had chosen him. The Cycle Clan received him. They explained to him the truth of the world: the Overseer, the Gap, the Gemhearts, the ritual.

And for the first time, Simon understood what he had been carrying — not just the six blades, but **a purpose**. The Architect had chosen him. The Architect wanted him to become the next Valla.

Simon agreed. He did not hesitate. He was too kind to refuse.

**Lei Firsa** and **[[Grin Qain]]** were with him. Lei Firsa listened carefully to everything. Grin Qain said nothing.

## The Marriage

The Cycle Clan records do not describe the marriage of Simon and Lei Firsa. The official record is silent. But the **Berkas Penelitian Cycle Clan** contains a fragment: a note in Wiseman's hand, dated roughly two years before the ritual.

> *"The Halfling has been manipulated. I should have seen it. He is too good to see what she is doing. I am too old to stop her. Forgive me."*

The fragment does not say what was manipulated. But the oral histories of the Keeper of Secret contain one detail that the Cycle Clan tried to erase:

**Simon did not choose to marry Lei Firsa.** He was coerced into it. She told him she was pregnant — and she was, though the children were manufactured from his stolen essence. She told him he had wronged her. She told him he had to make it right. He believed her. He believed he had to marry her. He believed he had to be a good man.

The marriage was held in secret. Only a few witnesses. **[[Grin Qain]]** was there. She stood in the corner of the room, her eternal smile never fading. But those who knew her well — and few did — said her eyes were wet.

Simon did not look at her as he walked to the altar.

## The Power Grab

**~01 BV**

A year after the marriage, Lei Firsa moved.

She had spent that year learning everything the Cycle Clan knew. She had learned the location of the **Gemhearts**. She had learned the mechanisms of the ritual. And she had learned that Wiseman was old, and tired, and could no longer stop her.

She seized control of the Cycle Clan. The old members who opposed her were removed — quietly, without violence, but removed. The younger members who saw her determination and her intelligence chose to follow her. **[[Guanik Wiseman]]**, now ancient and fragile, could only watch as everything he had built was taken.

He warned Simon, once: *"Your wife is not who she says she is. She will use you. She will destroy what I have protected for thousands of years."*

Simon did not believe him. Simon believed in his wife.

He was wrong.

## The Ritual

The night of the ritual was clear, cold, and silent.

The ancient altar of the Cycle Clan was lit by the pulsing light of the three **Gemhearts** Lei Firsa had gathered — **[[Emelanica]]** green, **[[Rubion]]** red, **[[Topaju]]** yellow. She did not have **[[Safayer]]** — the blue purification stone. It was lost — swallowed by Vythrax, hidden in the deep.

Simon stood at the center of the altar. He did not understand what was about to happen. Lei Firsa had told him it was a ritual to strengthen him, to give him the power to protect the world. He had believed her.

Lei Firsa spoke the ritual. **[[Grin Qain]]** was brought to the altar.

She did not resist.

She walked to the altar on her own feet, her head high, her eternal smile unchanged. She had known this was coming. She had known for months. And she had chosen not to run.

Simon realized what was happening only when he saw her. He screamed. He tried to run to her. But the **Stasis Field** — created by the Gemhearts — held him in place. He could not move. He could not speak. He could only watch.

**[[Grin Qain]]** looked at him. She was crying. But she was still smiling. She always smiled.

She said, quietly, that this was how it had to be. That Simon was too good for the world. That as the Valla, he would protect everyone — including her. She asked him to be strong. She asked him to remember her.

And then **[[Swaith — The Soul-Thirsting Blade|Swaith]]** — the dagger Simon had given her, the dagger she had carried for twenty years — was drawn from her belt and driven into her heart.

Her blood poured across the altar. Elf blood, mixed with the faint shimmer of demonic heritage — the mark of Zargoth, the mark of the Grin. It flowed into the Gemhearts, and the Gemhearts began to pulse faster.

Simon felt his body **stretch**. His soul was pulled — outward, upward — toward the void. Toward the Overseer. Toward the endless hunger.

The last thing he heard before the darkness took him was Grin Qain's voice, small and quiet:

> *"You were the best friend I ever had. The only one who saw me — not as a curse."*

Then she was gone. Her body crumbled to dust. **Swaith** fell with her, still warm.

## The Valla

When Simon woke, he was not in his body.

He was **everywhere**. His consciousness stretched across the world — the sky, the sea, the earth. He could feel every mortal life. He could feel the hunger above him, and the seal holding it back, and the terrible cost of every breath he took.

He was the **Valla**.

He was the **second Valla** — the first mortal to hold the seal after the Architect. He would hold it for as long as his soul could bear it. And when it broke — when the soul of the Halfling shepherd finally cracked — the Cycle Clan would perform the ritual again.

Lei Firsa stood at the altar, alone, watching her husband become a god.

She was satisfied.

## The Legend

The world was told a different story.

The world was told that **Lei the Great** — a male wizard, a great hero, a man of wisdom and sacrifice — had become the Valla to protect the world from cosmic hunger. The story was written into every history book. The statues were raised in every city.

**Lei Firsa** erased herself from the record. She became "Lei," the legendary wizard. She became the founder of **[[Klan Lei]]** — the four bloodlines, the four elements, the four Gemhearts. She used the Gemhearts to create her dynasty.

And **[[Simon Greenbottle]]** — the Halfling shepherd who gave everything — was forgotten.

**[[Grin Qain]]** was forgotten too. Her name was removed from the Cycle Clan records. Her sacrifice — the dagger in her heart, the blood on the altar — was rewritten as "a necessary ritual component."

Only the **Keeper of Secret**, founded by **[[Mira Nouveau]]** centuries later, preserved the truth.

Only they remembered that the greatest hero in the world was a Halfling who could not say no.

Only they remembered that **Lei the Great** was a lie.

## The Two Shells

**~01 BV – 690 AV**

When **Lei Firsa** seized the Cycle Clan, she found the hidden laboratory beneath the Observatorium. She found the two glass tubes — **HVS-01** and **HVS-02** — with their empty, waiting bodies.

She did not know what they were. **[[Guanik Wiseman]]**, before she silenced him, had refused to explain.

She ordered the laboratory cleaned. The tubes were broken. The liquid spilled. The two shells were washed away by the current — swept into the darkness, lost.

Wiseman's last act, before he was silenced, was to whisper to the shells: *"Live. Find your own way."*

The shells did not hear him. They were empty.

But they did not stay empty.

## HVS-01 — The Golden-Cracked

**Hundreds of years later — Hutan Domido**

A small child was found in the forest by an old dwarf named **[[Gordon]]**. The child had no clothes, no memory, no name. He remembered only darkness, fire, and someone screaming.

Gordon named him **[[Husgus]]**.

Husgus grew up. He learned to hunt. He learned to cook. He learned to laugh. He learned to love.

When the wizard **[[Malakor]]** struck him with a spell, Husgus's body **shone gold**. And when the light faded, he was not a human boy anymore. He was a **Halfling**.

He did not know why. He did not know that the golden light was the trace of his true nature — that he was **HVS-01**, the shell designed to hold the Architect. He did not know that the Architect was never placed inside him. He did not know that, in the emptiness of that shell, a new soul had slowly grown — a soul born from nothing.

The golden cracks in his skin, which appeared only in times of stress, were his true structure showing through. The foundation of Inbu biology, visible beneath the surface of a mortal life.

He had no idea what he was.

## HVS-02 — The Shell

**Hundreds of years later — Elis**

The second shell was thrown further — into a remote valley in **Elis** that no map recorded. It was buried for centuries, asleep in the dark, waiting.

But inside the shell, something had happened.

When the tube broke and the liquid spilled, a fragment of **[[Zargoth]]**'s consciousness — the demon who had agreed to be transferred — was trapped inside. Not the whole consciousness. Just a fragment. An echo. A shadow.

The fragment slept. It dreamed. It waited.

Centuries later, it woke.

It did not remember who it was. It remembered only darkness, fire, and someone screaming. Just like Husgus.

It took the name **[[Shin Van Hel]]**.

And it began to search for who it really was.

## The Truth Beyond the Player-Facing Text

The player-facing text above is **what the party could reconstruct** from the Berkas Penelitian Cycle Clan, Keeper of Secret oral histories, and the Edisi Law fragments. It is accurate in its broad strokes but **sanitized** — it does not contain the darkest details.

### What the Party Does Not Know Yet

**Lei Firsa's status is ambiguous.**

The Cycle Clan records say "Lei the Great" ascended to godhood. The truth is that **Lei Firsa may or may not be alive**. She may have used the Gemhearts and forbidden rituals to extend her life for 700 years — or she may have died centuries ago, leaving her legacy (Klan Lei, the Triase, the Commonwealth) to carry on her work. She may be something between a lich and a god — or she may be a memory, a name that still pulls strings.

**The DM must decide before Season 3.** The choice shapes the final arc:
- **If she is alive** — she is the final boss. The party must find her, confront her, and decide what to do with her.
- **If she is dead** — her legacy is the final boss. The party must dismantle the system she built — the twin tradition, the Triase, the lie — and build something new.
- **If she is something else** — she is a mystery. The party must discover what she became, and whether she can be redeemed, destroyed, or understood.

**She was not a hero.**

She was born in Harka as the illegitimate daughter of a merchant family. She grew up in poverty, surviving on scraps and garbage. Her name — "Lei" — was the only thing her mother gave her. She had no surname. She had no status.

She grew up with one burning need: **recognition**. She wanted the world to know her name. She wanted to be seen. She wanted to be loved.

But her need for love was not the love of a child for a parent. It was the love of a tyrant for a throne. She wanted to **own** love. To **control** it. To **use** it.

**She manipulated Simon into marriage through a lie — and a truth.**

The Cycle Clan records say only that Simon was "manipulated." The truth is worse.

**Lei Firsa coerced Simon into sexual relations against his will.** He was too kind, too weak, too confused to stop her. She did not ask. She did not seduce. She **took**.

She became pregnant — not with a normal child, but with the **quadruplets** she had conceived through magical means. The children were biologically Simon's — but they were manufactured. She used his essence before the ritual. She created her dynasty from stolen pieces of him.

When she told him she was pregnant, it was true — but she used it as a weapon. She told Simon he had **wronged her**. She told him he had to **make it right**. She told him he could not let their children be born as bastards — like her.

Simon, who could not say no, said yes.

He married her because he believed he had to. He did not love her. He did not want her. But he had been raised to believe that a good man takes responsibility for his mistakes — even the ones he did not make.

**She murdered Grin Qain in cold blood.**

Grin Qain's sacrifice was not voluntary. She was a **scapegoat** — a soul whose demonic blood made her a useful ritual component. Lei Firsa chose her because she was close to Simon, and because killing her would **destroy him**.

The ritual did not require Grin Qain's death. Any soul would have worked. But Lei Firsa wanted to hurt Simon. She wanted to break him. She wanted to make him **her** Valla — a broken, obedient seal that would not fight back.

She succeeded.

**Guanik Wiseman was silenced.**

The records say he "could not stop her." The truth is that Lei Firsa had him killed. Not immediately — she let him live long enough to see his life's work destroyed. Then she had him silenced, quietly, in the night. His body was never found.

**She seized the Cycle Clan and erased its history.**

She rewrote the Cycle Clan's records. She changed the date of the calendar. She rewrote Simon's story — turning a Halfling shepherd into a male wizard named "Lei the Great." She erased Grin Qain's name. She erased Wiseman's name.

She built **[[Klan Lei]]** on the lie. The four bloodlines, the four Gemhearts, the four elements — they were not gifts from the Architect. They were **stolen** by a woman who wanted to control the world.

**The HVS shells were never used.**

Wiseman built them to hold the Architect and Zargoth. But the transfer rituals were never performed. The shells were broken before they could be filled.

**Husgus is HVS-01.** The Architect's consciousness was never placed inside him. But in the emptiness, a new soul grew. The golden cracks are his true structure — Inbu biology showing through.

**Shin Van Hel is HVS-02.** A fragment of Zargoth's consciousness was trapped inside him when the shell broke. The "curse" he has carried for 690 years is not a curse — it is a **fragment of a loving father** who has been separated from his children for 10,000 years.

**The truth about Simon Greenbottle's soul.**

Simon is still conscious. He is still aware. He hears the Echoes. He knows what was done to him. He has been holding the seal for 700 years, and his soul is cracking.

The **[[The Echoes|Echoes]]** — Sam, Gzirmo, Atreus, Atlan, and Olaf — are the result of **Lei Alejandro's** incomplete feeding ritual in 200 AV. Without **[[Safayer]]**'s purification, their souls were not erased cleanly. They became fragments — trapped between worlds.

**Alejandro is not an Echo.** He is alive. He is hiding in the Bahtera Bawah Tanah. He is guarding the Edisi Law. He is waiting for someone worthy.

**The "Puppeteer" in Atreus's Letter.**

Atreus's letter — found in Session 5 — refers to the "Puppeteer." The party assumes it is **[[Lei Alejandro]]**. It is not. It is **Lei Firsa** — or her legacy. Whether she is alive, dead, or something else, her design is still being carried out. The Klan Lei, the Triase, the Commonwealth — they are her will made manifest.

### The Major Contradictions — Resolved

**Contradiction 1: The Valla Count (Resolved)**

- This document (Age of Nurturing) says: *"Ribuan tahun telah berlalu dengan puluhan Valla berganti"* — **dozens of Vallas** over thousands of years.
- **`The Valla's History.md`** says: *"Between the Architect and Simon, there were no other Vallas. The Architect held the seal for the entire First Age. Simon is the second. He is the only one."*
- **`The Valla.md`** says: *"The Cycle Clan performs a ritual to replace the Valla every few centuries."* — implying many.

**Resolution: There have only ever been two Vallas — the Architect and Simon Greenbottle.**

The "dozens of Vallas" story is **propaganda** invented by Lei Firsa's scribes after 01 BV to make the ritual sound routine. The "Valla replaced every few centuries" claim is the same lie. The Cycle Clan performed **maintenance rituals** for the Architect — not replacements. The Valla was replaced **only once** — when Lei Firsa forced Simon into the role in 01 BV.

**Echo is not a Valla.** Echo is the name given to the **failed feeding ritual** performed by **Lei Alejandro** around 200 AV. He tried to pour the souls of the Initial Six into Simon to keep him alive. Without Safayer, they became Echoes. Echo is a mistake — not a Valla.

**Contradiction 2: Simon's Age at the Ritual (Resolved)**

- The narrative in this document says Simon is **40+** when collecting the weapons, **42** when he meets the Cycle Clan, and **~44-45** at the ritual.
- The **`Timeline of Oversee.md`** says Simon is born **~60 BV** and the ritual happens **~01 BV** — meaning he is **~44-45** at the ritual.

**Resolution: Simon was born ~60 BV.** He was ~44-45 at the ritual in 01 BV. The timeline has been updated. The narrative ages in this document are correct.

**Contradiction 3: Where Was the Observatorium? (Resolved)**

- This document says the Cycle Clan met Simon at **Danau Kuno** — which "would later become **Kota Juto**."
- **`Session 02 — The Wizard's City`** establishes that the Observatorium in **Juto Port City** (formerly Kota Juto) was built on top of **Atlan Nouveau's** base, which was itself built on Cycle Clan foundations.
- **`Tarakita University of Heroism.md`** says Danau Kuno is now **Universitas Tarakita** — not Juto Port City.

**Resolution: Danau Kuno is Universitas Tarakita.** The First Observatory — the optical observatory — was built by Guanik Wiseman in 1040 BV. It was abandoned. The lake was drained. The tower was excavated. **Universitas Tarakita** was built on top of it. The **Second Observatory** — the high-energy astrophysics observatory — was built centuries later in **Juto Port City** (formerly Kota Juto). Atlan Nouveau repurposed it for oceanography. The **Third Observatory** — the radio observatory — was built in the **Pegunungan Arbor**.

- **Session 11** — **Lei Firsa** appears — or her legacy does. The party learns the truth about Simon, about Grin Qain, about the HVS shells. They must decide what to do.
- **Endgame** — The party decides the fate of the Valla, the Gemhearts, and the world.

### What This Document Is

This is the **Cycle Clan's archive entry** for the Age of Nurturing — the version that would be taught to an initiate. It is sanitized. It omits the worst of Lei Firsa's crimes, softening them into "manipulation" rather than what they truly were.

The truth is in the DM secrets.

The party will have to decide how much of the Cycle Clan's version to trust.

## Timeline Note

The Age of Nurturing spans roughly **900 years** — from the last recorded Rapture to the moment Simon Greenbottle becomes the Valla. The exact dates are approximate; the Cycle Clan kept imperfect records, and Lei Firsa later **falsified** many of them.

The era ends when **Lei Firsa** declares the new calendar — year **1 AV** — and rewrites history to make herself the hero. That is where **The First God** begins.
