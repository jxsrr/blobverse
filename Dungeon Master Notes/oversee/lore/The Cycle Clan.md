> [!info] The Keepers of the Secret
> The organization that maintains the seal. Founded by Guanik Wiseman. Now scattered.

## Origins
- Founded by **Guanik Wiseman** in 1040 BV.
- Wiseman saw the Shell in the sky. The **Architect** spoke to him — not in dreams, but through direct consciousness transfer. The Architect was **inside him**.
- He learned the truth: the world is a seal. The Valla is a sacrifice. The Overseer is always hungry.
- He founded the Cycle Clan to protect this secret and maintain the seal.
- He was the **first Kanselir Agung** of the One Voice Commonwealth — the search for the next shepherd.
- He signed the **original Edisi Law** — the promise — with the Architect inside him, guiding his hand.

## Purpose
- **Maintain the seal** for the Architect — the first Valla.
- **Protect the secret** from those who would misuse it.
- **Find the shepherd** — the next Valla, the next person willing to hold the seal.
- **Perform the maintenance rituals** — the quiet, small acts that slowed the Architect's burning and kept the seal stable.

## The Fall of the Cycle Clan
- **Lei Firsa** seized the Cycle Clan in 01 BV.
- She manipulated **Simon Greenbottle**. She killed **Grin Qain**. She forced Simon into the Valla role.
- She scattered the six legendary blades across the world.
- She rewrote history: Simon became "Lei the Great." She became a male wizard.
- She corrupted the One Voice Commonwealth. She created the Triase. She placed her descendants in key positions.
- The Cycle Clan was shattered. The original members — the ones who remembered Guanik's promise — were killed, silenced, or erased.
- Some joined **Keeper of Secret**. Some joined **Will of Grin**. Some went mad.

## What We Know
- The Cycle Clan is not evil. It is **desperate**.
- It made terrible choices for the survival of the world.
- Its secrets are the key to the endgame.

## What the Party Does NOT Know
- **Guanik Wiseman is alive.** The Architect is inside him. It has kept him alive for over a thousand years.
- The Cycle Clan was **originally good**. It was a search for the shepherd, not a dynasty.
- The Cycle Clan was **shattered by Lei Firsa**. The current Cycle Clan does not exist as an organization — it is scattered fragments, hiding.
- **Rivan** is the main Cycle Clan contact the party will meet. He is an artist who drew the HVS schematics under Guanik's guidance. He lives in the Half House in Zundabun.
- There is no four-Cipher structure. The Ciphers were a fabrication — or a later corruption — invented to replace the original organization.

### The Original Cycle Clan — Guanik's Vision
- The Cycle Clan was founded by **Guanik Wiseman** in 1040 BV. It was not a secret society. It was a **brotherhood of scholars** — people who had seen the truth and wanted to protect the world.
- It was founded to **maintain the seal** for the Architect — the first Valla — and to **find the shepherd** who would replace it when the time came.
- It was **good**. It was not perfect. But it was honest. It was searching for a shepherd.
- The original members were scholars, historians, and witnesses. They were the ones who wrote the original Edisi Law. They were the ones who guarded the Bahtera Bawah Tanah. They were the ones who knew Guanik's promise.

### The Fall — Lei Firsa
- Lei Firsa arrived in 01 BV. She seized the Cycle Clan. She corrupted it.
- She manipulated Simon. She killed Grin Qain. She forced Simon into the Valla role.
- She scattered the six legendary blades. She rewrote history. She created the Triase. She placed her descendants in key positions.
- She killed the original members of the Cycle Clan — the ones who remembered Guanik's promise. She silenced them. She erased them.
- The Cycle Clan was shattered. It did not survive as an organization. It survived as **fragments** — individual members who hid, who remembered, who waited.

### The Fragments — Where the Cycle Clan Went
- Some joined **Keeper of Secret** — founded by **Mira Nouveau**, dedicated to finding the truth about the Initial Six.
- Some joined **Will of Grin** — founded by **Hildag Grin**, dedicated to fighting the corrupted Commonwealth.
- Some went mad. Some hid. Some died.
- **Rivan** is one of the fragments. He is an artist who drew the HVS schematics under Guanik's guidance. He lives in **Zundabun** — in the Half House. He has a copy of the Peta Bahtera and a fragment of the Edisi Law in his basement.
- **Guanik Wiseman** is the last living founder. He is hidden. He is waiting. The Architect is inside him.

### No Four-Cipher Structure
- The four-Cipher structure is **not canon**. It was removed.
- There is no Cipher Livia (Nenek Laksmi), no Cipher Otoritas (Jenderal Kael), no Cipher War/Miracle (Jenderal Bisu), no Cipher Kabut (Varek).
- These characters may exist — but they are **not** Cycle Clan Ciphers. They are independent agents, or members of other factions, or simply people the party meets. They do not hold pieces of a map. They do not guard the truth in a structured way.
- **Rivan** is the main Cycle Clan contact. He is not a Cipher. He is an artist. He remembers. He has records. He is waiting for someone to come.

### Guanik Wiseman — The Last Founder
- Guanik is **alive**. The Architect is inside him. It has kept him alive for over a thousand years.
- He is hidden. He is waiting. He is the last living founder of the Cycle Clan.
- He does not know that Lei Firsa corrupted the Commonwealth. He does not know that Simon is the Valla. He does not know that the Cycle Clan was shattered. He has been hidden, waiting, for someone to come.
- When the party meets him, he will ask them: *"Is the promise kept?"* If they say yes, he will smile. If they say no, he will nod. He has waited a long time for the answer.
- He will give them the **original Edisi Law** — if they can prove they are worthy. The proof is not combat. The proof is **compassion**.
- He is the key to the override — his presence is required for the Architect's Mercy.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- The Cycle Clan was **originally good**. It was a search for the shepherd, not a dynasty.
- The Cycle Clan was **shattered by Lei Firsa**. The current Cycle Clan does not exist as an organization.
- **Guanik Wiseman is alive.** The Architect is inside him. He is the last living founder.
- **Rivan** is the main Cycle Clan contact. He is not a Cipher. He is an artist.
- There is no four-Cipher structure. The Ciphers were a fabrication — or a later corruption — invented to replace the original organization.
- The original members of the Cycle Clan were killed, silenced, or erased by Lei Firsa. They did not survive as an organization. They survived as fragments.
