> [!info] The People Who Rejected Magic
> The builders of Arujaya. The creators of technology. The outcasts of Oversee.

## Origins
- The Tinkers were originally **Cycle Clan members** — engineers, smiths, and inventors.
- They believed that **technology could replace magic** — that the world did not need to rely on the Valla.
- They were expelled from the Cycle Clan in 500 AV. Their leader was **Guanik Wiseman** (the second one, named after the founder).
- They wandered for 100 years. They were hunted by the Commonwealth. They were called heretics.

## The Discovery
- In 600 AV, they found the **First Tree** — a dead tree from before the Rapture.
- Under the tree, they found a **pre-Rapture ruin** — an ancient city of machines.
- They renamed it **Arujaya** and built their home there.

## The Philosophy
- **Meritocracy.** Status is based on skill, not blood.
- **Practicality.** Magic is not sacred. It is a tool. Technology is a better tool.
- **Isolation.** The surface is dangerous. The underground is safe.

## The Factions
- **Immortalists** — they want to use technology to live forever. Led by **Dr. Vektor**.
- **Preservationists** — they want to preserve knowledge. Led by **Echo Gzirmo**.
- **Young Ones** — they want to explore the surface. Led by **Adam**.

## What the Party Knows
- Arujaya is a city of technology.
- The Tinkers rejected magic.
- They are not hostile, but they are not friendly.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- The Tinkers are not just engineers. They are the **last link** to the world before the Rapture.
- The ruins under the First Tree contain **pre-Rapture technology** — including a device that can **block the Gap** (temporarily).
- The Tinkers have been secretly building a weapon to fight the Overseer. It is incomplete. It is in the lowest levels of Arujaya.
- If the party helps the Tinkers complete the weapon, they will gain a **powerful ally** in the final battle.
- But the weapon requires a **sacrifice** — a piece of a Gemheart. The party will have to choose.
