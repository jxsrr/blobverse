> [!info] The Mother of All Races
> Before humans, elves, dwarves, or dragonborn, there were the Inbu. They are the ancestors of every mortal race in Oversee.

## Origins
- Born from the **fruit of the First Trees** — many primordial trees created by the Architect to filter Ambitio-Ether from the air and crystallize it into life.
- The Inbu were the first creatures with **consciousness** and **free will**.
- They were powerful mages. They could shape reality with their thoughts.
- They were not many. Perhaps a few thousand at their peak.

## The Differentiation
- Over thousands of years, the Inbu diverged into the races of Oversee:
  - **Humans** — the Inbu who chose comfort. They lost their special senses and gained adaptability.
  - **Elves** — the Inbu who chose the forests. They sharpened their senses and bonded with the wild Ambitio-Ether.
  - **Dwarves** — the Inbu who chose the underground. They gained resilience and craftsmanship.
  - **Halflings** — the Inbu who chose peace. They shrank and learned to be happy with less.
  - **Gnomes** — the Inbu who chose curiosity. They stayed small and discovered magic and invention.
  - **Dragonborn** — the Inbu who chose the mountains. They were changed by the breath of ancient dragons.
  - **Half-Elves, Half-Orcs** — the children of mixed lines, carrying the traits of both.

## The Inbu Today
- The pure Inbu are **extinct**. They died out during the Rapture. The few who survived interbred with their descendants.
- Their legacy is the **races themselves**. Every humanoid is a distant child of the Inbu.

## What the Party Knows
- The Inbu are a myth. Most people do not believe they existed.
- Scholars debate whether they were real or a creation story.
::/pf

### The Inbu — Full Canon
- The Inbu were the **first conscious race** — created by the Architect from **part of itself**, in its own image.
- The Architect split off a portion of its essence and planted it in the **First Trees** — the many primordial trees it created to filter Ambitio-Ether from the air and crystallize it into life. The trees grew, filtered the energy, and bore fruit. The Inbu emerged from that fruit — carrying the Architect's essence, but also the filtered Ambitio-Ether.
- They were **powerful**. They were **free**. They were **conscious**. They were the first creatures to know they existed — and that knowledge triggered the production of Ambitio-Ether on a small scale.
- They were **called "failed"** — not because they were weak, but because they were too strong. They held **too much Ambitio-Ether**. They were a beacon burning bright in the cosmic dark. They attracted the Overseer's attention.
- The Architect realized what it had done. It could not unmake them — they were part of itself. It could only **change them**.

### The Differentiation — The Deliberate Scattering
- The Architect **manipulated its "other self"** — the world it had created. It changed the weather. It changed the soil. It changed the geography. It created environments that would force the Inbu to adapt, to change, to **diverge**.
- Some Inbu chose the fertile plains. They lost their specialized senses and became **Humans**.
- Some chose the deep forests. They sharpened their senses and became **Elves**.
- Some chose the underground. They became **Dwarves**.
- Some chose peace. They became **Halflings**.
- Some chose curiosity. They became **Gnomes**.
- Some chose the mountains. They were changed by the breath of ancient dragons and became **Dragonborn**.
- The pure Inbu — the ones who held too much Ambitio-Ether — **died out**. They could not adapt. They were too bright. They were consumed — by the Rapture, by the Overseer, by their own power.
- **The Architect scattered them into lesser races to hide them from the Overseer.** This was not a punishment. It was a **mercy**. It was the only way to save them.

### The Shell — The Inbu-Shaped Skin
- The **Shell** in the sky — the 20-kilometer humanoid skin that Guanik Wiseman saw — is **exactly like an Inbu, but far larger**.
- This is not a coincidence. The Architect's original body — the body it wore before it became the Valla — was **Inbu-shaped**. The Inbu were created in its image. They are small copies of what the Architect once was.
- When the Architect shed its skin to become the Valla, the Shell retained that shape. It is a giant, empty Inbu. A dead god's molting.
- **The Shell is not connected to the First Trees.** The First Trees are from the Inbu era. The Shell is from the Architect's own body. They are different things.

### The Blobverse — Many Architects
- The Architect is **not unique**. It is one of **many crystallized entities** — fragments of the Overseer's failed digestion that became aware, each one building its own world in the void.
- **Oversee** is our Architect's world. **Aede** is another Architect's world. There may be others — worlds beyond counting, each one hidden behind its own Gap, each one threatened by the same Overseer.
- Each Architect made the same choice: to sacrifice part of itself, to build a refuge, to become a seal. Each one is still holding. Each one is still fading.
- The Overseer is **singular**. It is the hunger that consumes all worlds. It does not know about the Architects. It does not care. It simply eats.
- **This is why the third path matters.** If the party can free their Architect, they can teach the others. They can show the Architects that there is another way. They can save the blobverse — not just Oversee.

### The First Trees — The Many, Not the One
- The **First Trees** are not a single tree. They are **many** — a network of primordial trees created by the Architect when it made Oversee.
- They were the **lungs of the world** — giant trees and luminescent forests that absorbed wild mana from the air and stored it as crystalline sap. They were not just collections of trees. They were **organisms** — living filters that kept the world stable.
- **The Inbu were born from their fruit.** The fruit contained the essence of Ambitio-Ether that had been filtered and purified by the tree. When consumed, it sparked consciousness — the first free will in the world.
- **The First Trees were cut down** during the High Magic Age. The civilizations that came before the Rapture cut them for magical wood, for settlement land, for fuel. The natural filters died out. The filtering system built since the beginning of creation collapsed.
- **The last First Tree** — a petrified root — is in the deep jungle of **Elis**. It is 10,000 years old. It is dead. It has been dead since the Rapture. It is not the Shell's root system. It is just the last remnant of the trees that once covered the world.
- **The party can find seeds of the First Trees** — if they look hard enough. They are rare. They are hidden. But they exist. If planted, they can grow into new filters — new lungs for the world. This is a **Season 3 side quest**.

### The Races — The Children of the Inbu
- The Inbu diverged because the Architect forced them to. They were not scattered randomly. They were pushed into different environments. They adapted. They became the races.
- **Humans** — the Inbu who chose comfort. They settled on fertile plains. They lost the specialized senses of their ancestors but gained adaptability, ambition, and diversity.
- **Elves** — the Inbu who chose the forests. They sharpened their senses to survive. They became creatures of the night. They developed a deep connection to the wild Ambitio-Ether around them.
- **Dwarves** — the Inbu who chose the underground. They hid from surface predators. They became sturdy, resilient craftsmen and unparalleled miners.
- **Halflings** — a distant branch of Humans who chose peace over ambition. They shrank in stature. They inherited the cheerfulness and prosperity of their Human kin.
- **Gnomes** — a mystery. Perhaps a lighter branch of Dwarves, perhaps an early cross between Elves and fey. Small, curious, and naturally drawn to illusion and the stranger side of nature's magic.
- **Dragonborn** — the Inbu who migrated to the highest mountains. They were exposed to the magical weather cycles of the ancient dragons. Their bodies slowly changed. Scales grew. Their breath carried elemental force. Their lifespans lengthened.
- **Half-Elves, Half-Orcs** — the children of mixed lines. They inherited traits from both. They carry the legacy of the Inbu in their blood.

### The Inbu and Magic
- The Inbu were **powerful mages**. They could shape reality with their thoughts. They were the first to touch Ambitio-Ether — the first to shape it, to use it, to **become** it.
- **Magic is not evenly distributed** in the races of Oversee. Some individuals have immense power. Some have none. Some Elven clans are entirely non-magical. Others carry deep traditions of magic. The same is true of Dragonborn, Humans, and all the rest.
- **Magic follows bloodlines** — but not in the way the Klan Lei claims. It is not a pure lineage. It is a **connection** to the Valla — to Simon. Everyone with magic is connected to him. The Klan Lei has the strongest magic because they are **closest** to him. They share his blood. But the Inbu's legacy is in every race — not just the Klan.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- The Inbu were created from **part of the Architect itself**, in its image.
- They were called "failed" because they held **too much Ambitio-Ether** — a beacon to the Overseer.
- The Architect **deliberately manipulated the environment** to force them to differentiate into lesser races — to hide them.
- The **Shell is Inbu-shaped** because the Architect's original body was Inbu-like. It is a giant, empty copy of the first race.
- **There are many Architects** across the blobverse — each one building its own world, each one holding its own seal.
- **The First Trees are from the Inbu era.** They have no connection to the Shell.
