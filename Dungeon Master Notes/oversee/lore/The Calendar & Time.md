> [!info] How Time Works in Oversee
> The current year is **700 AV** (After Valla). But time is not straightforward — and the calendar is a lie.

## The Calendar
- **AV** = After Valla. Year 1 AV is when **Lei Firsa forced Simon Greenbottle into the Valla role** — not when she "declared herself the first Valla." That was a rumor she encouraged.
- **BV** = Before Valla. The old calendar. Now used only by scholars.
- The current year is **700 AV**.

## Time Anomalies
- The **Tower of Time** in Padre City has a clock that runs **backwards**.
- **Emelanica** can modulate time.
- The **Gap** has no time. It is outside causality.
- **Echoes** exist outside time. They are fragments of moments.
- **The Valla** experiences time differently. Simon has been conscious for 700 years — but it feels like an eternity. He does not age. He does not sleep. He simply **is**.

## What the Party Knows
- The current year is 700 AV.
- The calendar was set by Lei Firsa after she forced Simon into the Valla role.
- The world believes "Lei the Great" ascended to godhood. The party does not know the truth yet.

## What the Party Does NOT Know
- The calendar is a lie. Lei Firsa changed the year count to make herself the center of history.
- The "real" year is closer to **1700 BV** — not 700 AV. The party is living in a rewritten timeline.
- If the party ever restores the true calendar, it will destabilize the Commonwealth. The legitimacy of the Triase, the Klan Lei, and the entire One Voice Commonwealth rests on the lie that 1 AV was a holy year.
- The Tower of Time's backwards clock is counting down to something. No one knows what. **The DM should decide.** It may be counting down to the Rapture. It may be counting down to Lei Firsa's return. It may be counting down to the moment the seal finally breaks.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### The Calendar — The Lie
- **1 AV** is not a holy year. It is the year **Lei Firsa forced Simon Greenbottle into the Valla role** — without his consent, without Safayer, without the Architect's release.
- **Lei Firsa rewrote the calendar** to make herself the center of history. She declared that the years before her were "Before Valla" — as if the Valla had not existed for thousands of years before her. She declared that the years after her were "After Valla" — as if she had brought the Valla into being.
- **The world believes** that "Lei the Great" — a male wizard — ascended to godhood in 1 AV. This is a lie. Simon Greenbottle is the Valla. He was forced into the role. He is still there, still suffering.
- **The "real" year** is closer to **1700 BV**. The party is living in a rewritten timeline. The world is not 700 years old. It is 1,700 years old — or older. The calendar is a fabrication.

### The Tower of Time — The Backwards Clock
- The **Tower of Time** in Padre City has a clock that runs **backwards**. No one knows why. No one knows who built it. No one knows what it is counting down to.
- **The DM must decide.** The clock may be counting down to:
  - **The Rapture** — the next time the Overseer notices the world. The clock is a warning. It is running out.
  - **Lei Firsa's return** — if she is alive, she is waiting. The clock is counting down to her awakening.
  - **The seal's collapse** — the moment the Architect and Simon finally break. The clock is counting down to the end.
  - **The third path** — the moment the party must choose. The clock is counting down to the choice.
- **The clock is a plot device.** The DM should use it to build tension. It should tick louder as the campaign progresses. It should stop — or shatter — at the moment of the final choice.

### Time and the Valla
- **The Valla experiences time differently.** Simon has been conscious for 700 years. He does not age. He does not sleep. He does not eat. He simply **is**.
- **He feels every second.** He feels every soul that passes through the seal. He feels every Echo that forms. He feels every moment of the world's history — from the Rapture to the present — pass through him like water through a sieve.
- **He is not insane.** He is not broken. He is **patient**. He has been waiting for someone to come. He has been waiting for someone to free him.
- **If the party ever meets Simon**, he will not be a god. He will be a small, weeping halfling in a cloak too large for him. His feet are bare. He has been standing for 700 years. He will look at them and say: *"You came. I did not think anyone would come."*

### Time and the Gap
- **The Gap has no time.** It is outside causality. It is a wound in reality — a place where the rules of the universe do not apply.
- **Echoes exist outside time.** They are fragments of moments — not ghosts, not revenants. They are **resonances**. They repeat behaviors, songs, memories — stuck in loops. They cannot move on. They cannot rest. They cannot die.
- **The Architect experiences time differently.** It has been holding the seal for thousands of years. It has been fading for thousands of years. It does not know how long it has been. It only knows it is tired. It only knows it is ready.
- **Guanik Wiseman experiences time differently.** The Architect is inside him. It has kept him alive for over a thousand years. He does not age. He does not sleep. He simply **is**. He is waiting for someone to come. He is waiting for the promise to be kept.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- The calendar is a lie. 1 AV is not a holy year. It is the year Lei Firsa forced Simon into the Valla role.
- The "real" year is closer to **1700 BV**.
- The Tower of Time's backwards clock is counting down to something. The DM decides what.
- The Valla experiences time differently. Simon has been conscious for 700 years. He feels every second.
- The Gap has no time. Echoes exist outside causality.
- **The Architect is still holding the seal.** It has been fading for thousands of years. It is tired. It is ready.
- **Guanik Wiseman is alive.** The Architect is inside him. He has been alive for over a thousand years. He is waiting for someone to come.

### Season 3 Reveal Schedule
- **Session 8–10** — The party reaches the Bahtera Bawah Tanah. They learn the calendar is a lie. They learn the "real" year is closer to 1700 BV.
- **Session 11** — The party meets Guanik Wiseman. They learn he has been alive for over a thousand years. They learn the Architect is inside him. They learn the promise.
- **Endgame** — The party must decide what to do with the truth. They can expose the calendar. They can restore the true year. They can leave it alone. The choice is theirs.
