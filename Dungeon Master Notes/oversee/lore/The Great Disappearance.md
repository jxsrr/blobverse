## What Happened
- At the height of their renown around **200 AV**, the six greatest heroes of the age — **[[oversee/lore/The Initial Six|The Initial Six]]** — vanished from the world without a trace.
- In the decades that followed, **[[oversee/npcs/Atreus Grin|Atreus Grin]]**'s name faded from official histories and **[[oversee/locations/Kerajaan Grin|Kerajaan Grin]]** slowly collapsed.
- Uncovering why the six heroes vanished — and why Atreus never returned — became one of the foundational goals of **[[oversee/factions/Will of Grin|Will of Grin]]**.

## What the Party Knows
- Through their encounters with **[[oversee/npcs/Sam Hurls|Sam Hurls]]** ([[oversee/sessions/Session 03 — The Bard|Session 03]]) and **[[oversee/factions/Will of Grin|Will of Grin]]**, the party has begun to suspect the heroes did not simply abandon the world.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- The Initial Six did not vanish voluntarily: they were lured into a **Cycle Ritual** betrayal orchestrated by **[[oversee/npcs/Lei Firsa|Lei Firsa]]** and the Commonwealth leadership to harvest their souls/vessels, turning five of them into **[[oversee/lore/The Echoes|The Echoes]]** while **[[oversee/npcs/Lei Alejandro|Lei Alejandro]]** escaped and went into hiding inside **[[oversee/locations/The Underground Ark|The Underground Ark]]**.
