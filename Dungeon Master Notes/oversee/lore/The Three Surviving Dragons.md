> [!info] The Last of the Ancient Dragons
> Once there were thousands. Now there are three. They are hiding.

## [[Vythrax]] — The Silver Wind
- **Color:** Silver. **Element:** Cold.
- **Location:** Spire of Whispering Ice (Frosthaven Peak).
- **Status:** Alive, weakened by Safayer poison.
- **Role:** Syl's mother. Patron of Klan Frostscale.
- **Personality:** Protective. Loyal. Broken by what was done to her.

## Verdantus — The Green Shadow
- **Color:** Green. **Element:** Poison.
- **Location:** Kanopi Tanpa Ujung (Eastern Forest).
- **Status:** Alive, hidden.
- **Role:** Guardian of the last primordial forest.
- **Personality:** Ancient. Slow. Wise. He has not spoken to a mortal in 1,000 years.

## Aurelia — The Blue Depths
- **Color:** Blue. **Element:** Water.
- **Location:** Danau Ajaib (Elis, deep lake).
- **Status:** Alive, hidden.
- **Role:** Guardian of the pure water sources of Elis.
- **Personality:** Unknown. She has not been seen in 2,000 years.

## What the Party Knows
- They know [[Vythrax]]. They do not know the others exist.
- The dragons are the last of their kind.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Verdantus is **not hidden**. He is sleeping. If the party enters the Kanopi Tanpa Ujung, they will wake him. He will be angry. He will attack. But if they can calm him, he will give them a **seed of the first tree** — a seed that can grow into a new filter for Ambitio-Ether.
- Aurelia is **dying**. She has been poisoned by something in the lake. If the party saves her, she will give them **a scale** — a scale that can breathe underwater.
- [[Vythrax]] will not survive the campaign. She is already dying. She will give her remaining strength to protect Syl in the final battle.
- The three dragons are the **last connection to the world before the Rapture**. If they die, the old world dies with them.
