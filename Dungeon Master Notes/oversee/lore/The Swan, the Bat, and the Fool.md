> [!info] The Swan, the Bat, and the Fool
> Nine ancient weapons. Three trios. One story. The royal families of Oversee have passed down this legend for centuries — but only the Crown remembers the whole of it.

## The Legend
- Long ago — before the Commonwealth, before the Klan Lei, before the names were written down — there were **nine ancient weapons**.
- They were not forged. They were **found** — pulled from the ruins of the world before the Rapture. They were already old when the first kingdoms were young.
- The old kings divided them into **three trios**:
  - **The Swan Trio** — the **passage** tools. They open doors. They allow travel between worlds.
  - **The Bat Trio** — the **seal** tools. They close doors. They prevent travel.
  - **The Fool Trio** — the **neutral** tools. They keep the Swan and the Bat from destroying the world.

## The Swan Trio — The Passage Tools
- **Gustaero** — the **neck**. A longbow. It reaches. It pierces the veil between worlds.
- **Niette** — the **wing**. A spear. It strikes. It flies where the hand cannot.
- **Alnean** — the **breath**. A breathing relic. It survives. It lets the wielder breathe in the Gap, in the void, in the deep.
- **Purpose:** To **open the Gap**. To allow passage between worlds.
- **Current holders:** Gustaero — <a href="/oversee/pcs/Husgus">Husgus</a>. Niette — the Tomb of Gavaevodata (Elis Selatan). Alnean — the Kuo Lau King (underwater kingdom).

## The Bat Trio — The Seal Tools
- **The Fang** — a **dagger**. It cuts the threads of passage. It severs the connection between worlds.
- **The Claw** — a **gauntlet**. It grips the doors. It holds them shut.
- **The Shroud** — a **cloak**. It hides the doors. It makes them invisible to those who would open them.
- **Purpose:** To **seal the Gap**. To prevent passage between worlds.
- **Current holders:** Hidden. Guarded by the **royal families of Oversee**.
- **The Bat is not evil.** It is **necessary**. Without it, the Swan would open every door — and the Overseer would pour through.

## The Fool Trio — The Neutral Tools
- **Swaith** — the **dagger**. It releases souls. It frees the trapped. It can also absorb them. It is the only weapon that can interact with the Echoes.
- **Kkana** — the **greatsword**. It stands firm. It anchors a position. It holds back collapsing reality.
- **Zenahhi** — the **longsword**. It protects. It parries any attack. It deflects a blow meant for another.
- **Purpose:** To **keep the Swan and the Bat in check**. To prevent them from destroying each other — and the world with them.
- **Current holders:** Swaith — <a href="/oversee/pcs/Shin%20Van%20Hel">Shin Van Hel</a>. Kkana — <a href="/oversee/npcs/Mira%20Nouveau">Mira Nouveau</a>. Zenahhi — lost in Canyon of Lament.
- **The Fool is the walker.** It does not open doors. It does not close them. It **chooses** which door to open and which to close.

## The Fool and the Tarot
- The name comes from the **Tarot of the Architect** — the original tarot used in the ritual that created the world.
- **The Fool** is the first card. It represents **the beginning**, **the journey**, **the walker between worlds**.
- The Fool Trio is the living embodiment of the Fool card — the one who chooses which door to open and which to close.
- If the party finds all three, they can use them to **choose the fate of the world** — to open the Gap, to seal it, or to find a third way.

## The Swan and the Bat
- The Swan and the Bat are **rivals**. They **neutralize** each other.
- Without the Swan, the world would be closed — no passage, no travel, no connection between worlds. The world would be a sealed box, safe but stagnant.
- Without the Bat, the world would be open — every door unlocked, every passage unsealed. The Overseer would pour through. The world would end.
- **Both are necessary.** Both are needed. The Swan opens what must be opened. The Bat closes what must be closed.
- **The Fool Trio** keeps them from destroying each other.

## The Royal Families — The Keepers of the Legend
- The legend of the Swan, the Bat, and the Fool is **not known to commoners**. It is **not taught in temples**. It is **not written in the Edisi Law**.
- It is passed down through the **royal families of Oversee** — the old bloodlines who ruled before the Commonwealth. They are not the Klan Lei. They are older. They are the **original kings and queens** — the ones who were already old when the Commonwealth was young.
- The royal families know the legend. They know the Bat Trio exists. They know the Fool Trio is the key. They have been waiting for the right moment — the moment when the Swan and the Bat would be needed again.
- **They are waiting for the party.** They have been waiting for centuries.

## The Six Blades — A Later Invention
- The "six blades" are **not ancient**. They are a **later invention** — a **corruption** of the old legend.
- They were forged by the **Klan Lei** — specifically by **Lei Firsa** — to **replace** the old legend. She wanted to erase the Bat and the Fool from history, and to make the Swan look like the only true set.
- **The six blades are the tools of the Swan — not the Swan itself.** They are the "arrows" the swan carries — the cutting tools that clear the path.
- The six blades are: Swaith, Rayio, Kkana, Frostfang, Zenahhi, Terrde.
- **But three of them are not blades at all.** Swaith is a dagger. Kkana is a greatsword. Zenahhi is a longsword. They are the **Fool Trio** — not blades of the Swan.
- The other three — Rayio (longsword), Frostfang (greataxe), Terrde (longsword) — are the **Bat Trio**, repurposed by Lei Firsa as "blades of the Swan."
- **Lei Firsa stole the Bat Trio and the Fool Trio** from the royal families and rebranded them as blades of the Swan. She hid the truth. She rewrote the legend.
- **The royal families have been waiting for someone to expose this lie.** They have been waiting for the party.

## What the Party Knows
- They know about the **Swan Trio** — they have seen Gustaero, and they have heard of Niette and Alnean.
- They know about the **Six Blades** — but the six blades are a **later invention**, a **corruption** of the old legend.
- They do not know about the **Bat Trio**.
- They do not know about the **Fool Trio**.
- They do not know that the nine ancient weapons were **originally three trios** — not six blades and three relics.

## What the Party Does NOT Know
- The **six blades** are not ancient. They are a **later invention** — a **corruption** of the old legend.
- The **Bat Trio** still exists. It is hidden. It is guarded by the **royal families of Oversee**.
- The **Fool Trio** is scattered. Swaith with Shin, Kkana with Mira, Zenahhi in Canyon of Lament.
- The **Swan and the Bat** are not enemies. They are **rivals** — two sides of the same coin.
- The **Fool Trio** is the key. Without it, the Swan and the Bat would destroy each other — and the world with them.
- **Lei Firsa stole the Bat Trio and the Fool Trio** from the royal families and rebranded them as blades of the Swan.
- The **royal families** have been waiting for someone to expose this lie.
- The **Fool Trio** is tied to the Tarot of the Architect — the same tarot the party drew from in Session 02.

### The Three Trios and the Endgame
- If the party unites **all three trios**, they can:
  - **Open the Gap wide enough** for the demons to pour through — and let the Overseer eat them instead of the world. (Hildag's plan.)
  - **Seal the Gap permanently** — ending all passage between worlds forever. (Valerius's plan, if he could achieve it.)
  - **Find the Fool's choice** — the third way. The Fool Trio is the key to the **third path** — to freeing the Valla without destroying the world.
- **The Fool Trio is the deciding factor.** Without it, the party is forced to choose between the Swan (open) and the Bat (seal). With it, they can find a third option.
- **This is the true purpose of the three trios.** They are not just weapons. They are **tools of choice**.
