> [!info] The Statues That Move
> Ancient dragonborn protectors, frozen in ice. They are not dead. They are waiting.

## What Are They?
- Hundreds of ice statues lining the path to the Spire of Whispering Ice.
- They look like dragonborn warriors — armor, weapons, horns.
- They are **alive**. They are frozen in time, not dead.
- They move when no one is looking. They only point in one direction: **toward Frosthaven**.

## The Legend
- They are the **original protectors** of Klan Frostscale. They fought an ancient threat — a creature from the Gap — and were frozen by <a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a> to save them.
- They will thaw when **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a> dies** or when **the Gap opens**.
- They will fight for the clan one last time.

## What the Party Knows
- They saw the statues in Session 5. They did not know they were alive.
::/pf
%% DM SECRETS %%
- The Frozen Legion is not just a legend. They are **real dragonborn**, frozen by <a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a> 1,000 years ago to stop a Gap invasion.
- If <a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a> dies, they will thaw. They will be confused, angry, and dangerous. They will not recognize the modern clan.
- If the party can **communicate with them** (using Dragonborn language or Syl's connection to <a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a>), they can recruit them as allies.
- The Legion is **1,000 strong**. They are all CR 3. They are enough to turn the tide of a battle.
- This is a **Season 3 optional quest**. The party can choose to thaw them or leave them frozen.
