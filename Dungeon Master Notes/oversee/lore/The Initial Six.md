## The Six
- **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Alejandro">Lei Alejandro</a>** — Leader. Wind mage. Descendant of Lei Wendigo. Betrayed the others. **Alive** — hiding in the Bahtera Bawah Tanah.
- **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Atreus%20Grin">Atreus Grin</a>** — Druid. Eternal smile curse. Wanted to prove Grin weren't monsters. **Deceased** — Echo in Chenalia.
- **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Sam%20Hurls">Sam Hurls</a>** — Bard. Collector of stories. Lute player. Never finished his last song. **Deceased** — Echo released by Swaith.
- **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Gzirmo%20Kopling">Gzirmo Kopling</a>** — Dragonborn. Mechanical genius. Kept his clan's sadness to himself. **Deceased** — Echo in Arujaya, slowly fading.
- **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Atlan%20Nouveau">Atlan Nouveau</a>** — Human. Oceanographer. Loved Reins. Father of <a href="/oversee/npcs/Mira%20Nouveau">Mira</a>. **Deceased** — Echo in Juto Port City.
- **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Olaf%20Guirela">Olaf Guirela</a>** — Half-orc. Ranger. Believed in action over gods. Taught Gordon. **Deceased** — Echo near Gordon's grave.

## What We Learned in Sam's Memory
- They were **betrayed by Lei Alejandro**.
- The ritual was not a summoning — it was a **feeding**. Alejandro drained their souls to sustain **Simon** (the Valla), whose soul was cracking.
- Without **Safayer** (blue Gemheart), the souls were not purified.
- **The Echoes** were born from this failure.

### The Initial Six — Full Canon
- The **Initial Six** were the greatest heroes of their generation. They were not chosen by prophecy. They were not chosen by gods. They were chosen by **Lei Alejandro** — a descendant of Lei Wendigo, a respected hero, a man who had lost his wife and child in tragic circumstances.
- Each of them had a reason to join:
  - **Alejandro** — grief. He wanted to save the world because he could not save Ghaniba.
  - **Atreus** — hope. He wanted to prove the Grin were not monsters.
  - **Sam** — stories. He wanted to write the greatest song ever written.
  - **Gzirmo** — duty. He wanted to protect the world his clan had abandoned.
  - **Atlan** — love. He wanted to protect Mira and Reins.
  - **Olaf** — justice. He wanted to protect the innocent from the powerful.
- **They were not fools.** They were not naive. They were heroes — the kind of heroes who had seen too much, lost too much, and still chose to fight. They trusted Alejandro because he was one of them. They trusted the ritual because they believed in the cause. They died because they were betrayed.

### The Ritual — What Really Happened
- **200 AV.** The Initial Six gathered at an ancient altar — a Cycle Clan ruin, hidden in the mountains. Alejandro told them it was a ritual to summon a new Valla — to find a soul willing to replace Simon.
- **This was a lie.** Alejandro did not want to replace Simon. He wanted to **feed** him. Simon's soul was cracking. The Architect was fading. The seal was weakening. Alejandro believed the only way to save the world was to pour strong souls into the Valla — to sustain him for another century.
- **He did not use Safayer.** He did not have it. Safayer was lost — swallowed by Vythrax, hidden in the deep. Without Safayer, the souls were not purified. They became **Echoes** instead of clean energy.
- **He chose the Initial Six because they were the strongest available.** He manipulated them into gathering. He lied to them about the ritual's purpose. He chose them to die.
- **The ritual was interrupted.** Gzirmo realized his armor had an external override. Atlan realized the mana was reversing. Olaf warned the others that the circle was a cattle pen. Atreus laughed — because he could not stop smiling. Sam heard the sour note of betrayal.
- **They died fighting.** They did not go quietly. They did not accept their fate. They fought until the light consumed them.

### The Echoes — What Happened to Them
- **Sam** became Echo Sam — the many-headed singer of Ghuffghuff. Released by Swaith in Session 03. At peace.
- **Atreus** became Echo Atreus — the guardian of Chenalia. He is protecting a dead village. He does not know it is already dead.
- **Olaf** became Echo Olaf — the guardian who waits near Gordon's grave. He is waiting for Husgus.
- **Atlan** became Echo Atlan — the father who watches over Juto Port City. He is looking for Mira and Reins.
- **Gzirmo** became Echo Gzirmo — the reactor guardian of Arujaya. He is slowly fading.
- **Alejandro** did **not** become an Echo. He is **alive**. He has been hiding in the Bahtera Bawah Tanah for 500 years.

### Alejandro — The Betrayer Who Repented
- **Lei Alejandro is alive.** He has been hiding in the Bahtera Bawah Tanah for 500 years. He has been punishing himself. He has been guarding the Edisi Law. He has been waiting for someone worthy.
- **He holds Topaju.** He found it centuries ago in the ruins of a Lei Roiga temple. He has kept it safe. He will give it to the party if they prove they are worthy.
- **He adopted Livia Vane.** He found her as a child. He raised her. He lied to her about her origin — told her she was a twin who should have died. He did it to protect her. He did it to give her purpose. He was wrong.
- **He is not the final antagonist.** He is a broken man who wants to help. He will offer the party everything he has — if they can prove they are worthy.
- **The "Puppeteer" in Atreus's letter is Lei Firsa** — not Alejandro. Atreus saw through Alejandro. He knew Alejandro was the betrayer. But he also knew Alejandro was a pawn. The real Puppeteer was Firsa.

### The Letter — What Atreus Really Meant
- *"He stands there with his arrogant green eyes"* — Alejandro. Atreus saw him. He knew.
- *"Thinking he can calm the storm with nothing but Wind's Whisper"* — Alejandro's wind magic. He thought he could control the ritual. He was wrong.
- *"He thinks himself the Gatekeeper, when he is no more than a page serving the night's meal to its master"* — Alejandro is not the mastermind. He is a pawn. He is serving Firsa — the true master.
- *"And Sam... ah, poor Sam. He keeps humming beside him"* — Sam was next to Alejandro during the ritual. He was playing his lute. He was trying to warn the others.
- *"Don't look for my body on this earth. I am no longer there. I have become roots"* — Atreus became an Echo. He is in Chenalia. He is protecting the dead.
- *"All keys reside in Emelanica"* — Emelanica is the green Gemheart. It is the key to the truth. It is the key to the third path.
- *"Laugh for me. I will perform my last show"* — Atreus knew he would die. He wanted the party to laugh. He wanted them to remember him smiling.
