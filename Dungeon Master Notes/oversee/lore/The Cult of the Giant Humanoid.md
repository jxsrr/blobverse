> [!info] Who Worships the Shell?
> A cult that formed when the Shell appeared in the sky. They believe it is a god.

## Beliefs
- The Giant Humanoid is a **divine messenger**.
- It came to **elevate** the worthy and **destroy** the unworthy.
- Its hollow body is a **test** — only the pure can enter it.
- The cult awaits the day the Humanoid **returns to claim its shell**.

## Practices
- Ritual fasting. Meditation. Chanting.
- Pilgrimages to high places to "see the Humanoid closer."
- Sacrifices of magical items — "food for the god."
- Secret cells in every major city.

## Current Status
- Suppressed by the Commonwealth. Declared heretical.
- Still active. Members hide in plain sight.
- The cult has infiltrated the **Lyceum University** and the **Observatorium**.
