> [!info] The Ages of the World
> This is the history the party has pieced together from Sam's memory, Atlan's diary, the Cycle Clan files, and Rivan's notes.

## The Primordial Era (Before Time)
- **The Overseer** exists in the void. It is hunger without form.
- **The Architect** is born from the leftovers of the Overseer's consumption. It is preservation without form.
- The Architect sacrifices part of itself to create **Oversee** — a dimension hidden behind **The Gap**.
- The Architect becomes the **first Valla** — the living seal.
- Its vital organs crystallize into the **four Gemhearts**: Emelanica, Safayer, Rubion, Topaju.

## Beginning of Time (1XXXX – 20XX BV)
- **The First Trees** — many primordial trees — are created by the Architect. They filter Ambitio-Ether from the air and crystallize it into life.
- **The Inbu** are born from the fruit of the First Trees. They are the first conscious race — created from part of the Architect itself, in its image.
- The Inbu **diverge into the races** — Humans, Elves, Dwarves, Halflings, Gnomes, Dragonborn. The Architect manipulates the environment to force the differentiation — to hide them from the Overseer.
- **The Rapture** happens multiple times. Each time, a High Magic civilization grows too bright and is consumed by the **Devourer Comet**.
- Only the **magic-deaf** survive. The cycle repeats.

## Age of Knowledge (19XX – 10XX BV)
- **~1040 BV** — **Guanik Wiseman** sees the **Shell** descend from the sky from the First Observatory at **Danau Kuno**. He records the video. He receives a vision from the Architect — **the Architect is inside him**.
- **~1040 BV** — Wiseman founds **The Cycle Clan**. He becomes the **first Kanselir Agung** of the **One Voice Commonwealth**.
- **~104X BV** — The Cycle Clan excavates the **Gemhearts**. The original **Edisi Law** is signed by Guanik — the promise.
- **~10XX BV** — The **maintenance rituals** for the Architect are established. The Valla is **not** replaced. The Architect is maintained.
- **~10XX BV** — **Gavaevodata Grin** steps into the Gap. She meets **Zargoth**. They marry. The **Grin bloodline** is born.

## Age of Nurturing (9XX – 01 BV)
- **~870 BV** — Wiseman receives a vision from the Architect about **HVS shells** — artificial vessels for gods.
- **~870 BV** — Wiseman creates **HVS-01** and **HVS-02** (with Rivan's schematics). They are lost when Lei Firsa seizes the Cycle Clan.
- **~50 BV** — **Lei Firsa** is born. Illegitimate daughter of a merchant family. She becomes a ranger.
- **~45 BV** — **Simon Greenbottle** is born in Greenvale. He is a halfling shepherd.
- **~21 BV** — Simon receives **Zenahhi** from a dying knight named **Ksatrian** (age 24).
- **~18 BV** — Simon receives **Terrde** from Ksatrian (age 27).
- **~15 BV** — Simon finds **Swaith** in the temple of Dur-Khazim (age 30).
- **~12 BV** — Simon receives **Frostfang** from **Lyra Frostweaver** (age 33).
- **~09 BV** — Simon receives **Kkana** from the stone giant **Grakkar** (age 36).
- **~06 BV** — Simon receives **Rayio** from a stranger (age 39).
- **~04 BV** — Simon receives **Gustaero** from **Edna Gustaero** in Wind's Rest (age 41).
- **~03 BV** — Simon meets **Lei Firsa** and **Grin Qain**. They travel together.
- **~02 BV** — Lei Firsa coerces Simon into marriage. She is already pregnant with quadruplets.
- **~01 BV** — **The Betrayal**. Lei Firsa seizes the Cycle Clan. She sacrifices **Grin Qain** to force Simon into the Valla role. Simon becomes the **second Valla** — the first mortal Valla. The Architect is **not** released. It is left in place, fading.

## The First God (01 – 70 AV)
- **01 AV** — Lei Firsa declares the new calendar. She rewrites history. Simon becomes "Lei the Great." She becomes a male wizard in official records.
- **01–20 AV** — The four branches of **The Lei Clan** are established. The Gemhearts are distributed — but not to the branches as claimed.
- **10 AV** — The **six legendary blades** are scattered across the world by Lei Firsa. **Swaith is magically delivered to Shin Van Hel** — centuries later, not now.
- **45 AV** — **Lei Watigi** assaults **Vythrax**. Syl's egg is conceived. The "Breathstone" lie begins.
- **50 AV** — The **twin-killing tradition** is invented by Lei Firsa.

## Initial Six Era (180 – 200 AV)
- **~180 AV** — **Gordon** is born in the mountains of Harka.
- **~180 AV** — **Gunnar Guirela** finds **Gustaero** in his fishing net in Wind's Rest.
- **~190 AV** — **Olaf Guirela** and **Sam Hurls** meet young **Gordon** in Domido Forest. They train him. Olaf gives him Gustaero. Sam teaches him the melody.
- **~190 AV** — **Lei Alejandro** falls in love with **Ratu Ghaniba** (goblin queen). She dies in childbirth. He gives **Emelanica** to the goblins.
- **200 AV** — **The Great Disappearance**. Lei Alejandro sacrifices the Initial Six to feed Simon. The **Echoes** are born. **Mira Nouveau** witnesses it. **Keeper of Secret** is founded.

## Power Surge (201 – 699 AV)
- **260 AV** — The **Gruffgud War** begins. The Commonwealth secretly funds both sides to hide the **Gunung Rayu** excavation.
- **270 AV** — The **Grin Kingdom** falls. **Will of Grin** is founded by **Hildag Grin** (with Geneva and Accarus).
- **650 AV** — The **Gruffgud War** ends. **Gordon "The Noisy Rhino"** brokers peace. He is forgotten by history.
- **674 AV** — **The Great Strike**. A meteor hits Rioquan. The continent splits. **Accarus** survives in a cellar. **Antrem** is swept away — lost.
- **682 AV** — Antrem leaves with Accarus. Vya is 4 years old.
- **683 AV** — Vreina makes a pact with **Kopling** to protect Vya.
- **10 AV** — **The Grace** falls. Shin's order is destroyed by **Zargoyle**. He wakes 690 years later — in **700 AV**.
- **690 AV** — **Gordon** finds **Husgus** in Domido Forest. He raises him.
- **694 AV** — **Zure Ama** falls into Oversee. He lands in Nesia.
- **695 AV** — **Gordon** is killed by **Malakor**. Husgus kills Malakor and transforms into a halfling.
- **699 AV** — **Valerius Thorne** does **not** take Topaju from Arbor's Observatorium. This never happened. Topaju is with **Lei Alejandro**.

## The Campaign (700 AV)
- **0700-06-01** — The Five Sticks form at The Yawning Guild. Session 1 begins.
- **Session 1** — Greyland Castle. Emelanica recovered. Castle collapses.
- **Session 2** — Juto Port City. Keeper of Secret joined. Gustaero mission assigned. **Emelanica and Rubion handed to Mira.**
- **Session 3** — Ghuffghuff. Echo Sam released. **Accarus** appears.
- **Session 4** — Padre City. Festival. Donta dies. Will of Grin chosen.
- **Session 5** — Maw Desert. Sam's memory. **Vythrax** healed. **Jarko** killed. **Safayer** recovered.
- **Session 6** — Chenalia. Darvin revealed. Arujaya entered. Ama's arm fused.
- **Session 7** — The Depths. Overseer seen. Livia captured. Escape.

### Corrections Applied
- **CLARIFIED:** The First Trees are **many** primordial trees — not a single dead root in Elis. The Inbu were born from their fruit. The dead root in Elis is **one of them**, 10,000 years old.
- **CLARIFIED:** The Inbu were created **from part of the Architect itself, in its image**. They were "failed" because they held too much Ambitio-Ether. The Architect **deliberately manipulated the environment** to force differentiation — to hide them from the Overseer.
- **CLARIFIED:** Guanik Wiseman was the **first Kanselir Agung** of the One Voice Commonwealth — not Geldfre. Geldfre came later, at the time of the Initial Six (200 AV).
- **CLARIFIED:** The maintenance rituals for the Architect are **not** Valla replacements. The Valla has been replaced only once — Simon.
- **CLARIFIED:** Lei Firsa was **already pregnant with quadruplets** when Simon was placed on the altar.
- **CLARIFIED:** Simon was born **~45 BV**, not ~30 BV. He was **44 at the ritual** in 01 BV.
- **CLARIFIED:** **Gustaero** is not one of the six blades. It is one of the three Swan relics. It was found by Gunnar Guirela, passed to Olaf, then Gordon, then Husgus.
- **CLARIFIED:** **Swaith** is one of the six blades — but in the older canon. In the **current canon**, Swaith is one of the **Fool Trio** — not a blade of the Swan. It was magically delivered to Shin — not found as loot.
- **CLARIFIED:** **Topaju** is with **Lei Alejandro**. The "Valerius took a fragment from Danau Kuno in 699 AV" entry is **removed**. It never happened.
- **CLARIFIED:** **Danau Kuno** is now **Tarakita University of Heroism** — not Juto Port City.
- **CLARIFIED:** **The Grace** was destroyed in **10 AV** by **Zargoyle** — not in 690 AV, not by Shin, not by Lei Firsa. Shin was a victim. He woke up **690 years later** — in **700 AV**.
- **CLARIFIED:** **The Valla has only been replaced once** — Simon. "Dozens of Vallas" is propaganda.
- **CLARIFIED:** **Lei Firsa's current status is ambiguous.** She may be alive, dead, or something else. The DM decides.
- **CLARIFIED:** **The Great Strike** separated the twins — but only Antrem was swept away. Accarus survived in a cellar.
- **REMOVED:** "Valerius took a fragment of Topaju from Arbor's Observatorium in 699 AV." — This never happened. Topaju is with Lei Alejandro.
- **REMOVED:** "The four-Cipher structure." — There are no Ciphers. Rivan is the main Cycle Clan contact.
- **REMOVED:** "Lei Firsa is the final antagonist." — Her status is ambiguous. The DM decides.
- **REMOVED:** "Simon is the third Valla." — He is the **second** Valla. The Architect was first. There is no Echo Valla.
- **REMOVED:** "The Cycle Clan performs a ritual to replace the Valla every few centuries." — False. The Cycle Clan performed **maintenance rituals** for the Architect. The Valla was replaced only once.
- **REMOVED:** "The Great Strike separated both twins." — Only Antrem was separated. Accarus survived in a cellar.
- **REMOVED:** "The Grace fell in 690 AV." — The Grace fell in **10 AV**. Shin woke up 690 years later, in **700 AV**.

### Date Calculation Note — The Grace
- **Original source said:** "The Grace fell in 690 AV. Shin woke up 690 years later."
- **Mathematical problem:** If The Grace fell in 690 AV and Shin woke 690 years later, he would wake in 1380 AV — not 700 AV.
- **Resolution:** The Grace fell in **10 AV** (690 years before the current campaign in 700 AV). This makes Shin ~700+ years old in the present era — which matches his description as "over 700 years old."
