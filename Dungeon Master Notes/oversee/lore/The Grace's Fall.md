> [!info] The Demon Hunters Who Died
> Shin's old order. Destroyed in **690 AV** during one of his awakenings. Only he survived.

## What Was The Grace?
- An order of demon hunters. Based in Elis. Trained to fight Gap-touched creatures.
- They were not a religious order. They were **soldiers**. They fought what others would not.
- They were the **only defense** against the things that slipped through the Gap.
- They were **not** a Cycle Clan front. They were independent. They were funded by local lords, by grateful villages, by the families of the people they saved.
- **They were a family.** The leader — **Helius** — was the father. Every member was his child. They called each other "brother" and "sister." They called him "Father."
- They found Shin when he was dying of starvation on the shore of Elis. They took him in. They fed him. They gave him a name. They gave him a home.

## The Members
- **Helius** — the father. The leader. An old man who had been an adventurer once, a long time ago. He had lost his party. He had lost everyone he loved. He founded The Grace as a family — a new family, one that would not die. He was not a warrior. He was not a mage. He was a **father**.
- **Shin Van Hel** — the youngest. The one with the smile. The one with the gloves. The one who never talked about his past. He was found on the shore, dying of starvation. Helius took him in. The others treated him as their younger brother.
- **Edra** — the warm one. She befriended Shin on his first day. She sat beside him without being asked. She never treated him as strange. She never asked about the gloves. She never asked about the smile. She was the heart of The Grace.
- **Crest** — the disciplined one. He observed. He documented. He suspected something was wrong with Shin — but he never blamed him. He tried to save him. He was the brain of The Grace.
- **Wati** — the quiet one. A young woman with a gift for healing. She was the one who patched up the others after every fight. She was the one who kept them alive. She was the one who died first.

## The Fall
- In **10 AV**, a **massive Gap tear** opened in Elis. Demons poured through.
- The Grace fought. They held the line. They died.
- **A barbed devil** — a creature from the Gap, a predator with bone spikes and burning eyes — led the assault.
- Shin, Edra, Crest, and Wati fought it together. They were **defeated**. The barbed devil was too strong.
- And then **something happened**.
- **Shin's body changed.** Something rose from behind him — not a creature, not a spell, but a **presence** — a shadow of something ancient and terrible. It was **Zargoth** — the father of the Grin, the demon who loved Gavaevodata, the fragment that had been sleeping inside Shin since he was created.
- Zargoth did not take control. He **helped**. He fought beside his family — beside Shin's family. He protected them.
- But the barbed devil was not alone. It called its master — **Zargoyle** — the predator, the servant of the Overseer, the monster who would one day destroy everything.
- Zargoyle came.
- And when Zargoyle **choked Shin** — when his claws closed around Shin's throat — Shin's body **froze**. A dome of ice erupted from him — not a spell, not a curse, but a **reaction** — a desperate, instinctive burst of power that froze everything around him.
- When the ice melted, **Wati was dead**. **Edra was dying**. **Crest was dying**. The barbed devil was gone. Zargoyle was gone.
- And Shin was **still alive** — holding both Zargoth and Zargoyle inside him, though he did not know it.
- **He remembers only white. And the screams of his siblings.**

## What Shin Knows
- He was found by The Grace. He was raised by Helius. He was loved by Edra and Crest.
- He does not remember what happened during the fall. He remembers only white, and screaming, and then falling back into slumber before waking again in **700 AV**.
- He believes he destroyed The Grace. He believes he killed his family.
- He does not know about Zargoth. He does not know about Zargoyle. He does not know about the frozen dome.
- He does not know that Edra and Crest did not blame him. He does not know that they tried to save him.
- He does not know that Edra wrote him a letter before she died. It is in Zundabun. He has never read it.

### The Grace — What It Really Was
- The Grace was **not** a Cycle Clan front. It was an **independent order** of demon hunters. They were funded by local lords, by grateful villages, by the families of the people they saved.
- They were **good people**. They fought the things that slipped through the Gap. They protected the innocent. They died doing it.
- They were **not** perfect. They were soldiers. They were hardened. They had seen too much. But they were good.
- **They were a family.** Helius was the father. Every member was his child. They called each other "brother" and "sister." They called him "Father." This was not a metaphor. This was how they lived.
- **Helius found Shin on the shore.** He was dying of starvation. He was a child — perhaps eight or nine years old. He had no memory. He had no name. Helius took him in. He fed him. He gave him a name. He gave him a home.

### The Fall — What Really Happened
- **10 AV.** A massive Gap tear opened in Elis. Demons poured through.
- The Grace fought. They held the line. They died.
- **A barbed devil** — a creature of the Gap, a predator with bone spikes and burning eyes — led the assault. It was not a common demon. It was a **hunter** — a creature bred for violence.
- **Shin, Edra, Crest, and Wati fought it together.** They were defeated. The barbed devil was too strong.
- **And then something happened.** Shin's body changed. Something rose from behind him — not a creature, not a spell, but a **presence** — a shadow of something ancient and terrible.
- **It was Zargoth.** The father of the Grin. The demon who loved Gavaevodata. The fragment that had been sleeping inside Shin since he was created. He woke. He fought beside his family — beside Shin's family. He protected them.
- **But the barbed devil called its master.** It called **Zargoyle** — the predator, the servant of the Overseer, the monster who would one day destroy everything.
- **Zargoyle came.** And when Zargoyle choked Shin — when his claws closed around Shin's throat — Shin's body **froze**. A dome of ice erupted from him — not a spell, not a curse, but a **reaction** — a desperate, instinctive burst of power that froze everything around him.
- **When the ice melted**, Wati was dead. Edra was dying. Crest was dying. The barbed devil was gone. Zargoyle was gone.
- **And Shin was still alive** — holding both Zargoth and Zargoyle inside him, though he did not know it.
- **He remembers only white. And the screams of his siblings.**

### Zargoth and Zargoyle — The Two Entities
- **Zargoth** is the father. The ancient demon who loved Gavaevodata. The fragment trapped inside Shin. He is **not hostile**. He is **not a predator**. He is **not a monster**. He is a father. He woke during the fall to protect his family. He fought beside them. He was driven back into dormancy when Shin froze — but he did not go willingly. He was protecting Shin.
- **Zargoyle** is the predator. A demon of the Gap. A servant of the Overseer. A creature of hunger and destruction. It came when the barbed devil called. It tried to kill Shin. It was driven back by the frozen dome — but it was not destroyed. It was **contained** — sealed inside Shin, alongside Zargoth.
- **Shin carries both.** He does not know this. He thinks the "curse" is one thing. It is two. One is a father. One is a monster.
- **Zargoth has been protecting Shin from Zargoyle.** He has been keeping the monster contained. He has been keeping Shin safe. He has been doing it quietly, subtly, in ways Shin does not understand.

### The Frozen Dome — What It Was
- The frozen dome was **not a spell**. It was not a curse. It was a **reaction** — a desperate, instinctive burst of power from Shin's shell.
- Shin is **HVS-02** — the Human Vessel Shell designed to hold Zargoth. When Zargoyle choked him, the shell **reacted**. It froze — not because Shin cast a spell, but because the shell itself was trying to protect its occupant.
- The frozen dome was the shell's **emergency protocol**: *"If the occupant is in mortal danger, release a burst of cryogenic energy to neutralize the threat."*
- It worked. Zargoyle was driven back. The barbed devil was frozen. But the dome also froze Wati, Edra, and Crest — not because Shin wanted to hurt them, but because the shell did not distinguish between friend and foe. It only knew that its occupant was in danger.
- **Wati died instantly.** She was closest to Shin. The cold was too much for her.
- **Edra and Crest survived** — barely. They were farther away. They were frozen, but not killed. They died later — of their wounds, of the cold, of the chaos that followed.
- **Shin does not know this.** He thinks he killed them. He thinks the frozen dome was his fault. It was not. It was the shell. It was the Architect's design. It was trying to save him.

### Edra and Crest — The Friends Who Did Not Blame Him
- **Edra** wrote Shin a letter before she died. It was never delivered. It is in **Zundabun**, held by **Moro** the shadowless bartender. It says: *"Shin, I know you're hiding something. Don't run. We are your family, whatever you carry."*
- **Crest** kept notes on Shin. They were recovered by the Commonwealth after the fall. They are in the Lyceum archives. They were not accusations. They were observations of a friend. One entry reads: *"Subject shows signs of possession. Recommend non-lethal containment if triggered. Subject is not a threat. Subject is Shin."*
- **They did not blame Shin.** They tried to save him. They died trying. He has never known this. He has spent 690 years believing he was the monster. He is not.

### Helius — The Father Who Knew
- **Helius was a former adventurer.** He had been part of a party once — a long time ago. They had fought demons. They had fought Gap-touched creatures. They had fought things that should not exist. They had died. He had survived. He had retired. He had chosen solitude.
- **He had seen HVS shells in his adventuring days.** He had seen the tubes. He had seen the empty bodies. He had seen the Cycle Clan's work. He knew what they were. He knew what they were for. He knew they were designed to hold something — something old, something powerful, something that should not be contained in a mortal body.
- **He knew what Shin was.** He knew the child he found on the shore was not a normal child. He knew the gloves, the smile, the silence — they were not quirks. They were **signs**. They were the marks of a shell.
- **He never told Shin.** He never told him he was HVS-02. He never told him he was a shell. He never told him he was meant to hold Zargoth. He wanted Shin to be human. He wanted Shin to have a childhood. He wanted Shin to have a choice.
- **His last words to Shin** — whispered on his deathbed — were: *"You are not a shell. You are my son. Whatever else you are, that is true."*
- **Shin has never understood what he meant.** He thinks it was just a dying man's rambling. He does not know Helius knew the truth. He does not know Helius was trying to tell him. He does not know Helius was trying to set him free.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- The Grace was **not** a Cycle Clan front. It was an independent order of demon hunters.
- The Grace was destroyed in **10 AV** — not in "the disaster that created the Echoes" (~200 AV).
- **Shin was not responsible** for the destruction of The Grace. He was a victim. He was used by Zargoyle — and he was saved by Zargoth.
- **Zargoyle** possessed Shin. **Zargoth** contained Zargoyle. The two entities are distinct.
- **Edra and Crest did not blame Shin.** They tried to save him.
- **Edra wrote him a letter.** It is in Zundabun. He has never read it.
- **Crest's notes** are in the Lyceum archives. They are not accusations. They are observations of a friend.
- **Helius knew Shin was HVS-02.** He never told him. He wanted Shin to be human.
- **The frozen dome was not a spell.** It was the shell's emergency protocol. It was trying to save Shin. It killed Wati by accident.
- **Shin does not know about Zargoth.** He does not know about Zargoyle. He does not know about the frozen dome. He remembers only white and screaming.

### Season 3 Reveal Schedule
- **Session 8–10** — The party reaches the Bahtera Bawah Tanah. They learn about Zargoyle. They learn about Zargoth. They learn Shin was not responsible.
- **Session 11** — Shin learns the truth about The Grace. He learns he was a victim. He learns Edra and Crest did not blame him. He reads Edra's letter. He breaks. He weeps. He smiles. He always smiles.
- **Session 11** — Shin learns the truth about Zargoth. He learns Zargoth is a father, not a monster. He learns Zargoth woke to protect him. He learns Zargoth has been protecting him ever since.
- **Session 11** — Shin learns the truth about the frozen dome. He learns it was the shell's emergency protocol. He learns it was trying to save him. He learns it killed Wati by accident. He learns it was not his fault.
- **Endgame** — Shin must face Zargoyle. He must destroy him. He must contain him. He must find a way to free himself — without freeing the monster. He must decide what to do with Zargoth — release him, or keep him.
