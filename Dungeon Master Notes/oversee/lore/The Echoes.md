> [!info] What We Know
> When Lei Alejandro sacrificed the Initial Six, their souls were not purified. Fragments remain — trapped between worlds.

## What Are Echoes?
- Fragments of the six heroes' souls.
- Not ghosts. Not revenants. **Echoes** — a resonance of identity.
- They appear where they have strong emotional connections.
- They repeat behaviors, songs, or memories — stuck in loops.
- They are **hungry**. An Echo without Safayer's purification is a starving thing. It wants to be whole. It wants to finish what was left unfinished.

## Echoes We Have Met
- **Echo Sam** — Ghuffghuff. A many-headed monster singing an unfinished song. Released by Swaith. Killed by **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Accarus%20Grin">Accarus</a>**. (See `Sam Hurls.md` — living form and Echo form are merged in one page.)

## Echoes We Have NOT Met
- **Echo Atreus Grin** — likely in the burned ruins of Chenalia or the forests he protected.
- **Echo Olaf Guirela** — likely near Gordon's grave in Domido Forest.
- **Echo Atlan Nouveau** — likely in Juto Port City or at the Observatorium.
- **Echo Gzirmo Kopling** — in Arujaya, the reactor guardian. (Already met — cooperative after Swaith.)
- **Echo Lei Alejandro** — **does not exist**. Alejandro is alive. He is not an Echo.

## The Safayer Key
- **Safayer** (blue Gemheart) can purify souls.
- If Safayer is used on the Echoes, they might be **freed**.
- If Safayer is used on **Simon**, the Valla might be healed or destroyed.

### The Echoes — Full Canon
- The Echoes are the **fragments of the Initial Six** — the heroes who were betrayed by Lei Alejandro in 200 AV.
- They were fed to **Simon** — the Valla — to keep him alive. But without **Safayer**, they were not purified. They became Echoes instead of clean energy.
- Each Echo is a **fragment of a soul** — not fully dead, not fully alive. They are aware. They are suffering. They are waiting.
- Each Echo is drawn to a place that resonates with its memories — a place it loved, a person it loved, a moment it cannot forget.
- Each Echo repeats a behavior, a song, a memory — stuck in a loop. They cannot stop. They cannot rest. They cannot move on.

### Echo Sam — Released
- Echo Sam was the first Echo the party met. He haunted Ghuffghuff for centuries. He was released by Swaith in Session 03.
- He returned to Simon. He is at peace — or as close to peace as an Echo can be. He is still trying to finish his song. He may never finish it. But he is no longer hungry. He is no longer alone.
- See `Sam Hurls.md` for the full story. (Living form and Echo form are merged in one page.)

### Echo Atreus — The Guardian of Chenalia
- Echo Atreus is in the **burned ruins of Chenalia** — the same village where Vya was born.
- He is **protecting the village**. He does not know it is already dead. He thinks the burning just happened. He is trying to save people who are no longer there.
- **Echo abilities** (if encountered):
  - **Eternal Smile:** His smile never fades. Creatures who can see him have disadvantage on Insight checks.
  - **Roots of the Forest:** Can move through plants and trees. Casts *Entangle* at will (DC 15).
  - **Verdant Wave (Recharge 5–6):** 30-ft cone, DC 15 Str save, 6d6 bludgeoning, restrained.
  - **Healing Bloom (1/day):** Heals all allies within 30 ft for 30 HP.
- **What his Echo is doing:** Protecting the burned village. He does not know it is already dead. He thinks the burning just happened.
- **When his Echo meets Vya:** He will recognize her as a Grin descendant. He will ask her to "help him save the village."
- **What his Echo will give the party** (if released with Swaith): he will thank them and fade. Before he fades: *"The Puppeteer is still alive. She is in the Ark. She is waiting."*
- **Reveal schedule:** Echo Atreus will appear in Season 3, when the party returns to Chenalia to find Vreina.

### Echo Olaf — The Guardian Who Waits
- Echo Olaf is near **Gordon's grave** in Domido Forest. He is **waiting** for Husgus. He knows Husgus is coming. He has been waiting for a long time.
- **Echo abilities** (if encountered):
  - **Ghostly Bow:** Spectral bow deals force damage instead of piercing. Ignores cover.
  - **Hunter's Mark (1/day):** Marks one creature as his quarry. Advantage on all attacks against it for 24 hours.
  - **Piercing Shot (Recharge 5–6):** Fires an arrow that pierces all creatures in a 60-ft line. DC 15 Dex save, 6d8 force damage.
- **The Three Questions:** Olaf will test Husgus before giving him the boon:
  1. *"Why do you fight?"*
  2. *"Who do you protect?"*
  3. *"What would you sacrifice?"*
- **If Husgus answers truthfully:** Olaf will give him a **spectral arrow** that can pierce any armor once.
- **If Husgus lies:** Olaf will attack.
- **Before he fades (if released with Swaith):** *"Tell Gordon... I'm proud of him. Tell him... I'm sorry I left."*
- **Reveal schedule:** Echo Olaf will appear in Season 3, when Husgus visits Gordon's grave after learning about HVS-01.

### Echo Atlan — The Father Who Watches
- Echo Atlan is in **Juto Port City** — near the Observatorium, or on the beach where he first found Reins.
- He is **wandering**. He is **looking for Mira**. He is **looking for Reins**. He does not know Reins is alive — trapped in the Gap. He does not know Mira is the leader of the Keeper of Secret. He only knows he lost them both.
- **Echo abilities** (if encountered):
  - **Echo of the Tide:** Can cast *Control Water* at will (DC 16).
  - **Navigator's Sight:** Can see through magical illusions and invisibility within 60 ft.
  - **Echo of the Father:** If reduced to 0 HP, becomes incorporeal, reforms in 1d4 hours.
  - **Healing Waters (1/day):** Heals all allies within 30 ft for 25 HP.
- **What his Echo wants:** To see Mira one last time. To tell her the truth about her mother. To apologize for not being there.
- **When his Echo meets Mira:** He will say: *"My daughter. You have grown so much. I am sorry I could not stay."*
- **What his Echo will give the party** (if released with Swaith): the location of the **Bahtera Bawah Tanah** (the Underground Ark), and confirmation that the **override clause exists**.
- **Reveal schedule:** Echo Atlan will appear in Season 3 — likely in Mira's dreams or in a trance at the Observatorium.

### Echo Gzirmo — The Reactor Guardian
- Echo Gzirmo is in **Arujaya** — the underground city. He is the **cyborg consciousness** that runs the reactor. He is the shadow of a will that refused to die.
- He is **cooperative** after being stabbed with Swaith. He explained the history of Arujaya. He showed the Shell video. He told the party that demons fear water.
- He is **slowly fading**. Each time he powers the reactor, he loses a little more of himself. He knows he is dying. He does not know how to stop.
- **What he wants:** To see the sun one last time. To know that the truth is safe. To rest.
- **What he gives the party** (if released with Swaith): a **data chip** with the Shell video, and a warning: *"Do not trust the surface. The Commonwealth knows you are here. They will be waiting."*
- **Reveal schedule:** Echo Gzirmo has already been met (Session 06). He will fade in Season 3 — after the party leaves Arujaya.

### Echo Alejandro — Does Not Exist
- **Lei Alejandro is alive.** He is not an Echo. He is hiding in the **Bahtera Bawah Tanah**. He is guarding the Edisi Law. He is waiting for someone worthy.
- **There is no Echo Alejandro.** The party will never meet an Echo of Alejandro. They will meet the man himself — broken, penitent, and waiting.
- **If the party ever encounters a hostile Echo that claims to be Alejandro**, it is lying. It is a trick. It is a trap. Alejandro is alive.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- The Echoes are **not evil**. They are hungry. They are lost. They are trying to finish what was left unfinished.
- **Echo Sam was not evil.** He was trying to finish his song. (See `Sam Hurls.md`.)
- **Echo Atreus is protecting a dead village.** He does not know it is already dead.
- **Echo Olaf is waiting for Husgus.** He is testing him. He is giving him a gift.
- **Echo Atlan is looking for Mira and Reins.** He does not know Reins is alive.
- **Echo Gzirmo is dying.** He is slowly fading. He will not survive the campaign.
- **Echo Alejandro does not exist.** Alejandro is alive. He is not an Echo.
