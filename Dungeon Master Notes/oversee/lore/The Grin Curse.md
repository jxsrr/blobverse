> [!info] The Eternal Smile
> A curse. A blessing. A stigma. The mark of the Grin bloodline.

## What Is the Curse?
- Every descendant of **Gavaevodata** and **Zargoth** is born with a **permanent smile**.
- It cannot be removed. It cannot be hidden.
- In joy, in grief, in rage — the smile remains.
- It is not a happy smile. It is a **frozen** smile.

## Why Does It Exist?
- Gavaevodata was an elf princess. Zargoth was a demon from the Gap.
- They fell in love. They married. Their children inherited the smile.
- The smile is the **mark of the Gap** — a reminder of the pact between elf and demon.
- It also grants **magic-deafness**. The Grin cannot use magic. They cannot be detected by the Overseer.

## The Social Stigma
- The Grin are feared and hated across Oversee.
- Their smile is seen as a sign of evil. They are called "demon-blooded."
- They were driven from their kingdom. They live in hiding.
- The **Will of Grin** exists to fight this stigma.

## The Truth About the Curse
- The curse is not a punishment. It is a **gift**.
- Because the Grin cannot use magic, they are invisible to the Overseer.
- They are the only race that can survive the Rapture without hiding.
- The smile is a **mask** — it hides their true emotions from the world, and from the Overseer.
::/pf
%% DM SECRETS %%
- The Grin curse can be broken. But doing so would make them visible to the Overseer.
- The **Will of Grin** knows this. They are divided: some want to break the curse, some want to keep it.
- Vya is the key. Her fire can burn the curse away — or strengthen it.
- If Vya breaks the curse, the Grin will become mortal, magical, and visible. They will be hunted.
- If Vya strengthens the curse, the Grin will remain hidden. They will survive. But they will never be free.
- This is a Season 3 moral choice.
