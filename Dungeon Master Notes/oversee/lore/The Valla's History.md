> [!info] The Living Seals
> The Valla is not a god. It is a **prisoner** — a soul stretched across reality to hold back the Overseer.

## The First Valla — The Architect
- Split itself to create **Oversee** and the **Gemhearts** (its own crystallized organs).
- Became the **first Valla** — a living seal at the threshold between Oversee and the Overseer.
- Has held the seal for **thousands of years**. Its strength is fading.
- It did not create the Valla tradition to replace itself — it created an **organization** to select the best candidate when the time came. That organization became the **Cycle Clan**.

## The Second Valla — Simon Greenbottle
- A halfling shepherd. Too kind for the world.
- Was **forced** into the Valla role by **Lei Firsa** in **01 BV** — not willingly. He did not know what the ritual would do.
- Has held the seal for **700 years**. His soul is cracking.
- The world does not know his name. Public legend credits **"Lei the Great"** — a lie invented by Lei Firsa.
- He is still conscious. He hears the Echoes. He knows what was done to him.

## There Have Only Ever Been Two Vallas
- The Architect — the first.
- Simon Greenbottle — the second.
- There has never been another. The "dozens of Vallas" story is **propaganda** invented by Lei Firsa's scribes after 01 BV to make the ritual sound routine.
- The "Valla replaced every few centuries" claim is the same lie. It never happened. The Architect was never released. Simon was never released.
- **Echo is not a Valla.** Echo is the name given to the **failed feeding ritual** performed by **Lei Alejandro** around **200 AV**. He tried to pour five strong souls — the **Initial Six** — into Simon to keep him alive. He did not use **Safayer**. The souls were not purified. They became **Echoes** instead of clean energy.
- Echo is the reason the Echoes exist. Echo is not a Valla. Echo is a **mistake**.

## The Ritual of Replacement (Corrupted)
- Designed by the Architect as a **voluntary transfer** — a willing sacrifice, purified by **Safayer**, so the old Valla could be released and the new one could take up the burden.
- **Lei Firsa corrupted it in 01 BV.** She forced Simon into the seal without Safayer purification, without consent, and without releasing the Architect. The Architect was left in place, weakened, while Simon was added as a second seal — a patch over a wound that was never meant to be patched this way.
- This is why the seal is unstable. This is why Simon is in pain. This is why the Echoes exist.

## What the Party Knows
- Simon is the Valla.
- The world believes "Lei the Great" is the Valla. He does not exist.
- The ritual exists. They have seen pieces of it.
- The Echoes are the result of an incomplete ritual.

## What the Party Does NOT Know
- The Architect is still holding the seal. It is **not dead**. It is **fading**.
- Simon is the **second** Valla — there has never been another mortal before him.
- Echo is not a Valla. Echo is a failed feeding.
- The "dozens of Vallas" story is a lie.

### The Valla — Full Canon
- **There have only ever been two Vallas: the Architect and Simon Greenbottle.**
- The Architect became the first Valla when it sacrificed itself to create Oversee. It has been holding the seal for thousands of years. It is still there. It is still holding. It is fading.
- Simon became the second Valla in **01 BV**, when **Lei Firsa** forced him into the role. He did not volunteer. He did not know what the ritual would do. He has been holding the seal for 700 years. His soul is cracking. The Echoes are the proof.
- **There has never been a third Valla.** The "dozens of Vallas" story is a **lie** — propaganda invented by Lei Firsa's scribes after 01 BV to make the ritual sound routine and prevent anyone from asking what happened to Simon.
- **Echo is not a Valla.** Echo is the name given to the **failed feeding ritual** performed by **Lei Alejandro** around 200 AV. He tried to pour the souls of the Initial Six into Simon to keep him alive. He did not use Safayer. The souls were not purified. They became **Echoes** — fragments trapped between worlds. Echo is the reason the Echoes exist. Echo is not a Valla. Echo is a **mistake**.

### The Architect — Still Holding
- **The Architect is still holding the seal.** It became the first Valla and never left. It is now a faint, burnt-out remnant of what it once was.
- It is not dead. It is not released. It is **still there** — at the threshold, at the edge of reality, holding back the Overseer.
- **If the Architect is released**, the seal will collapse. The Overseer will see everything. The world will end.
- **If the Architect dies**, the seal will collapse. The Overseer will see everything. The world will end.
- **The only way to release the Architect without destroying the world** is the **third path** — the Architect's Mercy. It requires the Gemhearts, the three trios, the Edisi Law, and the Architect's consent.

### Simon — Still Holding
- **Simon is still conscious.** He hears the Echoes. He knows what was done to him. He has been holding the seal for 700 years. His soul is cracking.
- **If Simon is released**, the seal will collapse. The Overseer will see everything. The world will end — unless the Architect can hold alone. It cannot. It is too weak.
- **If Simon dies**, the seal will collapse. The Overseer will see everything. The world will end.
- **The only way to release Simon without destroying the world** is the **third path** — the Architect's Mercy. It requires the Gemhearts, the three trios, the Edisi Law, and the Architect's consent.
- **If the party meets Simon**, he will not be a god. He will be a small, weeping halfling in a cloak too large for him. His feet are bare. He has been standing for 700 years. He will look at them and say: *"You came. I did not think anyone would come."*

### The Echoes — What They Really Are
- The Echoes are **not Vallas**. They are not failed Vallas. They are not failed replacements.
- The Echoes are the **fragments of the Initial Six** — the heroes who were betrayed by Lei Alejandro in 200 AV.
- They were fed to **Simon** — the Valla — to keep him alive. But without **Safayer**, they were not purified. They became Echoes instead of clean energy.
- Each Echo is a **fragment of a soul** — not fully dead, not fully alive. They are aware. They are suffering. They are waiting.
- Each Echo is drawn to a place that resonates with its memories — a place it loved, a person it loved, a moment it cannot forget.
- Each Echo repeats a behavior, a song, a memory — stuck in a loop. They cannot stop. They cannot rest. They cannot move on.
- **Safayer can free the Echoes.** If used on an Echo, it can purify the fragment — strip away the hunger, the grief, the memory — and release it. The Echo will fade. It will finally rest.
- **Swaith can also free the Echoes.** The soul-thirsting blade can release trapped souls. This was proven in Session 03, when Shin used Swaith to free Echo Sam.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- The Architect is **still holding the seal**. It is not dead. It is fading.
- Simon is the **second** Valla — there has never been another mortal before him.
- Echo is not a Valla. Echo is a failed feeding.
- The "dozens of Vallas" story is a lie.
- The Echoes are not Vallas. They are the fragments of the Initial Six.
- The only way to free Simon and the Architect without destroying the world is the **third path** — the Architect's Mercy.
