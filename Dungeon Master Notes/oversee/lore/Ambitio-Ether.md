> [!info] The Field That Runs Through Everything
> The magic itself. Not produced. Not owned. Always there.

## What Is It?
- **Ambitio-Ether is the ambient magical field of the universe.** It has always existed — since before the world, since before the Gap.
- It is not "produced" by anyone. It is the medium that magic users tap into.
- When a creature casts a spell, they are **shaping** the ambient field — pulling raw Ambitio-Ether from the universe and giving it form.

## Who Can Shape It?
- **Sorcerers** — born with an innate connection. Always tied to the **Lei bloodline**. They do not need to learn; they shape instinctively.
- **Wizards** — learn to tap into the field through study and discipline. Anyone can learn, if they have the patience.
- **Warlocks** — gain access through a pact or a curse. The field flows through the bond they have made.
- **Amagis (non-magic users)** — cannot shape the field. But they are not silent. They **ripple**. Their bloodlines carried magic before the The Lei Clan rose — the potential is still there, suppressed. They leave a faint trace in the field, like a ripple on still water.

## The Bloodline That Is Invisible
- **The Grin** — the devil-blooded. They are **invisible to the field**. They do not ripple. They cannot shape Ambitio-Ether. They cannot be detected by it.
- This is the Grin curse, and their protection.

## Residue — The Invisible Traces
- When magic is used, the shaped Ambitio-Ether leaves behind **residue**.
- Residue is **invisible** — cannot be seen, felt, or detected by ordinary senses.
- It carries a faint "signature" of whoever shaped it.
- **Renal** (Ama's Imp) can sense and feed on this residue. Ordinary people cannot.
- Residue is not dangerous. It is simply spent.

## The Sludge in Arujaya's Depths
- The black sludge beneath Arujaya is **visible, thick, and toxic**.
- It is **NOT residue**. Residue is invisible. The sludge is something else.
- Echo Gzirmo called it "residual Ambitio-Ether" — but he did not fully explain.
- Touching it is fatal. It corrodes flesh and metal.
- **Ama's Imp-arm can clean it** — but no one knows why yet.

## What the Party Knows
- Ambitio-Ether is the ambient magic field.
- Sorcerers, Wizards, and Warlocks can shape it.
- Amagis cannot shape it, but they ripple — their bloodlines carry suppressed magic.
- The Grin are invisible to the field.
- Using magic leaves invisible residue.
- Renal feeds on residue.
- The sludge is not residue — and it is toxic.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- **Ambitio-Ether is the food of the Overseer.** The Valla filters it so the Overseer does not taste the world.
- **The Overseer hunts strong will.** Creatures of ambition — heroes, tyrants, dreamers — attract its attention. This is why it is drawn to concentrations of power.
- **The sludge is the Overseer's waste** — digested Ambitio-Ether. It drips into the Gap.
- **Renal is a Gap-creature** — that is why it can eat what the Overseer discards.
- **The Valla is a filter, not a god.** It strips memory, ego, identity from the field before the Overseer consumes it.
- **When the Valla fails, the Echoes happen** — unfiltered pain, trapped.
- **The Amagis' ripple is a remnant of pre-Lei magic.** Before Simon became the Valla, other bloodlines had sorcerers and wizards. Lei Firsa suppressed them. The potential did not vanish — it went quiet.
- **The Grin are invisible to the field because of their devil heritage.** Zargoth's blood makes them deaf to Ambitio-Ether — they cannot shape it, and they cannot be tasted by the Overseer. This is their curse and their shield.
