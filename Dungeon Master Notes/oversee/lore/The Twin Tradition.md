> [!info] The Darkest Secret of Klan Lei
> When twins are born to the Klan, one must die. It is the law. It is the curse.

## What Is the Tradition?
- When a Klan Lei mother gives birth to twins, the elder twin is kept. The younger twin is **taken**.
- The younger twin is killed in a quiet ceremony. Their blood is offered to the Valla.
- The Klan believes this **concentrates the power** in the surviving twin.
- It has been practiced for roughly **650 years**.

## Why?
- The Klan believes magic is a finite resource. Twins split the magical potential in half. Killing one twin concentrates the power in the other.
- **This is a lie.** The Klan invented the tradition to **control** their bloodlines and to **feed** their matriarch.
- The real reasons are:
  - **Control.** Twins are unpredictable. They can be more powerful than expected. They can challenge the hierarchy.
  - **Fuel.** Every killed twin's soul is drawn toward the Valla — and the Klan's matriarch has learned to skim that energy for herself. The tradition is not just eugenics. It is a **feeding mechanism**.
  - **Weakness.** Twins are **weaker** than single-born heirs. Their power is split. Klan Lei could not afford weak children — not when their entire legitimacy rested on being the strongest.

## The Discovery — Lei Firsa's Research
- Lei Firsa discovered the truth about twins through decades of experimentation. She tested every combination of Lei blood she could find. She bred children. She measured their magic. She documented their power.
- **She discovered a pattern:**
  - When a child is born **alone** — no twin — the full elemental power of the bloodline flows into them. They are strong. They are compatible with the Gemhearts.
  - When **twins** are born, the power is **split**. Each child receives only half — sometimes less.
  - **The intelligent twin** — the one who develops faster, thinks sharper, speaks sooner — gets the **least** magic. Their mind takes the power that their body would have carried.
  - **The unintelligent twin** — the one who is slower, quieter, simpler — gets the **most** magic. Their mind does not compete for the power. Their body absorbs it.
- **This was the key.** The twin tradition was not just about concentrating power — it was about **which twin** to keep. The Klan kept the **elder** twin — usually the smarter one. But the smarter one was the **weaker** one. The tradition was flawed. It was killing the wrong twin.
- **Lei Firsa wanted to fix it.** She wanted a child who was **both** — intelligent **and** magical. A child who could wield the Gemhearts without being consumed by them. A child who was **whole**.

## The Fix — Hildag Grin's Experiments
- Hildag Grin — the Weaver, the Wakil Pertahanan who defected — had been studying the twin tradition for centuries. She had learned what Lei Firsa learned — but she had gone **further**.
- **She had read about the Arleschia family.** A minor noble family in Elis with a strange bloodline. The Arleschias carried a **fragment of the earth element** — not enough to be mages, but enough to **stabilize** other bloodlines. The Arleschia blood acted as a **bridge** — it allowed the Lei and Grin blood to coexist without canceling each other out.
- **Hildag realized the Arleschias were the key.** She needed a child who was **Grin** (from her line), **Lei Firage** (from the father), and **Arleschia** (from the mother). That child would be the first **True Heir** — a being who carried both anti-magic and pure elemental power, without the weakness of twins.
- **Accarus and Antrem were her rough drafts.** Twin sons. Both had the Grin anti-magic. Both had dormant Lei Firage fire. But they were **twins** — their power was split. Accarus got the intelligence. Antrem got the magic. Neither was whole.
- **Vya was her magnum opus.** She bred Vya after Jarko's death. Vya is not a twin. She is the **full inheritance** — Grin anti-magic, Lei Firage fire, and Arleschia stability. She is the first True Heir. She is the child Hildag had been trying to create for centuries.

## Who Invented the Tradition?
- **Lei Firsa.** Around **50 AV**. Not ancient. Not natural. A deliberate policy.
- Before 50 AV, twins were common and celebrated among the Lei. After 50 AV, they were a death sentence.
- Lei Firsa has never publicly admitted this. The Klan believes the tradition is older than it is.
- **But Lei Firsa's version of the tradition was flawed.** She kept the elder twin — the smarter one — because she believed intelligence was the key to power. She was wrong. The smarter twin was the **weaker** one. The tradition killed the wrong child.
- Hildag Grin learned from Lei Firsa's mistake. She did not kill her twins. She **studied** them. And she learned that the only way to create a **whole** heir was to **avoid twins entirely** — to breed a single child with the right combination of blood.

## The Survivors
- **Valerius Thorne** — the older twin. Kept. Raised in the light. He knows his twin was hidden, not killed. He has been hunting **Darvin** ever since.
- **Darvin Thorne** — the younger twin. **Hidden, not killed.** Valerius's twin. He carries the **claim** to **Lei Roiga** and Topaju — though **Lei Alejandro** currently holds Topaju itself. Valerius will let Darvin exhaust himself stabilizing the continent, then kill him and claim the bloodline.
- **Accarus Grin** and **Antrem Klaus** — the **only surviving twins** of the tradition. Sons of **Hildag Grin** and a **Lei Firage descendant**. Their survival was accidental. Hildag protected them. They are the exception that proves the rule.

## Who Is NOT a Twin
- **Livia Vane** is **not a twin.** She was alone from the start. She was adopted by an old man who was later revealed to be **Lei Alejandro**. Her "twin who should have died" story is a fabrication — possibly her own, possibly Alejandro's, possibly the Commonwealth's. She believes it. She is wrong.
- **Vya Arleschia** is **not a twin.** She is **Hildag Grin's magnum opus** — a later child, deliberately bred after **Jarko's death** to combine the anti-magic protection of the Grin bloodline with the dormant fire of Lei Firage and the stabilizing blood of the Arleschia family. She was never part of a twin pair. She is the refinement, not the experiment.

## What the Party Knows
- They know the tradition exists.
- They know Vya is a True Heir.
- They know Darvin is a True Heir.
- They do not know Livia's full story.
- They do not know Vya's full origin.

### The Tradition — Full Canon
- The tradition is **not ancient**. It was invented by **Lei Firsa** around **50 AV**. She wanted to concentrate the magical power of her bloodline. She has been feeding on twin sacrifices for roughly 650 years.
- The tradition is the **real reason** the Klan Lei is so powerful. They are not just hereditary mages. They are **accumulated power**. Every killed twin has added to the surviving twin's strength.
- The tradition is also the **real reason** Lei Firsa has lived so long (if she is alive). She has been feeding on the twin sacrifices. Every dead twin is a drop of life added to hers.
- **But the tradition was flawed.** Lei Firsa kept the **elder** twin — usually the smarter one. She believed intelligence was the key to power. She was wrong. The smarter twin was the **weaker** one. The tradition killed the wrong child.
- **The intelligent twin gets the least magic.** The unintelligent twin gets the most. Lei Firsa did not realize this until centuries after she invented the tradition.
- **Hildag Grin discovered the truth.** She studied Lei Firsa's research. She studied the twins she had bred. She realized the tradition was flawed — and she set out to create a child who was **both** intelligent and magical. A child who was **whole**.
- **Vya is that child.** She is Grin-Lei-Arleschia. She is not a twin. She is the full inheritance. She is the first True Heir.

### Valerius and Darvin — The Thorne Twins
- **Valerius and Darvin** are the surviving Thorne twins — one kept, one hidden. Valerius knows Darvin exists. He has been waiting for the right moment to kill him and claim the bloodline.
- **Valerius is the elder twin.** He was kept. He was raised in the light. He was groomed for power. He became Kanselir Agung.
- **Darvin is the younger twin.** He was hidden — not killed. He was raised in the shadows. He was told he was "disabled." He was not told about his bloodline. He was not told about Topaju.
- **Valerius believes Darvin carries Topaju.** He is wrong. Darvin carries the **claim**. Lei Alejandro holds the stone.
- **Valerius is hunting his brother for a lie.** If he kills Darvin, nothing will happen. Topaju will still be with Alejandro. Valerius will have killed his brother for nothing.

### Accarus and Antrem — Hildag's Rough Drafts
- **Accarus and Antrem** are the sons of **Hildag Grin**, not of a Lei mother. Hildag hid them from the Klan. This is the secret Will of Grin has been keeping. They are the **only surviving twins** of the Klan Lei's tradition — and they were never registered, never separated, never killed.
- **Accarus got the intelligence.** He is clever, restless, ambitious. He is the leader of Will of Grin's field operations.
- **Antrem got the magic.** He is simple, quiet, kind. He carries the dormant Lei Firage fire — weak, but present.
- **Neither is whole.** They are twins. Their power is split. They are Hildag's rough drafts — her proof that the twin tradition could be subverted, but not perfected.

### Livia Vane — Not a Twin
- **Livia Vane's story is a lie.** She was not a twin. She was alone. She was adopted by **Lei Alejandro** — the old man who raised her. The "twin who should have died" narrative may have come from Alejandro himself, or from Valerius, or from Livia's own need to explain why she felt "wrong." She believes it. It is not true. This is a Season 3 reveal.
- **Alejandro told her she was a twin** to protect her — to give her a reason to be strong, to give her a purpose. He did not know it would haunt her for the rest of her life.
- **If Livia ever learns the truth**, she will break. She will ask: *"Was any of it real?"* Alejandro will answer: *"You were real. You were always real. The rest was a story I told to keep you alive."*

### Vya — Hildag's Magnum Opus
- **Vya is Hildag's magnum opus.** She was bred after **Jarko's death**, when Will of Grin needed a new hope. Hildag combined her own Grin anti-magic with a Lei Firage descendant's dormant fire and an Arleschia noble's stabilizing blood. Vya is the result. She is not a twin. She is the **refinement** of Hildag's decades of experiments.
- **Accarus and Antrem were the rough draft. Vya is the final product.**
- **Jarko was not the True Heir.** He was Grin-Grin-Lei. He did not have the Arleschia blood. His Grin and Lei blood canceled each other out. He was a Grin warrior — not a True Heir. Hildag knew this from the moment he was born. She used him as a decoy.
- **Vya is the first True Heir.** She carries Grin anti-magic, Lei Firage fire, and Arleschia stability. She is the only Lei descendant with **both** the fire of Lei Firage and the anti-magic protection of the Grin. She is the only one who can break the Grin curse — or strengthen it.

### The Tradition — What It Really Means
- **The twin tradition is a tragedy.** It killed the wrong child for 650 years. It destroyed families. It created monsters. It fed Lei Firsa's power.
- **The tradition can be broken.** Exposing it will collapse the Klan Lei. Stopping it will weaken Lei Firsa (or her legacy).
- **Vya is the key.** She is the child the tradition was trying to create — a whole heir, without the weakness of twins. If the party can help her accept her inheritance, she can lead the Grin into a new era.
- **This is the true meaning of the tradition.** It is not about killing children. It is about the desperate search for a whole heir — a search that failed for centuries, and finally succeeded with Vya.
