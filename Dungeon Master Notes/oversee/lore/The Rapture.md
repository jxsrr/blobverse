> [!info] The End of the High Magic Age
> A cosmic event that destroyed every advanced magical civilization in Oversee. It happened many times. It will happen again.

## What Is the Rapture?
- A **cosmic harvest**. The Overseer notices a civilization that has produced too much Ambitio-Ether. It sends a **Devourer Comet** — a shard of its hunger.
- The comet does not burn. It **absorbs light**.
- When the comet passes over a region, every creature with magical talent is **pulled into the sky**. They do not scream. They do not resist. They simply dissolve into mist and rise.
- The comet leaves behind empty clothes, useless magic items, and silent cities.
- The Rapture has happened **at least four times** in recorded history.

## The Signs
- The sky flashes a color beyond the visible spectrum.
- Mages feel dizzy. Then euphoric. Then they begin to dissolve.
- Animals flee. Plants wilt. The air smells of ozone and salt.
- The comet appears in the sky, moving slowly. It is visible for weeks.

## The Survivors
- Only the **magic-deaf** survive. The Grin. The Amagis. The common people.
- They inherit the ruins. They rebuild. They forget.
- The cycle repeats.

## The Silent Rapture
- The **Silent Rapture** is the **same event** as the Rapture — not a separate phenomenon. It is the **quiet** version. The slow, invisible drain of Ambitio-Ether from a civilization that never notices it is being eaten.
- The Grin are the only race that is immune to both — because they are magic-deaf. They are invisible to the Oversight.

## What the Party Knows
- The Rapture is a legend. Most people believe it is a myth.
- The ruins of ancient cities are proof that it happened.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### The Rapture — Full Canon
- The Rapture is **not a natural event**. It is a **feeding mechanism**. The Overseer farms civilizations.
- The Architect built the Gap to hide Oversee from the Overseer. It works — mostly. But when a civilization produces too much Ambitio-Ether, the Gap thins. The Overseer notices.
- The Valla exists to **feed the Overseer** in controlled doses. That is why the seal is necessary. That is why the ritual exists.
- **The Rapture has happened at least four times** in recorded history. Each time, a High Magic civilization grew too bright and was consumed by the Devourer Comet. Only the magic-deaf survived. The cycle repeated.
- **The party is living in a civilization that is approaching Rapture-level magic.** The clock is ticking. The Rapture will happen again in **3 years** if the party does nothing.

### The Devourer Comet — What It Really Is
- The Devourer Comet is **not just a shard of the Overseer**. It is a **fragment of the Shell** — a piece of the Architect's shed skin that the Overseer has **reanimated** and sent back.
- The Shell in the sky is not the only piece of the Architect's body. The comets are pieces of it — animated by the Overseer's hunger, used as tools.
- When a comet passes over a region, it **reaches** for the Ambitio-Ether — it pulls it up, through the magic-users, into the sky. It does not burn. It absorbs. It feeds.
- **The comet is not alive.** It is a tool. It has no will. It has no hunger of its own. It is a spoon in the hands of a hungry god.

### The Silent Rapture — Same Event, Different Name
- The **Silent Rapture** is the **same event** as the Rapture. It is not a separate phenomenon. It is the **quiet** version — the slow, invisible drain of Ambitio-Ether from a civilization that never notices it is being eaten.
- The Grin call it the "Silent Rapture" because they are the only race that can see it — or rather, the only race that **cannot** see it, because they are invisible to the Oversight. They have watched civilizations fade without a single comet in the sky. They have watched mages grow weak, spells fail, and cities crumble — all without a single visible cause.
- **The Silent Rapture is the default.** The Devourer Comet is the **emergency valve** — what happens when the Valla fails, when the seal weakens, when the Overseer notices the world too directly. The Silent Rapture is what happens when the Valla works — when the Overseer feeds slowly, invisibly, without ever noticing what it is eating.
- **The Grin are immune to both.** They are magic-deaf. They are invisible to the Oversight. They are the true survivors.

### The Klan Lei's Role — The Darkest Secret
- **The first Rapture was not an accident.** It was **engineered** by the earliest Klan Lei — the ancestors of Lei Firsa.
- They knew the Overseer was real. They knew the Rapture was coming. They wanted to **survive** it — and they wanted to **eliminate their rivals**.
- They deliberately pushed their rival bloodlines into producing too much Ambitio-Ether. They waited for the Devourer Comet. They hid in the **Grin Kingdom** — which they knew was immune. They survived. Their rivals did not.
- **The Klan Lei engineered the first Rapture.** They have been hiding this fact for thousands of years. It is the darkest secret of the Klan.
- **If the party exposes this truth**, the Klan Lei will collapse. The Commonwealth will fracture. The world will change.

### What the Party Does NOT Know

- The **Devourer Comet is a fragment of the Shell** — a piece of the Architect's shed skin, reanimated by the Overseer.
