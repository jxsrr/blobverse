> [!info] The Living Seal
> The Valla is not a god. It is a **prisoner** — a soul stretched across reality to hold back the Overseer.

## What Is a Valla?
- A mortal soul, sacrificed and stretched into a **living seal**.
- The Valla sits at the threshold between Oversee and the Overseer.
- It absorbs the Overseer's hunger, filtering **Ambitio-Ether** through itself so the Overseer tastes nothing.
- The Valla is in **constant pain**. It is aware. It is awake. It cannot move.

## The First Valla — The Architect
- The Architect split itself to create **Oversee** and the **Gemhearts** — its own crystallized organs.
- It then became the **first Valla** — a living seal at the threshold between Oversee and the Overseer.
- It has held the seal for **thousands of years**.
- Its soul is **burning out**. It is weakening. It is **still there**.
- It created an **organization** to select a replacement when the time came. That organization became the **Cycle Clan**.

## The Second Valla — Simon Greenbottle
- A halfling shepherd. Too kind for the world.
- Was **forced** into the Valla role by **Lei Firsa** in **01 BV** — not willingly. He did not know what the ritual would do.
- Has held the seal for **700 years**.
- His soul is **cracking**. The Echoes are the proof.
- He is still conscious. He hears the Echoes. He knows what was done to him.

## There Have Only Been Two Vallas
- The Architect — the first.
- Simon Greenbottle — the second.
- There has never been another. The "dozens of Vallas" story is **propaganda** invented by Lei Firsa's scribes after 01 BV to make the ritual sound routine.
- The "Valla replaced every few centuries" claim is the same lie. It never happened. The Architect was never released. Simon was never released.

## What "Echo" Really Is
- **Echo is not a Valla.**
- Echo was a **failed feeding ritual** performed by **Lei Alejandro** around **200 AV**.
- He tried to pour five strong souls — the **Initial Six** — into Simon to keep him alive. He was pressured by the Lei bloodline to preserve pure magic.
- He did it **without Safayer** — so the souls were not purified. They became **Echoes** instead of clean energy.
- Echo is the reason the Echoes exist. Echo is not a Valla. Echo is a **mistake**.

## The Ritual of Replacement
- Designed by the Architect as a **voluntary transfer** — a willing sacrifice, purified by **Safayer**, so the old Valla could be released and the new one could take up the burden.
- **Lei Firsa corrupted it in 01 BV.** She forced Simon into the seal without Safayer purification, without consent, and without releasing the Architect. The Architect was left in place, weakened, while Simon was added as a second seal — a patch over a wound that was never meant to be patched this way.
- This is why the seal is unstable. This is why Simon is in pain. This is why the Echoes exist.

## What the Party Knows
- The Valla is not a god. It is a **person**.
- The current Valla is **Simon Greenbottle** — a halfling who never wanted this.
- The Cycle Clan has maintained the seal for thousands of years.
- **Lei Alejandro** betrayed the Initial Six to feed Simon's failing soul.
- The world believes "Lei the Great" is the Valla. He does not exist.

## What the Party Does NOT Know
- The Architect is **still holding the seal**. It is not dead. It is fading.
- Simon was **forced** into the role. He did not volunteer.
- There have only ever been **two Vallas** — not dozens, not hundreds.
- Echo is not a Valla. Echo is a failed feeding.
- The "dozens of Vallas" story is a lie.

- **Lei Alejandro is still alive.** He is hiding in the `The Underground Ark`. He has spent 500 years punishing himself for what he did. He is not the final antagonist. He is a broken man who wants to help. (See `Lei Alejandro.md`.)
- **Simon's true name** is known to very few. The Edisi Law uses it. The party found it in the document. They have not yet met the man.
