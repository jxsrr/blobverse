> [!info] The Four Stones
> Not gems. Not artifacts. **Organs** of a god.

## The Four
| Stone         | Color  | Function                | Current Holder      |
| :------------ | :----- | :---------------------- | :------------------ |
| **Emelanica** | Green  | Time and mana flow      | <a href="/oversee/npcs/Mira%20Nouveau">Mira Nouveau</a> (Keeper of Secret) |
| **Safayer**   | Blue   | Purification of souls   | The Party           |
| **Rubion**    | Red    | Instant annihilation    | <a href="/oversee/npcs/Mira%20Nouveau">Mira Nouveau</a> (Keeper of Secret) |
| **Topaju**    | Yellow | Structure and stability | <a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Alejandro">Lei Alejandro</a>   |

## What They Do
- **Emelanica** — modulates time. Slows the burning of the Valla's soul. Governs absolute mana flow.
- **Safayer** — purifies souls. Strips memory and ego so the Overseer tastes nothing. Without it, souls become Echoes instead of clean energy.
- **Rubion** — kills instantly. Severs soul from body. Used to prevent regeneration.
- **Topaju** — holds the world together. Without it, continents drift into the Gap. Anchors sacrificial rituals to the mortal world.

## The Ritual
- The Gemhearts are used in the **Ritual of Replacement**.
- They create a **Stasis Field** — a space where the old Valla is released and the new one is taken.
- Without all four, the ritual is incomplete. That is why the Echoes exist.
- The ritual was designed by the Architect as a **voluntary transfer**. **Lei Firsa corrupted it in 01 BV.** She forced Simon into the seal without Safayer, without consent, and without releasing the Architect.

## What We Know
- The Gemhearts are scattered.
- **Will of Grin** wants them to break the Grin curse.
- **Keeper of Secret** wants them to reveal the truth.
- **Valerius** wants Topaju — the bloodline claim, currently held by Lei Alejandro, is contested by **Darvin Thorne**.
- We have **Safayer**. **Emelanica** and **Rubion** were taken by **Mira** after Session 2. **Topaju** is with **Lei Alejandro**.

## What We Do NOT Know
- Where **Lei Alejandro** is. He is alive. He holds Topaju. He is hiding.
- What **Topaju** can do if fully awakened.
- What happens if all four are brought together.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- The Gemhearts are alive. They pulse. They remember.
- Touching a Gemheart gives you a vision of the Architect.
- The Gemhearts can be destroyed — but doing so would weaken the seal.
- If all four are united, they show the truth of the cosmos. No DM roll needed. Just narration.

### Current Holders (Locked Canon)
- **Emelanica** — taken by the party from Greyland Castle in Session 1. Handed to **Mira / Keeper of Secret** after Session 2. **Mira currently holds it.**
- **Rubion** — taken by the party from **Jarko** in Session 1. Handed to **Mira / Keeper of Secret** after Session 2. **Mira currently holds it.**
- **Safayer** — swallowed by **Vythrax** for 300 years. Returned to the party in Session 5. **The party currently holds it.**
- **Topaju** — never found by the Cycle Clan. Held by **Lei Alejandro**. **Lei Alejandro currently holds it.**
- **Darvin Thorne does NOT carry Topaju.** Darvin carries the **bloodline claim** to Lei Roiga and Topaju. He is the True Heir by blood, but the stone itself is in Alejandro's hands. Valerius hunts Darvin because killing the True Heir may transfer the claim — or because Valerius wants the claim legitimized by Darvin's death.

### Corrections Applied
- **Removed:** "Valerius took a fragment of Topaju from Observatorium Ancient Lake in 699 AV." This never happened. Ancient Lake is retconned to be the ancient site that became **Universitas Tarakita** (see `Timeline of Oversee.md` and `Ancient Lake` location). Valerius did not obtain Topaju.
- **Removed:** "Darvin carries Topaju inside him." Darvin carries the **claim**, not the stone.
- **Removed:** "Accarus carried Rubion." Accarus never carried Rubion. He carried **Gustaero**, which he gave to **Husgus** after the Frosthaven mission.

### What the Gemhearts Are (DM Layer)
- The Gemhearts are **crystallized organs of the Architect** — its heart, its brain, its vital essence, separated when it split itself to create Oversee.
- They are not "tools." They are **pieces of a living being**.
- **Emelanica** is the Architect's temporal processor.
- **Safayer** is its purifier.
- **Rubion** is its ability to sever — the last line of defense.
- **Topaju** is its spine — the foundation that keeps reality whole.
- If all four are united in the right hands, they can perform the **Architect's Mercy** — the override clause in the original `Edisi Law` that allows the seal to be transferred **without destroying the old Valla**.

### Reveal Schedule
- **Session 6** — Party learns the Gemhearts are organs of a cosmic entity (via the Shell video).
- **Session 8–10** — Party reaches the **Bahtera Bawah Tanah** and reads the original `Edisi Law`. They learn the override clause exists.
- **Session 11** — **Lei Alejandro** appears. He reveals he holds Topaju. He offers it to the party — if they can prove they are worthy.
- **Endgame** — The party must unite all four Gemhearts to pursue the **third path**.
