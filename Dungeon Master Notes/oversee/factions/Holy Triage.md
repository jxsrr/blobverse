## What We Know
- The governing body of the Commonwealth.
- Three leaders, each representing a pillar of civilization.
- They balance each other. They check each other. They rule together.
- The current Triase is **Valerius Thorne** (Chancellor), **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Livia%20Vane">Livia</a>** (Justice), and **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Iron%20Gort">Gort</a>** (Defense).
- They control most of Harka, Pirizia, and Rioquan.
- They are hunting us.

### The Triase — A Firsa Creation
- The **Triase was not the original structure** of the One Voice Commonwealth.
- The original Commonwealth was founded by **Guanik Wiseman** — the first Kanselir Agung. It was a **search** for the next shepherd, not a government.
- The Triase was created by **Lei Firsa** during her consolidation of power, after the Initial Six disappeared (200 AV).
- It was designed to **consolidate Klan Lei power**. It was designed to **hide the truth**. It was designed to **replace the original promise with a lie**.
- The current Triase is **Firsa's Triase**. It is not the original. It is the corruption.

### The Original Commonwealth — No Triase
- The original One Voice Commonwealth had a **single leader** — the Kanselir Agung.
- **Guanik Wiseman** was the first Kanselir Agung. He was not a politician. He was a shepherd — a man who had promised the Architect that he would find a way to save the world.
- The original **Garda Mawar** was a group of **scholars and witnesses** — not law enforcement.
- The original **Garda Besi** was a group of **volunteers** — not a military.
- There was no **Wakil Keadilan**. There was no **Wakil Pertahanan**. There was only the Kanselir Agung — and the search.
- The Triase was created later. It was not part of Guanik's design. It was part of Firsa's.

### Geldfre — The Kanselir Agung at the Time of the Initial Six
- **Geldfre** was the Kanselir Agung at the time of the Initial Six (200 AV). He was **not** the first. He inherited the role from Guanik's legacy.
- He was the **last Kanselir Agung who remembered the original promise**. He welcomed the Six. He trusted **Lei Alejandro**. He watched them disappear.
- After the Great Disappearance, Geldfre was the last one holding the line. He tried to keep the original Commonwealth alive. He failed.
- **Lei Firsa** had been pulling strings for centuries. She had been placing her descendants in key positions. She had been rewriting history. She had been waiting for the right moment.
- When Geldfre died — or was killed — the Commonwealth became the Triase's Commonwealth. And the Triase became Lei Firsa's.

### The Three Pillars — Current State
- **Valerius Thorne** — Kanselir Agung. He is not the final antagonist. He is a pawn. He is ambitious, dangerous, and smart — but he is not the one pulling the strings.
- **Livia Vane** — Wakil Keadilan. She was adopted by **Lei Alejandro**. She believes she was a twin who should have died. She is wrong. She is not a twin. She is a pawn.
- **Iron Gort** — Wakil Pertahanan. He is not loyal to Valerius. He is loyal to strength. He can be turned.
- **The Triase is not the original Commonwealth.** It is Firsa's Commonwealth. It is the corrupted version. It is the lie.

### The Previous Triase — At the Time of the Initial Six (200 AV)
- **Geldfre** — Kanselir Agung. The last one who remembered the original promise.
- **Erard Gelnne** — Wakil Keadilan. He built the current Rose Guard — but not the original. The original Rose Guard was already corrupted by Firsa's influence by 200 AV. Erard was a pawn.
- **Hildag Grin** — Wakil Pertahanan. She was the Weaver of the Will of Grin. She defected after the Initial Six disappeared. She is still alive today. She is the mother of **Accarus** and **Antrem**.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- The Triase was created by **Lei Firsa**. It was not the original structure.
- The original Commonwealth had a **single leader** — the Kanselir Agung.
- **Guanik Wiseman** was the first Kanselir Agung. He is **alive**. He is the Architect's vessel.
- **Geldfre** was the Kanselir Agung at the time of the Initial Six. He was the last one who remembered the original promise.
- **Hildag Grin** was the Wakil Pertahanan at the time of the Initial Six. She defected.
- **Erard Gelnne** was a pawn. He built the current Rose Guard — but not the original.
- **The Triase is not the original Commonwealth.** It is Firsa's Commonwealth. It is the corruption.

### Season 3 Reveal Schedule
- **Session 8–10** — The party reaches the Bahtera Bawah Tanah. They learn about Guanik and the original Commonwealth. They learn about the Triase's origin. They learn about the corruption.
- **Session 11** — The party confronts Valerius, Livia, and Gort. They learn about the Triase's history. They must decide what to do with it.

### The Triase in the Endgame
- If the party restores the original Commonwealth, they will need to find a new Kanselir Agung — a new shepherd, a new leader who remembers the promise. **Guanik** is the only one who can appoint a successor. He is alive. He is waiting.
- If the party destroys the Triase, they will create a power vacuum. The world will descend into chaos. They will need to find a way to fill it.
- If the party replaces the Triase, they will need to create a new structure — one that is not a lie, not a corruption, not a dynasty. The choice is theirs.
- **The choice is theirs.** The DM should present all three options clearly and let the party decide.
