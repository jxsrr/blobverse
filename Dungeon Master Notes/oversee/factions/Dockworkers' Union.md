## What We Know
- The dockworker union of Javan Town.
- Led by **Mr. Basir**, a burly man with a grudge against the Commonwealth.
- They control the docks. They know every ship, every cargo, every smuggling route.
- They have seen too many workers die in "accidents."
- They are **not** a fighting force. They are **workers**. But they will fight if pushed.

## What They Offer
- **Information** about smuggling routes and Commonwealth operations.
- **A safe passage** through the docks.
- **Muscle** (if needed — dockworkers with crowbars).

## What They Want
- Expose the Commonwealth's crimes. Protect their workers.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Mr. Basir is a **former adventurer**. He retired after his party was betrayed by the Commonwealth. He lost his brother.
- He has a **hidden ledger** of every Commonwealth crime in Javan Town. It is enough evidence to damage the Triase.
- He knows **Sasimo** is a Will of Grin agent. He does not care.
- If the party helps him expose the Commonwealth, he will give them the ledger. This is a **key item** for a Season 3 political arc.
