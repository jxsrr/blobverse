## What We Know
- A community of engineers, mechanics, and scientists.
- They rejected the Commonwealth's magic monopoly.
- They built **Arujaya** as a refuge.
- They created technology that imitates magic: geothermal power, cybernetics, mesh networks.
- They are **suspicious of outsiders**, especially magic-users.

## Their Philosophy
- Magic is not absolute. Technology is the future.
- The Commonwealth suppresses progress to maintain power.
- The Gap is a threat that can be studied scientifically.

## What They Offer
- **Cybernetics** (like Ama's arm).
- **Technology** (like the Go Ingsani submarine).
- **Data** (like the Shell video).
- **Shelter** (from the Commonwealth).

## Current State
- Active, but under threat. The Depths are contaminated. The reactor is failing.
- Echo Gzirmo's consciousness powers the city. When he fades, the city will too.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- The Tinkers are not a single faction. They are **fractured**.
- Dr. Vektor leads one faction: the **Immortalists**. They want to use technology to live forever.
- Echo Gzirmo represents another: the **Preservationists**. They want to preserve knowledge.
- Adam represents a third: the **Young Ones**. They want to leave Arujaya and explore the surface.
- If the party returns to Arujaya, they will find it in crisis. The factions are on the brink of civil war.
- The party can choose a faction to support. This is a **Season 3 side quest**.
