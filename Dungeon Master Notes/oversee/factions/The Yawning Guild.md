## What We Know
- The adventurer's guild. Registers new adventurers and assigns missions.
- **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Grilnax">Grilnax</a>** is the clerk. He gave the party their first mission.
- The Guild has branches in most major cities.
- It issues the **SIP** (Adventurer's Permit).
- It provides **insurance** (10% of loot to beneficiaries).
- It provides **cartography** (25 gp, accuracy questionable).

## What They Offer
- **Jobs** (ranging from escort duty to dungeon crawls).
- **Registration** (50 gp per group).
- **Contacts** (merchants, artisans, other adventurers).
- **A safe place to rest** (in some locations).

## Current State
- Active. Profitable. Politically neutral.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- The Yawning Guild is not politically neutral. It is **secretly funded by the Keeper of Secret**.
- <a href="/oversee/npcs/Grilnax">Grilnax</a> is a Keeper agent. He was placed in Yawning Town to watch for adventurers who might be useful.
- The Guild's regional master is **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Mira%20Nouveau">Mira</a>**. She uses the Guild to gather information and recruit agents.
- If the party is ever lost or needs a mission, the Guild will have one for them.
- The Guild has a **hidden vault** beneath Yawning Town. It contains artifacts recovered by adventurers over the years. Some are dangerous.
