## What We Know
- An ancient family of cooks. They guard the **Recipe Library** — 7 generations of recipes.
- They took in **Cindy** after the Ghuffghuff tragedy.
- They are not warriors. They are **cooks**. But they know how to defend themselves with a wok.
- They are **respected** in Gudameng. They are **neutral** in politics.

## What They Offer
- **Food.** Healing food. Magical food. Free for the party.
- **Shelter.** A place to rest in Gudameng.
- **Recipes.** Some of their recipes have magical effects.

## What They Want
- Protect the temple. Protect Cindy. Preserve their family's legacy.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- The Guo family is not just a family of cooks. They are **descendants of the first Cycle Clan cooks** — the ones who prepared the ritual meals for the Valla.
- Their Recipe Library contains a **hidden section** with recipes for **ritual food** — food that can heal souls, weaken the Overseer, and bind the Gap.
- Guo Lao knows about the Cycle Clan. He has been waiting for someone to ask.
- If the party helps Cindy, Guo Lao will give them a **Ritual Recipe** — a dish that can be cooked for the final battle. It will give the party a temporary buff (advantage on all saves for 1 hour).
