## What We Know
- Dragonborn clan of ice. Live at the top of Frosthaven Peak.
- Worship **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a>**, the silver dragon. Their "Breathstone" was actually Syl's egg.
- All members have **Ice Breath** — a sacred inheritance from Vythrax.
- Magic is taboo. Only ice breath is allowed. Self-taught magic is punishable by exile.
- They are **strict, disciplined, and honor-bound**.
- **Syl Frost is the son of Vythrax** and the heir to the clan. His name is carved on the family tree.

## The Rules
1. **Ice Breath** may only be used to freeze enemies and protect the clan.
2. **Absolute obedience** to the elders. Exile for refusal.
3. **Do not touch the Breathstone with magic.**
4. Exiles must bring back an enemy's head or a dragon relic to be allowed back.
5. **The Frostfang** may only be touched by elders. Violators must fight to the death or freeze.

## The Truth (as the Clan Now Knows It)
- Syl is the son of **Vythrax**.
- The "Breathstone" was Syl's egg.
- The clan lied for centuries to hide the truth.
- **Syl has been accepted back.** His name is carved on the family tree.
- Elder Jarl has named Syl as his successor.

## What They Want From Us
- Nothing directly. They are loyal to Syl.
- They will help the party if Syl asks.
- They can provide shelter, healing, and ice magic.

### The Frostscale Clan — Who They Are
- The Frostscale are a **dragonborn clan of ice** — the oldest and most isolated clan in Pirizia. They have lived at the top of **Frosthaven Peak** for thousands of years, since before the Rapture.
- They were **not always isolated**. In the First Age, they were one of the great dragonborn clans — protectors of the mountains, guardians of the ice. They served the ancient dragons. They were proud.
- They became isolated after the **Rapture** — after they watched the world destroy itself. They retreated to the peak. They closed their gates. They turned inward.
- They are **strict, disciplined, and honor-bound**. They have survived because they trust no one. They have survived because they follow the rules. They have survived because they have never broken them.
- **Until now.** The truth about Syl — about Vythrax, about the assault, about the lie — has broken the clan. They are divided. They are confused. They are trying to find a new way forward.

### The Breathstone Lie — The Cover-Up
- The Frostscale Clan worshipped **Vythrax** as a deity for thousands of years. She was the Silver Wind — the protector of the mountain, the mother of the ice. They built the temple in her honor. They carved her name into the monolith. They sang to her every full moon.
- When **Lei Watigi** assaulted Vythrax — with the help of some of the clan's own priests — the clan faced a crisis. Their deity had been violated. Their deity was pregnant. Their deity would bear a mixed child — a dragon and a Lei. A child that would destroy the sanctity of the clan.
- The priests — the ones who had not been involved in the conspiracy but now had to clean up the mess — made a choice. They would lie. They would tell the clan the egg was not a child — it was the **Breathstone**, a sacred heirloom, a gift from Vythrax. They would build an altar around it. They would command the clan to worship it. They would hide the truth forever.
- The lie worked. For centuries, the Frostscale Clan worshipped the egg. They believed it was holy. They believed it was a source of power. They believed it was the greatest gift their deity had ever given them.
- **They did not know it was a child.** They did not know it was a product of rape. They did not know their deity had been violated.
- **Until Session 5.** When Syl hatched, when the truth came out, the clan was shattered. They learned their deity had been violated. They learned their sacred relic was a child. They learned their priests had lied to them for centuries.
- **They are still processing it.** Some accept the truth. Some deny it. Some blame Syl. Some blame the priests. Some blame themselves. The clan is divided.

### The Priests — The Guilty and the Innocent
- Not all the priests were involved in the assault. Some were conspirators. Some were innocent — they inherited the lie without knowing it was a lie. Some were complicit in the cover-up. Some were victims of it.
- **The conspirators** — the ones who helped Lei Watigi, the ones who weakened Vythrax, the ones who made the assault possible — are dead. They died centuries ago. They never faced justice. They never answered for what they did.
- **The cover-up priests** — the ones who created the Breathstone lie, the ones who hid the truth, the ones who raised Syl in secret and then ostracized him — some are still alive. They are old. They are guilty. They are afraid.
- **The innocent priests** — the ones who inherited the lie, who worshipped the egg without knowing what it was, who believed the Breathstone was holy — they are the majority. They are not guilty. They are victims too. They are trying to understand what happened.
- **Elder Jarl** — the leader of the Council of Elders — is one of the innocent. He inherited the lie. He worshipped the egg. He believed the Breathstone was holy. He did not know the truth until Session 5. He has been carrying the guilt ever since. He has been trying to make it right.

### Elder Jarl — The Leader Who Knows
- **Elder Jarl** is the oldest dragonborn in Frostheart. His scales are nearly white and translucent. His horns spiral like a ram's. His voice is a whisper. Every word he speaks is treated as Vythrax's command.
- He is **not guilty**. He did not know about the assault. He did not know about the cover-up. He was as deceived as everyone else.
- But he **feels guilty**. He was the leader. He was responsible. He should have known. He should have questioned the Breathstone. He should have asked why the egg never hatched. He should have done something.
- **He has dedicated his remaining years to making it right.** He has named Syl as his successor. He has carved Syl's name on the family tree. He has begun the slow process of telling the clan the truth — not all at once, but piece by piece. He is trying to heal the clan.
- **He will die soon.** He is old. He is tired. He has seen too much. He will not survive the campaign. He will die knowing he did his best — and that his best was not enough.

### The Frozen Legion — The Protectors Who Wait
- The **Frozen Legion** is a line of hundreds of ice statues lining the path to the Spire of Whispering Ice. They look like dragonborn warriors — armor, weapons, horns.
- They are **alive**. They are not dead. They are frozen in time, not in death. They move when no one is looking. They only point in one direction: toward Frosthaven.
- They are the **original protectors** of Klan Frostscale. They fought an ancient threat — a Gap invasion — and were frozen by **Vythrax** to save them. They have been waiting for 1,000 years.
- **They will thaw when Vythrax dies** or when **the Gap opens**. They will be confused. They will be angry. They will not recognize the modern clan.
- **If the party can communicate with them** — using Dragonborn language, or Syl's connection to Vythrax, or the truth about the assault — they can recruit them as allies. They are 1,000 strong. They are all CR 3. They are enough to turn the tide of a battle.
- **The Legion is a Season 3 optional quest.** The party can choose to thaw them or leave them frozen. If they thaw them, they will have powerful allies — but they will also have to deal with the chaos of a thousand ancient warriors waking up in a world they do not understand.

### The Clan's Division — The Fracture
- The Frostscale Clan is **divided** into three factions:
  - **The Faithful** — the ones who still believe Vythrax is a deity, who still worship her, who still believe the Breathstone was holy. They are the majority. They are confused. They are trying to reconcile their faith with the truth.
  - **The Betrayed** — the ones who feel betrayed by Vythrax, by the priests, by the clan. They are angry. They are hurt. They want answers. They want justice. They are the minority. They are growing.
  - **The Heirs** — the ones who accept Syl as the son of Vythrax, who accept the truth, who want to move forward. They are the smallest faction. They are led by **Elder Jarl**. They are the future of the clan.
- **The clan is on the brink of civil war.** The Faithful and the Betrayed are at odds. The Heirs are trying to mediate. Syl is trying to lead. It is not going well.
- **The party can influence the outcome.** They can support the Faithful, the Betrayed, or the Heirs. They can help Syl lead. They can help Elder Jarl heal the clan. The choice is theirs.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- The Frostscale Clan is **divided**. The Faithful, the Betrayed, and the Heirs are at odds. The clan is on the brink of civil war.
- **Elder Jarl is dying.** He will not survive the campaign. He has named Syl as his successor. He is trying to heal the clan before he dies.
- The **Frozen Legion** is alive. They will thaw when Vythrax dies or when the Gap opens. They are 1,000 strong. They are potential allies.
- The **priests who covered up the assault** are still alive. They are old. They are guilty. They are afraid. They may try to silence Syl — or they may try to redeem themselves.
- **Oldrunn** — Syl's adoptive mother — is still in Frostheart. She is proud of her son. She is trying to help the clan heal. She is not a priest. She is not a warrior. She is just a mother.
- **Vythrax is dying.** The Safayer poison has been in her veins for 300 years. She will not survive the campaign. The clan does not know this. They will learn in Season 3. They will break. They will weep. They will have to find a new way forward — without their deity.
