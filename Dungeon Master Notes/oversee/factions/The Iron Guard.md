## What We Know
- The military arm of the Commonwealth. Officially called the **Garda Besi**.
- Led by **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Iron%20Gort">Gort</a>**. Composed of disciplined soldiers, war machines, and conscripts.
- They are the ones who killed **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Jarko">Jarko</a>**. They are the ones who attacked the Maw Desert hideout.
- They are the ones who are hunting the party.

## Their Tactics
- **Brute force.** They hit hard. They hit fast. They do not stop.
- **Antimagic.** They wear antimagic headgear. They use antimagic weapons.
- **Overwhelming numbers.** They do not fight fair. They fight to win.

## What They Want
- Crush Will of Grin. Crush the Keeper of Secret. Crush anyone who opposes Valerius.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### The Original Iron Guard — Not a Military
- The **original** Garda Besi was **not** a military. It was a group of **volunteers** — the ones who protected the search for the shepherd, the ones who kept the promise safe.
- Their role was to **protect the Cycle Clan**, to protect the search, to protect the truth.
- They were the ones who guarded the **Gemhearts**. They were the ones who guarded the **rituals**. They were the ones who stood outside the door while Guanik Wiseman spoke to the Architect.
- They did not conquer. They did not invade. They **protected**.
- **They are gone.** Lei Firsa killed them, silenced them, or erased them. Their oaths were broken. Their names were struck from history.

### The Current Iron Guard — Firsa's Version
- The current Garda Besi is **not** the original. It is Lei Firsa's version — a corrupted institution built to enforce Klan Lei supremacy through force.
- It was created during Lei Firsa's consolidation of power, after the Initial Six disappeared (200 AV).
- It is loyal to the **Triase** — not to the original promise.
- It is **everywhere**. It has barracks in every major city. It has war machines on every road. It has conscripts in every village.
- It is led by **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Iron%20Gort">Gort</a>** — the current Wakil Pertahanan. He is not loyal to Valerius. He is loyal to **strength**. He can be turned.

### Iron Gort — The Current Leader
- Gort is not a politician. He is a warrior. He respects strength above all else.
- He threw a bottle at **Husgus** during the Festival. Husgus caught it and drank it. Gort respected him for it.
- He killed **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Jarko">Jarko</a>** in front of the party — not out of cruelty, but out of necessity. Jarko was a Grin. Jarko was a threat. Gort eliminated him.
- He is **not evil**. He is **brutal**. He is a soldier who has been told who the enemy is — and he believes it.
- He can be turned. If the party defeats him in **single combat**, he will respect them. He will listen. He will consider.

### The Three Factions Within the Iron Guard
1. **Gort's Warriors** — they follow Gort. They respect strength. They are the core of the current Iron Guard. They can be turned if Gort is turned.
2. **Valerius's Loyalists** — they are political officers. They watch Gort. They report to the Kanselir Agung. They will try to kill Gort if he defects.
3. **The Conscripts** — they are forced soldiers. They hate the Commonwealth. They hate the war. They hate Gort. They can be turned — but they will need a leader.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- The original Iron Guard was a group of **volunteers** — not a military.
- The current Iron Guard is **Firsa's version** — a corrupted institution.
- **Gort is not loyal to Valerius.** He is loyal to strength. He can be turned.
- **Gort's Warriors** will follow him if he defects.
- **Valerius's Loyalists** will try to kill Gort if he defects.
- **The Conscripts** hate the Commonwealth. They can be turned — but they need a leader.

### Season 3 Reveal Schedule
- **Session 8–10** — The party reaches the Bahtera Bawah Tanah. They learn about the original Iron Guard — the volunteers. They learn what it used to be.
- **Session 11** — The party confronts Gort. If they defeat him in single combat, he will listen. He will ask them: *"Why do you fight?"* If they answer truthfully, he will consider defecting.
- **Endgame** — The party must decide what to do with the Iron Guard. Restore it to its original purpose? Destroy it? Replace it? The choice is theirs.

### The Iron Guard in the Endgame
- If the party restores the original Iron Guard, they will need to find new volunteers — new people who want to protect the search, the truth, the promise.
- If the party destroys the Iron Guard, they will create a power vacuum. The world will descend into chaos. They will need to find a way to fill it.
- If the party replaces the Iron Guard, they will need to create a new institution — one that is not a lie, not a corruption, not a dynasty.
- **The choice is theirs.** The DM should present all three options clearly and let the party decide.

### Gort's Fate
- If the party turns Gort, he will become an **NPC ally**. He is brutal, direct, and loyal — but he is not evil. He will fight for them if they earn his respect.
- If the party kills Gort, the Iron Guard will fracture. Valerius's Loyalists will take over. The Conscripts will rebel. The world will descend into chaos.
- If the party leaves Gort alone, he will continue to hunt them. He will not stop. He is a soldier. He has his orders.
- **The choice is theirs.**
