## What We Know
- A demon-hunter organization. Shin's old order.
- Trained hunters to fight demons, spirits, and Gap-touched creatures.
- Shin joined as a young man. He made friends: **Edra** and **Crest**.
- The order was **destroyed in 690 AV** — when a massive Gap tear opened in Elis and demons poured through.
- Edra and Crest died. Shin survived — but he was possessed by something. He destroyed everything. He woke up 690 years later.
- The symbol of The Grace is a **blue-silver seal** — a stylized wing.

## What Shin Knows
- The Grace had a base in Elis.
- They trained in demon-hunting, tracking, and combat.
- Edra was warm and welcoming. Crest was disciplined and observant.
- Crest suspected Shin was different. He documented anomalies.
- Edra never suspected anything. She trusted Shin completely.
- He does not know if they blamed him. He does not know if they died hating him.

### What The Grace Was
- The Grace was an **independent order of demon hunters**. It was **not** a Cycle Clan front. It was **not** a religious order. It was a **brotherhood of soldiers** who fought the things that slipped through the Gap.
- It was founded centuries before Shin joined — by a group of survivors who had lost their families to a Gap tear. They swore to protect the innocent. They swore to fight the darkness. They swore to never let another village burn.
- They were funded by local lords, by grateful villages, by the families of the people they saved. They were not rich. They were not powerful. They were **necessary**.
- They operated from a **fortress in Elis** — a stone keep built into the side of a mountain, hidden from the world, protected by old wards. The fortress is still there. It is ruined. It is abandoned. But it is still there.

### The Members
- **Shin Van Hel** — the young recruit. The one with the smile. The one with the gloves. The one who never talked about his past. He joined because Helius sent him. He stayed because Edra and Crest became his family.
- **Edra** — the warm one. She befriended Shin on his first day. She sat beside him without being asked. She never treated him as strange. She never asked about the gloves. She never asked about the smile. She was the heart of The Grace. She was the one who kept everyone together.
- **Crest** — the disciplined one. He observed. He documented. He suspected something was wrong with Shin — but he never blamed him. He tried to save him. He was the brain of The Grace. He was the one who kept everyone alive.
- **Helius** — the old man who raised Shin. He was not a member, but he was an ally. He sent Shin to The Grace. He believed Shin needed a purpose. He was the father of The Grace — not in blood, but in spirit.

### The Fall — 690 AV
- **690 AV.** A massive Gap tear opened in Elis. Demons poured through. The Grace fought. They held the line. They died.
- **Shin was possessed by Zargoyle.** He did not choose it. He did not want it. It happened in the chaos of the tear.
- **Zargoyle opened a small seal of Zargoth inside Shin** — accidentally, violently. The seal was a lock on the fragment of Zargoth that had been sleeping inside him since he was created. Zargoyle broke it. The resulting explosion destroyed The Grace. It destroyed Elis.
- **Shin was not responsible.** He was a victim. He was a weapon in the hands of a monster.
- **Zargoyle was driven out** — not by Shin, not by The Grace, but by **Zargoth**. The explosion of the seal woke Zargoth. The father fought the predator. Zargoth was stronger. He contained Zargoyle. He sealed him inside the curse mark. He has been keeping him contained ever since.
- When Shin woke, **690 years had passed**. He was the only survivor. The Grace was gone. Elis was a wasteland. He was alone.

### Lei Firsa's Involvement — Ambiguous
- **The DM must decide.** The original DM secrets said Lei Firsa opened the tear. This is **no longer canon**. The user has confirmed that Lei Firsa's status is ambiguous — she may be dead, she may be alive, she may be something else.
- **If Lei Firsa was involved**, she opened the tear to test HVS-02. She wanted to see what would happen if Shin was exposed to a Gap tear. She watched. She took notes. She is a monster.
- **If Lei Firsa was not involved**, the tear was a natural event — a thinning of the Gap, a random breach, a consequence of the world's failing seal. The Grace was in the wrong place at the wrong time. Shin was a victim of circumstance.
- **The truth is ambiguous.** The party may never know for sure. The DM should decide based on the needs of the campaign.

### Edra and Crest — The Friends Who Did Not Blame Him
- **Edra** wrote Shin a letter before she died. It was never delivered. It is in **Zundabun**, held by **Moro** the shadowless bartender. It says: *"Shin, I know you're hiding something. Don't run. We are your family, whatever you carry."*
- **Crest** kept notes on Shin. They were recovered by the Commonwealth after the fall. They are in the Lyceum archives. They were not accusations. They were observations of a friend. One entry reads: *"Subject shows signs of possession. Recommend non-lethal containment if triggered. Subject is not a threat. Subject is Shin."*
- **They did not blame Shin.** They tried to save him. They died trying. He has never known this. He has spent 690 years believing he was the monster. He is not.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- The Grace was **not** a Cycle Clan front. It was an independent order of demon hunters.
- The Grace was destroyed in **690 AV** — not in "the disaster that created the Echoes" (~200 AV).
- **Shin was not responsible** for the destruction of The Grace. He was a victim. He was used.
- **Zargoyle** possessed Shin. **Zargoth** contained Zargoyle. The two entities are distinct.
- **Edra and Crest did not blame Shin.** They tried to save him.
- **Edra wrote him a letter.** It is in Zundabun. He has never read it.
- **Crest's notes** are in the Lyceum archives. They are not accusations. They are observations of a friend.
- **Lei Firsa's involvement is ambiguous.** She may have opened the tear. She may not have. The DM decides.
- The **ruins of The Grace** are still in Elis. They contain **Edra's diary** — a full account of the fall. The party can find it in Season 3.
