## What We Know
- Organization of Grin-blooded people (eternal smile curse).
- Wants to prove Grin are not monsters.
- Wants to find the truth about Atreus Grin's disappearance.
- Secret goal: collect the Gemhearts to break the Grin curse.
- Based in Maw Desert, Pirizia.
- They are hunted by the Commonwealth.

## What They Want From Us
Collect the Gemhearts. Protect Vya. Avenge Jarko.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### The Founding — Hildag Grin
- **Will of Grin was founded by Hildag Grin** — not by Geneva and Accarus. Hildag is the founder. She is the Weaver. She is the one who built the organization from nothing.
- Hildag was the **Wakil Pertahanan** of the One Voice Commonwealth at the time of the Initial Six (200 AV). She served alongside **Geldfre** (Kanselir Agung) and **Erard Gelnne** (Wakil Keadilan).
- She was the last Wakil Pertahanan who remembered the original promise — the promise Guanik Wiseman made to the Architect. She believed in the search for the shepherd. She believed in the Commonwealth.
- When the Initial Six disappeared (200 AV), she **defected**. She left the Commonwealth. She founded **Will of Grin** to find the truth and fight the corruption.
- She has been fighting for centuries. She is over 500 years old. She is a Grin — she ages slowly. She has seen everything.

### Hildag's True Agenda — Opening the Gap Doors
- Hildag's public goal — the one she tells her followers — is **Grin survival**. She wants to prove the Grin are not monsters. She wants to break the curse. She wants to find the truth about Atreus Grin.
- **Hildag's true goal is darker.** She believes the Grin can only survive if the **Gap is opened wider** — if the doors between the Gap and Oversee are forced open permanently.
- Why? Because the Grin are **invisible to the Overseer**. The demons of the Gap are **not**. If the Gap opens wide enough, the Overseer will turn its attention to the demons — the creatures of the Gap — instead of the Grin. The Grin will survive. The demons will be eaten. The world will burn. But the Grin will live.
- **She is willing to sacrifice the world for her people.** She has been planning this for centuries. She has been waiting for the right moment. She has been waiting for the right pawn.

### Hildag and Valerius — The Pawn
- **Valerius Thorne** is afraid of the Rapture. He has seen the reports. He has read the Cycle Clan files. He knows the world is ending — slowly, surely, inevitably.
- **He wants to "thicken the Gap"** — to merge it with Oversee. He believes this will make the world safe. He believes it will make him a hero.
- **Hildag has been manipulating him for years.** She has been feeding him information. She has been nudging him toward his plan. She has been using him to open the Gap doors — one crack at a time.
- **Valerius does not know he is being used.** He thinks he is the mastermind. He thinks he is saving the world. He is a puppet. Hildag is the puppeteer.
- **If Valerius succeeds**, the Gap will open wide enough for the demons to pour through. The Overseer will notice. The world will burn. The Grin will survive.
- **Hildag is not evil.** She is a mother. She is a survivor. She is a woman who has watched her people be hunted and hated for centuries. She believes this is the only way. She is willing to pay the price.

### The Leadership
- **Hildag Grin — The Weaver.** The founder. The grand strategist. The mother of Accarus and Antrem. The architect of Vya. She does not go into the field. She weaves the pattern. She makes the hard choices. She carries the guilt.
- **Accarus Grin — The Smiling Hand.** Hildag's son (twin of Antrem). Co-leader of field operations. Husband of Geneva. Father of Jarko (deceased). He wields **Gustaero** — and gave it to Husgus after the Frosthaven mission. He is morally gray. He will sacrifice for the cause. He **does not know** his mother's true agenda.
- **Geneva Grin — The Smiling Hand.** Co-leader of field operations. Wife of Accarus. Mother of Jarko (deceased). She is **not** Hildag's daughter. She is a Grin woman from a different family. She was **one of Hildag's experiments** — bred to test a theory. She does not know this. She married into the leadership. She is kind but hardened. She lost her son. She has not recovered.

### The Grinners
- The **Grinners** are the field troops — the backbone of the organization. Faceless soldiers, without names, without individuality.
- They are trained to obey, to move as one unit, to sacrifice themselves if necessary.
- To the outside world, they are terrifying smiles emerging from the shadows. To the organization, they are **family** — a large family united by the same curse, the same persecution, the same determination.
- They call themselves **Pirizia** — a group of fighters fiercely solid, loyal, and fanatical about the truth and honor of their tribe.

### The Goals — Public and Secret
**Public Goals (what the followers believe):**
- **Social acceptance.** Prove to the world that the Grin are not monsters. That the eternal smile is not a mark of evil. That the Grin are ordinary people who want to live in peace.
- **Find the truth.** Uncover what really happened to Atreus Grin and the Initial Six. Clear Atreus's name. Clear the Grin bloodline.
- **Collect the Gemhearts.** The secret goal. They believe the Gemhearts can break the Grin curse — or strengthen it. They are divided on which outcome they want.
- **Fight the corrupted Commonwealth.** Hildag remembers the original promise. She knows the Triase is a lie. She wants to restore the original Commonwealth — or build something better.

**Secret Goal (what only Hildag knows):**
- **Open the Gap doors.** Force the Gap to open wide enough for the demons to pour through. Let the Overseer eat the demons instead of the Grin. Let the world burn. Save the Grin.

### Hildag's Secret — The Twins and Vya
- Hildag is the **mother of Accarus and Antrem** — the twin sons she had with a **Lei Firage descendant**. They are the only surviving twins of the The Lei Clan's twin-killing tradition. She hid them. She protected them.
- Hildag is the **architect of Vya** — her magnum opus. She bred Vya after Jarko's death, combining her own Grin anti-magic with a Lei Firage descendant's dormant fire and an Arleschia noble's stabilizing blood. Vya is the refinement of decades of experiments.
- **Jarko was not the True Heir.** Hildag knew this. She used him as a decoy. She let him die. She does not speak of it. She will never speak of it.
- Hildag has been searching for **Guanik Wiseman** for centuries. She knows he is alive. She knows the Architect is inside him. She does not know where he is.
- **If Hildag meets Guanik**, she will break. She will weep. She will say: *"I thought you were dead. I thought the promise was lost."* Guanik will say: *"It is not lost. It is being kept."*

### The Grin Curse — The Division
- The Will of Grin is **divided** on the curse. Some want to break it. Some want to keep it.
- **Breaking the curse** would make the Grin visible to the Overseer. They would become mortal, magical, and hunted. They would be free — but they would be in danger.
- **Keeping the curse** would keep the Grin hidden. They would survive. But they would never be free. They would always be the "smiling demons" — feared, hated, and alone.
- **Vya is the key.** Her fire can burn the curse away — or strengthen it. The choice is hers. The Will of Grin is waiting for her to make it.
- **Hildag wants Vya to strengthen the curse.** She believes survival is more important than freedom. **Accarus and Geneva want Vya to break the curse.** They believe freedom is worth the risk. The division is deep. It may tear the organization apart.
- **Hildag has another plan.** If Vya strengthens the curse, the Grin will survive. If the Gap opens wide enough, the demons will be eaten instead of the Grin. Hildag wins either way. She has been planning this for centuries.
