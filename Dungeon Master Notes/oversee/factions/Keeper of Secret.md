## What We Know
- Secret organization dedicated to finding the truth about the Initial Six.
- Founded by **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Mira%20Nouveau">Mira</a>** — daughter of **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Atlan%20Nouveau">Atlan Nouveau</a>** and **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Reins">Reins</a>**.

- Knows about the Gemhearts. Knows about the Echoes.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### The Keeper of Secret — Who They Are
- **Founded by Mira Nouveau** — not by a Cycle Clan splinter, not by a Cipher, not by anyone else. Mira founded it herself.
- Mira was **fourteen years old** when she witnessed the Great Disappearance. She saw her father, **Atlan Nouveau**, have his soul torn from his body. She saw **Lei Alejandro** watch it happen.
- She founded the Keeper of Secret the year after. She has spent her entire life building it. She is now in her forties.
- The Keeper of Secret is **not** a remnant of the Cycle Clan. It is an **independent organization** — born from Mira's grief, not from Guanik's promise.
- Some former Cycle Clan members **joined** the Keeper of Secret after the Cycle Clan was shattered by Lei Firsa. But they did not found it. They joined it. They are members, not leaders. They are welcome, but they are not in charge.

### The Purpose
- **Preserve the truth** about the Initial Six. Mira saw what happened. She knows the official story is a lie. She wants the world to know.
- **Find the Echoes** — the fragments of the Initial Six. Free them. Give them peace.
- **Expose Lei Alejandro** — the man who betrayed her father.
- **Protect the world** from the corrupted Commonwealth. The Keeper of Secret is not a rebel army. It is a network of scholars, spies, and witnesses. It does not fight. It remembers.
- **Find a way to free Simon** — though Mira does not know his name yet. She knows the Valla is a prisoner. She wants to free him. She does not know how.

### The Structure
- **Mira Nouveau** — the founder and leader. She is the heart of the organization.
- **The Observatorium** — the headquarters. The public face is a center of knowledge. The secret face is the Keeper's archive.
- **The Network** — cells in most major cities. **Elara** runs the cell in Kota Juto. **Pak Andreas's wife** (Ibu Ratna) is a courier. **Junior** is an informant. **Rivan** is a contact — not a Cipher, not a leader, just a man who remembers.
- **The Inner Circle** — a handful of people Mira trusts completely. They know the full truth about the Initial Six. They know about the Echoes. They know about the plan to expose Alejandro.
- **The Outer Circle** — the majority of the Keeper's members. They know the Commonwealth is corrupt. They know the Initial Six were betrayed. They do not know the full truth about the Echoes, the Valla, or the Gap. Mira protects them from the worst of it.

### Mira's Knowledge
- Mira knows **Lei Alejandro betrayed the Initial Six**. She saw it happen.
- Mira knows the **Echoes** exist. She has seen them. She has tried to free them. She has failed.
- Mira knows the **Commonwealth is corrupt**. She knows the Triase is a lie. She knows the public Edisi Law is a fabrication.
- Mira knows about the **Gemhearts** — at least, she knows they exist. She holds **Emelanica** and **Rubion** (taken from the party after Session 2). She knows they are important. She does not know they are organs of the Architect.
- Mira knows about the **Bahtera Bawah Tanah**. She has the **Twin Shell Amulet** — the key to the Ark. She has not told the party.
- Mira does **not** know:
  - That **Guanik Wiseman is alive**.
  - That the **Architect is inside Guanik**.
  - That **Lei Alejandro is alive** and hiding in the Bahtera Bawah Tanah.
  - That **Lei Firsa** corrupted the Commonwealth (she suspects it, but has no proof).
  - That **Simon Greenbottle** is the Valla (she knows the Valla is a prisoner, but not his name).
  - That the **Swan Trio** exists (Gustaero, Niette, Alnean).
  - That the **third path** is possible.

### Mira's Plan
- **Step 1:** Collect the Gemhearts. She has Emelanica and Rubion. She wants Safayer (the party has it) and Topaju (she does not know where it is).
- **Step 2:** Expose Lei Alejandro. She wants the world to know what he did. She wants him to face justice.
- **Step 3:** Find a way to free the Valla. She does not know how yet. She believes the Gemhearts are the key.
- **Step 4:** Dismantle the corrupted Commonwealth. She wants to replace it with something better — something that remembers the original promise.
- **Step 5:** Protect the world from the Overseer. She knows the Rapture is real. She knows the seal is failing. She wants to find a way to stop it.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Mira was **fourteen** when she witnessed the ritual. She has been carrying this for 25 years.
- Mira holds **Emelanica** and **Rubion**. She took them from the party after Session 2. She did not ask. She did not tell them. She believes she is protecting them.
- Mira holds the **Twin Shell Amulet** — the key to the Bahtera Bawah Tanah. She has not told the party.
- Mira knows about the **Bahtera Bawah Tanah**. She has known for years. She has not gone there. She is afraid of what she will find.
- Mira does **not** know that **Guanik Wiseman is alive**. She does not know that the Architect is inside him. She does not know that **Lei Alejandro is alive** and hiding in the Ark.
- Mira does **not** know about the **Swan Trio**. She does not know that the third path requires Gustaero, Niette, and Alnean. She thinks the Gemhearts are the only key.
- Mira is **not** a Cycle Clan member. She is not a Cipher. She is an independent agent who founded her own organization. She is not part of a larger structure. She is the structure.

### Mira and the Party
- Mira is **friendly** to the party. She recruited them in Session 2. She gave them a mission. She trusts them — to a point.
- Mira is **disappointed** that the party chose Will of Grin over Keeper of Secret (Session 4). She understands why. She does not hold it against them. But she is watching.
- Mira will **help** the party if they ask. She will give them information, resources, and shelter. She will not give them Emelanica or Rubion — not yet. She believes she is protecting them.
- Mira will **not** tell the party about the Twin Shell Amulet until she trusts them completely. She has been betrayed before. She is careful.
- Mira is **not** a villain. She is not a hero. She is a woman who saw her father murdered and has spent her life trying to make it right. She is tired. She is afraid. She is doing her best.

### Mira's Role in Season 3
- **Session 8–10** — The party reaches the Bahtera Bawah Tanah. They find Lei Alejandro. They learn the truth about the ritual. They learn about Guanik. They learn about the third path.
- **Session 11** — The party confronts Mira. They ask her about Emelanica and Rubion. They ask her about the Twin Shell Amulet. They ask her why she kept secrets. She will answer honestly. She will say: *"Because I was afraid. Because I did not know if you could be trusted. Because I have been alone for 25 years."*
- **Endgame** — Mira must decide what to do with the Keeper of Secret. She can join the party. She can leave. She can dissolve the organization and start something new. The choice is hers.
