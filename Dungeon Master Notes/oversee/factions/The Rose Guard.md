## What We Know
- The law enforcement arm of the Commonwealth. Officially called the **Garda Mawar**.
- Led by **[[Lei Livia Vane|Livia]]**. Composed of disciplined soldiers, mages, and spies.
- They enforce the **Edisi Law**. They suppress dissent. They hunt "terrorists."
- They are the ones who arrested the party in Kota Juto. They are the ones who burned Chenalia.
- They are **everywhere**.

## Their Tactics
- **Precision.** They do not waste soldiers. They use magic, spies, and intelligence.
- **Fear.** They make examples of rebels. They leave bodies in the street.
- **Control.** They control the narrative. They rewrite history.

## What They Want
- Capture the party. Kill the party. Erase the party.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### The Original Rose Guard — Not Law Enforcement
- The **original** Garda Mawar was **not** a law enforcement arm. It was a group of **scholars, historians, and witnesses**.
- Their role was to **remember the truth** — to preserve the history of the Cycle Clan, the promise of Guanik Wiseman, and the reality of the Valla.
- They were the ones who wrote the original Edisi Law. They were the ones who guarded the Bahtera Bawah Tanah. They were the ones who knew Guanik's promise.
- They did not carry weapons. They carried **records**. They did not arrest people. They **remembered** them.
- **They are gone.** Lei Firsa killed them, silenced them, or erased them. Their records were burned. Their names were struck from history.

### The Current Rose Guard — Firsa's Version
- The current Garda Mawar is **not** the original. It is Lei Firsa's version — a corrupted institution built to enforce the public Edisi Law and protect Klan Lei supremacy.
- It was created during Lei Firsa's consolidation of power, after the Initial Six disappeared (200 AV).
- It is loyal to the **Triase** — not to the original promise.
- It is **everywhere**. It has cells in every major city. It has spies in every court. It has agents in every faction.
- It is led by **[[Lei Livia Vane|Livia]]** — the current Wakil Keadilan. She was adopted by **Lei Alejandro**, the old man who raised her. She believes she was a twin who should have died. She is wrong. She is not a twin. She is a pawn.

### Livia Vane — The Current Leader
- Livia was **not** a twin. She was alone from the start. She was adopted by an old man who was later revealed to be **Lei Alejandro**.
- She believes the "twin who should have died" story. She carries the guilt of a sister she never had.
- She is **terrified of [[Lei Valerius Thorne|Valerius]]**. She knows he is waiting for the right moment to kill her. She does not know why.
- She is a hostage of the party (as of Session 07). She can be turned into an ally — but it will cost her everything.
- **She is not evil.** She is broken. She was raised by a man who lied to her. She was placed in a position she did not earn. She is trying to survive.

### The Three Factions Within the Rose Guard
1. **Livia's Loyalists** — they will follow her anywhere. They believe in her. They are the core of the current Rose Guard.
2. **Valerius's Agents** — they are embedded spies, watching Livia. They report directly to the Kanselir Agung. They are the ones who will kill Livia if she defects.
3. **The Old Guard** — they served the previous Wakil Keadilan, **Erard Gelnne**. They are uncertain. They remember a time when the Rose Guard was something else. They can be turned.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- The original Rose Guard was a group of **scholars and witnesses** — not law enforcement.
- The current Rose Guard is **Firsa's version** — a corrupted institution.
- **Livia was adopted by Lei Alejandro.** She is not a twin. She is not who she thinks she is.
- **Livia's loyalists can be turned** if the party earns Livia's trust.
- **Valerius's agents** will try to kill Livia if she defects.
- **The Old Guard** remembers the original promise. They can be turned.

### Season 3 Reveal Schedule
- **Session 8–10** — The party reaches the Bahtera Bawah Tanah. They learn about the original Rose Guard — the scholars and witnesses. They learn what it used to be.
- **Session 11** — The party confronts Livia. She reveals the truth about her adoption. She reveals that she was raised by Lei Alejandro. She asks the party for help.
- **Endgame** — The party must decide what to do with the Rose Guard. Restore it to its original purpose? Destroy it? Replace it? The choice is theirs.

### The Rose Guard in the Endgame
- If the party restores the original Rose Guard, they will need to find new scholars, new witnesses, new people who remember the truth.
- If the party destroys the Rose Guard, they will create a power vacuum. The world will descend into chaos. They will need to find a way to fill it.
- If the party replaces the Rose Guard, they will need to create a new institution — one that is not a lie, not a corruption, not a dynasty.
- **The choice is theirs.** The DM should present all three options clearly and let the party decide.
