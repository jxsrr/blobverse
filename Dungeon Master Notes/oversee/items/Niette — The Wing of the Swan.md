## What It Does
- +2 spear. 1d6 + Str/Dex (finesse, reach). Magical. Thrown (range 30/120).
- **Wing's Reach:** When thrown, Niette returns to the wielder's hand at the start of their next turn. It does not require an action to catch.
- **Span of the Swan:** Once per combat, when an ally within 60 ft is hit by an attack, the wielder may throw Niette to intercept. The ally takes no damage, and the attacker takes 2d6 piercing damage.
- **Severance:** Niette is the only weapon that can kill a Valla without breaking the seal. If used on a Valla, the Valla dies, and the seal holds — for now. The Overseer is one step closer to noticing.

## The Swan
- Niette is not one of the six blades. It is one of the **three ancient relics** — the **wing of the swan**.
- The wing is the span, the flight, the strike from above. Niette reflects this: it is a spear that flies, that protects, that reaches where the hand cannot.
- The other two relics are **Gustaero** (the neck — a longbow) and **Alnean** (the breath — a breathing relic). Together they form the swan that pierces the Gap.
- **Niette is not related to the Architect.** It predates the Architect — or exists separately from it. The Cycle Clan did not make it. They found it. They hid it. They have guarded it for millennia without knowing where it came from.

## History
- One of the three ancient relics. Origin unknown — even to the Cycle Clan.
- Never carried by the Architect. Never part of the Gemheart network.
- Buried with **Gavaevodata** in her tomb in **Elis Selatan**.
- It has never been recovered. No one has found the tomb.

## What the Party Knows
- They have never heard of Niette.
- They know there are three ancient relics, but they have only seen one — Gustaero.

### What Niette Is
- Niette is the **wing of the swan**. It is a spear — the span of the bird, the thing that gives it flight and reach.
- It is **not** one of the six blades. The six blades are cutting tools. The three relics are passage tools. Gustaero reaches. Niette strikes. Alnean breathes.
- Niette is the only weapon in existence that can **kill a Valla** without breaking the seal. It severs the soul from the role, not from the world. The Valla dies. The seal holds. The Overseer does not immediately notice.
- This is a **terrible weapon**. It should only be used if there is no other way. The party may need it if Simon cannot be freed by any other means — but using it will cost them something they cannot get back.

### Where It Is
- Niette is buried in the **Tomb of Gavaevodata** in **Elis Selatan**.
- The tomb has never been found. It is hidden in the deep jungle, guarded by the roots of the First Trees and by the Grin dead.
- No one has entered the tomb since Gavaevodata was laid to rest. No one has left it either.

### The Tomb
- The Tomb of Gavaevodata is not a building. It is a **grove** — a circle of First Trees that grew around her body after she died.
- The trees have grown into a dome. The roots have woven themselves into a door. Only a Grin descendant can open it — the trees recognize the blood.
- Inside: Gavaevodata's bones, wrapped in a cloak of leaves. The **Niette** spear lies across her chest.
- The tomb is guarded by **Echoes of the first Grin** — the children of Gavaevodata and Zargoth, long dead, still watching.
- They will not attack a Grin descendant. They will attack anyone else.

### How to Get It
- The party must bring a Grin descendant to the tomb. **Vya** is the most likely candidate — she is Hildag's magnum opus, and the trees will recognize her.
- The Echoes of the first Grin will test her. They will ask her: *"Why do you carry the blood of the Gap? Why do you smile when you should weep?"*
- If she answers truthfully — if she says she carries the blood because it is her inheritance, and she smiles because she cannot stop — they will let her pass.
- If she lies, they will attack. They are CR 8 each. There are six of them.
- If she passes, she can take Niette from Gavaevodata's chest. The spear will hum in her hand. It will recognize her as a child of the Grin.
- **Vya will not be able to wield Niette effectively** — it is a spear, and she is a ranger. But she can carry it. She can give it to someone who can use it. Or she can keep it as a symbol.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Niette is the **only weapon** that can kill a Valla without breaking the seal. This is a terrible option. It should be a last resort.
- **If the party uses Niette on Simon**, he will die. The seal will hold — for now. But the Architect is still there, barely. It will be the only thing holding the seal. It will not last long. The party will have bought themselves time — but not much.
- **If the party uses Niette on the Architect**, the Architect will die. The seal will hold — for now. But Simon is still there, and he is cracking. The party will have bought themselves time — but not much.
- **The only way to use Niette without killing anyone** is to use it as part of the **third path** — to pierce the Gap and reach the Overseer directly. Niette is the wing. It flies. It reaches. It can strike the Overseer.
- **Niette is sentient.** It has been waiting in the tomb for thousands of years. It remembers Gavaevodata. It remembers the first Grin. It does not remember the Architect — because it never knew the Architect. It will speak to whoever carries it — not in words, but in feelings. It will feel like flight. It will feel like grief. It will feel like coming home.
- **The spear's connection to Gavaevodata:** Gavaevodata was not a warrior. She was a princess who stepped into the Gap to save her people. Niette was not her weapon — it was her **hope**. She carried it because it was the only thing that could protect her children if the Gap ever opened again. She never used it. She never had to. But she kept it close.
- **If Vya ever holds Niette**, she will feel her ancestor's presence. She will feel the weight of the Grin bloodline. She will feel the love of a mother who never met her children. This is a Season 3 emotional beat.
- **Niette was not created by the Architect.** It is not a fragment of the Gemhearts. It is older — or separate. The Cycle Clan's records say only: *"It was here before us. It will be here after us. Do not ask what it is. Ask what it does."*
- **Niette's origin is an open question** — same options as Alnean:
  - **A:** It predates the Architect.
  - **B:** It was made by a mortal civilization that reached the Gap before the Cycle Clan existed.
  - **C:** It was made by the Grin — Gavaevodata's bloodline — before Zargoth. (This would make it a Grin relic, given to Gavaevodata as a child.)
  - **D:** It was always there — a natural formation of Ambitio-Ether that the Cycle Clan shaped into a relic.
