## What It Does
- Pull a fuzzy object from the bag. Roll 1d6:
  1. **Goat** (friendly, follows you, lasts 10 minutes)
  2. **Owl** (scouts ahead, 10 min)
  3. **Koala** (cuddly, sleeps, 10 min)
  4. **Elephant** (large, useful for pushing, 10 min)
  5. **Snake** (poisonous but controllable, 10 min)
  6. **Rhino** (charges in a straight line, 10 min)
- The animal disappears after 10 minutes.

## History
- Found in Greyland Castle's supply room (<a href="/Session%2001%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Sorcerer">Session 01</a>).
- Used occasionally. Mostly a joke item.
::/pf
%% DM SECRETS %%
- The bag is not actually "worn out." It is a fragment of a Cycle Clan artifact.
- The animals are not real — they are **echoes** of creatures that once lived near the bag.
- If the bag is destroyed, the echoes are freed.
