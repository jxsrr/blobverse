## What It Does
- +2 longbow.
- Wind arrows form silently.
- First attack against surprised/asleep targets is a critical hit.
- Ignores half and three-quarters cover.
- 3/day: Fire two arrows as a bonus action.
- 1/day: **Ribuan Belahan** — 60-ft cone, DC 16 Dex save, 8d8 piercing.

## The Swan
- Gustaero is not one of the six blades. It is one of the **three ancient relics** — the **neck of the swan**.
- The neck is graceful, silent, and reaching. Gustaero reflects this: it reaches farther than any other bow, it makes no sound, and it pierces the veil between worlds.
- The other two relics are **Niette** (the wing — a spear) and **Alnean** (the breath — a breathing relic). Together they form the swan that pierces the Gap.
- **Gustaero is not related to the Architect.** It predates the Architect — or exists separately from it. The Cycle Clan did not make it. They found it. They hid it. They have guarded it for millennia without knowing where it came from.

## History
- One of the three ancient relics. Origin unknown — even to the Cycle Clan.
- Never carried by the Architect. Never part of the Gemheart network.
- Thrown into the sea by **Lei Firsa** after she scattered the six blades.
- Found by **Gunnar Guirela** in his fishing net in Wind's Rest.
- Passed to **Olaf Guirela**, then **Gordon**, then **Husgus**.
- Carried briefly by **Accarus Grin**, who gave it to **Husgus** after the Frosthaven mission.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Gustaero is connected to the Swan Trio network — not the Gemheart network.
- It remembers its wielders. It remembers Gunnar, Olaf, Gordon, and now Husgus.
- **Gustaero is the neck of the swan.** It is not a blade. It is one of the three ancient relics. This is important: the six blades are tools of cutting. The three relics are tools of passage. The neck reaches. The wing strikes. The breath survives.
- **What the neck does:** It reaches through cover. It reaches through the Gap's veil. It reaches farther than any mortal bow should. When all three relics are gathered, the neck allows the swan to **extend** — to reach across the Gap and touch the Overseer.
- **What the party does NOT know:** Gustaero is sentient. It has been waiting for a wielder who understands that it is not a weapon of war. It is a tool of passage. Husgus understands this, even if he cannot say it. That is why the bow chose him.
- **If Gustaero is ever taken from Husgus**, the swan cannot be completed. The third path requires all three relics. Without the neck, the swan cannot reach.
- **The bow's connection to Olaf and Gordon:** Olaf carried it for decades. He gave it to Gordon. Gordon carried it for centuries. It slowed his aging — a side effect no one understood. The bow does not want its wielder to die. It holds on. Husgus has not noticed this yet. He will.
- **If Husgus ever meets Olaf's Echo**, the bow will recognize him. It will hum. Olaf will see his old bow in the hands of Gordon's boy — and he will smile.
- **Gustaero was not created by the Architect.** It is not a fragment of the Gemhearts. It is older — or separate. The Cycle Clan's records say only: *"It was here before us. It will be here after us. Do not ask what it is. Ask what it does."*
- **Gustaero's origin is an open question** — same options as Alnean:
  - **A:** It predates the Architect.
  - **B:** It was made by a mortal civilization that reached the Gap before the Cycle Clan existed.
  - **C:** It was made by the Grin — Gavaevodata's bloodline — before Zargoth.
  - **D:** It was always there — a natural formation of Ambitio-Ether that the Cycle Clan shaped into a relic.
