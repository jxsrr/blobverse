## What It Does
- **Breathe Anywhere:** The wielder can breathe in water, in the Gap, in the void between worlds, and in any environment where air should not exist.
- **Gap Resistance:** While holding Alnean, the wielder has advantage on saving throws against the Gap's corrupting influence.
- **Swan Song (1/long rest):** The wielder can exhale a breath of pure Ambitio-Ether. This breath heals all allies within 30 ft for 4d8 HP and removes one level of exhaustion from each. If the wielder is at 0 HP when they use this, they stabilize and the breath still takes effect.
- **The Breath Is Not a Weapon:** Alnean cannot attack. It cannot cut. It cannot block. It is not a blade. It is a way to survive where no mortal should.

## The Swan
- Alnean is not one of the six blades. It is one of the **three ancient relics** — the **breath of the swan**.
- The breath is what lets the swan swim in water, dive into the deep, and rise again. Alnean reflects this: it lets the wielder survive the Gap, the void, and the depths.
- The other two relics are **Gustaero** (the neck — a longbow) and **Niette** (the wing — a spear). Together they form the swan that pierces the Gap.
- Without the breath, the swan cannot swim. Without Alnean, the third path is impossible.

## Why "Swan Song"?
- The name was given by the Cycle Clan, not by the Architect.
- A swan sings only once — at the moment of its death. Alnean is the breath that lets the swan sing.
- The Cycle Clan's records say: *"The swan does not fear the deep. It fears the silence. Alnean is the song that breaks the silence."*
- It is not a weapon of death. It is a song of survival.

## History
- Alnean is one of the three ancient relics. **It is not related to the Architect.** Its origin is older, or separate, or simply unknown.
- It was never carried by the Architect. It was never part of the Gemheart network.
- It has been kept by the **Kuo Lau Kingdom** — the underwater kingdom — for as long as their records go back. The Kuo Lau King is its current guardian.
- The Kuo Lau are the origin of **Babibubebo** — the small fish-man the party met in Juto Port City (Session 02). Babi is an exile, a fragment of the Kuo Lau who was lost in the mortal world.
- Alnean has never been recovered by anyone outside the Kuo Lau. The Cycle Clan knows it exists. They do not know where it is.
- No one has ever seen the Kuo Lau Kingdom and returned to tell of it.

### What Alnean Is
- Alnean is the **breath of the swan**. It is a breathing relic — not a weapon, not a crown, not a blade.
- It is the thing that lets the swan swim in water, dive into the deep, and rise again. It is what lets the swan **survive** the places where no mortal should go.
- It is **not** one of the six blades. The six blades are cutting tools. The three relics are passage tools. Gustaero reaches. Niette strikes. Alnean breathes.
- **Alnean is not related to the Architect.** It is not a piece of the Architect's body. It is not a fragment of the Gemhearts. It is something older — or something separate. The Kuo Lau have kept it for millennia. They do not know where it came from either.

### Where It Is
- Alnean is kept in the **Kuo Lau Kingdom** — an underwater kingdom deep beneath the ocean, far from any shore the party has walked.
- It is in the possession of the **Kuo Lau King** — the ruler of the underwater kingdom.
- The Kuo Lau are the origin of **Babibubebo** — the small fish-man the party met in Juto Port City. Babi was separated from his people and washed up in the mortal world. He does not know the way home. He does not know what his people guard.
- The Kuo Lau do not trade with the surface. They do not welcome visitors. They do not trust air-breathers. The party will have to earn their trust.

### The Kuo Lau Kingdom
- The Kuo Lau Kingdom is not a myth. It is a real place — a city of coral, pearl, and bioluminescence, deep beneath the Elis Ocean.
- It is protected by a **pressure veil** — a magical barrier that keeps the water from crushing the city and keeps the surface world from finding it.
- The Kuo Lau are not hostile. They are **isolationist**. They have seen what the surface world does to the sea. They have chosen to stay below.
- The **Kuo Lau King** is old — older than most mortals. He has held Alnean for centuries. He knows what it is. He knows what it does. He has never used it.
- The King will only give Alnean to someone who **brings Babibubebo home**. He does not know Babi is alive. He has mourned him for years.

### What Alnean Does
- **Breathe Anywhere:** The wielder can breathe in water, in the Gap, in the void between worlds. This is not a spell. It is a permanent passive effect. The wielder simply **can**.
- **Gap Resistance:** The Gap is not just a place. It is a condition. It corrupts. It dissolves. It unmakes. Alnean protects against this. The wielder has advantage on saving throws against the Gap's influence.
- **Swan Song (1/long rest):** The wielder exhales a breath of pure Ambitio-Ether. It is not an attack. It is a **gift**. It heals. It restores. It reminds the world that the swan is not just a weapon — it is a living thing. The breath still works if the wielder is at 0 HP — they stabilize as they exhale. This is the moment when the swan sings.
- **The Breath Is Not a Weapon:** Alnean cannot attack. It cannot cut. It cannot block. It is not a blade. It is a way to survive. The party must understand this. Alnean is not a sword. It is a lung.

### How to Get It
- The party must find the **Kuo Lau Kingdom**. The only one who knows the way is **Babibubebo** — and he does not remember. He was too young when he was lost.
- The party must help Babi remember. They must take him to the **Kuil Lautan** in Juto Port City — the Sea Temple — and let him swim deep. The temple's underwater caves connect to the Kuo Lau's outer tunnels.
- Babi will lead them down. He will remember the way as he swims. The party will need **water-breathing magic** — or they will need to find a way to survive the deep. (Alnean is not the only solution — but it is the best one. If they already have Alnean, they would not need to find it. This is a chicken-and-egg problem. The DM must decide how the party survives the descent. Options: potions, spells, Syl's blood, or a favor from Vythrax.)
- The party must bring **Babibubebo home**. The Kuo Lau King will weep when he sees him. He will not give Alnean immediately — he will ask the party to prove they are worthy.
- The test is not combat. The test is a **question**: *"Why do you want to breathe where you were not meant to breathe?"*
- If the party answers truthfully — if they say they want to survive so they can save someone else — the King will let them take Alnean.
- If they lie, the King will not attack. He will simply **close the gates**. Alnean will be lost to them.
- If the party passes, they can take Alnean. The King will bless them. The Kuo Lau will sing them home.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- **Alnean is not a weapon.** It is a breathing relic. It cannot attack. It cannot cut. It cannot block. It is a way to survive.
- **Without Alnean, the third path is impossible.** The party cannot enter the Gap without it. They will suffocate. They will dissolve. They will become nothing.
- **Alnean is sentient.** It has been waiting in the Kuo Lau Kingdom for thousands of years. It remembers nothing of the Architect — because it never knew the Architect. It will speak to whoever carries it — not in words, but in feelings. It will feel like taking a deep breath after drowning. It will feel like coming home.
- **Babibubebo is the key.** The Kuo Lau King has been waiting for his lost child to return. Babi is not just a hungry fish-man. He is a prince of the deep. He does not know this. He will learn.
- **The Kuo Lau Kingdom is in danger.** The Gap is thinning. The pressure veil is weakening. The Kuo Lau do not know this yet — but the King has felt something stirring in the deep. He will ask the party to help, if they earn his trust.
- **If the party takes Alnean**, the King will give them a **Kuo Lau Pearl** — a small pearl that allows the bearer to breathe underwater for 1 hour per day. It is not Alnean. It is a gift. It is a thank you.
- **If the party refuses to take Alnean** — if they decide the third path is too dangerous — the King will not force them. He will simply say: *"Then you have chosen a different path. I hope it is the right one."* Alnean will remain in the Kuo Lau Kingdom. The party will have to find another way. There is no other way.
- **The name "Swan Song" is a Cycle Clan invention.** It is not the relic's true name. The relic's true name is **Alnean** — a word in the Kuo Lau tongue. The Cycle Clan called it "Swan Song" because they did not know what else to call it. The name stuck.

### Alnean's Origin — Open Question
- **Alnean is not related to the Architect.** This is confirmed canon.
- Where it came from is **unknown** — even to the Kuo Lau. They found it. They kept it. They have guarded it for millennia without knowing its origin.
- Possible origins (DM decides):
  - **A:** It predates the Architect — a relic of whatever existed before the Overseer and the Architect.
  - **B:** It was made by a mortal civilization that reached the Gap before the Cycle Clan existed — and was destroyed for it.
  - **C:** It was made by the Grin — Gavaevodata's bloodline — before Zargoth, as a way to survive the Gap. (This would tie it to the Grin, not the Architect.)
  - **D:** It was always there — a natural formation of Ambitio-Ether that the Kuo Lau shaped into a relic.
- **The Kuo Lau's records say only:** *"It was here before us. It will be here after us. Do not ask what it is. Ask what it does."*
