## What It Does
- One of the four Gemhearts. Green.
- Connected to **time and absolute mana flow**.
- Part of the seal that protects the world from the Overseer.
- Can modulate temporal flow at the center of the seal.
- Slows the burning of the Valla's soul.

## History
- Found in **Greyland Castle** by the party (Session 01).
- Was hidden in an anti-magic chest. Taking it woke Will of Grin.
- Handed to **Mira / Keeper of Secret** after Session 02.
- Mira has held it ever since. She has not told the party.
- **Accarus Grin never carried Emelanica.** He carried Gustaero — and gave it to Husgus after the Frosthaven mission.

## What the Party Knows
- They found it in Session 01. They know it is one of the Gemhearts.
- They do not know Mira took it. They do not know where it is.
- They do not know what it can do when fully awakened.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### Emelanica — The Green Gemheart
- Emelanica is the **green Gemheart** — the organ of the Architect that governs **time and absolute mana flow**. It is the temporal processor of the Architect — the part of it that keeps every part of its body moving in temporal harmony.
- In the primordial body, Emelanica kept the Architect's consciousness synchronized with its body. As a Gemheart, it became the **temporal modulator** and **absolute mana controller** for all of Oversee. Its primary function is to manipulate **causality** at the center of the seal — to slow the burning of the Valla's soul, so that a single soul can survive for centuries in its resistance against the Overseer.
- Emelanica is the **heartbeat of time itself** — the pulse that keeps the world alive.
- It is **not a weapon**. It is **not a tool of war**. It is a **piece of a god** — a crystallized organ of the Architect, separated when it split itself to create Oversee.

### Current Holder — Mira Nouveau
- **Mira holds Emelanica.** She took it from the party after Session 02. She did not ask. She did not tell them. She believes she is protecting them.
- She knows it is one of the Gemhearts. She knows it is important. She does not know it is an organ of the Architect. She does not know what it can do when fully awakened.
- She has kept it hidden. She has kept it safe. She has kept it secret. She has not told the party. She has not told anyone. She is afraid of what it can do. She is afraid of what it might mean.
- **She will give it to the party** if they prove they are worthy. The proof is not combat. The proof is **trust**. If they can show her they are not like the others — if they can show her they are not in it for the power, not in it for the glory, not in it for themselves — she will give them Emelanica.

### What Emelanica Does — Full Canon
- **Temporal Modulation:** Emelanica can manipulate the flow of time at the center of the seal. It can slow the burning of the Valla's soul. It can extend the life of the seal. It can buy the world time.
- **Absolute Mana Control:** Emelanica governs the flow of Ambitio-Ether across the world. It can open the floodgates. It can close them. It can divert the energy. It can starve the Overseer — or feed it.
- **Seal Anchor:** Emelanica is part of the seal. It is one of the four Gemhearts that hold the world together. Without it, the seal would collapse.
- **Override Requirement:** Emelanica is required for the **Architect's Mercy** — the override clause that frees the Valla without killing him. Without it, the override cannot be performed.
- **Third Path Requirement:** Emelanica is required for the third path. If the party chooses to pierce the Gap and face the Overseer directly, they will need Emelanica to manipulate the temporal flow of the Gap. Without it, they cannot reach the Overseer. Without it, they cannot return.

### Emelanica and the Endgame
- If the party chooses the **third path**, Emelanica will be used to slow the Overseer's perception of time — to give the party a chance to strike. It will be a weapon of liberation — not of destruction.
- If the party chooses the **sacrifice path**, Emelanica will be used to extend the life of the new Valla. It will be a mercy — not a murder.
- If the party chooses the **tyranny path**, Emelanica will be used to control the flow of magic in the world. It will be a weapon of oppression — not of protection.
- **The choice is theirs.** The DM should present the options clearly and let the party decide.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- **Mira holds Emelanica.** She took it from them after Session 02. She has not told them.
- **Accarus never carried Emelanica.** He carried Gustaero — and gave it to Husgus after the Frosthaven mission.
- **Emelanica is an organ of the Architect.** It is not just a stone. It is a piece of a god.
- **Emelanica can manipulate time.** It can slow the burning of the Valla's soul. It can extend the life of the seal.
- **Emelanica is required for the override.** Without it, the third path is impossible.
