## Stat Block
- +3 greataxe. 1d12 + Str slashing + 2d8 cold damage. Magical. Heavy, two-handed.
- **Freezing Touch:** On hit, target must succeed on a DC 18 Con save or be restrained by ice for 1 minute. Repeat save at end of each turn.
- **Petrification:** If a target fails 3 consecutive saves against Freezing Touch, they are petrified in ice permanently until freed by Greater Restoration.
- **Frost Aura:** The air around the axe is so cold that torches within 10 ft flicker and may go out.

## History
- One of six legendary weapons.
- Thrown into a storm on Frosthaven Peak by Lei Firsa.
- **Not yet recovered.** It is buried in <a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a>'s lair.

## What the Party Knows
- The The Frostscale Clan believe it is a sacred weapon.
- If Syl finds it, it may be her signature weapon.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Frostfang was created from the **tooth of an ancient ice dragon** — possibly one of <a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a>'s ancestors.
- It is sentient. It chooses its wielder. It has been waiting for Syl.
- <a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a> will give it to Syl freely if asked. She knows it is her daughter's birthright.
- If Syl wields it, she will gain a **permanent +1 to her cold damage** and can use **Freezing Touch** once per day without expending a spell slot.
