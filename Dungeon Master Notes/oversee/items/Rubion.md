## What It Does
- One of the four Gemhearts. Red.
- Connected to **instant annihilation** and the severing of astral bonds.
- Can break the connection between soul and body in nanoseconds.
- Part of the seal that protects the world.
- Prevents anomalous regeneration at the moment a soul is drawn into the cosmic seal.

## History
- Taken by the party from **Jarko** in Session 01 (after the fight at Greyland Castle and the Will of Grin ship).
- Handed to **Mira / Keeper of Secret** after Session 02.
- Mira has held it ever since. She has not told the party.
- **Accarus Grin never carried Rubion.** He carried Gustaero — and gave it to Husgus after the Frosthaven mission.

## What the Party Knows
- They saw it in Session 01. They know it is one of the Gemhearts.
- They do not know Mira took it. They do not know where it is.
- They do not know what it can do when fully awakened.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### Rubion — The Red Gemheart
- Rubion is the **red Gemheart** — the organ of the Architect that governs **annihilation** and **severance**. It is the part of the Architect that can cut, separate, and end.
- In the primordial body, Rubion was the **last line of defense** — the ability to cut off a threatened part so the whole could survive. As a Gemheart, it became an **instant antimatter weapon**. Its primary function is to sever the astral bond between soul and body, permanently.
- In nanoseconds, Rubion turns biological flesh to ash — preventing anomalous regeneration at the moment the soul is drawn into the cosmic seal. It is the Architect's **right hand** — the organ that can end anything, quickly and cleanly, for the sake of a greater survival.
- It is **not a weapon of war**. It is a **weapon of necessity**. It is used to prevent the Overseer from consuming the world — not to conquer it.

### Current Holder — Mira Nouveau
- **Mira holds Rubion.** She took it from the party after Session 02. She did not ask. She did not tell them. She believes she is protecting them.
- She knows it is one of the Gemhearts. She knows it is important. She does not know it is an organ of the Architect. She does not know what it can do when fully awakened.
- She has kept it hidden. She has kept it safe. She has kept it secret. She has not told the party. She has not told anyone. She is afraid of what it can do. She is afraid of what it might mean.
- **She will give it to the party** if they prove they are worthy. The proof is not combat. The proof is **trust**. If they can show her they are not like the others — if they can show her they are not in it for the power, not in it for the glory, not in it for themselves — she will give them Rubion.

### What Rubion Does — Full Canon
- **Instant Annihilation:** Rubion can sever the astral bond between soul and body in nanoseconds. It turns biological flesh to ash. It prevents regeneration. It ends things.
- **Seal Anchor:** Rubion is part of the seal. It is one of the four Gemhearts that hold the world together. Without it, the seal would collapse.
- **Override Requirement:** Rubion is required for the **Architect's Mercy** — the override clause that frees the Valla without killing him. Without it, the override cannot be performed.
- **Third Path Requirement:** Rubion is required for the third path. If the party chooses to pierce the Gap and face the Overseer directly, they will need Rubion to sever the connection between the Overseer and the world. Without it, the Overseer cannot be defeated.

### Rubion and the Endgame
- If the party chooses the **third path**, Rubion will be used to sever the Overseer's connection to the world. It will be a weapon of liberation — not of destruction.
- If the party chooses the **sacrifice path**, Rubion will be used to prevent the Valla's body from regenerating. It will be a mercy — not a murder.
- If the party chooses the **tyranny path**, Rubion will be used to destroy anyone who opposes them. It will be a weapon of oppression — not of protection.
- **The choice is theirs.** The DM should present the options clearly and let the party decide.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- **Mira holds Rubion.** She took it from them after Session 02. She has not told them.
- **Accarus never carried Rubion.** He carried Gustaero — and gave it to Husgus after the Frosthaven mission.
- **Rubion is an organ of the Architect.** It is not just a stone. It is a piece of a god.
- **Rubion can sever the astral bond between soul and body.** It can end things. It can prevent regeneration. It can kill.
- **Rubion is required for the override.** Without it, the third path is impossible.
