> [!quote] Four entries from a water-stained journal found in the hidden basement beneath the Observatorium.

## Entry 1
*Day 10 in this new world. I found another one. She was floating in the sea near the lighthouse, just like me... and she also "Fell" from Aede. Her name is Reins. Finally, someone who understands my lost world.*

## Entry 2
*We built a life here, in Rioquan. Our daughter, <a href="/oversee/npcs/Mira%20Nouveau">Mira</a>, has been born. But Reins is restless. She knows her destiny is to fight the "Destroyer God" in Aede, but she chose to stay with me. I fear the shadows of our old world will find us here.*

## Entry 3
*I have done it. I made a pact with the "Keeper of Secret." They are a secret organization, originally just personal servants of the Initial Six, but they are the only ones who witnessed the final ritual and know the true fate of the Six. They will protect our bloodline. They will keep <a href="/oversee/npcs/Mira%20Nouveau">Mira</a> and her descendants safe, in exchange for my service as a sailor for them.*

## Entry 4
*If you are my descendant and reading this, the pact still holds. The compass brought you here, as designed. Now, take the compass to the "Locked Sea Archive." Show it to the Keeper. They will give you the answers you seek.*
