## Part 1: The Dream of Fate

The Will of Grin ship moved without a crew.

No sails. No wheel. No one to speak to. Only the hum of the engine, the endless sea, and the slow creep of hunger.

For days, the party drifted. They rationed what they had. They rationed again. The walls of the ship were metal and cold, and the sea beyond the portholes was a mirror of nothing.

And then, in the space between hunger and sleep, they dreamed.

Not one dream. A **shared** dream.

Each of them saw a figure — not a face, but a presence. A dealer. A reader of cards. And each was offered a card.

Each card carried a blessing and a curse.

- **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Syl%20Frost">Syl</a>** was dealt **The Magician** — cursed to be the center of every magical failure, to feel her own spells turn wild and burn her. But **Temperance** balanced it — her blood was purified, a universal cure for poison, disease, and curse.
- **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Vya%20Arleschia">Vya</a>** drew **Wheel of Fortune** — a systematic erosion of her luck, small chaos chasing her every step. But **Death** gave her peace — a quiet, unshakable calm in the face of terror.
- **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Husgus">Husgus</a>** saw **Judgement** — a legend written before the deeds, his name known wherever he walked. But **The Lovers** bound him — a magical dread of losing the companions beside him.
- **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Zure%20Ama">Zure Ama</a>** was cursed by **The Hanged Man** — his smile frozen forever, maddening to all who saw it. But **The Devil** gave him **Qain** — a bound Imp, later renamed **<a href="/oversee/items/Renal">Renal</a>**, whose loyalty was absolute.
- **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Shin%20Van%20Hel">HVS</a>** was given **The Tower** — the blade **<a href="/oversee/items/Swaith%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Soul-Thirsting%20Blade">Swaith</a>**, able to steal life from the dying and return the dead to breath. But it whispered. It gnawed at his peace.

They woke separately. They did not speak of the dream — not yet.

But each of them felt heavier.

## Part 2: The Confession Field

They searched the ship for the galley. Along the way, they found a room that was not on any schematic — the **Observation Room**.

Inside, the air was wrong. It was heavy. It pressed against the mind.

And then the questions began.

The room forced truth.

Each of the Five Sticks was asked three questions by the others. The room would not let them lie.

- **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Zure%20Ama">Zure Ama</a>** was asked about **Reins**. He spoke, for the first time without defending himself, about the friend he had lost. About the guilt that had driven him across worlds.
- **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Shin%20Van%20Hel">HVS</a>** was asked about his gloves. About the Curse Mark. About **Zargoyle**. He spoke, and the room listened.
- **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Syl%20Frost">Syl</a>** was asked why his magic was self-taught. Why his clan had never accepted him. He answered.
- **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Vya%20Arleschia">Vya</a>** was asked about the burning of her village. About **<a href="/oversee/factions/Will%20of%20Grin">Will of Grin</a>**. About the nightmares she could not explain.
- **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Husgus">Husgus</a>** was asked about **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Gordon">Gordon</a>**'s last words. About the spell that had turned him from a boy into a halfling. About the curse that had made him what he was.

When the last question was answered, the room released them.

And in the center of the room, a small object appeared: the **Meal Key**.

## Part 3: Arrival in Juto Port City

They ate.

And then — without knowing how — the ship's course changed.

**<a href="/oversee/locations/Juto%20Port%20City">Juto Port City</a>**.

A city of coral and steel. A city that climbed cliffs on pillars of ancient reef. A city built vertically, where the poor lived in the tidal districts and the wealthy lived above the floods.

The party arrived at the **Floating Docks**.

Within minutes, they were arrested.

Commonwealth police had been watching the docks for weeks. A group of heavily armed strangers, dressed in **<a href="/oversee/factions/Will%20of%20Grin">Will of Grin</a>** armor, arriving on a ship with no registered crew — the arrest was automatic.

**Ama's** armor was confiscated as evidence.

They were held for a day. Questioned. Released with a warning: *"You are not the first to cosplay as Grin. Do not make us regret letting you walk."*

They had no money, no lodging, and no plan.

## Part 4: Exploring the City

They needed a place to sleep.

Their first stop was the **<a href="/oversee/locations/Juto%27s%20Observatorium">Juto's Observatorium</a>** — the Center of Knowledge that had, over centuries, become the heart of Juto Port City. They thought it might have information about where they were, or what had happened to them.

But the Observatorium was closed for the evening.

So they went to **Mutiara** — the Pearl — an elite tavern where wealthy adventurers and merchant captains gathered. It was full. It was expensive. It was also hosting a celebration of *"the great adventurers of Oversee."*

They did not stay long.

But they did meet a fighter named **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Agus">Agus</a>** — a young man with a confident smile and a story about a legendary weapon called *Drei*. The party would learn later that Agus's smile hid more than it showed.

They left Mutiara. They could not afford it.

## Part 5: The Fall into the Tortle's Shop

Rather than pay for the **Lift Karang** — the vertical transport that moved people between the city's tiers — the party decided to jump.

This was a mistake.

They fell through the awning of a small shop — **Teritip Tua** — and landed in a sprawl of diving helmets, harpoons, and strange oxygen tanks made from giant nautilus shells.

The shop belonged to **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Pops%20Tortle">Pops</a>** — an ancient Tortle, a thousand years old, deaf as stone, and slow as the tide.

They had to shout to be heard.

But Pops remembered. He remembered a name — **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Atlan%20Nouveau">Atlan Nouveau</a>** — and a children's book Atlan had written. He told them about Atlan's love of the sea, and about the lighthouse where Atlan had once stood and watched the horizon for someone who never came.

They did not understand the significance yet.

That night, they found **<a href="/Nenek%20Elara">Karang Tidur</a>** — a former private residence of a Juto mayor, now a free guesthouse for adventurers. It was old, and small, and smelled of salt and coral.

They slept.

## Part 6: The Stolen Gems

At dawn, a scream.

**<a href="/oversee/npcs/Elara">Elara</a>** stood at the entrance, pale, hands over her mouth.

> *"Someone... something... came in the night!"*

The party's emerald and ruby — the gem from Greyland and the gem from Dima Island — were gone.

**<a href="/oversee/items/Atlan%27s%20Compass">Atlan's Compass</a>** vibrated in **Ama**'s pocket. It had been silent for days. Now it was alive.

They followed it.

It led to the **Kuil Lautan** — the Sea Temple — a cave shrine on the lower tier of the city where the priests read the patterns of waves.

They descended.

And inside, they found **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Babibubebo">Babibubebo</a>** — a small fish-man, hunched and trembling, surrounded by the shells and crumbs of a dozen stolen meals.

Not the gems. Just food.

He was not the thief. He was simply hungry.

The party realized the compass was pointing at the same location — but at a different layer. Directly **above** the Sea Temple, carved into the same cliff, was the **<a href="/The%20Observatorium">Observatorium</a>**.

## Part 7: The Hidden Base

They went up.

The Observatorium was quiet. Scholars moved with silent efficiency. One of them — a woman with sharp eyes and a scholar's posture — watched them pass.

They found a locked secretary's office. A little persuasion. A little luck. A little **Ama** shouldering the door.

Behind a bookshelf, a hidden staircase.

They descended into a space that had not been touched in centuries.

**<a href="/oversee/npcs/Atlan%20Nouveau">Atlan Nouveau</a>**'s base.

There was a stone pedestal with a pale blue eternal flame — a flame that did not flicker in the drafts. Beside it, a bowl of silver ash. And carved into the pedestal:

> *"Burn here, forever in my heart, the one I love."*

On a table, a diary — water-stained, the ink faded but still legible.

They read it.

- **Entry 1:** *"Day 10 in this new world. I found another one. She was floating in the sea near the lighthouse, just like me... and she also 'Fell' from Aede. Her name is Reins."*
- **Entry 2:** *"We built a life here, in Rioquan. Our daughter, Mira, has been born. But Reins is restless."*
- **Entry 3:** *"I made a pact with the Keeper of Secret."*
- **Entry 4:** *"If you are my descendant and read this, the pact still holds. Bring the compass to the 'Locked Sea Archive.'"*

**Ama** went very still.

**Reins.**

The name he had been chasing across two worlds. The friend he had lost. She had been here. She had been alive. She had fallen into Oversee **500 years before him.**

He had been searching for her across time without knowing it.

## Part 8: Mira

To open the final door, the party had to write two names in the silver ash.

**Reins** and **Atlan**.

They wrote them.

The door opened.

Behind it stood a woman — half-elf, sharp-eyed, carrying a sword that glowed faintly with ancient magic. Beside her, **<a href="/Nenek%20Elara">Elara</a>**, holding the green gem and the red gem in her hands.

The woman spoke.

> *"So. My father was right. Someone would come."*

Her name was **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Mira%20Nouveau">Mira Nouveau</a>**. Daughter of **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Atlan%20Nouveau">Atlan Nouveau</a>** and **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Reins">Reins</a>**. Founder of the **<a href="/oversee/factions/Keeper%20of%20Secret">Keeper of Secret</a>**.

She told them she had been a child when her father disappeared. She had seen it — the ritual, the light, the man called **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Lei%20Alejandro">Lei Alejandro</a>**. She had spent her life building an organization to find the truth.

She offered them a place in it.

They accepted.

She also told them something else: **<a href="/oversee/items/Safayer">Safayer</a>** — the second Gemheart — had been swallowed by a silver dragon in the mountains of **Pirizia**. The dragon was called **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Vythrax">Vythrax</a>**. It had been poisoned for centuries.

To face a dragon, they would need **<a href="/oversee/items/Gustaero%20%E2%80%94%20The%20Swan%20Bow">Gustaero — The Swan Bow</a>** — the legendary bow once carried by **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Olaf%20Guirela">Olaf Guirela</a>**, then passed to **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Gordon">Gordon</a>**, then to **<a href="/oversee/pcs/Husgus">Husgus</a>**.

And that bow, she believed, was in **<a href="/Ghuffghuff">Ghuffghuff</a>** — the village of fried rice, deep in Harka.

She assigned **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Agus">Agus</a>** to travel with them.

The party accepted.

And they did not know — not yet — that Agus was already working for someone else.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- **The Fate Cards are permanent.** They cannot be removed. They cannot be traded. Each PC is bound to what they drew.
- **The Confession Field** was a Grin interrogation mechanism. It forced truth. It was designed to break spies. The party passed it, and the mechanism rewarded them with the Meal Key.
- **Elara was testing the party.** She moved the gems herself. The "theft" was a test of how they solved problems — with violence or with patience. They passed.
- **Pops knows more about Atlan than he says.** He was Atlan's friend. He is also **not actually a Tortle** — he is a Gap-touched creature who took the shape of a Tortle centuries ago. His real name is **Oos**.
- **Agus was compromised** — but not by choice. He was compelled by **Echo Sam's** song. In Session 3, he will kill **<a href="/oversee/npcs/Bud-eg">Bud-eg</a>** and be taken by Echo Sam.
- **The "Destroyer God" in Aede** — the entity Reins feared — is the same entity as **The Overseer**. The party does not know this yet.
- **Mira's childhood:** She saw her father disappear at age 12. She has spent the last 25 years building the Keeper of Secret. She has never told anyone the full story of what she saw.
- **The Twin Shell Amulet** — found in Atlan's base — is a key item. It allows the wearer to take 10 on one attack roll per day. **It is also a key to the Bahtera Bawah Tanah.** Mira has not told the party this yet.
- **Session 02 stakes:** The party thinks they are solving a theft. They are actually being recruited into a war that has been fought in secret for 500 years.
