## Part 1: The Road to Ghuffghuff

The party left **[[Juto Port City]]** with a mission from **[[Mira Nouveau|Mira]]**: find **[[Gustaero — The Swan Bow]]** in **[[Ghuffghuff Town]]**. Their guide was **[[Agus]]** — the same fighter they had met at Mutiara.

They sailed from the docks of Kota Juto to **[[Javan Town]]**, then took a rhino cart inland. The journey would take days.

The cart carried more than the party. Along for the ride were:

- **[[Memeng]]** — a plump, cheerful cook who wanted to learn the secret fried rice recipe of Ghuffghuff
- **[[Yahanda]]** — a quiet priest assigned to teach at the new temple
- **The Mektol family** — **[[Herman Mektol]]** (father), **[[Faska Mektol]]** (mother), and **[[Cindy Mektol]]** (daughter), moving to Ghuffghuff to start a new life

Along the road, **[[Agus]]** told them about the **Gruffgud War** — a centuries-long civil conflict between **[[Ghuffghuff]]** and **[[Gudameng Town|Gudameng]]**. The cause? A dispute over whether **fried rice** or **fried noodles** was the superior dish.

The party assumed this was a joke.

It was not.

## Part 2: The Deaf Old Man

Midway through the journey, the cart stopped.

An old man stood in the road — filthy, ragged, and smelling of rot. He was deaf, or nearly so, and did not respond when the driver shouted. His hands moved in strange gestures.

Then the ground split.

Roots erupted from the soil. Wooden branches wrapped around the cart's wheels, lifted it off the ground, and held it suspended. The old man watched for a moment, then vanished into the forest.

The party was trapped.

**[[Zure Ama]]** acted first. His Imp, **[[Renal]]** — formerly **Qain** — shifted into a swarm of termites and consumed the wooden restraints from within. The cart dropped. The wheels turned.

They continued.

And then, faintly, they heard it.

**Singing.**

The village of **[[Ghuffghuff]]** was close.

## Part 3: Arrival at a Quiet Village

Ghuffghuff looked normal. Small. Warm. Rice fields on the edges, wooden homes, a teater in the center.

The village was almost empty. The driver explained: *"It is late. People are home. Nothing to worry about."*

**[[Memeng]]**, **[[Yahanda]]**, and the **Mektol family** split off to their various destinations. The party, still with **[[Agus]]**, went to the **teater** — the source of the singing.

Inside, a handful of villagers sang the same melody, over and over, faces blank, eyes unfocused.

**[[Husgus]]** stepped forward. His Fate Card — **Judgement** — should have made the villagers stare at him as a hero. They did not. They did not notice him at all.

Something was wrong.

They retreated to **Inn Sutisna** — the inn **[[Mira Nouveau|Mira]]** had booked for them — but chose instead to stay in **[[Gordon]]**'s old house at the edge of the village. Husgus's childhood home.

## Part 4: The Missing Priest

Morning.

The party learned that **[[Yahanda]]** was missing. No one remembered him. Not the innkeeper, not the drivers, not the villagers.

They bought maps from **[[Bu Mamat]]**'s warung and went into **[[Hutan Domido]]** — the forest between Ghuffghuff and Gudameng.

There, they found the old deaf man again. This time, he was trying to communicate. His hands moved urgently. His face was frightened.

He pointed at the village. He pointed at them. He pointed at his ears.

Before he could finish, an arrow flew.

**[[Agus]]** had shot him.

> *"He was in the way. We can use his house."*

**[[Bud-eg]]** — the old man — collapsed. Dead.

The party said nothing. But something between them had broken.

Deeper in the forest, they found the ruins of an old fortress — a remnant of the Gruffgud War — and beside it, the **dried corpse of Yahanda**. His skin was tight across his bones. No blood. No wound.

They went to **[[Gudameng Town|Gudameng]]** — the rival village — and slept. It felt normal there. Everyone remembered them.

They returned to Ghuffghuff.

## Part 5: The Pattern of Forgetting

At the new temple, the priests did not recognize **[[Yahanda]]**. They had never heard his name.

At the Mektol house, only **[[Cindy]]** was home. Her parents, she said, had gone to the market.

At the market, **[[Memeng]]** — the cook — remembered that **ten people** had arrived in the cart. Not eleven. Not Yahanda.

The party returned to the Mektol house.

**[[Herman]]** appeared. His words were the same as Memeng's. *"Ten people arrived. Not eleven."*

The party slept.

In the morning, they returned. **[[Herman]]** did not remember his wife.

> *"My wife? No. It's just me and my daughter. We came alone."*

**[[Faska]]** was gone. And no one — not even her husband — remembered she had ever existed.

The party went back to the ruined fortress. This time, they found **two** bodies: **[[Yahanda]]** and **[[Faska]]**, side by side, dried and hollow.

## Part 6: The Night Watch

They needed to protect the survivors.

A plan:
- **[[Husgus]]** would watch **[[Herman]]**
- **[[Vya Arleschia|Vya]]** would watch **[[Cindy]]**
- **[[Syl Frost|Syl]]** and **[[Shin Van Hel|HVS]]** would watch **[[Memeng]]**
- **[[Zure Ama|Ama]]** and **[[Agus]]** would sleep together

But that night, **[[Husgus]]** slept early. He did not hear the singing.

At eight in the evening, **[[Agus]]** stood. His eyes went blank. He walked out of the house and into the dark.

**[[Zure Ama|Ama]]** followed. Something invisible struck him — he swung, missed.

The party woke. They chased Agus into **[[Hutan Domido]]**.

There, in the moonlight, they found it.

**A creature of pale flesh and many heads.** Each head was a human face. Each face sang the same melody. They were draining Agus — draining him dry, the way Yahanda and Faska had been drained.

The party attacked. The creature was too strong. And when the sun began to rise, it sank into the earth and vanished.

## Part 7: Awakening

The party returned to the village.

**[[Husgus]]** saw something wrong. The village looked… cracked. Empty. Rotted. The people were gone. The houses were ruins.

He had woken up.

The others — the ones who had heard the singing the night before — were still inside the illusion.

The party realized the truth: **Hearing the song traps you in the illusion.** Those who covered their ears saw the real Ghuffghuff. Those who did not, stayed inside the dream.

That night, they tested it. **[[Syl Frost|Syl]]**, **[[HVS]]**, and **[[Vya Arleschia|Vya]]** covered their ears. **[[Zure Ama|Ama]]** and **[[Husgus]]** did not.

By morning, Ama and Husgus were free. They saw the ruined village. They saw the empty houses. They found **[[Cindy]]** in the Mektol house, alone, crying, making her own food.

## Part 8: The Many-Headed Creature

They planned the confrontation.

- **[[Syl Frost|Syl]]** would take **[[Cindy]]** and **[[Memeng]]** to **[[Gudameng Town|Gudameng]]** — out of the illusion.
- **[[HVS]]** and **[[Zure Ama|Ama]]** would wait at the temple.
- **[[Husgus]]** and **[[Vya Arleschia|Vya]]** would go to the **teater**.

The plan: cover their ears when the singing started.

At the teater, dozens of villagers sang — but their forms were translucent now. Fading. And at the center of the crowd stood the **many-headed creature**, singing with them.

The party covered their ears and attacked.

The battle was brutal. **[[Zure Ama|Ama]]** cut off a head — it grew back. **[[Vya Arleschia|Vya]]** fired arrow after arrow — none of it mattered. The creature was not a living thing. It was a **grief made manifest**.

**[[HVS]]** had been preparing. He drew **[[Swaith — The Soul-Thirsting Blade|Swaith]]** and stabbed himself, feeding the blade a Hit Die. Then he drove it into the creature's heart.

Swaith **released**.

The trapped souls poured out — and for a moment, the many-headed thing was not a monster.

It was a man.

A chubby human with wavy black hair, wearing purple and red clothing embroidered with gold. A lute in his hands. Grey, glowing eyes.

He looked at the party with something like relief.

> *"Thank you. I was… looking for the ending of the song."*

His name was **[[Sam Hurls]]**. One of the **[[The Initial Six|Initial Six]]**.

He did not have long. He began to say something else — something urgent — and then an arrow flew from the dark.

He fell.

**[[Accarus Grin|Accarus]]** stepped out of the shadows.

## Part 9: The Offer

He was an elf with a permanent smile — the **Grin curse** — and a calm, unreadable face.

> *"You did well. Better than I expected. But you are wasting your time with the Keeper of Secret. Mira Nouveau is a child playing at history. The Will of Grin has been fighting the real war for 500 years."*

He offered them a choice.

Join **[[Will of Grin]]** — fight for the truth, for the Grin, for the forgotten. Or stay with **[[Keeper of Secret]]** — and stay blind.

He gave them **seven days** to decide.

Then he walked into the dark and was gone.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- **Echo Sam was the first Echo the party met.** There are five more: Atreus, Olaf, Atlan, Gzirmo, and Alejandro.
- **Echo Sam was not evil.** He was **hungry** — an Echo without **[[Safayer]]**'s purification becomes a starving thing. The song was his unfinished melody, and it consumed minds the way his own mind had been consumed by the ritual.
- **Agus was not a traitor.** He was compelled by Echo Sam's song to kill **[[Bud-eg]]** — Echo Sam wanted to prevent the party from learning the truth. Agus was eaten that same night. His body was never found. He may return as an Echo in Season 3.
- **Bud-eg knew.** He was deaf — immune to the song — and had been watching Echo Sam for years. His journal (in his hut) contains everything: the song's patterns, how to see through it, how to survive it.
- **Accarus killed Echo Sam** to prevent the party from learning too much. Echo Sam was about to reveal the **betrayal of the Initial Six** — and Accarus is not ready for them to know yet.
- **Cindy is the only survivor of the Mektol family.** She does not remember her parents. Echo Sam's song erased them from her mind. She is now under the care of **[[Guo Lao]]** and the **[[The Guo Family|Guo family]]** in Gudameng.
- **The Gruffgud War is real** — and its true cause is not fried rice vs. fried noodles. It was engineered by **the Commonwealth** to hide the excavation at **[[Gunung Rayu]]**. The party does not know this yet. **[[Gordon]]** knew. It killed him.
- **The seven-day decision** is the first branching point of the campaign. Whichever faction the party chooses shapes Season 2. They will choose **Will of Grin** (per Session 4).
- **Session 03 stakes:** The party learns that memory can be weaponized, that the dead do not always stay dead, and that the world has more secrets than they can carry.
