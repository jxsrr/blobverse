> [!info] At a Glance
> **Part of:** West Harka
> **Status:** Destroyed — sunk into the sea (Session 01)

## What Mattered Here
- Ruined castle. Crumbling stone. Six-pointed star banner.
- Goblin-held. King **Grolnok** ruled here.
- Hidden sea cave with a carving from **Lei Alejandro**:
  > *"The star has fallen. Six have left us. We hid the key of the sea lord's legacy here, beneath forgotten watch."*
- The **Green Emerald (Emelanica)** was hidden here in an anti-magic chest.

## What Happened
- The party infiltrated, fought Grolnok, took the gem.
- The castle collapsed into the sea.
- The party nearly drowned. Rescued by [[Guanik Smith|Guanik]].

## Current State
- Gone. Underwater. Only the sea cave may remain accessible.


%% DM SECRETS %%
- The castle was already unstable before the party arrived. Grolnok had been planning to demolish it himself.
- The sea cave carving is still intact. It can be re-read.
- There are other carvings in other goblin ruins. Each one points to a Gemheart.
