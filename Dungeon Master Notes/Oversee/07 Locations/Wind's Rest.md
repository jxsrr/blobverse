> [!info] At a Glance
> **Part of:** South Harka
> **Notable People:** Gunnar Guirela (deceased), Olaf Guirela (from here)
> **Status:** Small fishing village. Quiet.

## What Matters Here
- A small fishing village on the southern coast of Harka.
- **Gunnar Guirela** lived here. He was Olaf's grandfather.
- Gunnar found **Gustaero** in his fishing net. He hid it in his attic.
- The village is unchanged. It is a place of origin, not a destination.

## What Happened Here
- Gunnar found Gustaero. He gave it to Olaf. Olaf gave it to Gordon. Gordon gave it to Husgus.
- The party has not visited Wind's Rest yet.

## Current State
- Quiet. The villagers do not know the significance of their village.


%% DM SECRETS %%
- The village has a **shrine to Lei the Great** — not a Valla temple, but a small stone marker. It was built by Gunnar after he found Gustaero.
- The old lighthouse is still standing. It is where Gunnar first saw the bow.
- If the party visits, an old woman (Gunnar's daughter, Olaf's aunt) will recognize Husgus as "a friend of Olaf's."
- She will give the party a **tattered map** — Gunnar's old sea chart. It marks the location of the **Wind Silo** in Ghuffghuff.
