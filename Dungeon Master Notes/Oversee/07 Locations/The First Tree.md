## What Matters Here
- A **colossal dead tree**. Scaly bark. No leaves. Trunk wider than a castle.
- **Not a tree** — it is a **petrified root** of one of the **First Trees**. It predates all recorded history. It is from the **Inbu era**.
- It was **drained of life** 10,000 years ago. Something beneath it fed on it. No one knows what.
- The **entrance to Arujaya** is hidden in its roots.
- The elevator and monorail station were built into the roots.
- **A second First Tree** — a living one — is hidden in the deep jungle of Elis. It is the last living First Tree in Oversee. It is guarded by **Verdantus**, the Green Dragon.

- The party found the First Tree and descended into Arujaya ([[Session 06 — Ethical Choices|Session 06]]).
- [[Lei Livia Vane|Livia]] amputated Ama's arm here.

### The First Tree — What It Really Is
- The **First Tree** in the deep jungle of Elis is **one of the First Trees** — the primordial trees created by the Architect when it made Oversee. The Inbu were born from their fruit. The First Trees were the **lungs of the world** — living filters that kept the world stable.
- This tree is **dead**. It has been dead for **10,000 years**. It was drained of life — not by the Shell, not by the Overseer, but by **something else**. No one knows what. The Cycle Clan believed it was a **Gap-touched creature** that fed on the tree's Ambitio-Ether. The tree died, and the creature disappeared.
- **The roots of this tree became the foundation of Arujaya.** The Tinkers built their city in the hollows beneath the roots. They used the tree's residual Ambitio-Ether to power their first machines. The tree did not die in vain — it became the lungs of a new city.
- **The tree is not the Shell's root system.** The Shell is a **20-kilometer humanoid skin** that fell from the sky. It has no connection to the First Trees. The Shell is shaped like an **Inbu** — because the Architect's original body was Inbu-like. The First Trees are older. They came before the Shell. They are not the same thing.
- **The tree is not connected to the Inbu's scattering.** The Inbu were scattered by the Architect — but not by this tree. The tree is just a tree. It is a dead tree. It is a memory of the world before the Rapture.

### The Second First Tree
- **There is another First Tree.** It is **alive**. It is hidden in the **deep jungle of Elis**. It is the **last living First Tree** in Oversee.
- It is guarded by **Verdantus** — the Green Dragon, the guardian of the last primordial forest. Verdantus has slept beneath its roots for thousands of years. He will wake if the tree is threatened.
- **The tree bears fruit.** The fruit is the **same fruit that birthed the Inbu** — the crystallized Ambitio-Ether that sparked the first consciousness. If the party eats the fruit, they will gain a **permanent +1 to one ability score** (DM's choice). If they plant the fruit, they can grow a **new First Tree** — a new lung for the world.
- **Verdantus will not give the fruit freely.** He will test the party first. He will ask them: *"Why do you want to eat the fruit of the first tree? To become stronger? Or to become something more?"*
- If the party answers truthfully — if they say they want to save the world — Verdantus will give them the fruit.
- If they lie, Verdantus will attack. He is CR 17. He is a green dragon. He is not a joke.

### The Shell — What It Really Is
- The **Shell** in the sky is a **20-kilometer humanoid skin** — a dead, hollow molting of the Architect's original body.
- **It is shaped like an Inbu** — because the Architect's original body was Inbu-like. The Inbu were created in the Architect's image. The Shell is a giant, empty copy of the first race.
- **The Shell is not connected to the First Trees.** The First Trees are from the Inbu era. The Shell is from the Architect's own body. They are different things.
- **The Shell fell at Danau Kuno** — now Universitas Tarakita. The crater that became the lake was formed by its descent. The Shell in the sky is the main body. The thing that fell at Danau Kuno was a **piece** of it.

### The Drained Tree — What Fed on It
- **The Cycle Clan never found out what drained the First Tree.** They believed it was a **Gap-touched creature** — something that slipped through the Gap and fed on the tree's Ambitio-Ether.
- **The Tinkers believed it was the Shell.** They thought the Shell fell on the tree and drained it. They were wrong. The Shell fell at Danau Kuno. The tree is in Elis. They are not the same place.
- **The truth is open.** The DM can decide what drained the tree. It may have been a Gap-touched creature. It may have been a **Cycle Clan experiment gone wrong**. It may have been the **first Shell fragment** — a piece of the Shell that fell in Elis, not at Danau Kuno, and drained the tree before it died. The DM should choose what fits the campaign.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- The First Tree in Elis is **one of the First Trees** — the primordial trees from the Inbu era.
- It has **no connection to the Shell**. The Shell is Inbu-shaped, but it is not a First Tree.
- **There is a second First Tree** — alive, hidden in the deep jungle of Elis, guarded by Verdantus.
- **The fruit of the second First Tree** can grant a permanent +1 to one ability score, or be planted to grow a new First Tree.
- **Verdantus** guards the second tree. He is CR 17. He will test the party before giving them the fruit.
- **Something drained the First Tree 10,000 years ago.** The Cycle Clan never found out what. The DM decides.
