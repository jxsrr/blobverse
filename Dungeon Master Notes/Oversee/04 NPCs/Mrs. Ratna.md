> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** The "merchant wife" the party escorted to Padre City. A Keeper of Secret courier.
> **Where:** Javan Town (travels frequently)
> **Status:** Alive

## What the Party Knows
- The wife of **Pak Andreas** the blacksmith. Hired the party to guard her on the road to Padre City in Session 4.
- Paid 200 gp. Was cheerful, sharp, and quiet in equal measure.
- Carried more goods than a merchant should — crates, sealed boxes, careful to always sit beside them.
- Left the party at the Festival. Was not seen again.

## What the Party Remembers
- She argued with the Commonwealth soldiers **without fear** — a woman who knew she was untouchable.
- She watched the crowd carefully during the parade. Not like a merchant — like a **watcher**.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Ibu Ratna is a **Keeper of Secret courier**. She carries sealed messages between cities. The crates she carries are **not goods** — they are the sealed archives of the Keeper of Secret.
- **Pak Andreas knows.** He does not approve. But he loves her.
- **She was carrying a message to Mira** — from one of the other Ciphers — when the party escorted her. The message is still in Kota Juto.
- She reports directly to **Mira**.
- **Her role in Season 3:** She can become the party's **contact** inside the Keeper of Secret. She can deliver messages to Mira when the party is on the run.
- **Her husband will be sad if she dies.** Pak Andreas will turn against the party if they fail her.
- **Her true name:** Her full name is **Ratna Andreas** — she took her husband's name when they married. Before that, she was **Ratna Vek** — a minor noble from Padre City who defected to the Keeper of Secret 15 years ago.
