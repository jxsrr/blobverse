> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Bartender at The Tipsy Trident.
> **Where:** Yawning Town
> **Status:** Alive

## What the Party Knows
- A friendly, talkative bartender.
- Knows **[[Mr. Poise]]** personally. Recommends him for transport.
- Knows the local rumors. Can point the party to the Guild, the temple, or the market.
- Does not fight. Does not travel. Just pours drinks.

## What He Offers
- **Rumors.** For a few silver, he'll tell you what's happening in Yawning Town.
- **Contacts.** He knows everyone in town. He can introduce you to Senta, [[Grilnax]], or [[Mr. Poise]].
- **A safe place to sleep.** The Tipsy Trident has rooms for rent (5 sp/night).

## Current Goal
Keep the bar running. Stay out of trouble.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Samual is not just a bartender. He is a **retired adventurer**. He was a rogue in his youth. He has a **hidden crossbow** under the bar.
- He knows about the **Keeper of Secret** and the **Will of Grin**. He has contacts in both organizations. He will not share this unless the party asks directly.
- He is the one who first told [[Grilnax]] about the party. He thought they looked "interesting."
- His real name is **Samual Dusk**. He is a former member of the Yawning Guild. He left after a mission went wrong.
