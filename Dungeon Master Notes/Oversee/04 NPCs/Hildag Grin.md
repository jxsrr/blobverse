### Who Hildag Really Is
- Hildag Grin is **older than she looks**. She is a Grin — a bloodline that ages slowly. She is **over 500 years old**. She was alive at the time of the Initial Six (200 AV).
- She was the **Wakil Pertahanan** of the One Voice Commonwealth at the time of the Initial Six. She served alongside **Geldfre** (Kanselir Agung) and **Erard Gelnne** (Wakil Keadilan).
- She was the **last Wakil Pertahanan who remembered the original promise** — the promise Guanik Wiseman made to the Architect. She believed in the search for the shepherd. She believed in the Commonwealth.
- When the Initial Six disappeared (200 AV), she **defected**. She left the Commonwealth. She founded **Will of Grin** to find the truth and fight the corruption.
- She is **over 500 years old**. She has been fighting for centuries. She has seen everything.

### Hildag's Children — The Twins
- Hildag is the **mother of Accarus and Antrem** — twin sons she had with a **Lei Firage descendant**. They are the **only surviving twins** of the Klan Lei's twin-killing tradition. She hid them. She protected them. They were never registered. They were never separated.
- **She did not have them by accident.** She bred them. She studied the twin tradition for decades. She learned that the **intelligent twin** had the **least magic**, and the **unintelligent twin** had the **most magic**. She wanted to see if she could produce a child with both — or if she could produce a **new** kind of heir.
- **Accarus and Antrem were the accidental success.** They survived. They were not killed. They were her proof of concept.

### Hildag's Experiments
- Hildag spent decades trying to breed a child who was both **anti-magic** (invisible to the Overseer) and **Lei-blooded** (carrying the pure elemental power of Simon's lineage).
- She experimented with different Lei bloodlines. She experimented with different Grin families. **Geneva was one of her experiments** — a Grin woman from a smaller, less influential bloodline, bred to see if a specific combination would produce a True Heir. Geneva did not know she was an experiment. She still does not know.
- **Every attempt produced a child who was one or the other — never both.** The Grin blood and the Lei blood refused to coexist. They canceled each other out. The children were either anti-magic or magical. Never both.
- **Until the Arleschia family.** Hildag read about the **Arleschia family** of Elis — a minor noble family with a strange bloodline. The Arleschias carried a **fragment of the earth element** — not enough to be mages, but enough to **stabilize** other bloodlines. The Arleschia blood acted as a **bridge** — it allowed the Lei and Grin blood to coexist without canceling each other out.
- **Hildag realized the Arleschias were the key.** She needed a child who was **Grin** (from her line), **Lei Firage** (from the father), and **Arleschia** (from the mother). That child would be the first **True Heir** — a being who carried both anti-magic and pure elemental power.
- **Vya is that child.** She is **Grin-Lei-Arleschia**. She is Hildag's magnum opus. She is the culmination of decades of research. She is the future of the Grin.

### The Twins — Why They Survived
- Hildag hid Accarus and Antrem from the Klan Lei. She did not kill them. She did not let anyone know they existed.
- **Why?** Because she had learned the twin rule — that twins split their power. She wanted to see if she could **reunite** the power without killing one of them. She wanted to see if there was another way.
- **She failed.** Accarus and Antrem are twins. Their power is split. They are not True Heirs. They are not Vya. They are her **rough drafts** — her proof that the twin tradition could be subverted, but not perfected.
- **Jarko** — Accarus and Geneva's son — was not the True Heir. He was **Grin-Grin-Lei**. He did not have the Arleschia blood. His Grin and Lei blood canceled each other out. He was a Grin warrior — not a True Heir. Hildag knew this from the moment he was born. She used him as a decoy.

### Hildag and Vya — The Magnum Opus
- Vya is Hildag's **magnum opus** — the final refinement of decades of experiments. She bred Vya after Jarko's death, combining her own Grin anti-magic with a Lei Firage descendant's dormant fire and an Arleschia noble's stabilizing blood.
- **Vya is the only Lei descendant with both** the fire of Lei Firage and the anti-magic protection of the Grin. She is the only one who can break the Grin curse — or strengthen it.
- **Hildag loves Vya.** Not in the way a grandmother loves a granddaughter — in the way a sculptor loves a statue. Vya is her masterpiece. Vya is the future of the Grin.
- **Hildag will do anything to protect Vya.** She will lie to her. She will manipulate her. She will sacrifice anyone — including Jarko, including Accarus, including Antrem — to keep Vya safe. She has already done it. She will do it again.

### Hildag and Geneva — The Experiment
- **Geneva is not Hildag's daughter.** She is a Grin woman from a different family — a smaller, less influential bloodline. Hildag **bred her** as part of her experiments — she wanted to see if a specific combination of Grin and Lei blood would produce a True Heir.
- **Geneva does not know she was an experiment.** She thinks she is just a Grin woman who married into the leadership. She thinks Hildag is her mother-in-law. She does not know Hildag studied her. She does not know Hildag **used her** to test her theories.
- **If Geneva ever learns the truth**, she will break. She will ask: *"Was any of it real?"* Hildag will answer: *"You were an experiment. But your son was real. Your love for him was real. That is enough."*

### Hildag's Role in Season 3
- If the party learns about Hildag's role as Wakil Pertahanan, they will have to decide: is she an ally or an enemy? She is both. She is a mother. She is a murderer. She is a strategist. She is a pawn. She is a queen.
- If the party confronts Hildag about Jarko's death, she will not deny it. She will say: *"I did what I had to do. You would have done the same."* She will not apologize. She will not weep. She will not show emotion. She is the Weaver.
- If the party confronts Hildag about Geneva, she will not deny it. She will say: *"Geneva was an experiment. But she gave me Jarko. For that, I am grateful."* She will not apologize.
- **Hildag's fate is in the party's hands.** They can kill her. They can forgive her. They can leave her. The choice is theirs.
