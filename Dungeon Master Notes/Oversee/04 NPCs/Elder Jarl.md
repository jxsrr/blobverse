### Who Elder Jarl Is
- **Elder Jarl** is the oldest dragonborn in Frostheart. His scales are nearly white and translucent — the color of ice that has been frozen for thousands of years. His horns spiral like a ram's. His voice is a whisper.
- He has led the Council of Elders for **centuries**. He is not a warrior. He is not a mage. He is a keeper of tradition — a man who has spent his whole life preserving the rules, the rituals, the stories of Klan Frostscale.
- He is **not guilty** of the original crime. He did not help Lei Watigi. He did not weaken Vythrax. He did not create the Breathstone lie. He inherited the lie. He believed it. He worshipped the egg. He did not know what it was.
- But he **feels guilty**. He was the leader. He was responsible. He should have known. He should have questioned the Breathstone. He should have asked why the egg never hatched. He should have done something.
- He has dedicated his remaining years to making it right.

### The Secret He Carried
- Elder Jarl knew about the assault on Vythrax. Not from the beginning — he learned the truth years later, from a dying priest who confessed on his deathbed. The priest told him everything: the conspiracy, the rituals, the potions, the rape.
- He did not tell anyone. He was afraid. He was ashamed. He did not know what to do with the truth. He buried it. He carried it alone for decades.
- When Syl hatched — when the egg cracked and a child emerged — Elder Jarl knew what he had to do. He led the ritual to hide Syl's identity. He told the clan the child was the Breathstone's "gift." He told the adoptive mother to raise him in secrecy. He told the priests to spread rumors of "anak haram" to divert attention.
- He did it to protect Syl. He did it to protect the clan. He did it to protect Vythrax's honor. He did not know if it was the right choice. He still does not know.
- **He has never forgiven himself.** He was the one who hid the truth. He was the one who let Syl grow up hated. He was the one who let the clan worship a lie.

### The Truth Comes Out — Session 05
- In Session 05, the party arrived at Frostheart. They found the Breathstone — cracked. They found the mural — Syl's name missing. They found the truth.
- Elder Jarl did not deny it. He did not fight. He did not run. He stood before the party and told them everything: the conspiracy, the rituals, the potions, the rape. He told them about the cover-up. He told them about the lie.
- He wept. He is old. He is tired. He has been carrying this for decades. He could not carry it anymore.
- When Vythrax was healed — when Syl fed her his blood and the silver dragon returned — Elder Jarl bowed. He bowed to Syl. He bowed to his new heir. He bowed to the son of his deity.
- **The clan followed.** They did not understand — not yet — but they followed their elder. They carved Syl's name on the family tree. They accepted him as the heir.

### Elder Jarl's Current State
- Elder Jarl is **dying**. He is old — older than any dragonborn in Frostheart. He has lived for centuries. His body is failing. His scales are fading. His voice is barely a whisper.
- He has **months**, perhaps a year. He will not survive the campaign. He knows this. He has made his peace with it.
- He has **named Syl as his successor**. He has stepped down from the Council of Elders. He is trying to prepare Syl for leadership — but Syl does not want the role. Syl does not know how to lead. Syl is learning.
- He is **trying to heal the clan**. The clan is divided — the Faithful, the Betrayed, and the Heirs. Elder Jarl is trying to mediate. He is trying to tell the truth — not all at once, but piece by piece. He is trying to prepare the clan for a future without Vythrax.
- **He will not succeed fully.** He will die before the clan is healed. But he will do what he can. He will die knowing he tried.

### Elder Jarl and Syl
- Elder Jarl loves Syl. Not in the way a father loves a son — in the way a shepherd loves a lamb that was lost and found. Syl is the son of Vythrax. Syl is the future of the clan. Syl is the hope Elder Jarl has been waiting for.
- **Elder Jarl is the one who named Syl as his successor.** He did it after Session 05 — after the truth came out, after Vythrax was healed, after the clan bowed. He did it because he believed Syl was the only one who could lead the clan into the future.
- **Syl does not want the role.** He does not know how to lead. He is a sorcerer, a healer, a young man with a dying mother and a blood that is killing him. He is not a leader. Elder Jarl knows this. He is trying to teach him anyway.
- **Elder Jarl's last words to Syl** will be: *"You do not have to be perfect. You only have to be present. The clan will follow you if you let them."*

### Elder Jarl and Vythrax
- Elder Jarl has worshipped Vythrax his whole life. He believed she was a deity. He believed she was the Silver Wind, the protector of the mountain, the mother of the ice. He believed she was perfect.
- He was wrong. She is not a deity. She is a dragon. She is a mother. She is a victim. She is dying.
- **Elder Jarl does not know Vythrax is dying.** He thinks she is recovering. He thinks the healing worked. He will learn the truth in Season 3. He will break. He will weep. He will ask: *"Why didn't she tell me?"* The answer will be: *"Because she wanted you to be happy. Because she wanted you to be free."*

### Elder Jarl's Role in Season 3
- **Session 8–10** — The party learns about the clan's division. They learn about Elder Jarl's dying state. They learn about his attempts to heal the clan.
- **Session 11** — The party must help Elder Jarl heal the clan. They must mediate between the Faithful, the Betrayed, and the Heirs. They must help Syl prepare for leadership.
- **Session 11** — Elder Jarl dies. He dies peacefully. He dies knowing he tried. He dies knowing he named the right successor. He dies knowing the clan will survive.
- **Endgame** — The party must help Syl lead the clan. They must help him heal the fracture. They must help him prepare for the future.
