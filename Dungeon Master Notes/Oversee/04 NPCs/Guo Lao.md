## What the Party Knows
- The elder of the Guo Family — the cooks who guard the **Recipe Library** in the Legendary Noodle Temple in Gudameng.
- **Took in Cindy** after the Ghuffghuff tragedy. Raised her as his own.
- Has not met the party directly — but has heard about them from Cindy.

## What the Party Has Heard
- Cindy speaks of him fondly: *"Grandpa Guo says the party are always welcome. He says the ones who save children are the ones the gods smile on."*
- He is respected in Gudameng. Neutral in politics. Does not take sides.
- Runs the best noodle stall in the village.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Guo Lao is **not just a cook**. He is the **last living descendant of the first Cycle Clan cooks** — the ones who prepared ritual meals for the Valla.
- He knows about the **Cycle Clan**. He knows about the **Valla**. He knows the **Ritual of Replacement**. His family has kept the secret for 1,000 years.
- **The Recipe Library contains a hidden section** — recipes for **ritual food** that can heal souls, weaken the Overseer, and bind the Gap.
- **He is waiting.** He has been waiting his whole life for someone to ask the right question. If the party asks him about the "first cooks" or "the ones who served the Valla," he will reveal the truth.
- **What he will give them:** a **Ritual Recipe** — a dish cooked for the final battle. It will give the party **advantage on all saves for 1 hour** when eaten before combat.
- **His motive:** he is old. He wants the secret to pass to the next generation. He hopes it will be Cindy, but he knows it might be the party.
- **His line:** *"My family cooked for the first Valla. My family cooked for the last. And if the gods are just, my family will cook for the one who ends this."*
- **His role in Season 3:** He will become the party's **cook and counsel**. He cannot fight, but he can feed them, hide them, and tell them what they need to know.
- **If Cindy dies:** Guo Lao will **never cook again**. He will close the temple. He will walk into the mountains and not return.
