- Now runs a small food stall in Gudameng.

- **A place to rest** in Gudameng.

Master the fried rice recipe. Open a restaurant.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Memeng is not just a cook. He is a **former adventurer**. He retired after losing his leg to a trap. He uses a peg leg now.
- He knows the **Gruffgud War** better than most. He fought in it. He lost friends on both sides.
- He has a **hidden recipe book** in his stall. It contains recipes for magical food — healing soups, strength stews, etc. He will share them with the party if they help him find a rare ingredient.
- The ingredient is **Jestnut flour** from the Lembah Jestnut. He does not know the valley is infected.
