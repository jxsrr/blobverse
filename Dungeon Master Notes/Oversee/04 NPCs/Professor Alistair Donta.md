> [!info] At a Glance
> **Role:** Rector of Lyceum University. Oldest priest of Valla in Oversee.
> **Where:** Lyceum University of Arcane Arts, Padre City
> **Status:** Deceased

## What the Party Knows
- Old, wise, visibly tired. Had seen too much.
- Showed the party the only known painting of **The Initial Six**.
- Identified the green gem as **Emelanica**.
- Directed them to **Professor Howdy**.
- Became visibly shaken when the party returned from Howdy's house.
- Told them Howdy had died of a heart attack.
- **Committed suicide in front of the party** after being accused of covering up Howdy's murder.
- Revived by HVS with Swaith for 24 hours to clear their names.

## Legacy
- His death was the party's first "we broke something we can't fix."
- His research on the Initial Six was suppressed by the Commonwealth.
