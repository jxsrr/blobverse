## Living Form — The Bard
- One of the **Initial Six**. A chubby human man with wavy black hair, purple and red clothing embroidered with gold.
- Carried a lute. Played music that could open doors between dimensions.
- Collected stories from every corner of Oversee. Never finished his last song.
- Was **betrayed by Lei Alejandro** in 200 AV. His soul was fed to the Valla.
- Without **Safayer** (the blue Gemheart), his soul was not purified. He became an **Echo**.

## Echo Form — The Many-Headed Singer
- A pale, bloated creature. Multiple human heads rotate around a central mass. Each head is a fragment of someone Sam met in his travels.
- They all sing the same unfinished melody.
- Lured villagers of Ghuffghuff into an illusory world. Drained them dry.
- **HVS stabbed him with Swaith.** Released the trapped souls. He briefly returned to his true form.
- Said only a few words before **[[Accarus Grin|Accarus]] shot him with an arrow**.
- His body crumbled. His Echo returned to Simon.

## Personality
- Warm. Theatrical. Loves music.
- Deeply sad. His last words were an apology.
- Wanted to finish his song.

### Who Sam Is
- **Sam Hurls** was the **bard of the Initial Six** — a chubby human man with wavy black hair, purple and red clothing embroidered with gold, and a lute that never left his hands. He was not a warrior. He was not a mage. He was a **storyteller** — a collector of songs, a keeper of memories, a man who believed that the world was made of stories and that stories were worth dying for.
- He was **warm**. He was **theatrical**. He was **kind**. He was the one who kept the others laughing when the road was dark. He was the one who sang to them when they were afraid. He was the one who reminded them that there was more to life than fighting and dying — there was also music, and laughter, and the sound of rain on a roof.
- He was **not a fool**. He knew the world was cruel. He knew the world was broken. He knew that the Six were walking into something dangerous. But he believed that the stories were worth it. He believed that the songs were worth it. He believed that if he could just finish his last song, everything would be okay.
- **He never finished it.** He died at the altar, betrayed by Alejandro, his soul fed to Simon. His last song was never completed. It became an echo — a fragment of melody that haunted Ghuffghuff for centuries.

### Sam and Olaf
- **Olaf Guirela** was Sam's closest friend in the Initial Six. They were opposites — Olaf was gruff, Sam was warm. Olaf was practical, Sam was theatrical. Olaf was an atheist, Sam believed in the power of stories (which is a kind of faith).
- **They traveled together before the Six was formed.** They met Gordon together. They trained him together. They gave him the bow (Olaf) and the melody (Sam).
- **Sam taught Gordon the melody** — the one that would later call Echo Sam to Ghuffghuff. He told Gordon: *"If you ever find yourself in trouble, sing this melody. Maybe someone will hear. Maybe I will hear. Maybe the world will hear."*
- **Gordon sang it every night.** He passed it to the villages. The children of Ghuffghuff and Gudameng sing it to this day. They do not know what it does. They do not know it calls the dead.
- **Olaf and Sam were separated by the ritual.** They died together. They became Echoes together. But their Echoes went to different places. Olaf waited near Gordon's grave. Sam haunted Ghuffghuff.

### The Ritual — What Sam Knew
- Sam was **uneasy from the start**. He did not trust **Lei Alejandro**. He did not trust the ritual. He did not trust the "vortex of energy" that Alejandro was summoning.
- **He heard the notes.** Not the notes of his lute — the notes of the ritual. He heard them. They were wrong. They were not a fanfare of victory. They were a **requiem**. They were a song of death, not a song of life.
- **He warned the others.** He told them something was wrong. He told them the music was wrong. He told them they were walking into a trap.
- **No one listened.** The ritual was already in motion. The circle was already closed. The trap was already sprung.
- **He died playing.** He did not go quietly. He did not accept his fate. He played his lute until the strings broke. His final words — recorded in the ritual memory — were: *"A sour note of betrayal... a bad climax, Lei. You cut the music right before the best part."*
- **He was right about everything.** He was the second one to see through Alejandro. He was the second one to try to stop it. He was the second one to die fighting.

### Echo Sam — The Many-Headed Singer
- **Echo Sam** was the first Echo the party met. He haunted **Ghuffghuff** for centuries. He was not evil. He was not malicious. He was **hungry** — an Echo without Safayer's purification is a starving thing.
- **His form was horrifying.** A pale, bloated creature with multiple human heads rotating around a central mass. Each head was a fragment of someone Sam had met in his travels. Each head sang the same unfinished melody.
- **He lured villagers into an illusory world.** He did not mean to kill them. He did not mean to drain them. He was just trying to finish his song. He was just trying to find someone who could play the final note.
- **He killed Yahanda.** He killed Faska Mektol. He erased them from the memories of everyone who knew them. He did not mean to. He was just trying to find the ending. He was just trying to finish his song.
- **HVS stabbed him with Swaith.** The blade released the trapped souls. The many-headed creature dissolved. For a moment — just a moment — Sam returned to his true form.
- **He was chubby. He had wavy black hair. He wore purple and red. He held a lute.** He looked at the party with something like relief. He said: *"Thank you. I was... looking for the ending of the song."*
- **He did not have long.** He began to say something else — something urgent — and then **Accarus Grin** shot him with an arrow. He fell. His body crumbled. His Echo returned to Simon.
- **Accarus killed him to prevent the party from learning too much.** Echo Sam was about to reveal the truth about the Initial Six — the betrayal, the sacrifice, the murder. Accarus was not ready for the party to know.

### The Melody — The Key
- **The melody is a key.** It is not a magic item. It is not a relic. It is a **song** — a specific frequency that can attract Echoes.
- **Sam taught it to Gordon.** Gordon sang it every night. The children of Ghuffghuff sang it without knowing what it did. They were calling Echo Sam without realizing it.
- **The melody can be used to find other Echoes.** If the party ever wants to find Echo Atreus, Echo Olaf, Echo Atlan, or Echo Gzirmo, they can sing the melody in a place that resonates with that Echo. It may work. It may not. The melody is a key, but not every door is the same.
- **The melody can also be used to finish Sam's song.** If the party ever finds Sam's original lute — in the ruins of the altar, or in the Bahtera Bawah Tanah — they can play the melody and finish what he started. The song will be complete. Echo Sam will find peace. His Echo will finally rest.

### Sam's Connection to the Swan Trio
- Sam did not carry any of the Swan relics. He was not connected to the Swan Trio. He was connected to **the story** — the song, the melody, the echo that would one day call the party to the truth.
- **His melody is a key.** It is not a magic item. It is not a relic. It is a **song** — a specific frequency that can attract Echoes.
