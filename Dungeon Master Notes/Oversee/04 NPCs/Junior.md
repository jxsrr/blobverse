> **Role:** Bartender at The Salty Gill.
> **Where:** Javan Town Docks

## What the Party Knows
- A young man. Quick with a joke. Quicker with a knife.
- Knows the docks. Knows the smugglers. Knows the Commonwealth.
- Will tell you: *"People with weird smiles (Will of Grin) often smuggle weapons through here."*
- Has a **grudge** against the Commonwealth. They killed his father.

## What He Offers
- **Rumors** about the docks.
- **A safe place to hide** (the back room of the bar).
- **A contact** with Sasimo (if the party needs it).

## Current Goal
Survive. Avenge his father.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Junior's father was a dockworker who was killed by the Commonwealth for witnessing a smuggling operation. Junior saw it happen.
- He is a **Keeper of Secret informant**. He reports to [[Mira Nouveau|Mira]].
- He has a **hidden crossbow** under the bar. He is a good shot.
- He wants to join the party. He is too young. If they take him, he will be a liability — but also a loyal friend.
- His real name is **Juniper**. He goes by Junior because his father did.
