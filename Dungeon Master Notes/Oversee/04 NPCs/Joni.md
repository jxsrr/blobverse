> **Role:** Ama's adoptive father. Innkeeper at The Golden Sheaf.
> **Where:** Nesia Town

## What the Party Knows
- Farmer in **Nesia Town**. Adopted **Zure Ama** after he arrived in Oversee 6 years ago.
- Taught Ama to farm. Gave him a home. Loved him like a son.
- Owns **The Golden Sheaf** — an inn in Nesia.
- Married to **Siti**.

## What Happened
- In Session 6, Ama returned to Nesia seeking shelter.
- **Joni called the Commonwealth.** He saw Ama as a "terrorist" — brainwashed by Commonwealth propaganda.
- Held Ama in a controlling hug while waiting for soldiers to arrive.
- The party escaped. Joni was left behind.

## Current State
- Still runs The Golden Sheaf. Under Commonwealth surveillance.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Joni genuinely believes Ama is a terrorist. He is not evil — he is **scared**.
- His loyalty was bought with promises of safety. The Commonwealth threatened his village.
- If the party ever confronts him, he will break down. He will say: *"I didn't know what else to do. They said they'd burn Nesia."*
- He has a **hidden compartment** under the inn bar. It contains Ama's childhood belongings — a wooden toy, a letter Joni never sent, and a photo of the three of them.
- Ama's redemption arc in Season 3 may involve forgiving Joni.
