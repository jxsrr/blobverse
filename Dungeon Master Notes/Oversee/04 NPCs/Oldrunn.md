### Who Oldrunn Is
- **Oldrunn** is the cabbage merchant of Frostheart. She has been selling cabbages for decades — since before Syl hatched. She is the woman who raised the son of Vythrax.
- She is **old** — older than most dragonborn in Frostheart. Her scales are faded. Her horns are worn. Her back is bent from years of hauling cabbages. She does not look like a hero. She does not look like a mother of legends. She looks like a cabbage merchant.
- She is **infertile**. She could not have eggs of her own. She had always wanted a child. The elders knew this. They used it. They gave her a child they could not explain — a child they told her was "special."
- She did not know Syl was Vythrax's son. She did not know about the assault. She did not know about the lie. She only knew she had a child. She only knew she loved him.
- **She raised Syl with love.** She fed him. She clothed him. She taught him to read the ice, to respect the mountain, to be kind. She did not teach him magic — she did not know how. She did not teach him to fight — she was not a warrior. She taught him to be a person.
- **She cried every night for years after Syl was exiled.** She believed she had failed him. She believed she had not protected him. She believed she was not enough.
- **She is still in Frostheart.** She is still selling cabbages. She is still waiting for her son to come home.

### The Name — Oldrunn
- **Oldrunn** is her name. It is the only name she has ever used. The clan calls her "the cabbage merchant" — not out of disrespect, but out of habit. She has been selling cabbages for so long that her name and her stall are the same thing.
- There is a **cook** in Frostheart also named **Oldrunn** — no relation. It is a coincidence. The clan distinguishes them by their trades: "Oldrunn the cook" and "Oldrunn the cabbage merchant." No one has ever been confused. No one has ever cared.
- **Syl calls her "Mother."** He has always called her "Mother." He will always call her "Mother." Her name is Oldrunn. Her role is mother. They are the same thing.

### The Adoption
- When the egg cracked — when Syl hatched — the elders panicked. They could not let anyone know the truth. They could not let anyone know the child was Vythrax's son. They needed a place to hide him.
- **Oldrunn was chosen.** She was infertile. She was kind. She was not important. She was perfect.
- The elders gave her the child. They told her he was "special." They told her to raise him in secrecy. They told her to keep him hidden. They told her she belonged to the clan, but no one must know he existed.
- **She accepted.** She did not ask questions. She did not need to. She had a child. She had a son. She had a family.
- She named him **Syl Frost**. She gave him her surname. She gave him her love. She gave him everything she had.
- **She never told him she was not his birth mother.** She never told him about the Breathstone. She never told him about the elders. She wanted him to have a normal childhood. She wanted him to be happy.
- **She failed.** The clan found out he was different. The rumors spread. The ostracism began. She could not protect him. She tried. She failed.

### The Exile
- When Syl was exiled — when the clan drove him out — Oldrunn was not allowed to go with him. The elders forbade it. They said he had to leave alone. They said it was the will of Vythrax.
- She watched him leave. She watched him walk into the snow. She watched him disappear. She did not know if she would ever see him again.
- **She cried for years.** She cried every night. She cried so much that the other villagers stopped asking if she was okay. They knew. They did not want to know.
- She kept selling cabbages. She kept living. She kept waiting. She did not know what else to do.

### The Reunion — Session 05
- In Session 05, Syl returned to Frostheart. He found the truth about his origin. He found Vythrax. He found his birth mother.
- **He also found Oldrunn.** She was at the market, selling cabbages, as she had always done. She saw him. She froze. She did not believe it was him.
- He approached her. He said: *"Mother."*
- She wept. She held him. She touched his face. She said: *"My little ice shard. You came back. You came back."*
- She did not ask where he had been. She did not ask what he had done. She did not ask why he had left. She did not care. She had her son back. That was enough.
- **She is proud of him.** He is the son of Vythrax. He is the heir to the clan. He is a hero. But to her, he is still her little ice shard. He is still the child she raised. He is still her son.

### Oldrunn and Syl
- Oldrunn loves Syl more than anything in the world. She raised him. She fed him. She clothed him. She taught him to be kind. She did not birth him — but she is his mother. In every way that matters.
- **Syl does not know she is not his birth mother.** He never knew. He never asked. He has always called her "Mother." He always will.
- **If Syl ever learns the truth** — that she is not his birth mother, that she was chosen by the elders to hide him — he will not love her any less. He will love her more. Because she chose him. Because she wanted him. Because she raised him when no one else would.
- **The reunion of Syl and Oldrunn is one of the most emotional moments of Session 05.** It is not the biggest reveal. It is not the most important plot point. But it is the most human. It is a mother and her son. It is a family.

### Oldrunn and Vythrax
- Oldrunn does not know Vythrax. She has never seen her. She has never spoken to her. She has worshipped her from a distance — as all Frostscale do — but she has never met her.
- **She does not know Vythrax is Syl's birth mother.** She does not know Vythrax is dying. She does not know the truth about the assault. She only knows Syl is special. She only knows he is the son of the dragon. She does not ask questions. She does not want to know.
- **If she ever met Vythrax**, she would not know what to say. She would not know what to do. She would simply bow. She would simply say: *"Thank you. Thank you for my son."*
- Vythrax would look at her. She would see the woman who raised her child. She would see the woman who loved him when she could not. She would say: *"No. Thank you. Thank you for raising my son."*

### Oldrunn's Role in Season 3
- Oldrunn is **not a fighter**. She is not a mage. She is not a leader. She is a cabbage merchant.
- But she is **the heart of Frostheart**. She is the one who kept the market running while the clan argued. She is the one who fed the hungry when the priests were praying. She is the one who kept her son alive.
- **If the party ever needs help in Frostheart**, Oldrunn will help them. She will feed them. She will shelter them. She will tell them stories. She will not ask for anything in return. She is a mother. She knows what it is like to worry about the people she loves.
- **If Vythrax dies**, Oldrunn will be one of the ones who weeps. She will weep for Syl. She will weep for the dragon she never met. She will weep for the family she never knew she was part of.
- **If Syl becomes the leader of the clan**, Oldrunn will be there. She will be at his side. She will be the voice of kindness. She will be the one who reminds him that leadership is not about power. It is about people. It is about cabbages.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Oldrunn is **not Syl's birth mother**. She was chosen by the elders to raise him. She was infertile. She was kind. She was perfect.
- She **does not know** about the assault. She does not know about the Breathstone lie. She only knows Syl is special. She only knows she loves him.
- She **cried every night for years** after Syl was exiled. She believed she had failed him.
- She **is proud of her son**. He is the son of Vythrax. He is the heir to the clan. He is a hero. But to her, he is still her little ice shard.
- She **will die before the campaign ends**. She is old. She is tired. She has lived a long life. She will not survive the final battle. But she will die knowing her son is safe. She will die knowing he is loved.

### Oldrunn's Last Words
- If Oldrunn dies — and she will — her last words to Syl will be:
  > *"You were never a burden. You were never a curse. You were my son. You were always my son. I love you, my little ice shard. I always will."*
- Syl will break. He will weep. He will hold her. He will say: *"I love you too, Mother. I love you too."*
- She will smile. She will close her eyes. She will be gone.
