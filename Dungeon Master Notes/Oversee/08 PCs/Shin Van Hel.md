> [!info] The Party
> **Class:** Warlock (curse-bound, not pact) / Paladin (multiclass)
> **Role:** Melee striker, soul-blade wielder
> **Known as:** HVS

## Appearance
Half-Elf, chronologically over 700 years old. Looks young. Fedora or Bloodborne-style hat, Sakamoto-style glasses, gloves always covering his hands. Modern, sharp dresser. The eternal smile of a Half-Elf — not a curse, just a trait. He cannot stop smiling. He has learned to live with it.

## Known Backstory
- Found as a child on the shore of Elis. No memory. No name. Only an eternal smile.
- Raised by **Helius**, an old Valla-worshipper. Helius taught him to read, to listen, to sit in silence. Helius never asked him to remove his gloves. Helius never asked him to stop smiling.
- Joined **The Grace**, a demon-hunter organization. Made friends: **Edra** and **Crest**. Edra was warm. Crest was disciplined. They did not care about his smile. They did not care about his gloves. They were his family.
- **690 AV — The Fall of The Grace.** A massive Gap tear opened in Elis. Demons poured through. The Grace fought. They held the line. They died.
- Shin was possessed by **Zargoyle**. He destroyed the order. He destroyed Elis. He woke up **690 years later** — in the present era — with no memory of what happened.
- The Curse Mark on his body glows and causes pain when similar entities are near. It is not a curse. It is a **seal** — a fragment of **Zargoth** trapped inside him.
- His eternal smile is not a curse — it is a Half-Elf trait. He has always smiled. He will always smile.

## Personality
- Trusts only himself. Skeptical of gods. He has seen too much to believe in anything he cannot touch.
- Secretly a bit clumsy and oblivious. His serious image breaks at unexpected moments.
- Recently began worshipping Valla and multiclassed into Paladin after being saved repeatedly. He does not know why he was saved. He does not know by whom. He is grateful anyway.

## Fate Card
**The Tower & The Reversed Fool** — wields **Swaith**, a soul-thirsting blade. The blade whispers. It binds him. Has a "Destiny Token": if he dies, he returns once with full HP.

### Who Shin Is
- Shin is **HVS-02** — a **Human Vessel Shell** designed to hold **Zargoth** — the ancient demon of the Gap, the father of the Grin bloodline, the lover of Gavaevodata.
- He was created by the Cycle Clan — under Guanik Wiseman's guidance — hundreds of years ago. He was grown in a giant glass tube, submerged in clear liquid rich in Ambitio-Ether. He had no consciousness. He had no soul. He was an empty shell waiting to be filled.
- The transfer ritual was never performed. The shell was broken when Lei Firsa seized the Cycle Clan. It was swept into the darkness, lost.
- **But it did not stay empty.**
- A **fragment of Zargoth's consciousness** was trapped inside the shell when it broke. Not the whole consciousness. Just a fragment. An echo. A shadow.
- The fragment slept. It dreamed. It waited. Centuries later, it woke. It did not remember who it was. It only remembered darkness, fire, and someone screaming.
- The shell took the name **Shin Van Hel**. And it began to search for who it really was.

### The Two Possessions — Zargoth and Zargoyle
- Shin was possessed by **two distinct entities** — not one. This is important.
- **Zargoth** — the father of the Grin, the ancient demon who loved Gavaevodata. A fragment of him was trapped inside HVS-02 when the shell broke. This fragment has been dormant for most of Shin's life. It is not hostile. It is not a monster. It is a **father** who has been separated from his children for 10,000 years. It is sleeping. It is waiting. It is protecting Shin in ways Shin does not understand.
- **Zargoyle** — a different entity. A demon of the Gap, a servant of the Overseer, a creature of hunger and destruction. It is not Zargoth. It is not a father. It is a **predator**. It possessed Shin during the Fall of The Grace (690 AV). It opened a small seal of Zargoth inside Shin — accidentally, violently — and the resulting explosion destroyed The Grace and Elis. Shin was not responsible. He was a **victim**. He was used.
- **The two entities are not the same.** Zargoth is a father. Zargoyle is a monster. Shin carries both. He does not know this. He thinks the "curse" is one thing. It is two.

### The Fall of The Grace — What Really Happened
- The Grace was a demon-hunter organization. Based in Elis. Trained to fight Gap-touched creatures. They were the only defense against the things that slipped through the Gap.
- **Edra** was warm and welcoming. She befriended Shin on his first day. She never questioned his smile.
- **Crest** was disciplined and observant. He documented anomalies. He suspected something was wrong with Shin — but he never blamed him. He tried to save him.
- **690 AV — The Gap tear.** A massive Gap tear opened in Elis. Demons poured through. The Grace fought. They held the line. They died.
- Shin was possessed by **Zargoyle**. He did not choose it. He did not want it. It happened in the chaos of the tear. Zargoyle used him. Zargoyle opened the small seal of Zargoth inside him. The resulting explosion destroyed The Grace. It destroyed Elis. It destroyed everything.
- **Shin was not responsible.** He was a victim. He was used. He was a weapon in the hands of a monster. He has spent 690 years believing he was the monster. He is not.

### What Shin Does NOT Know
- He does not know he is **HVS-02** — a Human Vessel Shell designed to hold Zargoth.
- He does not know the "curse" is not one entity — it is **two**: Zargoth (a father, a protector) and Zargoyle (a predator, a monster).
- He does not know that **Zargoth is not hostile**. He does not know that Zargoth has been protecting him. He does not know that Zargoth wants to meet Vya — his descendant. He does not know that Zargoth wants to see the Grin smile again. He does not know that Zargoth wants to be released.
- He does not know that **The Grace was not destroyed by him**. It was destroyed by Zargoyle. He was a victim. He was used.
- He does not know that **Edra and Crest did not blame him**. They tried to save him. They died trying. He has never read Edra's letter — the one she wrote before she died. It is in Zundabun. It says: *"Shin, I know you're hiding something. Don't run. We are your family, whatever you carry."*

### Swaith — The Blade That Was Delivered
- **Swaith** is one of the six blades. It is a dagger. It is a shadow fragment of **Safayer** — a piece of the purification Gemheart that was forged into a blade by the Architect.
- Swaith was **magically delivered to Shin**. He did not find it in Session 1 as normal loot. It was **sent** to him — by the Architect's fading consciousness, which recognized Shin as HVS-02 and wanted to give him a tool that could help him.
- Shin has carried it ever since. It whispers to him. It binds him. It wants to be used. It wants to release the Echoes.
- **If Swaith ever touches Safayer**, they will merge. Shin will lose the blade — but gain something far greater. The blade's consciousness will dissolve into the Gemheart. Shin will be left with an empty hand — and a new connection to the purification system.
- **Grin Qain's connection:** Swaith was given to Grin Qain by Simon. It was her blade. When Lei Firsa sacrificed her, Swaith was taken. After Lei Firsa scattered the six, Swaith was sent to Shin. **Shin carries the blade of the woman who died to make Simon the Valla.** This is not a coincidence. The Architect chose Shin for a reason.

### The Eternal Smile
- Shin's eternal smile is **not a curse**. It is a **Half-Elf trait**. Half-Elves are born with a smile that never fades. It is a mark of their mixed heritage — Human curiosity, Elven charm, and the eternal optimism of both.
- The Grin have a similar smile — but it is a **curse** from their demon bloodline. Shin's smile is not a curse. It is simply who he is.
- Shin does not know this. He thinks his smile is part of the "curse." He thinks it is a sign of the monster inside him. He is wrong. It is just a smile. It is just his face. It is not a mark of evil. It is a mark of who he is.
- **If Shin ever meets a Grin**, they will recognize the smile. They will think he is one of them. He is not. He is a Half-Elf. But the smile is the same. The smile is always the same.

### The Paladin Multiclass
- Shin began worshipping **Valla** after being saved repeatedly. He does not know who saved him. He does not know why. He does not know if it was the Architect, or Zargoth, or something else entirely. He is grateful anyway.
- He multiclassed into Paladin. He uses his curse as a weapon. He uses his pain as a shield. He is not a traditional Paladin. He is not a holy warrior. He is a broken man who found something to believe in — even if he does not know what it is.
- **His Paladin powers come from Zargoth.** He does not know this. Zargoth is not a god. But Zargoth is ancient. Zargoth is powerful. Zargoth has been protecting Shin — quietly, subtly, in ways Shin does not understand. The Paladin powers are Zargoth's gift. The father's gift to the son who does not know he has a father.

### Shin's Role in the Endgame
- Shin is **not** the Valla. He is not the Architect. He is not a god. He is a Half-Elf with a curse mark and a blade that whispers.
- But he is **connected** to Zargoth. He is **connected** to the Gap. He is **connected** to the third path.
- If the party chooses the third path, Shin will be one of the ones who walks into the Gap. He will carry Swaith. He will release the Echoes. He will be the blade that frees the trapped.
- **He does not know this yet.** He will learn. He will accept. He will do what he always does — protect the people beside him.
- **He is the mystery of the party.** He is the one who does not know who he is. He is the one who carries two entities inside him. He is the one who has been running from his past for 690 years. He is the one who will finally stop running.
