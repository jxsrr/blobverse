## Part 1: The Storm

The Go Ingsani cut through the **Elis Ocean** — a small submarine, repainted black, running on stolen Commonwealth fuel. The storm was not natural. The waves rose like walls. The sky was the color of a bruise.

They were heading for the **anomaly** — a coordinate Syl had seen in a dream, a place where the Gap was thin, a place where the world was **cracked**.

The sea **curved**. Not a wave — the water itself bent. The Go Ingsani groaned. The hull buckled. And then the **vortex** opened — a hole in reality, a tear in the world, a mouth that swallowed the ship whole.

They did not drown. They did not die. They **fell**.

## Part 2: Argent

They landed in **Noorlimpost** — a nation in the world of **Aede**. The city was **Argent** — industrial, gray, choked with smog. Factories. Smokestacks. Sirens.

This was not Oversee. This was another world — another dimension in the **blobverse**. A world where magic was **banned**. A world where the Rapture had already come — and was still coming.

They were captured within the hour. They had caused a disturbance. They had used magic. They had broken the law.

The man who captured them was **Torv** — a member of the **Regalia**, the elite guardians of Aede. He was disciplined, stiff, pessimistic. He carried a giant shield. He did not smile.

They were taken to a holding cell. They were questioned. Then — **darkness**.

## Part 3: The First Reset

They woke on the deck of the **Go Ingsani**. The storm was the same. The waves were the same. The vortex opened the same way. They fell.

They landed in **Argent**. The same city. The same smog. The same sirens. They were captured by **Torv** again. The same man. The same words. The same cell.

**Something is wrong.**

They broke out again. They went to the **Blackened Lung** again. The Regalia was there again — Torv, Dust, Ornis, Lyrith, Reins. The same vigil. The same suicide mission. The same words.

Reins said: *"Tonight. Regnum Solara. We end this."*

It was the same conversation. Word for word. Beat for beat.

**They died.** Or the world did. It did not matter. They woke on the Go Ingsani.

## Part 4: The Loop

They lost count.

**Loop 4:** They tried to stop the Regalia. They convinced Torv not to execute his brother. They kept Dust's wife alive. They saved Ornis from execution. They saved Lyrith from herself. **No one died.** The Regalia was whole. **The world ended anyway.** The gap opened. The vortex consumed everything. They woke on the ship.

**Loop 7:** They tried to give the Regalia **Safayer**. They thought purification might help. **It didn't.** The world ended. They woke on the ship.

**Loop 11:** They tried to **leave**. They tried to find another way back to Oversee. **There was no other way.** The world ended. They woke on the ship.

**Loop 14:** They tried to **reason** with Reins. They told her about Oversee. They told her about Ama. They told her the truth. She listened. She nodded. She jumped into the gap anyway. **The world ended.** They woke on the ship.

**Loop 19:** They tried to **kill the Regalia**. They thought if the Regalia couldn't do the ritual, maybe the world would survive. **They were wrong.** The world ended. They woke on the ship.

They were trapped. Every choice led to the same ending. Every death led back to the ship. The loop would not break.

## Part 5: Something Is Wrong With Ama

They noticed it in **Loop 23**.

**Zure Ama** was not acting like himself. He was too calm. Too certain. Too knowing. He said things before they happened. He made choices that were not his own. He was **steering** the party — subtly, quietly, in ways they did not notice at first.

When they confronted him, he **panicked**. He said: *"There is no other way. This is how it has to be. This is how it always has been."*

But he was wrong. There **was** another way. He just did not want to see it.

## Part 6: The Waking

They realized it in **Loop 27**.

**Echo Atlan** was inside Zure Ama. Or rather, he was **controlling** him. The Echo of the oceanographer — the fragment of the man who had loved Reins, who had married Reins, who had died in the ritual — had **hijacked** Zure Ama's body. He was using Ama's connection to Reins — their friendship, their bond, their shared grief — to keep the timeline on track. To make sure Reins jumped into the Gap. To make sure Aede died.

The party had to wake Ama up. They had to break Echo Atlan's control. They had to reach the man underneath.

**Present-day Ama** — the real one, the one with the frozen smile and the Imp arm — punched himself. Hard. Again and again. He screamed: *"WAKE UP! WAKE UP! THIS IS NOT YOUR MEMORY! THIS IS NOT YOUR DEATH!"*

**Zure Ama woke up.**

The loop broke. The truth came out.

## Part 7: The Truth About Echo Atlan

**Echo Atlan** appeared. Not as an ally — as an adversary.

He was **broken**. He was **grieving**. He was a fragment of a man who had loved his wife so much that he had used his Echo abilities — the wild, uncontrollable powers of a soul outside time — to **reset the timeline** over and over again. He was trying to save Aede. He was trying to save Reins. He was trying to be with her, one more time.

He did not care that the world was doomed. He did not care that the loop was torture. He did not care that the party was suffering. He only cared about **her**.

> *"She is my wife. She is my life. She is everything I ever loved. I cannot let her die. I cannot let this world burn. I will reset it again. I will reset it forever. I will find a way to save her."*

The party tried to reason with him. They told him the truth: **Aede is doomed**. The Rapture is a certainty. The Gap is too thin. The world has always been dying. The Regalia's mission was always a suicide mission. There was never a way to save them. There was never a way to save her.

Echo Atlan **refused to listen**.

## Part 8: The Choice

The party had to let Aede die.

They had to stop trying to save everyone. They had to let Torv execute his brother. They had to let Dust kill his wife. They had to let Ornis be executed. They had to let Lyrith hang herself. They had to let Reins jump into the Gap.

**They had to let fate happen.**

They let it happen. The timeline played out. The Regalia fought. The Regalia died. Reins jumped. The anchor exploded. The world ended.

**Echo Atlan stopped time.**

The ash froze in the air. The light stopped moving. The world was silent. Echo Atlan stood in the center of the frozen crater. He screamed: *"NO! NO! NO! I WON'T LET YOU! I WON'T LET HER DIE! I WON'T LET HER DIE AGAIN!"*

**The party fought him.**

## Part 9: The Last Reset

The battle was **impossible**. Echo Atlan was an Echo — a fragment of a soul outside time. He could not be killed with a sword. He could not be cut. He could not be burned. But he could be **reached**.

**Ama** — the real Ama, the one with the frozen smile and the Imp arm — spoke to him. Not as an enemy. As a **friend**.

> *"I loved her too. I loved her more than I ever told her. I am sorry. I am so sorry. But we cannot save her. We cannot save this world. We can only let her rest. Let her rest, Atlan. Let her rest."*

Echo Atlan **broke**. He wept. He screamed. He was not a monster. He was a **grieving man** who had lost everything. He had used his Echo abilities to reset the timeline because he could not accept the truth. He had trapped the party in a loop because he could not let go.

**He let go.**

The time stop ended. The ash rained down. The world of Aede was gone. The Regalia was gone. Reins was gone.

Echo Atlan faded. He did not die — he was already dead. He simply **stopped**. He returned to the Gap. He returned to the void. He returned to his wife — not in life, but in memory.

## Part 10: The Bottom of the Sea

They woke at the **bottom of the Elis Ocean**.

The Go Ingsani was gone. The storm was gone. The vortex was gone. They were in the dark. They were in the deep. They were alive.

They did not know how. They did not know why. They only knew they had survived. They had done the impossible. They had let the world die — and they had survived.

### The Truth About Reins
- **Reins knew.** She knew Aede was doomed. She knew the Rapture would consume everything. She knew the only way to save the dimension was to seal it — to detonate the Artificial Gap and prevent the Overseer from ever finding it again.
- **She hid it from Ama.** She did not tell him the world was ending. She did not tell him the Regalia was a suicide mission. She did not tell him she was going to die. She wanted him to have hope. She wanted him to live. She wanted him to remember her smiling.
- **She designed the Artificial Gap.** She built the portal. She built the machine that would seal the dimension. She built her own grave.
- **She jumped into the Gap.** She did not survive. She became part of the Gap — a fragment of a soul, a memory of a woman, a whisper in the dark. She is still there. She is still watching. She is still waiting.

### The Truth About Aede
- **Aede is one of the worlds in the blobverse.** It is not Oversee. It is another **dimension** — another world in the same cosmic ecosystem, another farm for the Overseer.
- **Aede had a thin Gap layer.** This is why the Rapture happened so often there. The Gap is the barrier between the world and the Overseer. When it is thin, the Overseer notices the world more easily. When it notices, it feeds. Aede was unlucky. Its Gap was always thin. The Rapture came again and again — not once every few thousand years, but every few **centuries**.
- **The government of Aede banned magic.** This was the only way to survive. If no one used magic, the Overseer would not notice. If the Overseer did not notice, the Rapture would not come. It was a desperate measure. It was a losing battle. But it was all they had.
- **Only one city remains in Aede — Argent.** The rest of the world is ruins. The rest of the world is ash. The rest of the world is **gone**. Argent is the last city. Argent is the last hope. Argent is the last meal.
- **The blob Syl saw in Arujaya is the Overseer.** Its tentacles reach into Aede through the sky. It is always there. It is always watching. It is always hungry.

### The Truth About Vya
- **Vya is Hildag's design.** She was bred to combine the pure magic of Klan Lei with the anti-magic invisibility of the Grin. She is the only one who can use magic without being detected by the Overseer. She is the only one who can fight the Rapture without becoming its victim.
- **She does not know this.** She thinks she is just a ranger. She thinks the fire is a curse. She thinks the smile is a burden. She is wrong. She is the hope of the blobverse. She is the only one who can save the world.
- **The party must protect her.** They must keep her alive. They must help her master her fire. They must help her become what she was designed to be. If she dies, the blobverse dies with her.

### The Mechanics of the Session
- **The loop is the session's structure.** The DM should run the first loop normally. The party should experience Aede. They should meet the Regalia. They should try to save everyone. They should fail.
- **The second loop should feel familiar.** The party should notice things repeating. The DM should describe the same scenes, the same words, the same beats.
- **The third loop should be a shock.** The party should realize they are trapped. They should start experimenting. They should try everything.
- **The fourth loop should be the breaking point.** They should notice something wrong with Ama. They should confront him. They should wake him up.
- **The fifth loop is the confrontation.** They should let Aede die. They should fight Echo Atlan. They should break the loop.
- **The sixth loop is the escape.** They should wake at the bottom of the Elis Ocean. They should survive. They should move on.

### The DM's Role
- **The DM should not force the party to save Aede.** Aede is doomed. It was always doomed. The party must learn that they cannot change fate. They cannot save everyone. They can only accept it.
- **The DM should not punish the party for trying.** They should try. They should fail. They should try again. They should fail again. That is the point. The point is to learn that they cannot change the past — only accept it.
- **The DM should let the party break the loop.** They should feel the weight of the loops. They should feel the frustration. They should feel the desperation. And then they should feel the release when they finally let go.
- **The DM should make Echo Atlan sympathetic.** He is not a monster. He is a grieving husband. He is a man who lost everything. He is a man who could not let go. The party should feel sorry for him. They should pity him. They should understand him.
