> **Role:** Literature teacher at FBA. Teaches with a well-kept moustache and excessive hand gestures.
> **Where:** FBA Campus, Calamara
> **Status:** Alive

## Appearance
- A middle-aged man with a thick moustache he is always trimming.
- A velvet jacket. A shirt with ruffles. Leather boots.
- Always carrying a book. Always reciting something. Always dramatic.
- Excessive hand gestures. Excessive facial expressions. A voice that always rises and falls.

## Personality
- Dramatic. Theatrical. A storyteller.
- Teaches students to tell stories — and to listen to them.
- Believes that **story is the best way to understand the world**. "History is a story. Poetry is a story. Even a battle is a story. Everything is a story."
- Never talks about his past. Never.

## What the Party Knows
- Literature teacher. One of the most dramatic instructors at FBA.
- Teaches with stories. Makes literature feel alive.
- Knows many stories about Calamara. Knows many stories about FBA.

## What He Offers
- **Storytelling skill.** If the party wants to learn to tell stories, to write, to make others listen — he is the best teacher.
- **Information about stories.** He knows every story in Calamara. He knows every legend. He knows every myth.
- **Clues about The Bell.** If the party asks about The Bell, he may speak. But he will speak in **story**.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### Who Maestro Lorenzo Is
- **Maestro Lorenzo** is the finest storyteller in Calamara. He was born into an artistic family. He grew up with stories. He lives with stories. He will die with stories.
- He has taught at FBA for over twenty years. He has taught many students. He has heard many stories. He has told many stories.
- He **knows about the Battery**. He knows about Continuity Work. He knows about Bellweather. He knows because he **hears** — not from people, but from **stories**. Stories that were never written. Stories that were never told. Stories that only exist in **whispers**.
- He **does not care**. He does not care about politics. He does not care about power. He only cares about **story**. And the story about the Battery is a story that **ends**. The story about Bellweather is a story that **ends**. The story about Lilie is a story that **interests** him.
- He will help the party — not because he cares about the world, but because he wants to **see the end of the story**.

### Maestro Lorenzo and The Bell
- He has heard The Bell his whole life. He knows it is not a myth. He knows it is a **story**.
- **If the party asks about The Bell**, he will stop trimming his moustache. He will look at the party with bright eyes. He will say: *"The Bell rings before exams. That is a story about preparation. The Bell rings before the dance. That is a story about courtship. The Bell rings before graduation. That is a story about... departure."*
- He will add: *"But the real story is not about the bell. The real story is about **what happens afterward**. And that story... has never been told."*

### Maestro Lorenzo and Mr. Rababrie Bellweather
- He knows **Rababrie Bellweather** as headmaster. He also knows him as **Bellweather**. He knows about that family. He knows about their power. He knows about **what they do**.
- *"Rababrie is a good man,"* he once said. *"But he is a Bellweather. And Bellweather always finishes what they start. Even if it means destroying the world."*
- He does not hate Rababrie. He is not afraid of him. He only **wants to know**. He wants to know how this story will end. He wants to know whether Rababrie will become a hero — or a villain. Or both.

### Maestro Lorenzo's Role in Season 3
- If the party wants to learn storytelling, Lorenzo will teach them. He will teach them structure. He will teach them emotion. He will teach them **truth**.
- If the party wants to fight Bellweather, Lorenzo will help with **story**. He will tell them stories about Bellweather. He will tell them stories about FBA. He will tell them stories about **what is going to happen**.
- **If Lorenzo dies**, FBA will lose its storyteller. And the world will lose one of the few people who knows **the real story**.

### Maestro Lorenzo's Secret
- He was once part of **Bellweather** — not as a family member, but as a **writer**. He is the one who created the myth about The Bell. He is the one who created the story about the Bride of Bellweather. He is the one who made **nonsense** into **legend**.
- He stopped when he realised that the story was **true**. When he realised that The Bell really rings. When he realised that the Bride really exists. When he realised that he had helped a **monster**.
- He keeps the **original draft** of the myth of The Bell. The draft contains **the truth** — not the story about the bell, but the story about **the Battery**. He keeps it on his desk. He has never destroyed it. He has never published it. He only keeps it as a reminder.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Lorenzo was once part of Bellweather.
- Lorenzo is the creator of the myth of The Bell.
- Lorenzo keeps the original draft of the myth — containing the truth.
- Lorenzo will help the party — because he wants to see the end of the story.
