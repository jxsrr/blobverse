> **Role:** Vivienne's father. Researcher of magical artefacts. Head of the primary Ashbourne lineage.
> **Where:** Ashbourne Manor, Outskirts of Calamara
> **Status:** Alive

## Appearance
- Elf. Middle-aged. Dark brown hair with streaks of white at the temples.
- Gold-framed reading glasses. Classic clothes — a woollen suit, a white shirt, a bow tie.
- Always carrying a book or manuscript. Hands that are always clean — a scholar's hands, not a fighter's.
- A calm gaze. A rare smile — but warm when it appears.

## Personality
- Calm. Analytical. Never rushed.
- Deeply loves **knowledge** — and **his daughter**.
- Worried about Vivienne — but never says so directly.
- Knows more about Ashbourne history than he reveals.

## What the Party Knows
- Vivienne's father. Researcher of magical artefacts.
- Head of the primary Ashbourne lineage.
- Lives at Ashbourne Manor.
- Gave Vivienne a silver ring, an heirloom of the family.
- Left her with a warning: *"Be careful. Not everything at FBA is what it appears to be."*
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- Edmund **knows** about the manuscript Vivienne found. He has not forbidden her from reading it — but he is **worried**.
- He **knows** that the Ashbourne family is hiding something. He does not know **what** — but he knows there is something.
- He **does not know** about the FBA Battery, RAIB, or Bellweather. He only knows that FBA holds **secrets** — and he hopes Vivienne does not find them.
- **DM Note:** Edmund is a good father. He is not an antagonist. He is a parent trying to protect his child from a truth that may be too heavy to carry.
