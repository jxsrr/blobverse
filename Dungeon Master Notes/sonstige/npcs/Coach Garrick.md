- Sports trousers. A whistle at his neck. Sports shoes that are always clean.
- Short hair. A strong jaw. A duel scar on his left arm.

- Never speaks about his past as a duel athlete. Never.
- Trains hard. But fair. He never asks for something that cannot be done.

- The Physical Education teacher. Former duel athlete.

### Who Coach Garrick Is
- **Coach Garrick** is a former professional duel athlete. He was a champion. He was a legend. He was a name known throughout Calamara.
- He stopped after **the accident**. An accident he never speaks of. An accident that ended his career. An accident that cost him something — not just ability, but also a friend.
- He became a teacher. He became a coach. He teaches students to fight. He teaches students to win. He teaches students not to repeat his mistakes.
- He **does not know** about the Battery. He does not know about Jounce as a harvest. He only knows that Jounce is a sport. He only knows that The Bateleur Eagle is a good team. He only knows that Lilia is a good captain.
- He **will be furious** if he finds out. He will be furious at Bellweather. He will be furious at FBA. He will be furious at himself for not noticing.

### Coach Garrick and The Bell
- He has heard The Bell his whole life. He has never thought about it. It is only a myth. It is only a story.
- **If the party asks about The Bell**, he will shrug. *"The Bell rings. That is all. Nothing to think about. Focus on training."*
- But he will add: *"There is one thing. Every time the bell rings before graduation, I lose a student. Not a bad student. A good student. A strong student. A student who could become a great athlete. They leave. And they never come back."*

### Coach Garrick and Mr. Rababrie Bellweather
- He knows **Rababrie Bellweather** as the headmaster. He respects him. He has never spoken to him outside of school matters.
- *"The headmaster is a good man,"* he once said. *"He supports the team. He supports the athletes. He supports Jounce. But he never comes to practice. He never watches us train. He only comes to the matches. And he is always smiling."*

### Coach Garrick and Lilia
- **Lilia Etheridge II** is the current captain of **The Bateleur Eagle**. Coach Garrick is **proud** of her. He sees Lilia as the **best athlete** he has ever trained.
- He **does not know** that Lilia will become the Bride. If he knew, he would be **furious**. He would **fight**. He would **die** to protect her.
- **If Brougha asks about Lilia**, Coach Garrick will smile. He will say: *"Lilia is a good kid. She is a good captain. She is a good athlete. But she is too... sacrificial. She always gives everything. That is what makes her great. But it is also what makes her... fragile."*
- He will not say more than that. But he will **look** at Brougha with eyes that **know**.

### Coach Garrick's Role in Season 3
- If the party wants to become stronger, Garrick will train them. He will train them until they cannot stand. He will train them until they can stand again.
- If the party wants to fight Bellweather, Garrick will help with **strength**. He will not fight with magic. He will fight with fists. He will fight with his body. He will fight with **rage**.
- **If Garrick dies**, FBA will lose its best teacher. Duel athletes will lose their best coach.

### Coach Garrick's Secret
- He was once part of **The Bateleur Eagle** — the first Jounce team. He was its captain. He was a legend.
- He stopped after **The Bateleur Incident** — 2016. He watched his team erased by RAIB. He watched them disappear. He has never been able to forget.
- He does not know what happened. He does not know about RAIB. He only knows his team is gone. And he has never been able to explain it.
- He keeps a **team photo** in his wallet. The photo is faded. But he has never thrown it away.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Garrick is the former captain of The Bateleur Eagle.
- Garrick witnessed The Bateleur Incident in 2016.
- Garrick does not know about RAIB. He only knows his team is gone.
- Garrick will be furious if he finds out about the Battery.
- Garrick will be furious if he finds out about Lilia.
