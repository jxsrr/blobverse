## Appearance
- A middle-aged man with grey hair always neatly kept.
- A woollen suit with a classic cut. A white shirt. A bow tie.
- Reading glasses that always sit at the tip of his nose.
- Always carrying a folder. Always carrying documents. Always carrying **evidence**.

## Personality
- Analytical. Logical. Never emotional.
- Teaches using real case studies from Calamara.
- Believes that **law is the foundation of everything**. "Without law, there is no society. Without society, there is no civilisation. Without civilisation, there is no **us**."
- Never speaks about his past as a lawyer. Never.

## What the Party Knows
- Civics & Magical Law teacher. Former lawyer.
- Teaches using real case studies from Calamara.
- Knows the law. Knows the loopholes. Knows how to avoid them.

## What He Offers
- **Knowledge of the law.** If the party wants to learn about Anderes law, about their rights, about how to stay out of trouble — he is the best teacher.
- **Information about Continuity Work.** If the party asks about the legality of Continuity Work, he may speak. But he will speak about **the law**.
- **Legal help.** If the party needs a lawyer, he can help. But he will ask for **evidence**.

%% DM SECRETS %%

### Who Prof. Quill Is
- **Prof. Quill** is the finest lawyer in Calamara. He has won many cases. He has freed many clients. He has defeated many opponents.
- He stopped practising law after **his final case**. A case he never speaks of. A case that cost him his faith in the system. A case that made him realise that **the law is not about justice** — but about **who can pay more**.
- He became a teacher. He teaches students about the law. He teaches students about justice. He teaches students about **the truth**.
- He **knows about the Battery**. He knows about Continuity Work. He knows about Bellweather. He knows because he **reads** — not books, but **documents**. Documents he should not have read. Documents he found in the FBA archives.
- He **knows that Continuity Work is legal**. He knows that the Anderes government has passed legislation permitting FBA to "send away" students. He knows that **nothing can be done** under the law.
- He is **angry**. Not because the law is wrong — but because the law is **right**. Because the system **works**. Because nothing can be done.

### Prof. Quill and The Bell
- He has heard The Bell his whole life. He knows it is not a myth. He knows it is an **alarm**.
- **If the party asks about The Bell**, he will stop reading. He will look at the party with sharp eyes. He will say: *"The Bell rings before exams. That is legal. The Bell rings before the dance. That is legal. The Bell rings before graduation. That is legal. All of it is legal. All of it is **lawful**. And that is the problem."*
- He will add: *"Law is not always about justice. Sometimes, law is only about **power**. And Bellweather has the power."*

### Prof. Quill and Mr. Rababrie Bellweather
- He knows **Rababrie Bellweather** as the headmaster. He also knows him as **Bellweather**. He knows about that family. He knows about their power. He knows about **what they do**.
- *"Rababrie is a good man,"* he once said. *"But he is a Bellweather. And Bellweather always finishes what they start. Even if it means breaking the law. Even if it means **making** the law."*
- He does not hate Rababrie. He is not afraid of him. He is only **frustrated**. Because he knows that Rababrie has not broken the law. Rababrie has **made** the law. And no one can stop him.

### Prof. Quill's Role in Season 3
- If the party wants to learn about the law, Quill will teach them. He will teach them about their rights. He will teach them about loopholes. He will teach them about **how to survive** in a corrupt system.
- If the party wants to fight Bellweather, Quill will help with **the law**. He will find a loophole. He will find evidence. He will find a **way** to stop them — legally.
- **If Quill dies**, FBA will lose the teacher who knows the law best. And the world will lose one of the few people who can **defeat** Bellweather in court.

### Prof. Quill's Secret
- He has a **copy** of the law that legalises Continuity Work. That copy has **marginal notes** — notes he wrote himself. Notes about **loopholes**. Notes about **weaknesses**. Notes about **ways** to overturn the law.
- He keeps that copy on his desk. In a locked drawer. He has never shown it to anyone. He is waiting for someone who can **use it**.
- If the party finds it, he will give it. But he will warn them: *"This is the **only way** to stop them legally. But it is also **dangerous**. Because if you use this, you will make a very powerful enemy. And they will not stop until you are **destroyed**."*

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- Quill knows about the Battery. He knows that it is legal.
- Quill has a copy of the law with marginal notes — loopholes to overturn it.
- Quill will help the party — with the law. But he will not fight.
- Quill will warn the party about the **danger** of fighting Bellweather.
