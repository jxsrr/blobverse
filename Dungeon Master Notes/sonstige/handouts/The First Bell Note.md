> [!quote] A slip of paper, found on the roadside on the way home, after The Bell rang in the afternoon.

*"Don't let them hear you."*

Handwriting. Black ink. The paper is cold — colder than it should be.

On the back of the paper, another line — smaller, almost unreadable:

*"The Bell rings for those who are about to leave. You will not leave. Not yet."*

---

*No signature. No name. Just two sentences that make no sense.*
