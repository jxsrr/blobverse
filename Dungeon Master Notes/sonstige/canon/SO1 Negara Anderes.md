> [!info] Negara Anderes
> The primary country of the Sonstige campaign. Home to the city of **Calamara** and **The Free Bird Academy**.

## What Negara Anderes Is
- A country with modern technology (2026 equivalent).
- It has a central government, a military, and a bureaucracy.
- One of its secret programs: **Continuity Work** — using the school to thicken the Void.
- Capital: (TBD).
- Primary campaign city: **Calamara**.

## The Continuity Program
- **The Free Bird Academy (FBA)** is built atop an ancient **Void-Well**.
- The school's basement is the **core battery**.
- Students are not told they are fuel.
- They are told they are "extraordinary."
- **Jounce** is a key extracurricular. The sport produces the emotional energy the Battery needs: trust, debt, rivalry, sacrifice.

## Alumni Delivery
- After graduating, students are "placed" by the government.
- In reality, they are taken to **Continuity Houses** and made into **void-anchors** — human ballast inside the Battery.
- No one sees them again.
- **The Black Cloak Figure** is the delivery agent.
- **The Bell** rings before graduation — a sign that the harvest is about to begin.

## What the Party Knows
- Anderes is the country where the campaign takes place.
- There is a school that has been turned into a battery.
- Alumni are "sent away" and never seen again.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- The Anderes government knows about the Overseer. They know the Void is thinning. They know the Rapture is coming.
- They have chosen a cheap, fast, inhumane solution: a **school battery**.
- The program is led by **Bellweather** — the family/entity that has existed since 2001.
- **The Black Cloak Figure** is a Bellweather agent. They appear at every delivery, every erasure, every RAIB.
- The Anderes government are not villains. They are frightened people. They are trying to save the world in the wrong way.
