## Family — Grafsten (Not Actually Family)
- **Grafsten** is the ruling family of **Grafstenmouth** — a small, underdeveloped port town at the tip of the **Southeast Peninsula**.
- It has recently suffered a **dispute with the imperial family**. It is currently trying to restore its reputation.
- **Zenroy is NOT an official member of the Grafsten family.** He is an **employee** hired by the family as part of their effort to produce an expert who can develop their region.
- Home: one of the empty rooms in the Grafsten family's great house. The house is large, but Zenroy is not given much space of his own. Enough to live comfortably and simply.
- Situation at the residence: quiet. The family looks after its own members. But it is currently facing ostracisation from other noble families because of the dispute with the imperial family.

## Family Demands
- Build relationships with various factions that will support Grafsten's development.
- Restore the honour of the Grafsten family through academic and non-academic achievements.

### Who Zenroy Is — The Employee, Not the Heir
- **Zenroy Dala** is an **employee** of the Grafsten family — not a family member. He was hired because he can read, write, and do arithmetic. He is the Grafsten family's **investment** to develop their region.
- **His original name and age are a state secret of Grafstenmouth.** Registered as: **Zenroy Dala (20)**. His true age — and true name — are unknown. Possibly even to Zenroy himself.
- He is **not from this world**. He is from Earth. Narnia'd. Amnesiac about his past.
- **Contradictions the DM must resolve:**
  - He remembers Earth. But he also remembers **Calamara** — though he has never been there.
  - He remembers **FBA** — though he has never set foot in it.
  - He remembers **The Bell** — though he has never heard it.
  - **How?**

### The RAIB Question — Player Input
- The player answered **"Yes"** — Zenroy has remembered something that never happened, and that memory felt more real than his own memories.
- **The contents of that memory are UNDECIDED.** This is a DM-player hook. Possible directions:
  - Does Zenroy remember **a life in Calamara** — though he has never been there?
  - Does Zenroy remember **FBA** — though he is only about to enter it?
  - Does Zenroy remember **The Bell** — though he has never heard it?
  - Does Zenroy remember **someone else's life** — someone who was once a RAIB?
- **The DM must discuss with Dimas** to decide the contents of that memory — and whether that memory is tied to **Zenroy (NPC)**, **RAIB**, or **Oversee**.

### Zenroy — Canon Conflict
- **Two Zenroys in Sonstige canon:**
  1. **Zenroy (NPC)** — a Lei from Oversee who arrived in Sonstige in 2001 through a Void rift. Mentioned in `SO3 The Three Dates.md`.
  2. **Zenroy Dala (PC)** — a Grafsten employee, a person from Earth, joining FBA in 2026.
- **DM must decide the relationship:**
  - **Option A (Recommended):** Zenroy Dala is a **descendant** of Zenroy from Oversee. His Lei blood is still there, though he does not realise it. He does not remember Earth — he remembers **Oversee**, and has mistaken that memory for a memory of Earth. This explains why he remembers Calamara, FBA, and The Bell.
  - **Option B:** Zenroy Dala is the **Zenroy from Oversee** who lost his memories and was reprogrammed by the Grafstens. The name "Dala" is a name given to him. He remembers Earth because that is a **false memory**.
  - **Option C:** Zenroy Dala is a **different person**. The name "Zenroy Dala" is a **borrowed name** — a name the Grafstens use for every employee they send to FBA.
- **Recommended:** Option A — preserves the Earth backstory, ties into Oversee lore, and explains the "Yes" on the RAIB question.

### Zenroy and the Grafsten Dispute
- The Grafsten family is being **targeted** by the imperial family. Carrying the Grafsten name outside Grafstenmouth is **dangerous**.
- This is **external pressure** — Zenroy must be careful. He cannot make friends carelessly. He cannot speak carelessly.
- **If Zenroy stands out too much at FBA**, the imperial family may notice. And they may use him to pressure Grafsten.

### Zenroy's Role in the Campaign
- The party's **brain**. Wizard. Reads books. Solves puzzles.
- The party's **mystery**. Has memories he should not. Connected to something larger.
- The party's **diplomat** — or at least, the one who wants to befriend everyone.

### Zenroy's Fate
- If Zenroy finds **his journal** — a journal he wrote in a language he does not know — he may be able to read what he wrote. He may be able to understand the symbols.
- If Zenroy finds **the truth** about himself — that he is a descendant of the Lei, that he is connected to Oversee, that he carries Valla blood — he will face a choice: **accept** or **reject**.
- If Zenroy becomes a RAIB, he will be erased from everyone's memory. But the party will remember him — because they hold a **secret about him**. That secret may be **his journal** — or **his Earth-memory** that is not actually Earth.
