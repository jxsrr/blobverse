## What Matters Here
- A **magical forest** — home to a Wood Elf community.
- The **Cortenya family's treehouse** — a fancy-shmancy house in a big tree. Elite and wealthy.
- Mushrooms grow everywhere. Some species rare. Some dangerous.
- Far from Calamara — a journey of several hours.

## What the Party Knows
- Mya is from this forest.
- The Cortenya family lives here.
- The forest is full of mushrooms.

%% DM SECRETS %%
- This forest is **home to a Wood Elf community** — a closed community that does not welcome outsiders.
- The **Cortenya family** is one of the most respected families here — thanks to their silk trade.
- **DM Note:** The forest is a **beautiful** place — but also a **stifling** one for Mya. She never felt accepted here.
