> [!info] The Hidden Garden
> A hidden garden at the school. Where fruit shaped like a baby's head grows. The source of RAIB.

## What It Is
- A hidden garden within the school grounds.
- Not on any map. Not in any brochure.
- Only certain students know of it. Only certain staff know of it.
- Fruit shaped like a baby's head grows here.

## The Baby-Headed Fruit
- This fruit is the manifestation of **envy**.
- If **X** is envious of **Y** and eats the fruit, reality rewrites: **Y becomes a RAIB**, and **X receives Y's life, possessions, relationships, and place**.
- Everyone forgets Y. X steps into Y's life.

## What the Party Knows
- No one knows about this garden — not yet.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%

### The Hidden Garden — Bellweather's Darkest Secret
- **This garden belongs to Bellweather.** It was **planted** by Bellweather. It is **tended** by Bellweather. It is **hidden** by Bellweather.
- **It is Bellweather's darkest secret** — darker than the Bride of Bellweather, darker than the Battery. Because this garden is the **source** of RAIB. And Bellweather **created it**.
- **How Bellweather found it:**
  - Bellweather did not create the baby-headed fruit. The fruit **grew on its own** — as a manifestation of the envy accumulated around the Void-Well.
  - Bellweather **found it** and **cultivated it**. They built the hidden garden to protect it. They studied how to harvest it.
  - **They realised that envy is a powerful source of energy.** Every X who is envious of a Y, every Y who becomes a RAIB — all of it feeds the Battery.
- **Why Bellweather does it:**
  - They believe they are **saving the world**. The Void is thinning. The Rapture is coming. The only way to hold back the Void is to **fill the seal** — with energy, with sacrifice, with **life**.
  - RAIB is their **means** of filling the seal. Every Y erased is a patch. Every X who gains Y's life is a **patch-filler**.
  - **This garden is a patch factory.**
- **How Bellweather hides it:**
  - The garden is not on any map. Not in any brochure. Not in the school archives.
  - **The Black Cloak Figure** is the garden's guardian. They appear when a RAIB occurs — to ensure no one remembers Y.
  - **Mr. Rababrie Bellweather** knows about this garden. He **guards it**. He **keeps its secret**. He will do **anything** to ensure no one finds it.
- **What happens if this garden is found:**
  - If the party finds this garden, they will find the **truth** about RAIB. They will find that Bellweather **created** RAIB — or at least **exploits it**.
  - If they destroy the garden, RAIB will **stop** — but the Battery will **lose its primary energy source**. The Void will thin faster. The world will end sooner.
  - If they leave the garden, RAIB will keep happening. Students will keep disappearing. Bellweather will keep preying on them.
  - **There is no right answer.** This is a **moral choice** the party must face.
- **The Baby-Headed Fruit — What It Really Is:**
  - The fruit is **not** a natural fruit. The fruit is a **physical manifestation of envy** — accumulated around the Void-Well.
  - It takes the shape of a baby's head because **envy is the most primitive emotion** — like a baby who wants what others have. Bellweather did not create it. Bellweather **found it** and gave it its shape.
  - **The fruit is alive.** It pulses. It is warm. It makes a sound like a heartbeat. This is one reason Bellweather hides it — the fruit is **horrifying**.
  - If someone eats the fruit — an envious **X** — reality rewrites. Y becomes a RAIB. X gains Y's life. **But X also becomes part of the garden.** Every X who eats the fruit will **return** to the garden — as a new tree. A tree grown from their envy.
  - **The garden is a forest of X's who have eaten the fruit.** Every tree is a **student who was once envious**. Every tree is a **beneficiary** who has paid the price.
  - **Bellweather does not tell X about this price.** X only knows that they will gain Y's life. X does not know that they will become a tree.

### What the Party Does NOT Know
- This garden belongs to **Bellweather**.
- Bellweather **found** the fruit and **cultivated it**.
- Bellweather uses RAIB to **fill the Void seal**.
- **The Black Cloak Figure** is the garden's guardian.
- **Mr. Rababrie Bellweather** is the keeper of its secret.
- Every X who eats the fruit will **become a tree** in the garden.
- Destroying the garden will stop RAIB — but will accelerate the Rapture.
