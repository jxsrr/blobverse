> A myth passed down through generations at FBA. A bell that rings before major exams, before the social season's dances, and above all before graduation.

## What The Bell Is
- **The Bell** is a myth passed down through generations at FBA.
- A story of a bell that rings before major exams, before the social season's dances, and above all before graduation.
- No one knows where the sound comes from.
- No one knows who rings it.
- No one knows why it happens.

## What the Students Say
- "The Bell rings before exams. That's a good sign."
- "The Bell rings before the dance. That means the social season will be busy."
- "The Bell rings before graduation. That means we're about to become alumni."
- No one asks **where** the alumni go.

## What the Party Knows
- The Bell is a myth.
- The Bell rings before exams, dances, and graduation.
- No one knows where the sound comes from.
::/pf

%% DM SECRETS %%
- **The Bell is not a myth.** It is the **Battery's alarm**.
- Every toll is a countdown to the next harvest.
- **Major exams** — the Battery needs energy from stress and competition.
- **The social season's dances** — the Battery needs energy from social drama and adolescent emotion.
- **Graduation** — the Battery needs **alumni**. Every final toll is a sign that the harvest is about to begin.
- **Bellweather** — the family/entity behind FBA — took its name from **The Bell**. They are the ones who ring it.
- The Bell does not actually ring. It is a **Void resonance**. Only those connected to the Battery can hear it. But every student hears it — because every student is part of the Battery.
- If the party hears The Bell, they are already connected to the Battery. There is no way out.
